四副一样的牌

AI Parable (inspired by Amanda Askell)·每日精选·2026年10月22日·2分钟阅读

早上七点，温叙把那张图放在黎照桌上，说她不签。图上，这家小小的电梯维保公司被发成四支一样的队伍、四辆车，连库房都没有了。「库房是我九年的膝盖。」她说，「你一笔把它画没了。」窗外晨雾未散，四栋新塔楼各占城区一角，明天联合委员会就把维保合同整批发标，要么给他们，要么给河对岸那家。

Wen Xu laid the chart on Li Zhao's desk at seven in the morning and said she would not sign it. The chart showed their little elevator-maintenance firm dealt into four identical crews and four vans, with no depot at all. "The depot is nine years of my knees," she said. "You've drawn it out of existence." Outside, the morning fog hadn't lifted, and the district's four new towers each held down one corner of the map. Tomorrow the joint committee would award the maintenance contracts as one bundle, to them or to the firm across the river.

「还是同一副牌。」黎照说，「从前我们攥成两个人守库、两支队伍在外。改发成四副一样的手，每副手里压一沓库房牌、三沓外勤牌，每辆车带上你那份家当的四分之一。对，工具要买齐四套，眼下两份合同怎么也盖不住这笔钱；多出来的两份新合同付清了还有富余。账就是这笔账。」

"It's still the same deck," Li Zhao said. "We've been holding it as two keepers and two crews. Dealt into four identical hands instead, every hand carries one stack of depot cards and three of field cards, so each van holds a quarter of what you keep. Yes, the tools get bought four times over. Our two current contracts could never cover that. The two new ones cover it with room to spare. That's the whole arithmetic."

「那你就拿笔把图改了。」温叙说，「委员会以为这套东西是你设计出来的，协会下个月还要登你的专访，什么『黎照四队法』。第四辆车我可以开，只有一个条件：签字之前把实话告诉委员会，别等签完。我的名字不挂在一段创业神话上。」

"Then take your marker and fix it," Wen Xu said. "The committee believes you designed this. The association runs your profile next month — 'the Li Zhao four-crew method.' I'll drive the fourth van on one condition: you tell the committee the truth before they sign, not after. My name doesn't hang on a founding myth."

黎照沉默了很久，久到暖气片嗒嗒响了两次。「四支队伍已经跑了五个星期了。」她终于开口，「你知道的，油料单上有你的签字。头两个星期我们在赔钱，后来他们自己停进了各自的道，上周末我才照着路上已经跑成的样子画了这张图。先有那个形状，图是我讲给不在场的人听的说法。委员会那边，我确实说过这套系统是我造的。我最喜欢的就是那一截——当那个造出它的人。」

Li Zhao was quiet long enough for the radiator to tick twice. "The crews have been running five weeks," she said at last. "You know that; you signed the fuel logs. We bled money the first two weeks, and then the four of them settled into their own lanes, and last weekend I drew the chart from what was already on the road. The shape came first. The chart is how I explain it to people who weren't there. And I did tell the committee I'd built a system. That was my favorite part, being the one who built it."

「那哪张是真的，你的，还是唐迟的？」温叙问，「他那上面连片区都没有，谁空着谁接下一个电话。」「两张都真。」黎照说，「他那套跟我这套，我对着跑了一个月：响应时长、油耗、发票总数，一路对到零头，一个样。这家公司能拿出的任何报表都裁决不了哪张是实话，因为两张记的是同一家公司。你挑一张说得出口的用。」

"So which one is real, yours or Tang Chi's?" Wen Xu asked. "His has no territories at all. Whoever's free takes the next call." "Both are real," Li Zhao said. "I ran his version against mine for a month. Same response times, same fuel, same invoice totals, down to the rounding. No report this company can produce will ever tell you which one is the truth, because both of them book the same company. You pick the one you can say out loud."

第二天上午，黎照对委员会说，这套法子是自己长出来的，她不过照着描了一份；给协会的回信只有两行。主席从图上方看了她一眼。「自己站稳的东西，照样能用。」她说着，把四栋楼整批签给了他们。四个角离得够开，哪两条路线都不相交，谁也不用挤进谁的地界。库房改成了休息室，温叙接了老医院新楼那辆车——那是最讲书面凭证的一家客户——到周五，她那本库房牌文件夹比另外三本都厚出几页，任何单子上都找不着这一笔。

The next morning Li Zhao told the committee the method had grown on its own and she had merely copied it down; her reply to the association ran two lines. The chairwoman looked at her over the chart. "A thing that settles is still a thing that works," she said, and signed all four towers over. The corners sat far enough apart that no two routes ever crossed and no crew ever stood in another's doorway. The depot became a break room, and Wen Xu took the van for the old hospital's new wing — the client that wanted everything in writing — and by Friday her folder of depot cards ran thicker than the other three, which no form anywhere recorded.

第一个星期一，温叙把物料单从头到尾核了一遍，跟另外三辆车一行一行对得上。黎照端着两个杯子倚在休息室门口。「顺眼吗？」她问。温叙合上夹板。「牌发得是一样的。」她说，「拿牌的手不一样。」

On the first Monday Wen Xu checked her stock list top to bottom, and it matched the other three vans line for line. Li Zhao leaned in the break-room doorway with two cups. "Look all right?" she asked. Wen Xu closed the clipboard. "The hands are dealt the same," she said. "The one holding them isn't."

注解：轨道杂化（orbital hybridization）指同一个原子内能量相近的几条轨道被重新调配成一组彼此等价、指向四个尽量散开的方向的新轨道，由此解释甲烷四个键的一模一样；但它是事后挑定的记账方式——先有的是能量最低的结构，换一套记法能得出同样的结果，现实分子里的配比也常常不是整数。

Note: Orbital hybridization means an atom's comparable-energy orbitals being re-dealt into a new, equivalent set pointing in four widely spread directions, which explains why methane's four bonds come out identical; but it is a bookkeeping scheme chosen after the fact — the lowest-energy structure exists first, a different accounting yields the same predictions, and real molecules often mix in less tidy proportions.
