第四条腰带

AI Parable (inspired by Amanda Askell)·每日精选·2026年10月1日·3分钟阅读

二更的梆子敲过，更房的灯还亮着。岑队正把一份文书推过桌案。「府库批了我的章程：守夜四班并作三班，皂字那班裁散，人并进其余三班去，一年省饷银一百二十两。省不出来，从我俸禄里坐扣，画押的是我。只差你落一颗印。」阮书办的手在纸边上摸了一遭，没有去够印盒。「这印落不得，岑队正。三班腰带守不住这座城。」她身后的木架上，四只线轴各占一格：青、朱、皂、褐。

The clapper of the second watch had gone by, and the watch-house lamp still burned. Captain Cen pushed a paper across the desk. "The treasury has approved my proposal: fold the four night squads into three, disband the black, spread its men among the other three, and save a hundred and twenty taels of wages a year. If the saving falls short, it comes out of my pay; my name is on the pledge. Yours is the only seal still wanting." Registrar Ruan's hand traveled once along the edge of the paper and did not reach for the seal box. "That seal I cannot give, Captain. Three squads' sashes cannot hold this city." On the shelf behind her, four bobbins sat each in its own slot: green, vermilion, black, brown.

「守不住？」岑把墙边那块界板搬下来，墩在两人中间。板上密密钉着街口的钉子，彩线绕出全城四十多坊的界线，线尾都缠在各自格里的线轴上：哪个坊绕着哪色的线，那坊的守夜人就系哪色腰带。她伸手按住一段线。「这好办。坊界年年改，今晚我就重画一遍，看你拿三条腰带怎么分。」「你画。」阮的指头搭上钉帽，一枚一枚慢慢数。「画出什么来，我一股一股线分给你看。」

"Cannot hold?" Cen hauled the boundary board down from the wall and set it between them. Small nails stood wherever streets met, and colored string wound out the lines of the city's forty-odd wards, each string tying off onto the bobbin in its slot: the color wound around a ward was the color of sash its watchmen wore. She set her hand on a stretch of string. "Then this is easily tried. The ward lines change every year; tonight I'll redraw them all, and we'll see how you manage with three sashes." "Draw," said Ruan. Her fingertips settled on the nailheads, counting them one by one. "Whatever you draw, I will hand out string by string."

岑不忙画新的。她的手指顺着线走到河南，停在三岔桥头。「先问眼前这一处。这两坊都绕的朱线，腰带一样，桥头碰上了，你怎么讲？」阮的手跟过去，在那枚钉子上按住。「碰是碰了，只碰这一枚钉。桥头的石桩尖得像笔锋，两坊的人背靠背站一夜，谁也不碍谁。当年立约写得死死的：共一段街的，腰带必得两样；只搭一个角的，随它去。」她顿了顿。「共一段街，出了事才分得清是谁家的街；只搭一个角，连街都没有，争无可争。」

Cen did not start drawing. Her finger followed a string to the river's south side and stopped at the three-way bridge. "First, then, what already stands on the board. These two wards are both wound in vermilion, the sashes match, and they meet at the bridgehead. What do you say to that?" Ruan's hand followed hers and pressed down on the single nail. "They touch, yes, at this one nail. The bridge post is sharp as a brush tip; the two wards could stand back to back all night and never be in each other's way. The compact was written hard and plain: wards that share a stretch of street must wear different sashes; wards that meet only at a corner are let be." She paused. "Where a street is shared, trouble on it can be traced to its squad. Where only a corner is shared, there is no street to argue over."

「角上的，我不争了。」岑把线拢到城心。「谷仓那四坊，你我都数过，两两都共着一段街。三条腰带，这四坊你自己分。」阮取下三只线轴，摆在岑面前。「三只，四坊，你来绕。」岑绕了足有一炷香。四坊围着谷仓咬得死紧，她怎么绕，总有两条一样的线搭进同一段街里。她松开手，指节在板沿上敲了两下。「那就四条。四条真的怎么画都够？」

"The corners I'll grant you." Cen drew the string in toward the heart of the city. "But the four wards around the granary, we've both counted them, every pair shares a stretch of street. With three sashes, assign those four yourself." Ruan lifted three bobbins from the shelf and set them before Cen. "Three bobbins, four wards. You wind." Cen wound for the better part of a burning incense stick. The four wards gripped the granary tightly among themselves, and however she wound, two strings of one color always came to lie along the same stretch of street. She let go and rapped her knuckles twice on the edge of the board. "Then four. Is four truly enough, however the lines are drawn?"

阮沉默了半晌，从抽屉里取出一封信，推到岑的手边。信封上字已写好，火漆也封了口。「辞呈，三年前就写下了。你收着。哪天有人画出一种坊界，非添第五条腰带不可，你拆开来念给我听。」她的眼睛就是在那些灯下熬坏的：她把坊界能变出的花样拆成九百多盘，一盘一盘摆到底，摆完一盘收一张底稿，如今坊界的文书得靠跑腿的小满一个字一个字念给她听。「岑队正，我拿不出一句痛快话给你。九百多盘，盘盘摆到了头，盘盘都收在四条里。你疑心，就去把架上的底稿一页一页翻。」

Ruan was quiet a long while, then took a letter from the drawer and set it by Cen's hand. The envelope was written and the wax already sealed. "My resignation, written three years ago. Keep it. The day someone draws ward lines that cannot be held without a fifth sash, you break the seal and read it to me." It was under those lamps that her eyes had gone: she had broken every arrangement the ward lines could be twisted into, nine hundred-odd boards, wound each one to its end, laid away a draft sheet for every one, and now the ward papers had to be read to her, word by word, by the errand boy Xiaoman. "Captain, I have no quick sentence to give you. Nine hundred-odd boards, every one wound to its end, every one closed inside four sashes. If you doubt it, go and turn the drafts on the shelf, sheet by sheet."

岑把辞呈揣进怀里，把自己那份文书折好收起。「印不必你落了。明天我亲自去府库回话，罚俸我认。」她顺手摘下钩子上那条皂腰带，搭上肩头。「今晚皂字跟我巡河沿。」走到门口，她又站住。「还有一桩。盐课司在河对岸的那座小院，为什么单给它一条坊名？」「当年它非要挂本坊的腰带，四条险些不够使。立约的老人们把它单算一坊，才拢得住。」岑点了点头，出门去了。灯下，界板上的四股彩线各归各位，没有一段街搭着两股一样的线。

Cen tucked the resignation letter inside her coat and folded her own paper away. "Your seal won't be needed. Tomorrow I go to the treasury myself and stand the forfeit." She lifted the black sash from its hook and slung it over her shoulder. "Tonight the black walks the riverbank with me." At the door she stopped once more. "One thing further. That compound the salt office keeps across the river, why does it carry a ward name of its own?" "Because the year the compact was drawn up, it insisted on hanging its home ward's sash, and the four all but failed. The old ones made it a ward on its own, and only then did everything hold." Cen nodded and went out. Under the lamp the four strings each lay in its place on the board, and no stretch of street carried two of the same.

注解：故事背后是四色定理（the four-color theorem）：无论一张平面地图被划成多少块，只要挨着的两块（共一段边界，只碰一个角不算）不同色，四种颜色永远够用；它的第一个证明主要靠计算机把大量情形逐一枚举查尽。若再要求同一家的飞地也同色，比如故事里那座非要挂本坊腰带的小院，四种颜色就可能不够。

Note: Behind the story stands the four-color theorem: however a plane map is divided, if two blocks that share a stretch of border (touching only at a corner does not count) must differ in color, four colors are always enough; its first proof rested mainly on a computer's grinding through a great mass of cases, one by one. If a territory's detached holding must also wear its homeland's color, that little compound insisting on its home ward's sash, for instance, four colors may not suffice.
