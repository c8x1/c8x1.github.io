充足理由律能否使泛心论为真？

Sam Kimpton-Nye·Aeon·2026年10月14日·15分钟阅读·原文

当一个解释使某个事实变得可理解时，这个事实身上的神秘感便随之消散。我们对它理解得如此透彻，以至于几乎无法设想事情会是别的样子。想想一加一等于二这个简单的数学事实。一旦理解了初等算术，我们便不再有冲动去追问一加一为什么等于二。一加一等于二在我们看来不仅为真，而且是不可避免的；我们甚至无法连贯地想象它为假。哲学的许多工作，正是要把实在的其他基本特征也带入类似的光亮之中，使它们显得不是任意的，而是必然的，因而是可理解的。

When an explanation makes a fact intelligible, the mystery goes out of it. We understand it so well that we can hardly conceive of things being otherwise. Consider the simple mathematical fact that one plus one equals two. Once we understand elementary arithmetic, we no longer feel the urge to ask why one plus one equals two. That one plus one equals two strikes us as not merely true but unavoidable; we cannot even coherently imagine its falsity. Much of philosophy aims to bring other fundamental features of reality into a similar light, to make them appear not arbitrary but inevitable and, thus, intelligible.

这种让哲学把世界变得可理解的抱负，在德国博学家莱布尼茨（Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz）与荷兰哲学家斯宾诺莎（Baruch Spinoza）那里得到了最好的表达。莱布尼茨说：「我们找不到任何真实的或存在的事实……是没有一个充足理由说明它为何如此而非别样的。」斯宾诺莎则写道：「对于每一事物，都必须为它的存在和它的不存在各指定一个原因或理由。」用美国哲学家迈克尔·德拉·罗卡（Michael Della Rocca）的话说，这里表达的是一种「使世界及我们在其中的位置变得可理解」的愿望。而把握莱布尼茨和斯宾诺莎所追寻的那类理由或解释，「乃是哲学恒久的希望与承诺」。

This aspiration that philosophy render the world intelligible is best expressed by the German polymath Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz and the Dutch philosopher Baruch Spinoza. According to Leibniz: ‘we can find no true or existent fact … without there being a sufficient reason why it is thus and not otherwise.’ And, as Spinoza wrote: ‘For each thing there must be assigned a cause or reason, both for its existence and for its nonexistence.’ What’s being expressed here, in the words of the US philosopher Michael Della Rocca, is the desire ‘to make the world and our place in it intelligible’. To grasp the kinds of reasons or explanations sought by Leibniz and Spinoza ‘is the perennial hope and promise of philosophy’.

对可理解性的要求可能把我们引向一些非同寻常的地方。对莱布尼茨来说，这一要求使他提出：实在最终由杂多的单子（monad）构成——不可分割、无穷小的实体单元，每一个都是因果上孤立的、自成一体的宇宙。斯宾诺莎对普遍可理解性的坚定信奉，则使他得出结论：实在是一个单一无限的实体，他称之为神或自然。我们将看到，对可理解性的要求同样可能把我们引入泛心论（panpsychism）那奇特而迷人的世界。

The demand for intelligibility can lead to some extraordinary places. For Leibniz, the demand led him to propose that reality is ultimately made up of a multiplicity of monads: indivisible, infinitesimally small units of substance, each of which is a causally isolated, self-contained universe. Spinoza’s resolute commitment to universal intelligibility led him to conclude that reality is, instead, a single infinite substance that he called God or nature. As we will see, the demand for intelligibility can also lead into the strange and fascinating world of panpsychism.

泛心论是一种日益流行的关于意识的观点。你有意识，你感受到快乐、疼痛以及各种各样的其他体验。在每一个清醒的时刻，甚至在做梦时，「成为你」总有某种感觉。其他人类和动物也是如此，而这正是道德地位的根据：沮丧时一拳捶在桌上，在道德上是可以容许的，而打一个有意识的人或动物则不然。桌子、椅子、石头等没有意识——因而没有道德地位——而人类、倭黑猩猩、狗等有意识，至少应得到某种道德上的承认。但泛心论拒斥这种把事物划分为有意识与无意识两类的做法。它主张意识是物质的根本属性，因此从电子、质子到桌子、行星，当然还有人类和狗，一切事物都在某种程度上具有意识。

Panpsychism is an increasingly popular view about consciousness. You are conscious, you feel pleasures, pains and all manner of other experiences. At every waking moment, and even when you dream, there is something that it is like to be you. The same goes for other humans and animals, and this accounts for moral status: thumping a fist on a desk in frustration is morally permissible in a way that hitting a conscious person or animal is not. Desks, chairs, rocks and so on are not conscious – and are therefore without moral status – whereas humans, bonobos, dogs and so on are conscious, and deserving of at least some moral recognition. But panpsychism rejects this division into conscious and non-conscious things. It is the view that consciousness is a fundamental property of matter, so everything from electrons and protons to tables and planets, and, of course, humans and dogs, has consciousness to some degree.

泛心论是一个引人瞩目的论题，其动力来自世界终究可理解这一「恒久的希望」。为了考察泛心论是否真能使世界变得可理解，我们将探讨三个不同的论证，每一个都立足于一条把普遍可理解性这一宏愿凝聚成形的原则：充足理由律（principle of sufficient reason，PSR）。这条哲学探究的原则既简单又可信——一切事物都存在令人满意的解释——它能通向对实在本性的全新理解。而且，尽管我怀疑那种能消解世间一切奥秘的解释是否可得、甚至是否可取，但仅仅沉思这类方案本身，就具有非同寻常的价值。

Panpsychism is a remarkable thesis, motivated by the ‘perennial hope’ that the world is ultimately intelligible. To investigate whether panpsychism truly can make the world intelligible, we will explore three different arguments, each of which is grounded in a principle that crystallises the ambitious aim of universal intelligibility: the principle of sufficient reason (PSR). This simple and plausible principle of philosophical enquiry – that there exist satisfying explanations for everything – can lead to a whole new understanding of the nature of reality. And, though I’m sceptical about the availability or even desirability of explanations that dissolve all the world’s mysteries, there is exceptional value in the mere contemplation of such projects.

如果你下班回家，发现门口台阶上放着一束花，这背后必有缘由。也许是生日礼物，也许是恭喜你找到新工作，也许是答谢你做的某件好事。如果你的汽车仪表盘亮起警示灯，明智的做法是假定事出有因，比如发动机、油液或轮胎出了问题。位于墨西哥尤卡坦半岛（Yucatán peninsula）的希克苏鲁伯陨石坑（Chicxulub crater）宽200公里、深1公里，亟需科学的解释——而数百万年前一场灭绝级小行星撞击的假说，恰恰提供了这种解释。

If you come home from work to find a bouquet of flowers on your doorstep, there will be a reason for this. Maybe it’s a birthday present, a congratulations on a new job, a thank you for some good deed. If your car dashboard shows a warning light, you’d be wise to assume that there is some reason for this, such as a problem with the engine, fluids or tyres. The Chicxulub crater, 200 km wide and 1 km deep, in the Yucatán peninsula in Mexico, cries out for scientific explanation, which is precisely what the hypothesis of an extinction-level asteroid impact millions of years ago provides.

但真的万事皆有因吗？质子的质量是1.672 × 10⁻²⁷ kg。这有什么理由？我们关于这一问题最好的科学理论——量子力学——只能给某个特定的放射性衰变事件赋予一个概率，它又有什么理由？按照充足理由律，万事确实皆有因。无论世界处于何种状态，从你家门口的花束到质子的质量，总有某个理由说明它为何是这样而非那样。一切事实都有理由，而且——按照斯宾诺莎所捍卫的最强形式的充足理由律——那理由使它们可理解且必然，使其反面不可设想。

But is there really a reason for everything? The mass of a proton is 1.672 × 10-27 kg. What’s the reason for this? What’s the reason for a particular radioactive decay event to which our best scientific theory on the issue, quantum mechanics, assigns only a probability? According to the PSR, there is indeed a reason for everything. For any way the world is, from the flowers on your doorstep to the mass of a proton, there is some reason why it is this way and not some other way. All facts have a reason, and – according to the PSR in its strongest form, as defended by Spinoza – that reason makes them intelligible and necessary, and their contrary inconceivable.

尽管充足理由律在莱布尼茨的著作中得到了最充分的表达——他写道：「推理的根本原则，是没有什么事物是没有理由的」——它的谱系远为久远。正如德拉·罗卡在其2008年的著作《斯宾诺莎》（Spinoza）中所展示的：「（充足理由律的）各种版本在哲学中源远流长，在巴门尼德（Parmenides）、斯多葛学派（the Stoics）、阿奎那（Aquinas）等哲学家那里都能找到。」这条原则也并不限于西欧哲学。它在伊斯兰哲学家伊本·西那（Ibn Sīnā，欧洲亦称其为阿维森纳〔Avicenna〕）的形而上学体系中占据关键位置，伊本·西那借此论证：仅仅偶然存在的事物——即其存在并非必然的事物——必须有外在于自身的原因。在当代形而上学和物理学哲学中，这条原则的种种版本同样有人辩护。沙米克·达斯古普塔（Shamik Dasgupta）主张，每一个实质性事实都有使其成为必然的形而上学根据。埃迪·克明·陈（Eddy Keming Chen）则发展了一个关于宇宙的物理模型，依照该模型，每一个事实（从今天下午两点你头上的头发数量，到电子的质量）都被自然律所蕴含，并因此得到解释。

Though the PSR found its fullest expression in Leibniz’s work – ‘The fundamental principle of reasoning,’ Leibniz wrote, ‘is that there is nothing without a reason’ – its lineage is far greater. As Della Rocca showed in his book Spinoza (2008): ‘Versions of [the PSR] go way back in philosophy and can be found in philosophers such as Parmenides, the Stoics, [and] Aquinas.’ And the principle is not restricted to Western European philosophy. It occupies a key position in the metaphysical system of the Islamic philosopher Ibn Sīnā (also known in Europe as Avicenna), who used it to argue that things that exist merely contingently – that is, things whose existence is not necessary – must have a cause outside themselves. Versions of the principle have also been defended in contemporary metaphysics and philosophy of physics. Shamik Dasgupta has argued that every substantive fact has a metaphysical grounding that makes it necessary. And Eddy Keming Chen has developed a physical model of the Universe according to which every single fact (from the number of hairs on your head at 2 pm this afternoon, to the mass of the electron) is entailed and thus explained by the laws of nature.

充足理由律之所以如此诱人，是因为一切探究活动本身——无论是哲学的、科学的，还是幼儿问出的第一声「为什么？」——似乎都以万事万物皆有解释、皆有理由为前提。认可充足理由律，就是采取一种乐观的态度：世界及我们在其中的位置是可理解的，浸润着理由与意义。倘若有任何事物不可解释，人类的探究便很有可能终究徒劳。充足理由律为假，将意味着我们好问的天性与我们所处的世界之间存在一种根本的错位；它将意味着世界是不可理解的。

The PSR is so alluring because it would seem that the very activity of enquiry – whether philosophical, scientific, or the first ‘Why?’ question of a young child – is premised on there being an explanation, a reason, for everything. To endorse the PSR is to adopt an attitude of optimism that the world and our place in it are intelligible and infused with reason and meaning. If anything were unexplainable, there’d be a grave risk that human enquiry is ultimately futile. The falsity of the PSR would constitute a fundamental mismatch between our inquisitive nature and the world in which we find ourselves; it would imply that the world is unintelligible.

可理解性与有意义性是一体两面。当我们反躬自省，发现个人与职业的追求找不到什么好的理由（毕竟，往老板口袋里装钱一般不会让我们满足！），当这些事情显得荒谬或不可理解时，我们自然会用生活缺乏意义来描述这种困境。类似地，若关于实在本性的问题始终找不到令人满意的答案，也会引发深刻的哲学忧虑：世界本身或许终究没有意义。

Intelligibility and meaningfulness are two sides of the same coin. When, on reflection, we can find no good reason for our personal and professional pursuits (lining the boss’s pockets won’t generally satisfy us!), when these things seem absurd or unintelligible, it’s natural to express the predicament in terms of a lack of meaning in life. Analogously, failure to uncover satisfying answers to questions about the nature of reality can lead to deep philosophical worries that the world itself is ultimately meaningless.

但这并不是说充足理由律在哲学家中被普遍接受。它之所以有争议，是因为它似乎蕴含必然主义（necessitarianism）——即万事万物皆为必然的论题。此刻你正在读一篇关于充足理由律与泛心论的文章，但你本可以做点别的。然而，如果充足理由律为真，那么你此刻读这篇文章就有一个充足理由，而这使得你此刻读这篇文章成为必然，进而使得你做任何别的事都成为不可能。既然充足理由律适用于一切，它似乎就蕴含着一切事物的必然性。许多人对此难以接受，因为它既威胁自由意志，也威胁一个看似显而易见的事实：各种事情本可以以种种方式有所不同。

But this is not to say that the PSR is universally accepted among philosophers. It is controversial because it seems to entail necessitarianism – the thesis that everything is necessary. You are currently reading an essay about the PSR and panpsychism, but you could have been doing something else. If the PSR is true, however, then there is a sufficient reason why you are currently reading this essay, which makes it necessary that you are currently reading this essay and hence impossible that you could be doing anything else. Since the PSR applies to everything, it seems to entail the necessity of everything, and many find this unpalatable because it threatens free will and the seemingly obvious fact that all manner of things could have been different in all sorts of ways.

自称「休谟主义者」（Humean）的当代哲学家——亦即受苏格兰启蒙哲学家休谟（David Hume）影响的人——明确拒斥必然性与终极解释。大卫·刘易斯（David Lewis）或许是上个世纪最杰出的休谟主义者，他坚称：「世界上的全部存在，不过是一大堆局部特殊事实拼成的巨幅马赛克，一件件小事，一件接着一件。」这就是说，世界上发生的事既没有理由，也没有必然性。本着类似的精神，荷兰裔美国哲学家巴斯·范弗拉森（Bas van Fraassen）一贯质疑形而上学解释结构的必要性。这表现为拒斥那种使宇宙中的事件成为必然的强健自然律。在范弗拉森看来，没有使事物成为必然的深层解释，科学照样运转良好。

Contemporary philosophers who describe themselves as ‘Humean’, that is, as influenced by the Scottish Enlightenment philosopher David Hume, are explicit in their rejection of necessity and ultimate explanations. David Lewis, perhaps the most prominent Humean of the past century, maintained that ‘all there is to the world is a vast mosaic of local matters of particular fact, just one little thing and then another.’ That is to say, there are no reasons, and no necessity for what goes on in the world. In a similar spirit, the Dutch American philosopher Bas van Fraassen consistently challenges the need for a metaphysical explanatory structure. This manifests itself in the rejection of robust laws of nature that necessitate events in the Universe. Science, according to Van Fraassen, can get along just fine without deep explanations that make things necessary.

可见，对可理解性与理由的乐观执念要付出重大代价：它要求我们承诺普遍的必然性。不过，也有办法减轻这一代价。包括莱布尼茨本人在内的不少论者主张，充足理由律至少与某种意义上的「事情本可以不同」相容，而这将对应于较弱的解释含义。最强的解释——那些产生必然性的解释——能够在有限步骤内得到证明，数学与几何的演证即是典范。另一些解释无法在有限步骤内证明，尽管无限的神圣心智能够洞察它们。但在莱布尼茨看来，某些解释性关联无法被我们有限的人类理智所把握，这一事实本身就足以构成偶然性。

So, the optimistic attachment to intelligibility and reason comes at a significant cost: it demands a commitment to widespread necessity. There are, however, ways of mitigating this cost. Various authors, including Leibniz himself, have argued that the PSR is at least consistent with a sense in which things could have been different, and this will correspond to weaker senses of explanation. The strongest explanations, those that yield necessity, can be demonstrated in a finite number of steps; prime examples include mathematical and geometrical demonstrations. Other explanations are not finitely demonstrable, though they can be perceived by the infinite mind of God. But the fact that some explanatory connections cannot be grasped by our limited human intellect suffices, according to Leibniz, for contingency.

或者，人们也可以像斯宾诺莎那样拥抱必然主义，把它视为万物相互联结、皆可理解的又一个积极面向——若要坚持作为哲学乐观主义者的信念勇气，这似乎是必由之路。但必然主义并非充足理由律唯一有争议的推论。它还可以为泛心论提供动力。

Alternatively, one could embrace necessitarianism, as Spinoza does, and see it as just another positive facet of the interconnectedness and intelligibility of all things – this seems the way to go if one is to maintain the courage of one’s conviction as a philosophical optimist. But necessitarianism is not the only controversial upshot of the PSR. It can also motivate panpsychism.

在全部科学与哲学之中，最难捉摸的问题或许莫过于意识体验的本性。为什么构成人类和其他动物的物质排列会伴随着意识体验？为什么「成为你」总有某种感觉？为什么有些物理状态有意识，另一些却没有？无论我们把构成一个有意识存在的物质排列描述得多么完备，那种描述都恰恰遗漏了我们想要解释的东西：从内部感受起来是什么样子。如何提供一个在关于物质的第三人称科学描述与第一人称意识体验之间架桥的解释——这一挑战，被澳大利亚哲学家大卫·查尔莫斯（David Chalmers）称为「意识的难问题」（hard problem of consciousness）。

Perhaps the most elusive question in all of science and philosophy concerns the nature of conscious experience. Why are the arrangements of matter that constitute human beings and other animals accompanied by conscious experience? Why is there something that it is like to be you? Why are some physical states, but not others, conscious? However completely we describe the physical arrangements of matter that make up a conscious being, that description leaves out the very thing we’re trying to explain: what it feels like from the inside. This challenge to provide an explanation that bridges the gap between third-person scientific descriptions of matter and first-person conscious experience is what the Australian philosopher David Chalmers dubbed the ‘hard problem of consciousness’.

这个问题之所以如此棘手，是因为它似乎并不适合用科学的探究方法来处理。举例来说，磨坊的运转可以由它的部件来解释：水流推动轮子旋转，轮子带动与磨石相连的齿轮，磨石再把小麦碾磨成面粉。磨坊的整个运转及其产出（面粉）都可以对我们变得可理解。如果我们问「磨坊为什么产出面粉？」，可以给出一个完全令人满意的答案。不留任何神秘。

The question is so fiendishly hard because it does not seem amenable to scientific methods of enquiry. The operation of a mill, for example, can be explained by its components: water flow rotates a wheel, which drives gears connected to millstones, which grind wheat into flour. The whole operation of the mill and its output (flour) can be made intelligible to us. If we ask ‘Why does the mill produce flour?’ a fully satisfying answer can be given. No mystery remains.

磨坊的例子说明了科学解释的一般情形。水的沸点可以用H₂O分子的氢键来解释，光在巨大天体附近的路径可以用阿尔伯特·爱因斯坦（Albert Einstein）的广义相对论所描述的时空弯曲来解释。这些例子理解起来更费工夫，但原理与磨坊的例子如出一辙。

The mill example is illustrative of scientific explanation in general. The boiling point of water is explained in terms of the hydrogen-bonding of H2O molecules, and the path of light around a massive celestial body is explained in terms of the curvature of spacetime described by Albert Einstein’s general theory of relativity. These cases take more work to understand, but the principle is the same as in the mill case.

无论我们多么仔细地审视构成有意识存在的物质，都无法更进一步理解意识如何产生。莱布尼茨曾让我们设想把人脑放大到磨坊那么大，好让我们走进其中，巨细无遗地观察它所有正在运转的部件。倘若我们真的这样做，任何地方都看不到意识体验。意识体验就其本性而言是私密的、第一人称的。既然科学方法只能带来关于运动中之物质的客观的、第三人称可及的事实知识，意识的起源便始终存在一种本质性的神秘。「为什么有些物理状态有意识，另一些却没有」这个问题，似乎无法回答。

However closely we look at the physical matter that makes up conscious beings, we get no closer to understanding how consciousness arises. Leibniz encouraged us to imagine increasing the size of the human brain to the size of a mill so that we can walk around inside it and observe all its working parts in meticulous detail. If we did this, at no point would we observe conscious experience. Conscious experience, by its very nature, is private, first-personal. Since scientific methods can yield knowledge only of the objective, third-person-accessible facts regarding matter in motion, there is an essential mystery concerning the origin of consciousness. The question of why some physical states, but not others, are conscious seems unanswerable.

无法回答的问题，与我们好问的人类天性格格不入。如果意识在原则上就无法解释，那将是对充足理由律尤为严重的违反。不过，一旦承认还原性的科学解释难堪此任，我们为解释意识、从而维护充足理由律的种种选项，仍值得认真考虑。

Unanswerable questions do not sit comfortably with our inquisitive human nature. If consciousness cannot be explained in principle, this would be a particularly egregious violation of the PSR. However, it is worth considering our options for explaining consciousness and, thus, upholding the PSR once we accept that reductive scientific explanation is not up to the task.

一种选择是用一种全新的实体来理解意识，这种实体与物质完全不同。在这种观点下，实在被一分为二：以空间广延为特征的物质实体，与以思想为特征的心灵实体，后者负责解释意识。这就是所谓的二元论（dualism）。二元论最有影响的旗手是法国哲学家笛卡尔（René Descartes）。但笛卡尔最终未能守住充足理由律，因为他说不出为什么实体有两个，而不是只有一个。

One option is to understand consciousness in terms of a new substance entirely, one fully distinct from physical matter. On this view, reality is bifurcated into physical substance characterised by spatial extension, and mental substance characterised by thought, where the latter accounts for consciousness. This is called dualism. The most influential champion of dualism was the French philosopher René Descartes. But Descartes ultimately failed to uphold the PSR, since he couldn’t say why there are two substances as opposed to just one.

另一种办法，是质疑那个看似理所当然的假定：基本物质没有意识。谜团之所以出现，是因为我们无法理解在时空中排列开来的无意识物质如何能产生意识。但我们为什么必须认为基本物质没有意识？这个假定并不是非接受不可的。如果意识是物质的根本属性，那么上文所表述的那个解释意识的难题就不会出现。于是，为了避免这一解释意识的原则性障碍，许多人倾向于相信意识是物质的根本属性，相信泛心论为真。

Alternatively, we could question the seemingly obvious assumption that fundamental physical matter isn’t conscious. The mystery arises because there is no way to understand how non-conscious physical matter, arranged in space and time, can give rise to consciousness. But why must we think that fundamental physical matter is not conscious? This assumption is not mandatory. If consciousness is a fundamental property of matter, then the problem of explaining consciousness, as formulated above, does not arise. So, in the interest of avoiding this in-principle obstacle to explaining consciousness, many are inclined to believe that consciousness is a fundamental property of matter, that panpsychism is true.

「基本物质是物理的且无意识」这一假说，堵死了用物质的基本「单元」来可理解地解释像我们这样的生物为何有意识的可能性。如果意识在基本层面上缺席，物理与心灵之间就将永远存在一道解释鸿沟。这意味着：对于构成一个有意识存在的任何物质排列而言，意识在其中缺席似乎始终是可能的；没有任何物质排列看起来与意识有必然联系，因而能够解释意识。但如果在基本层面上就引入意识，这一障碍便被移除。按照泛心论，我们不再需要弥合从无意识的基本物质到更高组织层次上的意识之间的鸿沟，因为意识一路向下、无处不在。

The hypothesis that fundamental matter is physical and non-conscious blocks the possibility of an intelligible explanation – in terms of the fundamental ‘bits’ of matter – of why creatures like us are conscious. If consciousness is absent at the fundamental level, there will always be an explanatory gap between the physical and the mental. What this means is that, for any arrangement of physical matter constitutive of a conscious being, it always seems possible that consciousness was absent; no arrangement of matter seems to be necessarily connected with, and hence able to explain, consciousness. But if consciousness is introduced at the fundamental level, this obstacle is removed. According to panpsychism, we no longer need to bridge the gap from non-conscious fundamental matter to consciousness at higher levels of organisation, since consciousness is present all the way down.

当然，要解释基本的意识如何组合成人类层级的意识，仍有大量工作要做。但至少，泛心论提供了一条出路，避开那否则便可能对充足理由律造成毁灭性违反的局面。如果意识在基本层面上已经存在，那么在完全无意识的物质与更高层次的有意识存在之间，就不再有那同一种无处不在的解释鸿沟。

There is still significant work to be done to explain how fundamental consciousness combines to create human-level consciousness. But, at the very least, panpsychism offers a way of avoiding what would otherwise appear to be a potentially devastating violation of the PSR. If consciousness is already present at the fundamental level, there is no longer the same pervasive explanatory gap between wholly non-conscious matter and conscious beings at higher levels.

经由充足理由律通向泛心论，还有另一条路径。挪威哲学家海达·哈塞尔·默奇（Hedda Hassel Mørch）主张，要恰当地解释基础物理学所记录的物质的基本运动，就需要基本的意识。科学对物质如何行为、质子电子中子等各种亚原子粒子各在做什么，告诉我们很多。但它没有告诉我们物质是什么。从科学刻画物质的方式便能清楚地看到这一点。例如，电子是凭其属性来刻画的：质量、电荷与量子自旋。而这些属性又反过来通过它们使电子倾向于如何行为来得到理解。这幅科学的世界图景所缺失的，是对物理属性自身是什么样子的说明，因而也缺失了对它们为何有能力使物质以特定方式行为的说明。

There is another route to panpsychism via the PSR. The Norwegian philosopher Hedda Hassel Mørch argues that fundamental consciousness is required to properly explain the basic motions of matter, documented by fundamental physics. Science tells us plenty about how matter behaves and what different subatomic particles such as protons, electrons and neutrons do. But it does not tell us what matter is. This is evident from the way in which science characterises matter. The electron, for example, is characterised in terms of its properties: mass, charge and quantum spin. And these properties, in turn, are understood in terms of how they dispose the electron to behave. What is absent from this scientific description of the world is an account of what physical properties are like in themselves, and hence why they have the power to dispose matter to behave in certain ways.

为什么电子的属性使它倾向于某些行为？这如何才能按照充足理由律的要求变得可理解？想想疼痛。如果你正处于疼痛之中，这种心灵属性会使你避开致痛之物：不小心碰到滚烫的暖气片，你会立刻缩手。用默奇的话说：「在没有任何干扰性动机的情况下，疼痛似乎总是使主体试图避开它，而且很难设想有别的可能。」如果我们问某人为什么把手从暖气片上缩回来，「高温让他感到疼痛」就是一个完美的解释。这个解释使该行为变得完全可理解，因为疼痛的本性之一，就是试图避开疼痛的来源。心灵属性的特殊之处在于，它们是唯一能为行为提供可理解解释的属性。正如默奇所写：

Why do the properties of an electron dispose it towards certain behaviours? How can this be rendered intelligible as per the demands of the PSR? Consider pain. If you are in pain, this mental property disposes you to avoid the thing that’s painful: accidentally touch a hot radiator, and you’ll instantly remove your hand. As Mørch puts it: ‘In the absence of any interfering motives, it seems pain always makes subjects try to avoid it, and [it is] difficult to conceive otherwise.’ If we ask why someone withdrew their hand from the radiator, it is a perfect explanation to say that the heat caused them pain. The explanation renders the action fully intelligible because part of what it is to be in pain is to try to avoid the painful source. Mental properties are special in that they are the only properties with this ability to provide intelligible explanations of behaviour. As Mørch writes:

其他属性，比如负电荷，就我们所知，本可以使事物以截然不同的方式行为，或者根本不遵循任何系统的方式。但疼痛使我们去做避开疼痛之源以外的任何事情，则是不可设想的。「看起来不可设想的是，」默奇写道，「疼痛仅凭其内在本性，就使主体试图做避开它以外的任何事情。」这就开启了一个迷人的前景。为了让物质的行为变得可理解，我们应当把那些对行为负责的属性看作有意识的心灵属性。也就是说：电荷、质量、量子自旋等一切典型的物理属性，都是有意识的心灵属性。这一步棋回答了「物质从根本上说是什么」的问题，而这又反过来解释了物质为何如此行为。物质的根本属性是意识的属性。它们就其本性而言，使它们所依附的物质倾向于某些行为。这就堵上了仅凭科学方法所留下的解释鸿沟。再一次，这一论证背后的主要动机，是充足理由律所体现的、对世界之可理解解释的渴望。

Other properties, such as negative charge, could, for all we know, have disposed things to behave very differently or in no systematic way at all. But it is inconceivable that pain disposed us to do anything besides avoid the source of pain. ‘It does not,’ Mørch writes, ‘seem conceivable that pain makes subjects try to do anything else than avoid it in virtue of its intrinsic character alone.’ This raises a fascinating prospect. In the interest of rendering the behaviour of matter intelligible, we should take those properties responsible for that behaviour to be conscious mental properties. That is to say: all paradigm physical properties such as charge, mass and quantum spin are conscious mental properties. This move provides an answer to the question of what matter fundamentally is, which, in turn, explains why matter does what it does. The fundamental properties of matter are properties of consciousness. By their very nature, they dispose the matter of which they are properties towards certain behaviours. This plugs the explanatory gap left by the scientific method alone. Again, the primary motive in this argument is the desire for intelligible explanations of the world, as per the PSR.

对充足理由律的信奉，还有第三条通向泛心论的路。斯宾诺莎最出名的或许是他的泛神论（pantheism），即认为神就是自然。在这种观点下，神不是一个作为自然之外因而与之分离的实体；毋宁说，神与自然的边界完全重合。事实证明，为泛神论所作的论证，同样可以很好地充当泛心论的论证。而关键在于，斯宾诺莎的论证依赖于对自然之可理解性真正毫不动摇的信奉。

There is a third way that a commitment to the PSR leads us to panpsychism. Spinoza is perhaps most famous for his pantheism, the idea that God is nature. On this view, God is not a separate entity that is an external cause of nature; rather, God is coterminous with nature. It turns out that the argument for pantheism also works well as an argument for panpsychism. And, crucially, Spinoza’s argument depends on a truly unwavering commitment to the intelligibility of nature.

如果被问及「存在多少事物？」，我们大概会说，事物确实多得很——这里一只猫，那里一栋房子，还有质子、电子等等——甚至可能有人认为事物的数目无限多。但斯宾诺莎主张，事物只有唯一一个。如果我们主张存在的事物多于一个，就必须对这些事物各自是什么样子有所言说。于是，我们可能会说「这只猫是黑的」「那栋房子是砖砌的」「苏格拉底（Socrates）有智慧」等等。关键在于，对充足理由律的拥护者来说，不同的存在之物不可能具有完全相同的属性。如果两个事物——比如这张桌子和那张桌子——是不同的，那么它们的差异性就必须有某种解释，诉诸它们之间的某种差异、属性上的某种不同（比如一张是方的，另一张是椭圆的）。

If asked ‘How many things exist?’ we’d probably say that there are a great many things indeed – a cat here, a house there, some protons, electrons, and so on – one might even think that there are infinitely many things. But Spinoza argues that there is just one thing. If we hold that there exists more than one thing, we must say something about what each of these things is like. So, we might say ‘This cat is black,’ ‘That house is made of brick,’ ‘Socrates is wise’ and so on. Crucially, for the advocate of the PSR, the different things that exist cannot have all the same properties. If two things, this table and that table, for example, are distinct, there must be some explanation of their distinctness that appeals to some difference between them, some difference in their properties (eg, one is square, the other oval).

不同的事物由其属性来个体化。但这种个体化关系正是哲学家所说的依赖关系。例如，「这是一篇关于泛心论、可理解性与哲学之目标的文章」这一事实，依赖于「存在着以适当方式排列的词语」这一事实。这种依赖关系引发出一种基本性的排序：词语比文章更基本，因为后者依赖前者。

Distinct things are individuated by their properties. But this relation of individuation is what philosophers call a dependence relation. For example, the fact that this is an essay on panpsychism, intelligibility and the aim of philosophy depends on the fact that there are words arranged in the appropriate way. This relation of dependence induces an ordering of fundamentality: the words are more fundamental than the essay because the latter depends on the former.

但在斯宾诺莎看来，如果事物因属性将其个体化而依赖属性，那么结论就是：属性比事物更基本。然而，这个结果是不可接受的。事物——或者用斯宾诺莎的话说，「实体」（substance）——本应是绝对基本的。成为一个实体、一个存在着的独立事物，就在于独立于其他一切、完全自我持存。说属性占据基本层面，未免奇怪。苹果的红比苹果本身更基本吗？当然不是：必须先有苹果这个事物存在，红才能被例示。所以，我们必须另辟蹊径，解释为什么存在多个不同的事物。靠属性来做，行不通。

But, for Spinoza, if things depend on properties because properties individuate things, then it follows that properties are more fundamental than things. This result, however, is not acceptable. Things – or ‘substances’, as Spinoza would say – are supposed to be absolutely fundamental. What it is to be a substance, a distinct thing that exists, is to be independent of everything else, completely self-sustaining. It seems odd to say that properties occupy the fundamental level. Is the redness of an apple more fundamental than the apple itself? Surely not, since the apple, the thing, is required to exist before redness can be instantiated. So, we must find another way to explain why there are multiple distinct things. Doing so in terms of properties won’t cut it.

与其主张不同的事物仅仅具有不同的属性，也许我们应当说，它们在本质属性（essential attribute）上有根本的不同。什么是「本质属性」？回想一下，按照二元论，实在被一分为二：具有思想这一本质属性的心灵实体，与具有广延这一本质属性的物质实体。换言之，心灵的东西与物理的东西有着截然不同的本质属性。但这引出了一个新问题：为什么心灵实体不具有广延，物质实体不具有思想？充足理由律要求一个解释。人们也许会认为，心灵实体的本性排除了广延，反之亦然。但斯宾诺莎反对说，这违背了实体的独立性，因为要解释两者相互排斥这一事实，它就要求心灵实体与物质实体相互依赖。归根结底，斯宾诺莎认为，没有理由说明实体之间为什么彼此不同；既然没有理由，它们便不彼此相异。于是，只有一个实体、一个事物，而这个事物拥有一切属性，既广延又思想，因为不存在任何理由使它缺少任何属性或本质属性。

Instead of maintaining that different things merely have different properties, perhaps we ought to say that they are radically different in their essential attributes. What are ‘essential attributes’? Recall that, according to dualism, reality is bifurcated into mental substance with the essential attribute of thought, and physical substance with the essential attribute of extension. In other words, the mental and the physical have radically different essential attributes. But this raises a new question: why is mental substance not extended, and why is physical substance not thinking? The PSR demands an explanation. One might think that the nature of mental substance excludes being extended, and vice versa. But Spinoza objects that this violates the independence of substance, since it entails a mutual dependence of mental and physical substance to explain the fact that they exclude one another. Ultimately, Spinoza thinks there is no reason why substances would differ from each other and, since there is no reason, they do not differ. There is, then, just one substance, one thing, and this thing has all properties, and is both extended and thinking, because there is no reason that it should lack any properties or essential attributes.

一个拥有一切属性和本质属性的单一事物，恰好同时符合神与自然的身份，因此斯宾诺莎「只有一个事物存在」的论证，也是泛神论的论证。但它同样可以被表述为泛心论的论证。按照斯宾诺莎，这唯一存在的实体既广延又思想。因此，思想是实在的一个根本而无处不在的特征。不过，思想的这种根本性与无处不在是否就等于泛心论，仍有争论的余地，因为有理由相信，在斯宾诺莎的体系中并非一切思想都是有意识的思想。但至少，对斯宾诺莎来说，心灵性是物质的一个根本属性，而许多人乐于把它等同于意识，从而把斯宾诺莎归入泛心论者。比如美国哲学家马丁·林（Martin Lin）写道：「斯宾诺莎是一个泛心论者。在他看来，心灵性是自然世界无处不在的根本特征。」

A single thing with all properties and essential attributes fits the bill of both God and nature, hence Spinoza’s argument, that only one thing exists, is an argument for pantheism. But this can also be cast as an argument for panpsychism. The single existing substance is, according to Spinoza, both extended and thinking. Hence, thought is a fundamental and ubiquitous feature of reality. There is, however, scope for debate about whether the fundamentality and ubiquity of thought amounts to panpsychism since there is reason to believe that not all thought is conscious thought, in Spinoza’s system. But, at the very least, mentality is a fundamental property of matter, for Spinoza, and many are happy to equate this with consciousness and, thus, categorise Spinoza as a panpsychist. The US philosopher Martin Lin, for example, writes: ‘Spinoza is a panpsychist. For him, mentality is a pervasive and fundamental feature of the natural world.’

这个泛心论（以及泛神论）论证，源自对充足理由律不留死角的运用，尤其是源自对「存在多个具有不同属性或本质属性的独立实体」这一观念的解释要求。斯宾诺莎最终得出结论：这一要求无法得到满足，由此得出，存在一个单一的根本实体，它拥有一切属性，兼具思想与广延两种本质属性。这一结论可以用泛神论的措辞来表达：主张只存在一个拥有一切属性与本质属性的单一实体，就会诱使人们把这个实体同时等同于神与自然。它也可以用泛心论的措辞来表达：这个单一的根本实体在本质上就是一个有意识的、思想着的东西。因此，意识是实在的根本属性。

This argument for panpsychism (and pantheism) results from leaving no stone unturned in the application of the PSR, and, in particular, from the demand to explain the idea that there are multiple distinct substances with different properties or attributes. Ultimately, Spinoza concludes that this demand cannot be met, which leads to the conclusion that there is a single fundamental substance with all properties and with both essential attributes of thought and extension. This conclusion can be cast in pantheist terms: to maintain that there exists just a single substance with all properties and attributes invites the identification of this substance with both God and nature. It can also be cast in panpsychist terms: the single fundamental substance is essentially a conscious, thinking thing. Hence consciousness is a fundamental property of reality.

追求可理解性，会把我们带到出人意料、发人深省的地方。如果泛心论无法引起你的共鸣，那就值得追问：是否真的万事皆有因——世界终究是否可理解。反过来，如果你认为充足理由律不可或缺，那么泛心论或许就显得不可避免。

Pursuing the demand for intelligibility leads to unexpected and thought-provoking places. If panpsychism doesn’t resonate with you, it’s worth asking if there truly is a reason for everything – if the world is ultimately intelligible after all. On the other hand, if you consider the PSR indispensable, panpsychism may seem inevitable.

但后一条道路是否提供了更深刻的存在意义感？我不太确定。事实上，对终极意义的不确定性正是艺术的燃料，而艺术最有力量的时刻，恰恰是拥抱并礼赞生命的不可理解与荒诞的时刻。另一方面，当一件艺术作品的意义太过明显、寓意太过直白时，对它提出批评似乎也顺理成章。所以，正如清晰而明确的意义远非最高的美学目标，让实在变得完全可理解、提供「人生的意义」，是否是哲学一个可取而现实的目标，同样不甚清楚。

But does this latter path offer a deeper sense of meaningful existence? I’m not so sure. In fact, uncertainty about ultimate meaning fuels the arts, which, at their most powerful, embrace and celebrate the unintelligibility and absurdity of life. On the other hand, it seems legitimate to criticise art when its meaning is too obvious, its message too on the nose. So, just as clear and unambiguous meaning is far from the supreme aesthetic goal, it’s not clear that rendering reality fully intelligible, providing the ‘meaning of life’, is a desirable or realistic aim for philosophy.

生活中的不满、意义感的危机，可能源于我们为个人与职业的追求找不到好的理由；类似地，哲学上的不满也可能源于无力使世界变得可理解。但这种不满究竟能否靠提供更好的理由来真正治愈，还远不清楚。

Dissatisfaction in life, a crisis in meaning, may originate when we can find no good reason for our personal and professional pursuits, and, analogously, philosophical dissatisfaction may stem from an inability to render the world intelligible. But it is far from clear that this sort of dissatisfaction can ever really be remedied by the provision of better reasons.

一份更有声望或报酬更高的工作，或许可以构成一个早晨起床的理由。但关于价值与意义的深层问题将依然原封不动。荒诞不会那么轻易被击溃。同样，像斯宾诺莎的体系那样建立在充足理由律之上的哲学体系，其许诺之所以吸引人，也许只在于我们想通过回答任何可能的问题来「解决」哲学。但我们同样可以认为，对一切的解释等于什么也没解释。

A more prestigious or better-paid job may constitute a reason to get out of bed in the morning. But deep questions about value and meaning will remain untouched. The absurd is not so easily vanquished. Similarly, the promise of a philosophical system built on the PSR, such as Spinoza’s, may appeal to the extent that we want to ‘solve’ philosophy by providing an answer to any possible question. But we may equally take an explanation of everything as an explanation of nothing.

与其为我们的一切追求寻找完美的理由，也许我们应当只是拥抱我们所做的事——无论它是什么——如其本然地拥抱。正如法国哲学家、作家加缪（Albert Camus）所反思的，西西弗斯（Sisyphus）的慰藉只能来自积极地拥抱自己的处境：「向着高处的挣扎本身，就足以充实一颗人心。应当想象西西弗斯是幸福的。」

Instead of seeking perfect reasons for all our pursuits, perhaps we ought to just embrace what we do, whatever it may be, for what it is. As the French philosopher and writer Albert Camus reflected, solace for Sisyphus can only come from positively embracing his situation: ‘The struggle itself towards the heights is enough to fill a man’s heart. One must imagine Sisyphus happy.’

期待把世界变得可理解，这本身或许就搞错了方向；我们不如去享受沉思的过程——沉思世界那凌乱而不可理解的荣光，把我们哲学的巨石推上山去。投入这项沉思事业的一种方式，就是发展与探索系统性的哲学理论。一个凌乱而不可理解的世界，并不妨碍我们建造高度有序的哲学大厦。但若以为这些大厦哪怕在原则上也有能力彻底消解生命的奥秘，那就错了。像泛心论这样的哲学理论，最好被理解为为这些奥秘提供了新的、丰富的视角，而我们也应当对其可能崩塌、可能被取代保持开放。

Perhaps it’s misguided to expect to render the world intelligible, and we ought instead to just enjoy the process of contemplating its messy unintelligible glory, of pushing our philosophical boulder up the mountain. One way of engaging with this contemplative project is in the development and exploration of systematic philosophical theories. A messy, unintelligible world doesn’t preclude the construction of highly orderly philosophical edifices. But it would be a mistake to think these capable, even in principle, of definitively dissolving life’s mysteries. Philosophical theories such as panpsychism are better thought of as providing new and enriching perspectives on these mysteries, and we should remain open to the possibility of their collapse and replacement.

放弃那无所不包的可理解性要求，转而把哲学设想为对我们的处境提供不同视角的活动，我们便能更好地理解何谓哲学的进步。哲学中广泛存在的分歧，不应被当作哲学堕落的证据——比如说，与自然科学相比。毋宁说，我们应当庆幸：二元论、物理主义（physicalism）与泛心论这些对立的观点得以并存，为我们审视生命中最诱人的奥秘提供了丰富多样的视角。

By giving up the all-encompassing demand for intelligibility and, instead, conceiving of philosophy as providing different perspectives on our condition, we can better understand what it is to make progress in philosophy. Widespread philosophical disagreement should not be taken as evidence that philosophy is degenerate compared with, say, the natural sciences. Rather, we should celebrate the fact that opposing views such as dualism, physicalism and panpsychism exist to provide us with richly diverse perspectives on life’s most tantalising mysteries.

泛心论能使世界变得可理解吗？我不确定有什么能做到这一点，甚至不确定追求至高的理由是否说得通。但把泛心论作为对意识之深谜的一种回应来加以沉思，其中仍有大量乐趣可言，而且我想，我们对一种哲学理论所能要求的，也就仅此而已。

Can panpsychism make the world intelligible? I’m not sure that anything can, or that it even makes sense to pursue supreme reasons. But there is still plenty of joy to be had in the contemplation of panpsychism as a response to the deep mystery of consciousness, and I doubt we could ask any more of a philosophical theory.
