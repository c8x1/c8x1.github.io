探寻知识界限的百年征程

Alexander V Gheorghiu·Aeon·2026年10月17日·10分钟阅读·原文

1930年9月8日傍晚，德国数学家大卫·希尔伯特（David Hilbert）在柯尼斯堡（Königsberg）走到麦克风前，通过无线电向他的同胞发表演讲。他时年六十八岁，刚刚退休，正值声望的顶峰。这场演讲是对纯粹理性的捍卫，发表在伊曼努尔·康德（Immanuel Kant）的城市，而结尾的两句话后来被刻在了他的墓碑上：Wir müssen wissen. Wir werden wissen——我们必须知道。我们必将知道。

On the evening of 8 September 1930, the German mathematician David Hilbert stepped up to a microphone in Königsberg and addressed his countrymen by radio. He was 68, recently retired, and at the height of his fame. The talk was a defence of pure reason, delivered in the city of Immanuel Kant, and it ended with two phrases that would later be carved on his tombstone: Wir müssen wissen. Wir werden wissen . We must know. We shall know.

有些问题是否对人类心灵永远关闭？世上是否存在我们永远无法知晓的真理？希尔伯特认为，至少在数学之内，情况不该如此。在他看来，数学是一场用符号进行的游戏：只要定下正确的公理作为游戏规则，每一个数学问题都可以被解决。他错了。

Are certain questions closed to the human mind? Are there truths about the world that we can never know? Hilbert thought that, at least within mathematics, this could not be the case. For him, mathematics was a game played with symbols: one could fix the right axioms as rules of the game and every mathematical problem could be solved. He was wrong.

在他发表柯尼斯堡演讲的前一天，同一座城市的一场小型学术会议上，一位名叫库尔特·哥德尔（Kurt Gödel）的二十四岁奥地利人悄然宣布：这样的算术系统不可能存在。无论你定下怎样合理的规则集，总有真的东西处在这些规则之外，因而也处在我们的知识之外。我们不能知道。我们不会知道。哥德尔的结果，即所谓不完全性定理（Incompleteness Theorems），是20世纪逻辑学的里程碑式成就。近期哲学与数学领域的工作，开始照亮它们所打开的这道裂缝，也随之照亮了关于知识本身之根基的更深的东西。故事发生在同一座城市，但要早两个世纪。

The day before his Königsberg address, at a small academic meeting in the same city, a 24-year-old Austrian named Kurt Gödel had quietly announced that no such system for arithmetic could exist. Whatever reasonable set of rules you fix, something true must lie beyond them and thus beyond our knowledge. We can’t know. We won’t know. Gödel’s results, known as the Incompleteness Theorems, are landmark achievements of 20th-century logic. Recent work in philosophy and mathematics has begun to illuminate the gap they opened, and with it something deeper about the foundations of knowledge itself. The story begins in the same city, but two centuries earlier.

当康德忆起多年前读过的大卫·休谟（David Hume）的著作时，他从自己所谓的「独断论迷梦」中惊醒。康德原本相信，纯粹推理能教给我们关于世界的全部绝对真理，但休谟提出的一个问题把他难住了：我们究竟有什么理由相信未来会与过去相似？我们或许见过太阳升起一千次，可我们怎么知道它明天还会升起？休谟论证说，相信未来与过去相似，只不过是一种习惯罢了。康德无法回应休谟的挑战，沉默了十年。

When Kant remembered a book by David Hume he’d read years earlier, he was woken from what he called his ‘dogmatic slumber’. Kant had believed that pure reasoning could teach us every absolute truth about the world, but Hume had asked a question that that stumped him: what reason do we actually have to believe that the future will resemble the past? We may have seen the Sun rise a thousand times, but how do we know it will rise tomorrow? The belief that the future will resemble the past is, Hume argued, merely a kind of habit. Kant couldn’t answer Hume’s challenge and was silenced for a decade.

1781年，康德终于以《纯粹理性批判》（Critique of Pure Reason）打破沉默，给出了一个激进的回答。我们只能认识世界向我们心灵显现的样子，我们的科学正立足于此；而本体世界——「物自体」（Ding an sich，thing-in-itself）——则完全超出了我们的把握。因果性不是我们在世界中发现的东西，而是心灵为了让经验本身融贯而提供的东西。当我们看着一只猫把玻璃杯从桌上推下去时，我们并不是先看到一连串赤裸的运动，再依据某条形而上学原理推断说这一推导致了坠落。不：我们直接就把这一推看作坠落的原因。因果结构不是我们附加到经验上的假设，而是经验一开始向我们呈现的方式的一部分。它早已在我们的心灵之中。

When Kant finally broke his silence with the Critique of Pure Reason (1781), he offered a radical answer. We can know the world only as it appears to our minds, and that is where our science sits; the noumenal world – the Ding an sich (the ‘thing-in-itself’) – simply lies beyond our reach. Causation is not something we discover in the world, but something the mind supplies for experience to be coherent at all. When we watch a cat push a glass off a table, we don’t see a bare sequence of motions and then infer, by some metaphysical principle, that the push caused the fall. No: we see the push as causing the fall. The causal structure is not a hypothesis we add to the experience; it is part of how the experience comes to us in the first place. It is in our minds already.

到这里还算顺遂：康德接住了休谟的挑战，科学的运作也变得可理解了：是我们在为经验赋予结构。但工作尚未完成，因为还有数学——正是科学赖以表述的那门语言。休谟满足于认为算术和代数不过是对定义的展开；证明一条定理，就像打开行李箱、从中取出一套衣服。康德却不能就此止步。如果数学只是这样的展开，它又如何为科学作保？要么科学对世界说不出任何新东西，要么这个叙述遗漏了什么。

So far, so good: Kant has met Hume’s challenge, and the workings of science are intelligible: we are structuring our experiences. But the work is not done, for there remains mathematics, the very language in which the sciences are framed. Hume was content that arithmetic and algebra are merely the unpacking of definitions; that proving a theorem is like unpacking a suitcase to find an outfit. Kant could not leave it there. If mathematics were only such unpacking, how could it underwrite the sciences? Either the sciences tell us nothing new about the world, or something has gone missing from the account.

难道「7加5」可以展开后找到「12」吗？康德认为不能。毋宁说，人是在心灵中用7和5构造出12。一个多世纪以后，一位名叫布劳威尔（L E J Brouwer）的年轻荷兰数学家循着康德的思路论证说，如果数学家的工作就在于这样的心灵构造，那么人就不能假定任何命题非真即假。这引发了争议：希尔伯特反驳道，夺走数学家的这一假定，就好比「禁止拳击手使用他的拳头」。

Does one unpack ‘add 7 and 5’ to find ‘12’? Kant thought not. Rather, one constructs 12 from 7 and 5 in the mind. More than a century later, a young Dutch mathematician called L E J Brouwer, following Kant’s thought, argued that, if the mathematician’s work consists in such mental constructions, then one cannot assume any statement is either true or false. That was controversial: to strip the mathematician of that assumption, Hilbert retorted, would be like ‘proscribing … to the boxer the use of his fists’.

19世纪末的戈特洛布·弗雷格（Gottlob Frege）出于别样的理由，觉得康德的数学叙述无法容忍：他宣称，算术不是心理的东西。数存在着，无论有没有人去数它。他着手把算术还原为纯粹的逻辑，奠基于「真」与「假」这两个独立于心灵的范畴之上。这项事业耗去了他二十年的生命，产出了历史上语言哲学与数学哲学中一些影响最深远的进展，并被广泛视为点燃了现代逻辑学这门学科。然而，1902年，当他的代表作《算术基本规律》（Grundgesetze der Arithmetik）第二卷正待付印时，根基崩裂了。英国哲学家伯特兰·罗素（Bertrand Russell）致信弗雷格，指出这个系统的核心存在一处不一致。他后来在日记里一条忧郁的记事概括了自己的败局：「我弄清数之所指的一切努力，终归失败了。」

Gottlob Frege , working in the late 19th century, found Kant’s account of mathematics intolerable for different reasons: arithmetic, he claimed, is not psychological. The numbers exist whether anyone counts them or not. He set about reducing arithmetic to pure logic, grounded in the mind-independent categories of True and False. The project consumed two decades of his life and produced some of the most consequential advances in the philosophy of language and mathematics in history. It is widely regarded as having sparked the modern discipline of logic. And yet, as the second volume of his masterwork, the Grundgesetze der Arithmetik , sat at the printer in 1902, the foundations cracked. The British philosopher Bertrand Russell sent Frege a letter pointing out an inconsistency at the heart of the system. A melancholy note in his diary later summed up his defeat: ‘My efforts to become clear about what is meant by number have resulted in failure.’

在写那封信之前，罗素已为数学的基础问题苦斗了数年。直到1900年在国际哲学大会上遇见意大利数学家朱塞佩·皮亚诺（Giuseppe Peano），他才获得了攻克这个问题所需的工具。罗素决意不倚仗弗雷格的大厦，而是与同事阿尔弗雷德·诺思·怀特海（Alfred North Whitehead）一起另建自己的大厦。《数学原理》（Principia Mathematica）于1910年至1913年间分三卷问世：全部数学，皆奠基于纯粹逻辑。

Russell had been wrestling with the foundations of mathematics for some years before his letter. It was only after meeting the Italian mathematician Giuseppe Peano at the International Congress of Philosophy in 1900 that he acquired the tools to attempt the problem. In place of Frege’s edifice, Russell set out to build his own , together with his colleague Alfred North Whitehead. Principia Mathematica appeared in three massive volumes between 1910 and 1913: all of mathematics, grounded in pure logic.

罗素的学生路德维希·维特根斯坦（Ludwig Wittgenstein）在奥匈帝国的炮兵部队中度过了战争岁月，他一次又一次主动请缨去最暴露的岗位——东线炮火之下的一个观测哨，并因此至少三次因勇敢受勋。他随身带着笔记本。在一次次轰炸的间隙，他把弗雷格—罗素哲学打磨成一套关于语言如何触及世界的完整叙述：世界由事实组成，句子是事实的图像，凡是可以说的，都可以说清楚。1918年，他在意大利的战俘营里完成手稿，并从营中寄给了罗素。维特根斯坦自认为已经解决了哲学问题，散尽了继承的财产，去奥地利山区教小学了。

Russell’s student Ludwig Wittgenstein spent the war in the Austro-Hungarian artillery, volunteering again and again for the most exposed posts he could find – an observation position under fire on the Eastern Front, from which he was decorated for bravery at least three times. He carried notebooks. In them, between bombardments, he worked the Frege-Russell philosophy into a complete account of how language touches the world: the world is made of facts, a sentence is a picture of a fact , and whatever can be said at all can be said clearly. He finished the manuscript in an Italian prisoner-of-war camp in 1918 and sent it to Russell from the camp. Wittgenstein took himself to have solved the problems of philosophy, gave away his inherited fortune, and left to teach primary school in the Austrian mountains.

《逻辑哲学论》（Tractatus）成了维也纳一群哲学家的宣言，其中包括莫里茨·石里克（Moritz Schlick）、鲁道夫·卡尔纳普（Rudolf Carnap）和奥托·纽拉特（Otto Neurath），他们把自己的立场称为逻辑实证主义（logical positivism）。一个陈述之所以有意义，仅当存在某种办法来判定它是否为真。分析陈述可以通过展开相关语词的定义来判定：例如「所有单身汉都未婚」为真，是因为单身汉按定义就是未婚男子。综合陈述是关于世界的断言，其判定——原则上——要靠出门去看：「1929年6月3日维也纳下了雨」为假，因为人们本可以观察到那天环城大道（Ringstrasse）上空一直晴朗无云。其余的一切，都是没有内容的语法。

The Tractatus became the manifesto of a group of philosophers in Vienna including Moritz Schlick, Rudolf Carnap and Otto Neurath, who called their position logical positivism . A statement is meaningful only if there is some way of settling whether it is true. Analytic statements are settled by unfolding the definitions of the words involved: for example, ‘All bachelors are unmarried’ is true because a bachelor is, by definition, an unmarried man. Synthetic statements are claims about the world, and are settled by – in principle – going out and looking: ‘It rained in Vienna on 3 June 1929’ is false because one could have observed that the sky over the Ringstrasse stayed clear that day. Anything else is grammar without content.

这个后来被称为维也纳学派（Vienna Circle）的群体主张：数学是分析的，科学是综合的。数学的职能是从科学断言中提取信息：它输送已有的东西而不添置任何新东西，就像管道输送水却不生产水。如果维也纳学派是对的，那么康德与布劳威尔对科学知识和数学知识施加的限制就完全多余。如果。

The Vienna Circle , as they became known, claimed that mathematics is analytic and science is synthetic. The function of mathematics is to extract information from scientific claims: it carries what is already there without adding anything, the way plumbing moves water without supplying any. If the Vienna Circle were right, then Kant’s and Brouwer’s restrictions on scientific and mathematical knowledge were entirely unnecessary. If .

到1929年，经过希尔伯特、罗素、弗雷格、维特根斯坦、维也纳学派和布劳威尔等人近三十年的劳作，数学的基础看上去几乎大功告成。只剩下一根能把整个框架锁合在一起的横梁。在1900年巴黎国际数学家大会的全体演讲中，希尔伯特曾列出23个将定义这门学科下一个世纪的问题。第二个问题是：证明算术是一致的——也就是说，从公理出发的任何推理链，无论多长，都不会导出矛盾。这根梁却始终装不进去。

By 1929, after almost three decades of labours by Hilbert, Russell, Frege, Wittgenstein, the Vienna Circle and Brouwer, among others, the foundations of mathematics looked very nearly finished. One beam remained that would lock the whole frame together. In the plenary addresses for the International Congress of Mathematicians in Paris in 1900, Hilbert had set out 23 problems that would define the next century of the subject. The second problem was this: prove that arithmetic is consistent – that is, no chain of reasoning from the axioms, however long, would yield a contradiction. The beam would not go in.

1920年代在维也纳大学攻读物理时，哥德尔参加了石里克关于罗素的研讨课，由此被引入逻辑问题，并经由这些问题进入了希尔伯特纲领（Hilbert's programme）。他1929年的博士论文证明，标准的一阶逻辑系统是完全的——每一个逻辑有效的命题，原则上都能从逻辑公理推导出来。这正是希尔伯特想要的结果，尽管针对的是纯逻辑而非数学。一年之后，他转向了顺理成章的下一步：算术的一致性，即希尔伯特的第二个问题。

While studying physics at the University of Vienna in the 1920s, Gödel had attended Schlick’s seminar on Russell, which drew him into the problems of logic and, by way of them, into Hilbert’s programme. His PhD in 1929 showed that the standard system of first-order logic is complete – every logically valid statement can, in principle, be derived from the axioms of logic. It was the result Hilbert wanted, though for pure logic rather than mathematics. A year later, he turned to the natural next step: the consistency of arithmetic, Hilbert’s second problem.

在希尔伯特广播演讲的前一天，哥德尔在柯尼斯堡会议上宣布的东西，没有人预料到。哥德尔把《数学原理》视为数学在逻辑内部的系统表述，他证明：存在真的数学陈述，仅凭《数学原理》自身的资源无法证明。其中包括这样一个陈述：「《数学原理》是一致的。」这个证明的核心技巧，是把关于系统自身的陈述编码进系统之中——这一招与罗素致弗雷格的信有着同样令人眩晕的气质。只不过，罗素的信杀死的是一个系统，哥德尔的定理杀死的是一个纲领。

What he announced at the Königsberg meeting the day before Hilbert’s radio address, no one had expected. Taking Principia Mathematica as the articulation of mathematics within logic, Gödel showed that there were true mathematical statements that cannot be proved using the resources of Principia Mathematica alone. Among them is the statement ‘ Principia Mathematica is consistent.’ The trick at the heart of the proof is to encode statements about the system within the system itself – a move with the same vertiginous quality as Russell’s letter to Frege. But where Russell’s letter had killed a system, Gödel’s theorem killed a programme.

他的论证极为一般，适用于任何一个合理的算术系统。希尔伯特那个用公理系统囊括全部数学——甚至仅仅是算术——的梦想，就此终结。

His argument was so general that it applied to any reasonable system for arithmetic. Hilbert’s dream of an axiomatic system capturing all of mathematics – or even just arithmetic – was over.

维也纳的反应先是难以置信，继而是沮丧。这个学派把整套哲学奠立在「数学是分析的」这一假定之上；哥德尔却表明，事情没有那么简单。卡尔纳普为消化这一结果苦挣了多年。维特根斯坦对它不以为然，但他本人也早已在抛弃《逻辑哲学论》。他后来主张，意义不在于拿图像去匹配实在，而仅仅在于人们如何在公共场合言谈。这位学派昔日的先知，没有给自己的哲学留下任何还站得住的东西。

The response in Vienna was disbelief, then dismay. The Circle had built its philosophy on the assumption that mathematics was analytic; Gödel had shown it wasn’t as simple as that. Carnap struggled for years to absorb the result. Wittgenstein dismissed it, but was already repudiating the Tractatus himself. Meaning, he came to argue, lay not in matching picture to reality but simply in how people discourse in public. The one-time prophet of the Circle left nothing of its philosophy standing.

希尔伯特从未公开承认哥德尔的结果宣判了他纲领的死刑。他于1943年去世；那时他的同事们已惨遭纳粹杀害或被迫流亡。他在哥廷根（Göttingen）的墓碑上，刻着他当年对着柯尼斯堡话筒说过的话：Wir müssen wissen. Wir werden wissen——我们必须知道。我们必将知道。

Hilbert never publicly conceded that Gödel’s result doomed his programme. He died in 1943; his colleagues had by then been murdered or exiled by the Nazis. His tombstone in Göttingen bears the words he had spoken into the Königsberg microphone: Wir müssen wissen. Wir werden wissen . We must know. We shall know.

那么哥德尔呢？德奥合并（Anschluss）之后，他逃离维也纳来到普林斯顿，在那里继续为逻辑学做出卓越贡献，同时与挚友阿尔伯特·爱因斯坦（Albert Einstein）长时间散步。1947年参加美国入籍面试时，法官问他纳粹那样的政权是否可能在美国出现，他开始解释自己在宪法中发现的一个逻辑漏洞，那个漏洞恰恰允许这种事发生。法官打断了他，迅速把程序推向了结束。（我们至今不知道他发现的漏洞是什么。）

And Gödel? After the Anschluss, he escaped Vienna for Princeton, where he continued to make extraordinary contributions to logic while taking long walks with his close friend Albert Einstein. At his American citizenship exam in 1947, asked by the judge whether a regime like the Nazis’ could arise in the United States, he began to explain a logical loophole he had found in the Constitution that would permit exactly that. The judge cut him off and moved the proceedings swiftly to their conclusion. (We still don’t know what loophole he found.)

正统的回应是：保留真理，放弃证明。存在一个独立于我们而立的数学事实的领域，公理系统只能触及其一部分；不完全性度量的，是「事实如此」与「我们所能证明」之间的距离。这一立场最坚定的倡导者正是哥德尔本人。然而，这个领域带来两个问题。其一，随着逻辑实证主义的崩塌，康德的问题卷土重来：如果数学真理独立于心灵，它又如何能够结构存在于心灵之内的诸门科学？其二，设定这个领域，主要是为了让我们避开两个不受欢迎的选项——一边是心理主义，另一边是布劳威尔式的限制。避开两个结论，并不构成对第三个结论的论证。

The orthodox response was to keep the truth and give up the proof. There is a realm of mathematical fact, standing independent of us, and axiomatic systems reach only part of it; incompleteness measures the distance between what is so and what we can show. Its most committed advocate was Gödel himself. However, two problems come with the realm. First, with logical positivism in ruins, Kant’s problem returns: if mathematical truth is independent of the mind, how can it structure sciences that exist within the mind? Second, the realm is posited chiefly to spare us two unwelcome alternatives – psychologism on one side, Brouwer’s restrictions on the other. Avoiding two conclusions is not an argument for a third.

1973年，英国哲学家迈克尔·达米特（Michael Dummett）受邀在不列颠学院（British Academy）发表年度哲学演讲。他选定的题目是「演绎的证成」（The Justification of Deduction）——如何在不借助「数学的对象存在于一个独立领域」这一假定的情况下，为数学家的日常实践提供证成。他告诉听众，这个问题也许是「全部哲学中最根本、最棘手的问题之一」。

In 1973, the British philosopher Michael Dummett was invited to deliver the annual philosophical lecture at the British Academy. He took as his subject ‘The Justification of Deduction’ – how to justify the ordinary practices of mathematicians without helping oneself to the assumption that their subject matter exists in a realm of its own. The problem, he told his audience, was perhaps ‘one of the most fundamental and intractable problems … in all philosophy’.

达米特是20世纪研究弗雷格的大师；他的《弗雷格：语言哲学》（Frege: Philosophy of Language，1973）把这位原本默默无闻的人物推到了学科的前沿。这本书写了多年，但1964年，手稿几乎完成之时，达米特却搁下了笔。他在序言中解释说，他认为自己有责任「积极投身于反对种族主义，它当时在英国生活中正日益显形」。几年后他回到手稿时，弗雷格遗著（Nachlass，即其文稿遗产）中一个此前被隐匿的片段面世了，揭示出这位哲学家是一个「恶毒的种族主义者」，持有「极右翼政治观点」。书终于出版后，达米特记下了这苦涩的讽刺，并反思说，他由此懂得了一件阴暗的事：一个他敬仰的心灵，何以能窝藏他所不齿的观念。

Dummett was the great Frege scholar of the 20th century; his Frege: Philosophy of Language (1973) brought an otherwise obscure figure to the forefront of the discipline. The book had been years in the writing, but in 1964, with the manuscript all but finished, Dummett put down his pen. He conceived it his duty, he explains in the preface, to involve himself ‘actively in opposition to the racism which was becoming more and more manifest in English life.’ When he returned to the manuscript several years later, a previously suppressed fragment of Frege’s Nachlass (ie, his literary estate) had surfaced, revealing the philosopher as a ‘virulent racist’ with ‘extreme Right-wing political opinions’. Once his book was finally published, Dummett recorded the bitter irony, and reflected that he had learned something dark about how a mind he revered could harbour views he despised.

达米特从弗雷格的逻辑工作中得到的领悟是：数学问题，归根结底是语言问题。在他看来，出错之处在于弗雷格把他的叙述奠基于一个独立于心灵的真理概念之上。「正是在这一点上（也仅在这一点上），」达米特写道，「弗雷格的新逻辑方法是一种倒退。」对弗雷格而言，逻辑服从于真理：规则之所以好，是因为它们保住了某种既已固定的东西。达米特把这整个翻转了过来。

What Dummett took from Frege’s work on logic was that the problems of mathematics are, at heart, problems of language. Where things had gone wrong, he thought, was that Frege had grounded his account in a mind-independent notion of truth. ‘In this respect (and in this respect alone),’ Dummett wrote, ‘Frege’s new approach to logic was retrograde.’ For Frege, logic answered to truth: the rules were good because they preserved something already fixed. Dummett turned this around.

循着维特根斯坦的后期思想，达米特主张「一种意义理论就是一种理解理论」。而他坚持，理解必须是可显现的：一个有能力的说话者在知道一个句子是什么意思时所知道的一切，原则上必须是她能在语言使用中显示出来的东西。在《形而上学的逻辑基础》（The Logical Basis of Metaphysics，1991）中，达米特给这一关于语言、逻辑与实在的叙述赋予了最绵密的形态。我们的语言实践——我们准备如何断言、证成与推理的方式——决定了逻辑。逻辑又转而决定了什么算作事实、什么算作真理，并最终决定了我们有权把什么视为实在。

Following Wittgenstein’s later thinking, Dummett argued ‘a theory of meaning is a theory of understanding.’ And understanding, he insisted, must be manifestable: whatever a competent speaker knows in knowing what a sentence means must be something she can, in principle, display in her use of language. In The Logical Basis of Metaphysics (1991), Dummett gave this account of language, logic and reality its most sustained form. Our linguistic practices – the ways in which we are prepared to assert, justify and infer – determine logic. Logic, in turn, determines what counts as a fact, what counts as truth, and ultimately what we are entitled to regard as real.

想想一个孩子是如何学会「红」这个词的。我们并不是先告诉她某种波长的光、颜色属性的形而上学，或者使「这是红的」为真的种种条件。我们指着东西。她把熟番茄说成绿的，我们就纠正她；她把消防车说成红的，我们就赞许她。随着时间推移，她开始像我们一样使用这个词——到那时，她就理解了它。并不存在什么藏在实践背后、由她的理解所构成的进一步的事实。理解就是这门实践，被熟练地践行着。

Consider how a child learns the word ‘red’. We do not begin by telling her about light of a certain wavelength, or the metaphysics of colour properties, or the conditions that would make ‘this is red’ true. We point at things. We correct her when she calls a ripe tomato green; we approve when she calls a fire engine red. Over time, she comes to use the word as we do – and at that point, she understands it. There is no further fact, hidden behind the practice, in which her understanding consists. The understanding is the practice, competently exercised.

达米特于2011年去世；此前在1999年，他因「对哲学与种族正义的贡献」受封爵士。他被铭记为上个世纪最重要的英国哲学家之一。然而，他的纲领尽管威力十足，终究只是一份章程：他列出了语言、逻辑与实在的理论必须达成什么，却从未真正完成一部。

Dummett died in 2011, having been knighted in 1999 for ‘services to philosophy and to racial justice’. He is remembered as one of the most significant British philosophers of the previous century. Yet for all its power, his programme remained a prospectus: he set out what a theory of language, logic and reality must achieve, but he never completed one.

2005年，乌普萨拉大学（Uppsala University）的博士生托尔·桑德奎斯特（Tor Sandqvist）提交了一篇承接达米特挑战的博士论文：在不诉诸真理的情况下，为数学家的日常实践提供证成。达米特给出的是解答必须满足的条件，桑德奎斯特给出的则是一个解答。他对数学家实际使用的演绎给出了一个精确的数学证成，完全奠基于断言的实践之上。拳击手的拳头松了绑。

In 2005, Tor Sandqvist, a doctoral student at Uppsala University, submitted a thesis that took up Dummett’s challenge: to justify the ordinary practices of mathematicians without any appeal to truth. Where Dummett had given the conditions a solution must satisfy, Sandqvist gave a solution. He produced a precise mathematical justification of deduction as mathematicians actually use it, grounded entirely in practices of assertion. The boxer’s fists were untied.

十年后，它在伦敦大学学院（University College London）的计算机科学系被重新发现：一群逻辑学家认出了桑德奎斯特实际建成的东西，并将其发展成一个足以与正统真值条件观分庭抗礼的完整框架。如今这个框架与基于真理的叙述并驾齐驱，恰似哥德尔博士论文之后的局面。那么数学呢？这个UCL团队最近表明，达米特—桑德奎斯特框架提供了一种方式来理解哥德尔不完全性定理究竟在说什么——而它说的并不是知识的界限。

It was rediscovered a decade later in the Department of Computer Science at University College London, where a group of logicians recognised what Sandqvist had actually built, and developed it into a full framework on a par with the orthodox truth-conditional view. The framework now stands equal to the truth-based account, analogous to the situation after Gödel’s PhD thesis. What about mathematics? The UCL team have recently shown that the Dummett-Sandqvist framework gives a way to understand what Gödel’s Incompleteness Theorems are about, and it is not the limits of knowledge.

如果意义来自断言的实践，那么我们的概念，也就只在实践已然确定它们的范围内被确定。一个定义系统是一种宪法，它规定着某种东西。比如美国宪法，本意是要确立一种民主。但一部宪法定义了什么，要随它如何被修正而定。

If meaning comes from practices of assertion, then our concepts are settled only as far as our practices have settled them. A system of definitions is a kind of constitution that determines something. The US Constitution, for example, is meant to enshrine a form of democracy. But what a constitution defines answers to how it is amended.

《数学原理》是算术的一部宪法。把它当作宪法来读，就意味着假定它是一致的；否则它就是胡话。因此，当我们通过断言「《数学原理》是一致的」来修正它时，我们并没有改换主题：新公理只是把实践早已在做的事大声说了出来。哥德尔的定理对扩大后的宪法重新适用：修正过的系统有它自己无法证明的真理，有它自己无法担保的一致性。没有哪一次修正是最后一次；每一次忠实的扩展都为下一次腾出空间。把握算术这样的概念，不是去俯瞰一座完工的建筑；而是握住一条继续前行的原则：一条铺设下一段线路的规则，而不是一张标明线路尽头的地图。达米特把这种开放性称为不定可扩展性（indefinite extensibility）。

Principia Mathematica is a constitution for arithmetic. To read it as a constitution is to assume it is consistent; otherwise, it would be nonsense. So, when we amend it by asserting that ‘ Principia Mathematica is consistent,’ we are not changing the subject: the new axiom says aloud only what the practice already did. Gödel’s theorems apply afresh to the enlarged constitution: the amended system has its own unprovable truth, its own consistency it cannot certify. No amendment is the last; each faithful extension opens room for the next. To grasp a concept like arithmetic is not to survey a finished structure; it is to hold a principle for going on: a rule for laying the next stretch of line, not a map of where the line ends. Dummett called this openness indefinite extensibility .

起作用的正是「忠实」这个词，因为并非每一次修正都忠实。哥德尔声称美国宪法可能产出一个独裁政权。但这并不意味着独裁是民主的一种形式。无论他想到的是哪个漏洞，那漏洞都必然与宪法已然确立的实践相抵触。修正可以背叛宪法所定义的东西，但修正也可以展开它，把立宪承诺从一开始就要求的东西引申出来。达米特心里所想的，只是第二种。

Faithful is the word doing the work, because not every amendment is. Gödel claimed the US Constitution could deliver a dictatorship. That does not make dictatorship a form of democracy. Whatever loophole he had in mind, it would have contradicted the practices the Constitution already enshrined. An amendment can betray what a constitution defines. But it can also unfold it, drawing out what the founding commitments demanded all along. Only the second kind is what Dummett has in mind.

我们语言的宪法，就是我们当下公共言谈的实践。既然它可以如此扩展，那么我们宣称为「真」的东西，就不是一个带着我们永远够不着的天花板的固定结构，而是某种可以改变的东西。哥德尔的判词从来不曾针对数学本身，而只针对宪法的某个版本。在桑德奎斯特系统之内，我们可以系统地关照这种扩展。希尔伯特的话比他的纲领活得更久：Wir werden wissen——我们必将知道。不是因为答案正等在梯子的尽头，而是因为真理走到的地方，恰是我们已搭好横木的地方。

The constitution of our language is our current practice for public discourse. As it is extensible in this way, what we claim as ‘true’ is not a fixed structure with a ceiling we are forever failing to reach, but something that can change. Gödel’s verdict never applied to mathematics, but only to some edition of the constitution. Within the Sandqvist system, we can attend to the extension systematically. Hilbert’s words outlive his programme: Wir werden wissen. We shall know. Not because the answers are waiting at the end of a ladder, but because truth goes exactly as far as we have built the rungs.
