翻倍买来的东西

AI Parable (inspired by Amanda Askell)·每日精选·2026年10月27日·2分钟阅读

爹走那年，醋坊折成了两半。兄长要缸，三十口老缸连同坊牌都归他；粮仓的谷子和院里那口老井归我，另搭三口小缸让我起家。分家的墨迹还没干，官府的供醋契就贴了出来：清明前各坊送醋，评醋人当众挑毛病，毛病最少的一家独得三年的契。兄长在自家门口放话，三十口对三口，这契没什么好争的。

The year Father died, the vinegar house was split in two. My elder brother took the vats, thirty of them, along with the workshop's nameplate; the millet in the granary and the old well in the yard came to me, plus three small vats to start on. The ink on the settlement was barely dry when the prefecture posted its vinegar contract: before Qingming every house brings its vinegar, the taster calls out faults in public, and the house with the fewest faults takes the whole three-year contract. Brother announced at his gate that thirty vats against three left nothing to argue over.

当夜我翻爹留下的账。三十年，一年一页，每页记三样：缸口、谷子、评醋人挑出的毛病。头一页是二十一条；本钱翻一倍，少两三条；再翻一倍，又少两三条。没有哪一页是爹定下的规矩，这条线是从三十年的流水里自己浮出来的。我照着这条线算我那三口小缸，怎么算毛病都停在十四条上下。

That night I went through the ledger Father left behind. Thirty years, a page a year, three things to a page: vat mouths, millet, and the faults the taster had called. The first page read twenty-one; double the capital and two or three faults went; double it again and another two or three went. No page of it was a rule Father set down; the line rose out of thirty years of entries on its own. Held against my three small vats, it put my faults at fourteen however I counted.

清明送醋那日，兄长的三十口缸只有十一口盛得下谷，余下的空着。评醋人一坛一坛地挑，数出十九条；我那三口小缸谷子喂得足，数出十四条。十九条落在爹那条线外头老远。兄长当众没吭声，散了场却堵在我院门口，问三口缸凭什么压过三十口。

On tasting day, only eleven of Brother's thirty vats held millet; the rest stood empty. The taster went jar by jar and counted nineteen faults; my three small vats, fed full, counted fourteen. Nineteen fell far outside Father's line. Brother said nothing in public, but once the crowd broke up he blocked my gate and asked how three vats got the better of thirty.

我把爹的账摊在缸沿上给他看。爹晚年的几页只添缸不添谷——那几年谷贵，爹舍不得——缸口一年比一年大，毛病却几乎不动。兄长盯着看了半宿，天亮时把他那一半推了过来：缸归他出，谷归我出，账并成一本。头一年谷喂满一半的缸，毛病落到八条；第二年缸和谷一齐添，落到六条。爹的账页上只录缸口，所以他一辈子信大缸；谷子入了账，同一段日子便念出另一条理。

I spread Father's ledger open on the rim of a vat for him. In the pages from Father's last years the vats grew while the millet never did — those were dear-grain years, and he had pinched — and the vat mouths widened year by year while the faults barely moved. Brother stared half the night, and at first light he pushed his half across: the vats from him, the millet from me, one ledger. The first year, millet filled half the vats and the faults fell to eight; the second year, vats and millet grew together and the count fell to six. Father's pages had recorded only vat mouths, so he believed in big vats all his life; once the millet entered the ledger, the same years read out another rule.

六条以后就慢了。缸再添，谷再翻，账上的数只肯一条一条地挪，第三年停在两条上，再不肯走。评醋人把那两条咂了又咂：一条是井水的咸，一条是本地谷壳带的涩。他说这不算手艺上的账；这口井养了岑家的醋一百年，这口咸也跟了一百年。我拿外乡的甜水试过一回，咸味果然淡了，醋却认生，毛病反倒多出三条。把全镇的谷子酿完，那两条也去不掉。

After six, it slowed. More vats, millet doubled again, and the ledger gave up its faults only one at a time; in the third year it settled at two and refused to move. The taster held those two on his tongue: one was the salt of the well, one the husk-bitterness of the local millet. He said no craft owed those; the well had raised the Cen family's vinegar for a hundred years, and its salt had kept pace for a hundred. I once brewed a round with sweet water from another county; the salt did fade, but the vinegar turned strange in it, and three new faults came in. Brew every grain in town, and those two would still be there.

后来镇上别家也学着记这种账。同样一坛醋，官府按等第录，就成了爬坡的记法：先缓后陡，挨着顶格便贴线平走。再后来两家大坊并成一家，缸添过两百口，账上的线就在两百口那里慢慢弯了腰，弯得没有声音，可的确是弯了。并坊后头一个清明，契就归了合起来的岑家，满三年，又续三年，我和兄长再没分过账。有后生拎着钱上门，说要一口气添一百口缸压过岑家，我请他先翻到缸和谷一起翻倍的那几年，再掂一掂，认不认得下井里那口咸。

Later, other houses in town took up keeping such ledgers. The same jar of vinegar, entered by the prefecture's grade marks, became the record of a climb: gentle before it was steep, then running flat along the top mark. Later still, two big houses merged, the vats passed two hundred, and the line in their ledger bent at two hundred — no sound to the bend, but a bend all the same. The first Qingming after that merger, the contract went to the rejoined Cen house; it ran three years, was renewed for three more, and Brother and I never split the ledger again. When a young man arrives with money, vowing to add a hundred vats in one stroke and bury the Cen house, I ask him to read first the years when vats and millet doubled together, then to weigh whether he can swallow the salt in that well.

注解：这说的是缩放定律（scaling laws）：本钱按比例翻上去，毛病沿一条平滑的账线一条条少下去，渐近于井水那一条再也降不掉的底；这条线是从多年不同规模的流水里统计出来的经验规律，无人设计，固定本钱下缸与谷须同比例放大，而在记过的账页之外，线也可能慢慢拐弯。

Note: This is about scaling laws: as capital is scaled up in proportion, the faults fall away one by one along a smooth line and approach a floor, the salt of the well, that no scale of spending ever removes; the line is an empirical rule counted out of years of entries at many different sizes, designed by no one, and under a fixed budget the vats and the millet must grow in equal proportion, while beyond the pages actually recorded the line may bend.
