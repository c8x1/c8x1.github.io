最短的口诀

AI Parable (inspired by Amanda Askell)·每日精选·2026年10月1日·3分钟阅读

如今，记花楼门厅里挂着那四句歌，装在樟木框里，来卖布的娃娃张口就能背；镇上家家的机子都在织「过山云」，织得比白布还贱。楼上西间有一口柜，锁了十二年，柜里那块疯布没人能念，只有守楼的阿芦能从头背到尾，背完一遍要一天一夜。楼里那句老话应在它身上：传得下去的花样，从来是回字、缠枝这些肯回头的东西。

Now, in the front hall of the record house, those four lines hang framed in camphor wood, and any child come to sell cloth can recite them without pausing. Every loom in town weaves the Passing Cloud, so many of them that it sells below plain cloth. Upstairs, in the west room, a cabinet has stood locked for twelve years. No one can chant the mad cloth inside it; only Alu, who keeps the house, can recite it from start to finish, and finishing takes her a day and a night. The old saying of the house has come true on it: the patterns that travel down the years are the ones willing to turn back, the meander and the scrollwork, the shapes that repeat.

十二年前腊月，佟婆婆是听着阿芦把最后一行背完才咽气的。那年冬天本来有条出路：沈老板留过话，开了年阿芦去铺里坐誊诀的案，月月有银子，只是老太太一走，那块布归他。阿芦没有去。她守着一盏灯，一夜一夜把三千六百行念到头，第二天一早，亲手给西间那口柜上了锁。

Twelve years ago, in the twelfth month, old Tong held on until Alu had recited the last row to her, and only then let go. There had been a way out that winter: Shen the draper had left word that come the new year Alu could sit at the copying desk in his shop, silver every month, on one condition — when the old woman went, the cloth would be his. Alu did not take it. She kept one lamp and read the three thousand six hundred rows through to the end, night after night, and the next morning she locked the cabinet in the west room with her own hands.

再往前，腊月初，沈老板捧着契书跪在楼里，求一枚「无短记」的章：疯布一旦盖章，便是天下独一份，他肯出重金，楼里的炭钱也从此归他。掌事的几位长老轮番来劝。佟婆婆只问：「这章认得到多深？」章担保的深浅，不过持章人一辈子咽下去的句数；轮到这块布，她只肯写「至今最短：未见」。「世上没有人能担保一块布已经找齐了最短的口诀，」她说，「连织它的人也不能。」沈老板走了，炭钱跟着断了。

Further back, early in that twelfth month, Shen knelt in the record house with a contract in both hands, begging for the seal of No Shorter Chant: stamped, the mad cloth would be the only one under heaven; he would pay heavily, and the house's charcoal would be his to carry from then on. The elders took turns urging her. Old Tong asked only, how deep can a seal vouch? A seal answers to the depth of the lines its holder has swallowed in a lifetime, she said; for this cloth she would write only, shortest yet found: none. "No one under heaven can promise that the shortest chant has been found for a cloth," she said, "not even the one who wove it." Shen left, and the charcoal went with him.

那块布是佟婆婆临终前两个秋天织的。那时她的手已经抖了，纬线却排得密密实实，只是没有一梭回头：没有云头，没有回字，也没有一处在别处再出现过。楼里年轻的手都来试念过，最体面的一份也只念对了头四十行，往后就散了。镇上为它吵成两半，一半说这是宝，一半说不过是抖出来的错。阿芦问过一回，哪几行是她真要织的。老太太想了半晌：「我要是知道，就写下来了。」

She wove that cloth in the last two autumns of her life. Her hands shook by then, but the weft lay close and even; only no throw ever turned back: no cloud head, no meander square, and no row that appeared a second time anywhere. The young hands of the house all tried to chant it, and the most presentable attempt held true for the first forty rows before it came apart. The town quarreled in two halves, one half calling it a treasure, the other calling it the shaking dressed up as pattern. Alu asked her once which rows she had truly meant to weave. The old woman thought for a long moment. "If I knew that, I would have written it down."

再往前四十年，沈老板还是年轻的绸庄东家，顶着三间铺面的价钱收下那幅「过山云」，供在楼上让人看，看一回收一回钱。他来楼里，本是想求一枚「独幅」的章，把价钱再抬一抬。楼里的规矩不问灵感：布摊开，各家都来试念，念得回来的里头，留最短的一份。第七夜，佟婆婆念出了四句：「起云头，走三梭，回头两梭压旧路，到边流水分九股。」苏州楼里来的师傅不服，用自家的记法重录了一遍；两份摆在一起，只差各人起头背的那几句老话，再往下，一行是一样长的。「记法是各家的，」佟婆婆说，「深浅是布的。」半个月后，铺里一个学徒照着四句，十天织出一幅一样的。过山云的价钱塌了，沈老板折了半辈子的本钱。

Forty years further back, Shen was still the young master of the silk shop. He had bought the Passing Cloud shawl at the price of three storefronts, set it upstairs for viewing, and charged for every look. He came to the record house to beg a seal of uniqueness and lift the price higher still. The house asks nothing of inspiration: the cloth is spread out, every hand is let to try a chant, and of the chants that come back true, the shortest is kept. On the seventh night old Tong sang out four lines: "Raise the cloud head, throw three, go back two over the old road, and at the edge part the flowing water into nine." The master who had come from the Suzhou house refused to accept it and took the cloth again in his own notation; laid side by side, the two records differed only in the opening formula each had drilled as a child, and below that they matched, line for line, in length. "Notations belong to the schools," old Tong said. "Depth belongs to the cloth." Half a month later an apprentice boy in the shop followed the four lines and wove its equal in ten days. The price of the Passing Cloud collapsed, and Shen lost half a lifetime of capital.

最早的一层，在河埠的晒廊底下。佟婆婆那年十岁，娘刚走，留下一幅披帛，花样是她一辈子最好的活。她对着披帛坐了三天，念出了三句，高兴，站在晒廊上唱给所有人听。一年之内，沿河的机子都在织她娘的花，那幅披帛成了市面上的寻常货。这话她后来只跟阿芦讲过一回：「能念出来的，早晚都会被人念走。」再往后的事，楼里人人知道：她做了一辈子替人找最短口诀的手，最后两年，给自己织了一块谁也念不走的。

The earliest layer lies under the drying veranda by the river steps. Tong was ten that year, her mother newly dead, and the one shawl left behind held the best pattern of her life. The girl sat before it for three days and sang out three lines; proud of them, she stood on the veranda and sang them to everyone. Within a year every loom along the river was weaving her mother's pattern, and the shawl had turned into common market goods. She spoke of it only once, to Alu: "Whatever can be sung away will be sung away sooner or later." What came after, everyone in the house knows: she spent a lifetime as the hand that found other people's shortest chants, and her last two years weaving, for herself, the one no one could sing away.

注解：柯尔莫古洛夫复杂性（Kolmogorov complexity）量的是一件事物固有的描述代价：能把它一丝不差再造出来的最短口诀（即最短程序）有多长，它就有多深；换哪一门记法去量，只差各派起头那几句，深浅不变，而谁也念不短的布，就是真随机。故事守不住的地方也该说破：世上没有任何办法确证某块布真的没有更短的口诀（这个量不可计算），念不短也只说明省无可省，不说明它是宝，它看上去同乱线并无分别。

Note: Kolmogorov complexity measures the intrinsic descriptive cost of a thing: how long the shortest recipe (the shortest program) that re-creates it exactly would have to be; whichever school's notation you measure in, the depth barely moves, and the cloth no chant can shorten is what randomness means here. The honest limit of the fable: nothing under heaven can certify that a cloth truly has no shorter chant (the quantity is uncomputable), and being unshortenable only means nothing can be spared — it is not the same as being a treasure, and it looks no different from a tangle of slipped threads.
