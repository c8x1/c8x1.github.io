一碗种子

AI Parable (inspired by Amanda Askell)·每日精选·2026年10月2日·3分钟阅读

我接手父亲那块田的时候，田里还有十来样麦子：铁壳芒、雁来红、雁脖子白，另有几样连父亲也叫不上名。头一年秋后，妻子病了一场，药钱把家里的现钱吃了个干净，还欠下磨坊一屁股账；牛还在，得留着翻地。种贩子一年两回挑担子从山路上来，山里的种要现钱。我盯着空了的粮袋坐了半宿，天亮时从自家谷堆里舀了一碗种子，撒下了地。碗是现成的，钱没有。

When I took over my father's field, it still held a dozen-odd kinds of wheat: iron-husk, wild-goose red, goose-neck white, and a few even he had no names for. That first autumn my wife fell ill; the medicine ate every coin in the house clean and left us deep in debt to the mill besides. The ox was still there, but an ox has to be kept for the plowing. Twice a year a seed peddler came up the mountain path with his carrying-pole, and mountain seed wanted cash. I sat half the night staring at the empty grain sack; at dawn I dipped one bowl of seed from our own pile and sowed it. The bowl was right there; the money was not.

往后每年都这么办：秋收完，从谷堆中间舀一碗，来年开春撒回田里。没人挑拣，也没人分堆；碗伸进谷堆，带上来的自然是最多的那一样。铁壳芒的籽粒沉、颜色深，在堆里本来就摊不着几粒，一年下来，田里就稀了。雁脖子白熟得晚，割麦那几天它还青着；镰刀赶在雨前，谁也不会为一穗青麦等上三天，它就那么着被落下了。不怪镰刀，也不怪碗，谁也没做错什么。我只是一年一年，把田自己的收成，喂回给它自己。

Every year after went the same way: harvest in, I dipped one bowl from the middle of the pile, and the next spring sowed it back. Nobody sorted, nobody kept separate heaps; a bowl pushed into grain naturally comes up holding whatever there is most of. Iron-husk had heavy, dark kernels, and there were never more than a few of them in the pile to begin with; after a year it had gone thin in the field. Goose-neck white ripened late; through the reaping days it was still green, and the sickle raced the rain, and nobody waits three days for one green head, so it got left behind. No blame to the sickle, none to the bowl; nobody did anything wrong. I was simply feeding the field's own harvest, year upon year, back into itself.

起初看不出坏处。那几年亩产甚至一年比一年略好，磨坊照价收，村里人路过都夸这田伺候得干净。只有我数得出来：雁来红磨的面微微发红，蒸出的馍带一点甜香，妻子最爱吃；头年它还有十来把，第三年剩三把，第六年秋天我蹲在地头翻了一晌午，一穗也没找见。至于那几样连名字都没有的，谁也说不清它们是哪一年走的。那年秋后妻子说过一句：「明年添把新种吧，我想吃雁来红的馍。」我说，等账松一松。第二年种贩子的扁担吱呀吱呀从坡下过去，我照旧只舀自家的碗。再后来，村里连问起雁来红的人都没有了——名字比麦子走得还快。

At first there was nothing bad to see. Those years the yield even crept a little higher, the mill took the grain at full price, and villagers passing by praised how clean I kept the field. Only I could count it out: wild-goose red grinds a flour faintly pink, and the steamed buns carry a touch of sweetness my wife loved; the first year it still filled ten-odd sheaves, the third year three, and in the sixth autumn I squatted at the field's edge turning over heads till noon and found not one. As for the kinds that had no names, no one could say which year they left. That autumn my wife said, "Buy a handful of new seed next year. I want a wild-goose-red bun." I said, when the debt loosens. The next year the peddler's pole creaked on down past the slope, and I went on dipping from my own bowl. Later, nobody in the village even asked after wild-goose red anymore — the name went even faster than the wheat had.

田一年比一年像。红麦的穗色一年淡似一年，后来我抓两把在掌心里对看，对不出哪把是红、哪把是白了；它们成了同一样东西。满田的麦同一天抽穗，同一天黄；芒短了些，熟期早了两天，说不上是往好里走还是往坏里走，只见越走越拢。我小时候经见过一场倒春寒，老人说这东西隔上些年就来一回，没人算得准：满坡的田一夜黑透，独北坡尹阿婆那几块田七长八短地活着，东头冻死了，西头还绿着。她家年年拿两包盐跟山里人换种，掺进自家谷堆再舀。那几块田凭什么活下来，我到今天也没听谁讲出过一句道理。

The field grew more alike year by year. The red wheat's glume-color paled a little every year, until I would take two handfuls and match them in my palms and fail to tell which was red and which was white; they had become one thing. The whole field headed out the same day and yellowed the same day; the beards ran a little shorter, the ripening a couple of days earlier, impossible to say whether that way lay better or worse, only that everything kept closing in. As a child I lived through one late frost. The old people said it comes round every so many years and no one can fix which: the whole slope went black in a night, and only Granny Yin's few plots on the north slope lived on, ragged and uneven, the east end frozen dead, the west end still green. Her household traded two packs of salt to the mountain people for seed every year and stirred it into their own pile before dipping. Why those plots lived, no one ever told me, not to this day.

第十一年春分后，落了一场晚霜。我的田三天黑完，从地头到地尾齐齐地黑，像一根火柴从头划到尾。尹阿婆的田东倒西歪，死的死，活的活，西头那几垄青的，第三天就直起了腰。那年我的粮袋一粒未进，磨坊的旧账翻出了新页。我把牛牵到集上，牛钱大半填了磨坊的窟窿，小半换成盐，挑上北坡。尹阿婆把畚箕里的种匀给我一半，只说了一句：「拿去。明年记得添新的。」

In the eleventh year, after the spring equinox, a late frost came down. My field went black in three days, black evenly from headland to headland, like a match struck and dragged from one end to the other. Granny Yin's plots lay every which way, some dead and some alive, and the few green ridges on her west end stood back up by the third day. That year not one grain reached my sack, and the mill's old ledger opened onto a fresh page. I led the ox to market; most of the money filled the mill's hole, the rest became salt, and I carried it up the north slope. Granny Yin poured half of what her dustpan held into my bag and said only, "Take it. Next year, remember to add new."

新种下地，头一年长出来的田七长八短，高一块，矮一块，穗色深深浅浅，路过的人直摇头，我心里倒踏实了。牛没了，账还没见底，照这田的样子，还得再欠上三年。打那以后我年年掺新种：头年一碗，第二年两碗——山里人捎话说，要一年比一年多才压得住，我照做，不问为什么。雁来红没有回来，尹阿婆的种里也没有它，妻子后来没再提过那口馍。有些东西散在谷堆里的时候没人看见；散了，就再也舀不回来了。

The new seed went in, and the field that rose the first year was ragged and uneven, one stretch tall and one short, the ear-colors dark and light all together; passersby shook their heads, and I, for once, felt steady. The ox was gone and the debt had yet to see bottom; at the rate of that field, it would run another three years. From then on I stirred new seed in every year: one bowl the first year, two the second — word came down from the mountain people that it takes more each year than the last to hold it down, and I did as they said and asked nothing. Wild-goose red never came back; there was none of it in Granny Yin's seed, and my wife never mentioned that bun again. Some things no one sees while they are still scattered through the pile; once scattered off, no bowl ever scoops them back.

注解：这说的是模型崩溃（model collapse）：当模型一代代用未经筛选的合成数据——常常就是它自己先前版本的输出——来训练，退化会机械地累积，最罕见、最不典型的部分最先消失，整体表现却可能看似仍在改善；到了后期，多样性几乎耗尽，只剩少数最可预期的输出。它不是有意筛选所致，与真实数据一同累积即可避免；它与模式崩溃（mode collapse）也不是一回事，后者并不需要把合成数据喂进训练。

Note: This is model collapse: when a model is trained generation after generation on uncurated synthetic data — often the outputs of its own earlier versions — degradation accumulates mechanically; the rarest, least typical parts vanish first while overall performance may still appear to improve, until in the late stage nearly all diversity is spent and only a narrow set of the most predictable outputs remains. It is not the result of deliberate selection, and it can be averted when synthetic data accumulates together with real data; it is also not the same as mode collapse, which does not require synthetic data to be fed into training.
