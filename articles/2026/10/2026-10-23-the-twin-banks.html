双埂

AI Parable (inspired by Amanda Askell)·每日精选·2026年10月23日·4分钟阅读

谷里的水排着队走。最高那口田起一户放一户，闸开闸合，十日一周，没谁记得规矩哪年立的。规矩下另有活泛：西头嫁女水挪后晌、东头牛病跟上家换半晌、雨先到整轮后推一天。田挨田水挨水，一户的日子挪一挪满谷跟着挪。只有两块地，水从来不进去。

In the valley, water moves in a queue. From the highest field down, one household releases to the next, gate opens, gate shuts, a round every ten days, and nobody remembers which year the rule was set. Under the rule there is give: a wedding in the west shifts water to the afternoon, a sick ox in the east trades half a day with the neighbor, early rain pushes the whole round back a day. Field touches field and water touches water — one household shifts its day and the whole valley shifts with it. Only two plots the water never enters.

桑家蜂场十二年前来的，几十箱蜂落在挨车道的高地，买卖都在车上，蜜桶装了就走，与渠水无一处交道，谷里没人怪他——水里本来就没有他的进出。燕家石子地比渠还老，石头多，淹不成稻，一直放几只羊。水绕开这两块地走：湿处的水有去处，干处没有，挨着它们的田只能把水拦在自己这边。于是向家（挨蜂场）、靳家（挨石子地）的埂都比别家高半尺，放水时辰钉死在一刻上。最重的是阚家：一块长条地正夹在蜂场与石子地中间，两条长埂，一边拦着自家水别漫进蜂箱，一边拦着别渗去燕家。十二年来阚家的水逢六日午时来，早半个时辰晚半个时辰都不行。

The Sangs' bee farm came twelve years ago, a few dozen hives set on the high ground beside the cart road; their trade runs on wheels, honey loaded and gone, with no dealing at all in ditch water, and nobody in the valley resents them — the water never carried anything of theirs. The Yans' gravel field is older than the ditch itself, too many stones to flood for rice, always a few sheep on it. Water makes its way around these two plots: water in wet places has somewhere to go, dry places have none, and the fields beside them can only hold the water back on their own side. So the Xiangs, beside the bee farm, and the Jins, beside the gravel field, keep banks half a foot higher than anyone else, their watering hours nailed to the quarter. Worst off are the Kans: a long strip of field wedged between the bee farm and the gravel field, two long banks, one holding their own water back from the hives, one holding it back from seeping toward the Yans. For twelve years the Kans' water has come at noon on the sixth day of every ten — half an hour early or late and it won't do.

阚汛生在大水那年，爹取这名指望他跟汛水处得来；汛水没客气。这年七月河下得又早又急，下半谷的埂塌了不少；长条地两条埂一条塌一条漫，青苗顺水走了一多半。谷里照老例摊工，各修各埂。阚汛蹲在塌埂上算账：把两条长埂重新立起来要的工本，恰是攒给儿子小满的拜师钱。小满十五了，县城木匠铺师傅点了头，单等钱到。桑蜂倌那边有句现成话：想买长条地，东头挨着车道，雨天蜜桶也能干着装车。他出双埂地的价——双埂地的价是什么价，谷里人人心里有数。

Kan Xun was born in the flood year; his father gave him the name hoping he and the floodwater would get along, and the floodwater was not polite about it. This July the river came down early and hard, and banks broke all through the lower valley; on the long strip, of the two long banks one broke and one overflowed, and the young shoots went with the water. The valley shared out the work by old custom, each household mending its own banks. Kan Xun squatted on the broken bank doing sums: the labor and money to set both long banks upright again came to exactly the apprentice fee he had saved for his son Xiaoman. Xiaoman was fifteen; the master at the county-town carpenter's shop had nodded, only waiting on the money. The Sang beekeeper had a standing offer: he wanted the long strip, east end against the cart road, honey buckets dry to load even in rain. He offered what a double-banked field costs — and what a double-banked field costs, everyone in the valley knows in their heart.

他没有立刻应。长条地是太爷手里从苇塘剖出来的第一块田，传到他这辈第四代。他先放话看谷里谁肯接：来的人家把两条长埂从头走到尾，算了算往后的工，摇摇头，出的价跟租地差不多。秋里桑蜂倌又来一趟，问急了只有一句：车道的雨。腊月里阚汛画了押。他应的是卖地这一件事；至于地往后挨着谁，契上没写，他也没往心里去。两下里的心思，一头在车道上，一头在县城的木匠铺里。

He did not say yes at once. The long strip was the first field his great-grandfather cut out of the reed marsh, and it reached him in the fourth generation. He first let word out to see who in the valley would take it: the families that came walked the two long banks end to end, counted the work ahead, shook their heads, and offered about what rent would fetch. In autumn the Sang beekeeper came round again; pressed for his reason he had one line: the rain on the cart road. In the twelfth month Kan Xun signed. What he agreed to was the one thing, the sale; as for whom the land would lie against afterward, the contract said nothing, and he gave it no mind. The two sides' thoughts ran elsewhere — one on the cart road, one in the county-town carpenter's shop.

当年冬天，桑家把篱桩一路拔过阚家旧地，蜂箱排到原先长稻子的地方，东头临车道起了装桶的场院。两条长埂没人拆也没人修，两个汛期过后自己塌平，草从埂心长出来，把直线吃成一道缓坡。蜂场的篱笆这回直接搭在燕家石子地上。两块干地之间从此没有埂也没有闸。轮水册子上划去两个钉死的时辰；再摊工修埂，谷里的账短了一截。向家靳家时辰照旧，水照旧要拦，只是拦的地方少了。燕家的羊有回挤过塌埂拱翻两只蜂箱，桑蜂倌隔着篱笆扔土坷垃，两家为这个吵到今天。界是搭上了，界上没有和气，只有省下来的工。

That winter the Sangs moved their fence posts in a line across the old Kan land, set hives where rice had grown, and on the east end by the cart road laid out a yard for loading barrels. The two long banks nobody tore down and nobody mended; after two flood seasons they slumped flat on their own, grass growing out of the bank-cores, eating the straight line into a gentle slope. The bee farm's fence this time ran straight onto the Yans' gravel field. Between the two dry plots there was from then on neither bank nor gate. Two nailed-down hours were struck from the water register; when work on the banks was next shared out, the valley's ledger came up short. The Xiangs and the Jins kept their hours as before, kept holding water back as before — only there was less of it to hold. Once the Yans' sheep squeezed over the slumped bank and knocked over two hives; the Sang beekeeper threw clods across the fence, and the two households have been quarreling over it to this day. The boundary got built; on the boundary there was no goodwill, only the work saved.

重分的田里，阚家拈着东头一块寻常田，两边都是闸，不挨任何干地。头一轮水，西邻冯家隔着水喊：「后晌的水匀我半晌，明儿还你一早。」阚汛应了一个字：换。这个字他十二年没说过了，说出口跟旁人一样平常。开春小满进了县城。往后汛水还是一年一年地下，塌了的埂人们先修短的省力的，长而费工的那几条修着修着就没人修了；一年一年，谷里的样子慢慢变成最不必费力的样子。没有人商量过让两块干地挨着；它们挨着的这一种留了下来。

In the redistribution the Kans drew an ordinary plot at the east end, gates on both sides, touching no dry land. On the first round of water, the Fengs, their neighbors to the west, called across the water: "Lend me half a day of this afternoon's water, and I'll pay you back a morning tomorrow." Kan Xun answered with one word: "Trade." It was a word he had not said in twelve years, and it came out of his mouth as ordinary as anyone's. That spring Xiaoman went into the county town. The floodwater still came down year after year; of the banks that broke, people mended first the short and easy ones, and the long, costly ones, mended and mended, went unmended; year after year the valley's shape settled slowly into whatever took the least work. Nobody ever met to decide that the two dry plots should touch; the arrangement in which they touch is the one that stayed.

注解：疏水效应（the hydrophobic effect）——非极性分子与水结不成氢键，贴着它的水只好排成整齐的一层；它们靠拢、与水的交界缩到最短时，被捆住的整齐水松开散回各处，体系的熵随之增大，聚拢由水这一侧的热力学促成。常有人把油滴聚堆说成油与油相吸或被水弹开，那正是这个效应最常被认错的地方。

Note: the hydrophobic effect — a nonpolar molecule forms no hydrogen bonds with water, so the water hugging it must line up in an ordered layer; when such molecules draw together and their boundary with the water shrinks to the shortest, the ordered water that was tied up is released back into the bulk, the entropy of the system rises, and the clustering is driven by the thermodynamics of the water side. People often describe oil droplets gathering as oil attracting oil, or as water repelling the oil — that is exactly where this effect is most often misread.
