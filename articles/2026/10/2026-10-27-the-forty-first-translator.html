第四十一位译者

AI Parable (inspired by Amanda Askell)·每日精选·2026年10月27日·4分钟阅读

南埠的行商都认一个理：译坊吃的是没见过的饭。番船进港，舱单与书信上的番字没人识得，才轮得到译坊开口；至于架上那四十册——行会开埠时定下的税则、货名与书信体例——家家坊里都有，背得再熟也不值一文。每年开河第一船泊定，商会封一封新信进堂，各坊当堂开译，译得最妥的那家领走全年的行帖，也领走商家的生意。行会的祖规刻在碑上，只得四个字：册满即止。

The merchants of Southport all lived by one saying: a translation house eats the rice of the unseen. Only when a foreign ship came in, its manifests and letters written in script nobody could read, did the houses earn the right to speak; the forty volumes on the shelf — the tariff schedules, goods names, and letter forms laid down when the guild opened the port — stood in every house alike, and no one was ever paid a copper for reciting them. Each spring, when the first ship of the thawing river tied up, the merchants' council sealed one fresh letter and carried it into the hall; the houses translated it on the spot, and whichever rendered it truest took the year's trade seal, and the year's business with it. The guild's founding rule was cut into a stele, one line only: fill the shelf, then stop.

传到桑棠父亲手上时，桑家译坊只剩十二个译者。十二人读四十册，一人身兼三四册，只来得及记下各册相通的大路，译新信粗，却粗得稳。父亲一年年攒钱添人，新信译得一年比一年细，添到二十六个那年，桑家的译文已经能同百年老号郑家的并排摆上商会的案头。他咽气时数到三十八，离满架还差两人，把坊交了女儿。

By the time it passed to Sang Tang's father, the Sang house was down to twelve translators. Twelve readers for forty volumes meant each man carried three or four at once, time enough only for the broad road the volumes shared; their new letters came out coarse, but coarse and steady. Year by year he saved and hired, and the new letters came out finer year by year, until the season he counted twenty-six, when Sang renderings could lie on the council's table beside those of the hundred-year house of Zheng. He died counting thirty-eight, two men short of a full shelf, and left the house to his daughter.

桑棠接坊头一件事，是补满那两人。四十个译者，一人一册，问哪句答哪句，一字不错，连旧年虫蛀的缺口都描得分毫不差。满埠都说，今春行帖要换主了。郑家大掌柜差人送来一坛酒，帖上四个字：后生可畏。

Sang Tang's first act as mistress was to hire the two who were missing. Forty translators, one volume each; any line, any man, and the answer came back word-perfect, tracing even the gaps the worms had eaten from the old pages without a stroke astray. All over the port they said the seal would change hands this spring. The master of the Zheng house sent round a jar of wine with a card of four characters: beware the young.

开河第一船是爪哇（Java）来的香船。信当堂拆开，一页货单，下附三行短简，纸角浸过海水，一个数目晕成指甲大的一团墨。四十个译者传看，各人都在那团墨里认出自己那册的路数；守税则册的译者最笃定——他那册里恰有一处刻工留下的墨垢，坊里向来当真字背——于是那团墨被他念成了一个货名。苗家照着去接货，码头上点数对不上，当众折了半船利。商会总裁事把桑家的译文掷回案上：十二个人的时候，也译不出这样糟的东西。

The first ship of the thaw came in from Java, laden with cloves. The letter was unsealed in the hall: a page of cargo list, three short lines beneath it, one corner steeped in seawater, a single figure bloomed into a blot the size of a thumbnail. Forty translators passed it around, and each man recognized in that blot the grain of his own volume; the keeper of the tariff volume was the most certain of all — his volume carried a blot of its own, left by the block-cutter, which the house had always memorized as a true word — and so he read the blot out as a name of goods. The Miao firm took the consignment on that reading; the tally on the quay would not match; half a ship's profit was lost in front of the whole port. The head of the council flung the Sang rendering back across the table: even in the days of twelve men, he said, this house never translated anything this bad.

行帖吊了一年。桑棠把祖屋押给商会作保：来年开河，若译不成全埠最好，屋子归行会，坊散人去。老行尊们的方子现成：退回二十六人，那是几辈人试出来的稳数；往满架外再添一个，只会更乱。夜里桑棠把那页译坏的信从头看到尾，看到后半夜：译得最坏的那句，出自背得最熟的册。独守一册的人，墨垢到了他眼里也只能是字——除了照背，他没有别的办法处置它。病根不是添的人多了，是每册只剩一双眼睛。

The seal was suspended for a year. Sang Tang pledged the ancestral house to the council as surety: next thaw, if her house did not translate best in the whole port, the building went to the guild and the house dissolved. The old worthies had the prescription ready: fall back to twenty-six, the steady number proven over generations; hire one man past the full shelf and there would only be more confusion. At night Sang Tang read the ruined letter from head to tail, and past midnight she saw it: the worst sentence had come from the best-memorized volume. A man alone with one volume cannot afford to see a smudge as a smudge — short of memorizing it, he has no other way to hold it. The sickness was not that she had hired too many; it was that each volume was down to a single pair of eyes.

冬至前，桑棠做了全埠没人做过的事：往满架外雇人。第四十一位译者进坊那天，老账房卷起铺盖走了，说规矩不能破在他手里。开春前，坊里到了四十八人，最刁的八册各添了一双眼睛。满屋从早吵到晚，吵的全是墨垢与错刻，吵到末了，错刻总是输，没人教过他们这个。架上四十册，还是问不倒的老样子。那个冬天，商家的信全走了郑家，桑棠当掉母亲留下的镯子，给译者们过了年。

Before the winter solstice, Sang Tang did what no house in the port had done: she hired past the full shelf. The day the forty-first translator crossed the threshold, the old bookkeeper rolled up his bedding and left, saying the rules would not be broken on his watch. By the first thaw the house held forty-eight, the eight trickiest volumes given a second pair of eyes apiece. The room argued from morning to night, and all it argued about was smudges and misprints; by the end of every quarrel the misprint had lost. No one had taught them that. The forty volumes on the shelf still answered every challenge word for word. Through that winter every merchant's letter went to the Zheng house, and Sang Tang pawned her mother's bracelet to carry her translators through the new year.

又一年开河，头一船捎来一封雨打过的急信，数目糊了小半页。郑家先译，把糊处含混带过。轮到桑家，那页纸在四十八个人手里传了一圈，传到第八个人，一个年轻译者把纸举起来对着窗光：「先生们，这是水痕，数目在纸背上透着。」满堂静了一息。桑家当堂把信译完，从数目到口气，一字不错，船上大班拿原件对照，点了头。总裁事把行帖按回桑棠手里，祖屋的押字当场烧了。散场时，郑家大掌柜在阶下等她，问能不能送两个后生进坊坐着听。那年夏天，碑下多了一行后刻的小字：过满架，还有路。满屋四十八人，译出的每一封信，都像出自一条最平实的舌头。

The next thaw, the first ship carried a dispatch the rain had gotten to, its figures blurred across half a page. The Zheng house translated first and slid over the blur in a general way. When the Sang house's turn came, the page went around the circle of forty-eight, and at the eighth hand a young translator lifted it against the window light: "Gentlemen, this is water stain; the figure shows through on the back." The hall held still for a breath. The Sang house finished the letter on the spot, from figures to tone not a word wrong, and the ship's supercargo checked it against the original and nodded. The head of the council pressed the year's seal back into Sang Tang's hands, and the pledge on the ancestral house was burned then and there. On the steps afterward the master of the Zheng house was waiting, asking whether he might send two of his juniors to sit in her room and listen. That summer a small line was cut beneath the stele: past the full shelf, there is still road. Forty-eight people in one room, and every letter they let out that year might have come from one plain, unbroken tongue.

注解：双重下降（double descent）说的是，一个会学的东西，容量越大，应付没见过的事错处越少；待容量恰好罩得住全部旧例（即插值阈值，interpolation threshold），错处反而升至顶点；容量再往上加，错处又落回去，峰不在最大处，在恰好够用处。寓言把越过满架写成一场豪赌，现实的曲线不需要谁的胆量，自己走完，连最朴素的回归里也有它。

Note: Double descent is the name for this trajectory: as a learner's capacity grows, its errors on things it has never seen first shrink; when capacity just barely covers every known case — the interpolation threshold — the errors climb to their worst; past that point they fall again, and the peak sits at barely-enough, not at maximum. The fable turns that crossing into a wager staked on a house; the real curve needs no one's nerve and walks itself, even in the plainest regression.
