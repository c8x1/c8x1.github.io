港监的账簿

AI Parable (inspired by Amanda Askell)·每日精选·2026年8月1日·2分钟阅读

港监林站在栈桥尽头，把那本厚账簿摊在风里。商会的代表刚撂下话：裁掉一半巡丁，否则换人。副手阿渡攥着茶碗，低声问：「东家，他们要的是个数目，您总得给句准话。」

Harbor master Lin stood at the end of the pier, his thick ledger spread open in the wind. The guild's man had just laid down the terms: halve the inspectors, or find a replacement. Deputy A-du gripped his tea bowl and asked low, "Master, they want a number — you have to give them one straight."

「数目我给不了，」林说，「走私船落在哪座码头，是不确定的——七座码头，七种可能。我要是把巡丁全堆在常出事的北码头，北码头一有动静，谁都不意外，可那也说明不了什么。倒是有船摸到从来没人去的长堤码头，那一夜才真把事都说穿了。越是罕见的地方被踩中，越说明来者有备、有路子。」

"I can't give a number," Lin said. "Which pier the smugglers land at is uncertain — seven piers, seven possibilities. If I pile every inspector on the North pier, the usual haunt, a hit there surprises no one and tells us little. But let a boat slip into the Long Spur, a pier no one ever visits — that night tells us everything. The rarer the berth they choose, the more it reveals they have a plan, a route."

「那干脆每座码头摆一样多的人，最稳。」阿渡说。林合上账簿：「那正是最熬人的。七座码头，当走私船落在哪座都一样可能时，我连猜都没处下笔——那段最难，数目也压不下来。一旦风声偏了、十有八九走北码头，我反倒踏实，巡丁就能省下来。」

"Then put the same count at every pier, safest," A-du said. Lin closed the ledger. "That's exactly the part that wears me down. Seven piers, and when a smuggler's landing is equally likely at any of them, I've nowhere even to begin guessing — that's the hardest stretch, and the count won't come down. The moment word skews and nine times in ten they hit the North pier, I can breathe; I can pull men off the others."

「可要是先知道船从哪个海口进来呢？」阿渡问。「那就好办了，」林说，「知道来路，剩下要猜的只会变少，不会变多。从南口进来的，从不走长堤；这一条安上，长堤的巡丁就能撤一半。多知道一桩事，担忧只会减，不会增。」

"But what if we first learn which inlet the boat came in through?" A-du asked. "Then it's easier," Lin said. "Knowing the entry can only shrink what's left to guess, never grow it. Boats from the South inlet never take the Long Spur — fix that one fact, and I can halve the Spur's watch. Every added fact trims the worry, never adds to it."

商会要把数目砍一半。「我重排巡丁，把人压在要害上，让每个巡丁顶两个用——可这撑不起裁掉一半，」林说，「来船落在哪座码头，这事的不确定是它自己的，不是我想要多难就多难、想省就省的。我把人挪得再巧，也挪不动那七座码头底下的形势。账上的数，压不到那个形势以下。」

The guild wanted the count halved. "I can reshuffle the inspectors, weight the watch toward the piers that matter, make each man cover two — but that won't carry a halving," Lin said. "Where the boats land is uncertain on its own terms; it isn't made harder because I wish it, or cheaper because I wish it. I can move men cleverly, but I can't move the lay of those seven piers underneath. The number in this ledger won't press below that lay."

林在账簿上写下他能守住的数目，不是商会想要的那个。他知道：要么丢了这个差，要么等哪天清晨长堤码头被踩中、却没一双眼睛看着。他选了被撤——宁可交出铜印，也不肯签下一个把七座码头说成比实际更稳的数目。

Lin wrote down the number he could defend, not the one the guild wanted. He knew: either he loses the post, or one morning the Long Spur takes a hit with no pair of eyes watching it. He chose dismissal — would rather hand back the copper seal than sign a number that made the seven piers out to be steadier than they were.

注解：信息熵（information entropy）量化一个随机变量潜在结果的平均不确定度；越罕见的结果发生所携带的信息越多，等概分布时不确定度最大，而任何归约的下限都由这不确定度本身决定，不随意愿改变。

Annotation: Information entropy (信息熵) quantifies the average uncertainty of a random variable's possible outcomes; rarer outcomes carry more information, uncertainty is greatest when outcomes are equally likely, and the floor of any reduction is set by that uncertainty itself — not by will.
