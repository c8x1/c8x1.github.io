无人标出的中线

AI Parable (inspired by Amanda Askell)·每日精选·2026年8月15日·2分钟阅读

新修的木桥横跨神社前的深壑，第一个过桥的旅人每一步都踏在实心的木板上，仿佛桥的中线是照着他的脊骨量出来的。赶来看的村民都说，重建此桥的寡妇一定是个算学的奇才，才能在雾里定出如此居中的线。

The new footbridge crossed the chasm at the shrine, and the first traveler to use it set every foot on solid planking, as though the line of the bridge had been read off his own spine. Villagers who came to look said the widow who rebuilt it must have been a prodigy of calculation, to have found so centered a line across the mist.

但她没有做任何计算。她只是把捡来的旧木板沿一条已被磨光的崖石带铺下——那条带子正中踏痕最密，向两侧均匀变稀，直到两边只剩裸岩。从没有测量员标过它。

But she had done no calculation. She had only laid her salvaged planks along a band of cliff stone already worn smooth — a band dense with tread at its middle, thinning evenly to bare rock on either side. No surveyor had ever marked it.

那条磨出的带子并无作者。一季又一季，数以千计的朝圣者跨过深壑，每个人落脚的位置都由他自己的步态、他的负重、他的疲倦、壑底吹来的一阵风、或一个孩子的踉跄所决定。没有人瞄准中线。每个人都落脚不同——有的贴左崖，有的贴右崖，有的踉跄——而这些人各自的足迹，没有一条呈什么规整或匀称的形状。

That worn band had no author. Across the seasons, thousands of pilgrims had crossed the chasm, each stepping where his own gait, his load, his weariness, a gust from the gorge, or a child's stumble happened to put him. None aimed at a center. Each chose differently — some hugging the left cliff, some the right, some lurching — and not one of these individual paths followed any regular or balanced shape.

然而，当所有这些未经选择的脚步最终落定的位置铺开来看，它们密密地聚在正中，向两侧崖壁等量地变稀——一条圆顺、居中的带子，仿佛有一只看不见的手把每一脚印都往中间拢了拢。并没有谁的手拢过什么。每一跨都是有限的——没有哪个朝圣者能把自己无限制地抛出去——但除此之外各自随意；而那些互不协调的跨步，其总和自行地、就那么落成了那条居中的形状。

Yet where all those unchosen steps finally fell, they clustered thick at a middle and thinned in equal measure toward the cliffs — a smooth, centered band, as though some invisible hand had gathered each footfall toward the middle. No hand had gathered anything. Each stride was bounded — no pilgrim could launch himself without limit — but otherwise free; and the uncoordinated sum of those strides simply settled, of itself, into that centered shape.

在铺下第一块木板之前，寡妇面对深壑，手头只有窄窄的一批捡来的旧木——只够铺一条单行宽的桥面。村里的长者已在北面五十步外打下一排直测线石桩，要她沿它修：「这才是正经的、规划好的过道。」但那条磨出的带子却在别处，略偏南，且比任何一排石桩都宽。她若把单板桥铺在长者的线上，一只向来落在磨带里的脚，夜里可能踩空，坠入壑底；她若信那条无人标出的中线，便是忤逆长者，把全部旧木押在一个无人画过的形状上。

Before a plank was laid, the widow faced the chasm with a narrow store of salvaged timber — wide enough for only a single line of footing. The village elder had hammered stone pegs into a straight survey line fifty paces to the north and demanded she build along it: the proper, planned crossing. But the worn band lay elsewhere, slightly south, and it was broader than any single peg-line. If she laid her one-plank bridge on the elder's line, a foot that had always fallen within the worn band might miss it in the dark and meet the gorge; if she trusted the worn center, she defied the elder and bet her salvaged timber on a shape no one had drawn.

她选了那条磨出的中线。桥立住了；第一个旅人的脚，落在千只脚早已落过、而往后千只脚仍将落下的地方。她后来想，那条带子是不是需要朝圣者们彼此相像——随即记起并非如此：跛足的老者、奔跑的孩子、负重过甚的商客，各有各的狂态。他们所共有的，只是每个人都独自过壑，而每一跨，都无法把自己无限制地抛出去。

She chose the worn center. The bridge held; the first traveler's foot fell where a thousand feet had already fallen, and the next thousand would fall. She wondered, later, whether the band needed the pilgrims to be alike — and remembered they were not: limping elders, running children, overladen merchants, each wild in his own way. What they shared was only that each crossed alone, and no single stride could fling itself without bound.

注解：中心极限定理（the central limit theorem）。大量独立、各自有界、既不必正态也不必同分布的随机贡献叠加之后，其和经居中与标准化，形状会自行收敛成标准正态分布的钟形，与原来各变量的形状无关；这是叠加与标准化共同产生的涌现，并非谁的意图，它与强大数定律互补却不同——后者管均值收敛到期望，前者管均值围绕期望的波动如何呈正态形状。

Annotation: the central limit theorem. When many independent random contributions, each bounded and not necessarily normal or even alike, are summed, the shape of that sum—after centering and scaling—settles into the bell of a standard normal distribution, regardless of the original variables' shapes; it is the superposition together with standardization that produces this emergent form, not any intention, and it complements (but differs from) the law of large numbers, which concerns the mean's convergence to its expectation, while this theorem concerns how the fluctuations of that mean take on a normal shape.
