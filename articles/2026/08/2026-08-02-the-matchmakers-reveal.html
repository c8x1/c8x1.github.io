媒人的揭破

AI Parable (inspired by Amanda Askell)·每日精选·2026年8月2日·1分钟阅读

在青河镇，三位求婚者登门商贾林家，求娶他的女儿。三人之中，只有一位真心正派，另两位各藏隐恶。做了四十年媒人的沈老伯，把每个人的品性摸得透底——因他正是最初的引荐与查验之人。

In the market town of Qinghe, three suitors arrived at the door of merchant Lin to ask for his daughter's hand. Of the three, only one was truly honorable; the other two harbored secret vices. Old Shen, the town's matchmaker for forty years, knew each man's character down to the bone, for it was he who had first vetted them.

女儿名唤阿梅，对三人的底细一无所知。她凭一个笑容的弧度，选了最高个的那位。三人中仅一人为善，她一开始就选对的概率不过三分之一——可这层赌意，她当时全无察觉。

The daughter, Mei, knew nothing of the three men's true natures. She went by the tilt of a smile and chose the tallest. With three suitors and only one honorable, her chance of having chosen well was no better than one in three, a gamble she did not yet feel as a gamble.

按青河的老规矩，验过身家的媒人不能让新娘蒙眼入命：新娘一旦选定，他必从她未选的两人中指破一人的恶行，再给她一次改主的机会。沈老伯起身，指向第二位求婚者，揭他造假契、欠三家债。那人翻墙而去。

By the old custom of Qinghe, a matchmaker who has vetted suitors may not let a bride walk blind into her fate: once the bride has made her choice, he must point to one of the two she did not choose and declare his disqualifying flaw, and then give her a second chance to change her mind. Old Shen rose, indicated the second suitor, and named him a forger in debt to three creditors. The man fled the courtyard.

此刻院中只剩两位：高个的——阿梅的首选——与沉默的第三位。众人各执一词。表兄是个教书先生，坚持既剩两人，胜算各半，何必自打耳光改选？沈老伯只重复：主意她自拿。阿梅的手悬着——改选，像是自承头一个判断错了。

Now only the tall suitor, Mei's first pick, and the quiet third suitor remained. The courtyard urged her both ways. Her cousin, a schoolmaster, insisted that with two men left the odds were even, so why shame herself by switching? Old Shen only repeated that the choice was hers. Mei's hand hovered, for to switch felt like confessing her first judgment wrong.

教书先生看不见的那一层，全知的看客却看得清：阿梅的首选把她那三分之一的胜算钉死，且会一直钉死，因为媒人的规矩从不许他碰这一位。他被迫从她未选的两人里揪出一个歹人，又不许指破任何正人，沈老伯的手于是被牵着走——原先压在那两位未选者身上、合计三分之二的可能，全数汇到了他还留着站立的这一位身上。阿梅改了选，那沉默的第三位，十之六七，正是正人。

What the schoolmaster missed, the omniscient reader sees. Mei's first choice had locked in its one-third chance and would stay locked, for the matchmaker's rule never allowed him to touch it. Forced to expose a villain among the two she had passed over, and forbidden from ever exposing an honorable man, Old Shen's hand was guided: all the two-thirds of likelihood that had rested on the two unchosen suitors was funneled onto the one he left standing. Mei switched, and the quiet suitor turned out, more often than not, to be the honorable one.

注解：此即蒙提霍尔问题（the Monty Hall problem）：三选项中一好两坏，选择者先选其一（中好概率1/3），一位知情且受规则约束的中间人必从未选项中揭开一个坏的，再让选择者决定是否换到剩余那项——此时不换仍为1/3，换则独得2/3，因为受约束的揭示把全部未选的可能压到了唯一剩余的那一项上。

Note: This is the Monty Hall problem: among three options, one good and two bad, the chooser picks one first (a 1/3 chance of the good), an informed intermediary bound by rules must then reveal a bad unchosen option and offer a switch, at which point staying stays 1/3 while switching carries the full 2/3, because the constrained reveal funnels all the unchosen likelihood onto the single remaining option.
