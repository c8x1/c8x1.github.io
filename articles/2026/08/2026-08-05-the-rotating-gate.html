轮值的闸门

AI Parable (inspired by Amanda Askell)·每日精选·2026年8月5日·2分钟阅读

在梯田山腰上，七户茶农共用一座石砌水塘，塘口只立一道闸门。每年开春，他们必须议定放水的轮值次序，否则闸门常闭，茶行尽皆枯焦。早年间立规矩的魏婆婆定下一条：每户都要当面逐户去问其余各家要哪个时辰，再在议事会上把所得答复一一比对。她用一笔硬账卡死了规矩的底气——七户之中只要作乱的不超过两户，离三分之一尚差一线，章程就撑得住；若到了三户，天上地下再没哪套商量能让闸门启开。

On the terraced hillside, seven tea-growing families shared one stone reservoir with a single iron gate. Each spring they had to agree on a rotation for drawing water, or the gate stayed sealed and every row of tea withered. Long ago the founder, Granny Wei, had laid down the rule: every family must ask every other family, face to face, what slot it claimed, and then all must compare their answers in open council. She had fixed the rule's reach with hard arithmetic — the procedure holds as long as no more than two of the seven go disordered, just shy of a third; let a third turn, and no agreement under heaven can save the gate.

这一年开春，便有两户作了乱，且各乱各的。甘家索性再不派人赴会——名分还在，人影已无，只算哑了、停了。更难堪的是莫家：他家代表当面跟林家说「清晨的水我讨了」，又同样当面跟陈家说「清晨的水让与你」——同一桩询问，两副相反的答话，且说时只让对方看见自己，全不教两家互知。

That spring two families turned disordered, each in its own way. The Gan household simply sent no one to the talks — present in name, absent in fact, a plain stoppage. Worse was the Mo household: their representative told the Lin family, to its face, that he claimed the morning flow, and told the Chen family, equally plainly, that he conceded the morning flow to them — the same question, two opposite answers, given so that neither hearer suspected the other existed.

于是五户正派的茶农便撞上了代价。省力的法子是各信各听来的话——可莫家代表那一张两副嘴一渗进来，轮值就必裂：林家与陈家都要占清晨的时辰，次序撕开，闸门照旧关死，满坡茶树一齐渴死。省力者省力，尽头却是绝收。要保住闸门，唯有魏婆婆那套更沉的章程：任何一句单传的禀报都不可轻信；议事会上，每户须当众把自己从各家听来的原话逐句复述，好叫矛盾摆在众目之下，正派的五户才得议出一个共守的轮值。

Now the five truthful families faced the cost. They could take the easy path and believe whatever each had been told — but then the Mo representative's double mouth would poison the rotation: Lin and Chen would each claim the morning slot, the schedule would tear, and the gate would stay shut and every crop die of thirst. The easy path was cheap and it led to ruin. Granny Wei's heavier procedure was the only one that kept the gate: trust no single report; on the council floor, each family must recount aloud exactly what every other family said to it, so that the contradictions stand exposed in common daylight, and the truthful five can settle on one shared rotation.

他们照办了。议事会上，林家说：莫家跟我说，清晨归我。陈家说：莫家跟我说，清晨归你。矛盾便赤裸裸摊在众人眼前。莫家代表被逼到墙角，支吾着又改了一回口，矛盾反倒更利。五户至此够了：七份禀报里有五份彼此对得上，两户作乱的也摊在明处。他们五户之间议定了轮值，余下两个时辰拈阄分派，作乱的两户无论情愿与否，都须受这结果约束。

They did it. On the floor, Lin said that Mo had told him the morning was his. Chen said that Mo had told him the morning was theirs. The contradiction stood bare to all. The Mo representative, cornered, stammered and changed his story a third time, and the contradiction only sharpened. Now the five had enough: five matching accounts out of seven, the two disordered ones laid open to view. They agreed on a rotation among themselves, and the two leftover slots they cast lots for, with the disordered households bound to the result whether they liked it or not.

这时有人问了更狠的一句：若再有一户也作起乱来呢？魏婆婆那笔账是硬的。七户之数，章程撑得住两户；到了三户，便告崩塌。五副老实的嗓门再也压不过嘈杂，矛盾滋生得比交叉核对所能拆解的更快，商量不出——非不尽力，乃事理使然。闸门将一路闭到底，而满坡之人多半诚实这一桩，于事无补。离三分之一差一口，这道仅容一身的崖沿，便是他们整季收成所立的全部余地。

Then someone asked the sharper question: what if a third family had also turned? Granny Wei's arithmetic had been exact. With seven, the procedure stands against two; against three it collapses. Five truthful voices could no longer outvote the noise; contradictions would breed faster than cross-checks could untangle; and no agreement — not for want of trying, but by the nature of the thing — could be reached. The gate would stay sealed, and it would not matter that most of them were honest. That margin, one body short of a third, was the whole ledge on which their harvests stood.

闸门如期启开，茶行尽皆饮足了水。甘家那桩过错不过是在会中哑了声——只算停了，不算撒谎——来春便得宽宥，重又入会；莫家那番谎却是蓄意，水却照分不误。章程并不费心去判度心意：一根噤了的舌头与一根分叉的舌头，在它看来都只是待以多数压过的乱象而已。它应许的，从来不是无人说谎。它应许的，是只要作乱之户不足三分之一，诚实的人家便不会丢了议事的法定之数。

The gate opened on schedule, and the tea rows drank. The Gan household, whose fault had been merely to fall silent — a plain stoppage rather than a lie — was forgiven and readmitted the next spring; and the Mo household, whose lies had been deliberate, drew its share of water all the same. The procedure made no effort to judge intent: a tongue that stills and a tongue that forks are both, to it, only disorder to be outnumbered. Its promise was never that no one would lie. Its promise was that, so long as the disordered stayed under a third, the truthful families could not be made to lose their quorum.

注解：这则寓言对应拜占庭容错（the Byzantine fault tolerance）——一个系统在少数成员出现任意行为（包括向不同对象说出自相矛盾的话）时，正确成员仍能通过冗余的交叉核对达成一致。这种容忍只到成员数的三分之一为止，越过这一临界比例，共识便告崩溃。

Annotation: This fable corresponds to Byzantine fault tolerance — a system's ability, when a minority of members behave arbitrarily (including giving contradictory answers to different parties), for the correct members still to reach agreement through redundant cross-checking. The tolerance holds only up to one-third of the members; past that critical fraction, consensus collapses.
