庄子与对精英主义的诘问

庄子对「应得」观念的道家诘问

Christine Abigail L Tan·Aeon·2026年8月24日·12分钟阅读·原文

深植于正义与公平理想之中的，是这样一种观念：我们应当得到我们应得之物。因此，「应得」（deservingness）是一个直觉上极具说服力的概念，因为我们希望世界是可理解的。如果善人受苦而恶人得势，那么这个世界不仅成为一个令人痛苦的居所，也成为一个道德失序之地。即便世界充满了不公，「人们各得其所」仍是一种必要的幻觉。我们必须相信自己的行为有意义，才能产生追求所愿之物的必要动力。

Deeply embedded in the ideals of justice and fairness is the idea that we ought to get what we deserve. Deservingness is thus an intuitively compelling concept, because we want the world to be intelligible. If the good suffer and the wicked flourish, then the world not only becomes a painful place to live in, it also becomes a place of moral chaos. Even if the world is bountifully unjust, it remains a necessary illusion that people get what they deserve. We need to believe our actions matter if we are to generate the necessary motivation to pursue the things we want.

这便是精英主义（meritocracy）的理想。其核心理念在于：社会与经济的差异只要反映个人的努力或才华，便是正当的。它与我们新自由主义的自由市场民主制度颇为契合——后者将自身呈现为开放的机遇体系，奖励那些在竞争中胜出的人。精英主义暗示不平等是公正合理的。攀升者理当攀升；落后者则被鼓励更加努力。我们每个人都必须自力更生、凭己之力翻身。

This is the ideal of meritocracy. At its core, this ideology holds that social and economic differences are justified when they reflect individual effort or talent. It fits well with our neoliberal free-market democracies, which present themselves as open systems of opportunity that reward those who compete successfully. Meritocracy implies that inequality is just and fair. Those who rise deserve to rise; those who fall behind are encouraged to try harder. We must each pull ourselves up by our bootstraps.

「精英主义」（meritocracy）这个词本身或许相当新近，可追溯到迈克尔·杨（Michael Young）在1950年代的造词，但早在新自由主义之前，另一种更为古老的哲学就已将不平等论证为个人努力理当承担的后果：儒家思想（Confucianism）。考察儒家思想在何处出了偏差，能启发我们如何挑战精英主义这一强大的意识形态。

‘Meritocracy’ itself might be a relatively new word, dating back to Michael Young’s coinage in the 1950s, but long before neoliberalism another, much more ancient, philosophy justified inequality as the well-deserved consequence of individual effort: Confucianism. Examining where Confucianism goes wrong illuminates how we should challenge the powerful ideology of meritocracy.

孔子（Confucius，约公元前551—前479年）生活在周朝晚期，那是一个对立封建诸侯之间政治冲突激烈的时代。他是一位教师和低级官员，辗转于各国朝廷之间进言献策。他认为，所处时代的暴力乱象源于道德上的失败，而这种失败本可避免。于是孔子的事业是实践性的：通过道德教育，个人可以修养自身，而经由这种修养，一个秩序井然的等级体系便能恢复稳定。

Confucius (c551-c479 BCE) lived during the late Zhou dynasty, a time of intense political conflict between rival feudal lords. He was a teacher and minor official who moved between courts offering advice. He believed that the violent disorder of his era stemmed from moral failure that was ultimately preventable. So Confucius’s project was practical: through moral education, individuals could refine themselves and, through that refinement, a well-ordered hierarchy could restore stability.

儒家的政治哲学始于一个看似惊人地平等的前提：机会平等。对孔子及其后继者而言，人在出生时的才华大体平等。每个人都具备道德修养的潜能。教育、自律与伦理精进是一条向任何人敞开的道路，无论其出身何处。这一共同的起点奠定了儒家人文主义的基石：只要你努力，就能成功。

Confucian political philosophy begins from a premise that appears strikingly egalitarian: the equality of opportunity. For Confucius and his successors, human beings are more or less equal in talent at birth. Everyone possesses the capacity for moral cultivation. Education, self-discipline and ethical refinement remain open paths that anyone can undertake, no matter where they’re from. This shared starting point anchors Confucian humanism: you can make it if you try.

他对开放性与社会流动的承诺，源自一种坚定的道德能动性概念和人类意志的力量。儒家相信，个人对自身成为何种人负有责任。通过学习、礼乐实践和克己自律等持续努力，人们主动塑造自身的品格。因此，个人与其人生结果之间存在着道德上意味深长的关联。一个人的社会地位是对能动性的回应，而非受制于境遇。

His commitment to openness and mobility is born out of a robust conception of moral agency and the power of the human will. Confucians believe that individuals are responsible for what they become. Through sustained efforts such as learning, ritual practice and disciplined self-regulation, people actively shape their character. Individuals therefore stand in a morally significant relation to their outcomes. One’s social position is responsive to agency, despite one’s circumstances.

儒家哲学家孟子（Mencius，即孟轲）更为系统地阐述了这一思想，后世称之为「性善平等」（natural equality）：所有人类共享同一种基本的道德能力。每个人都具备向善的潜能。既然人人机会平等，那么不平等的结果与社会地位的差异便源于努力、自律与发展的差异。

The Confucian philosopher Mencius (Mengzi) articulated this idea more systematically, which was later called ‘natural equality’: all human beings share the same basic moral capacity. Everyone possesses the potential for goodness. Since everyone has the same equality of opportunity, unequal outcomes and differences in social position follow differences in effort, discipline and development.

「有些苗不开花，正如有些花不结果！」

‘There are some sprouts that fail to flower, just as surely as there are some flowers that fail to bear fruit!’

一个有助于我们理解这一点、且居于儒家思想核心位置的概念，是「贤」（xian，賢）。贤区分了那些已修养自身与未修养自身的人。这是一种伦理品级上的差异，因为某些人比另一些人更多地发展出了同情、正义判断与道德节制。的确，有些人将这些美德发展到足以承担集体生活责任的程度。因此，道德上的不平等不可避免地存在，尽管道德能力最初是人人平等共享的。

A concept that helps us make sense of this, and that lies at the very heart of Confucian thought, is worthiness (xian, 賢). Xian captures the difference between those who have cultivated themselves and those who have not. This is a difference in ethical standing, as some individuals have developed compassion, righteous judgment and moral restraint more than others. Indeed, some have developed these virtues to a degree that enables them to bear responsibility for collective life. Moral inequality therefore inevitably exists, even though moral capacity is something that is originally equally shared.

这一区分具有政治意义，因为儒家认为：当经济与政治的不平等映射道德上的不平等时，它们便是正当的。权威、影响力和物质保障应当归于那些因其修养而成的禀性而能以道德与正义方式行使权力的人。于是，当不平等与道德价值相匹配时，它便在伦理上变得可接受，甚至可取。

This distinction has political significance because Confucianism holds that economic and political inequality are justified when they mirror moral inequality. Authority, influence and material security should fall to those whose cultivated dispositions allow them to wield power in moral and righteous ways. Inequality thus becomes ethically acceptable, even advisable, when it tracks moral worth.

在儒家看来，这一切都是公正的，因为它还依赖于一个进一步的假设：道德修养并非人人都会选择去做的事。正如孔子在《论语》中所言：「有些苗不开花，正如有些花不结果！」每个人都有寻求道德教育、重塑自身品格的能力，但只有那些真正付诸行动的人，才凭借自身努力成为贤。

This is all fair, according to the Confucians, because it rests on a further assumption: that moral cultivation is something not everyone chooses to do. As Confucius says in the Analects: ‘there are some sprouts that fail to flower, just as surely as there are some flowers that fail to bear fruit!’ Everyone has the capacity to seek moral education and reshape their character, but only those who actually do so become xian through their own effort.

此外，将贤者与不贤者区分开来至关重要，如此方能把治理社会的权威赋予值得信赖之人。这便是儒家「正名」（zhengming，正名）原则的职责。名分充当规范性的角色。被命名或被授予君、臣或贤者的头衔，意味着被赋予与这些头衔相称的义务、期望与权威。正名将道德上的不平等转译为一张连贯的社会图谱，确保修养而成的能力与社会地位相对应。

Furthermore, it is of utmost importance that we distinguish those who are xian from those who are not, such that the worthy ones can be given the authority to govern society. This is the role of the Confucian principle of ‘rectification of names’ (zhengming, 正名). Names function as normative roles. To be named or be given the title of a ruler, a minister or a worthy person is to be assigned obligations, expectations and authority befitting those titles. Zhengming translates moral inequality into a coherent social map, ensuring that cultivated capacities align with social positions.

当然，承担更大责任者需要更多资源来履行其职能。政治等级于是意味着经济不平等。贤者不仅需要更多，也理应获得更多。正如儒家哲学家荀子（Xunzi）所言：「即便一个人的俸禄丰厚到拥有整个天下，也不算过分；即便一个人只是个看门人、接待员、守卫或更夫，也永不必觉得自己的薪俸微薄。」对荀子而言，不平等不仅不可避免，而且恰当合宜。政府的职责在于创造一个等级化的环境，为人们成为贤者提供条件，进而区分贤与不贤。不平等是社会秩序的必要前提。

Of course, those who bear greater responsibility require greater resources to fulfil their functions. Political hierarchy then implies economic inequality. Those who are worthy not only need more, they also deserve more. As the Confucian philosopher Xunzi tells us: ‘though one may have as his emolument the whole world, he need not consider it excessive, and though one be only a gatekeeper, receptionist, guard or nightwatchman, he need never think his salary too meagre.’ For Xunzi, inequality is not only inevitable, but fitting. It is the government’s duty to create a hierarchical environment that provides the infrastructure for people to be worthy, and then distinguish between the worthy and the unworthy. Inequality is the necessary precondition for social order.

这一结构在两千多年前便已确立，然而不难看出它如何持续回响于当代对自我提升和勤劳价值的呼吁之中。词汇或许从「美德」变为「生产力」，从「修养」变为「绩效」，但道德语法仍然惊人地相似。不平等之所以显得理所应得，是因为它呈现为开放体系中个人努力累积的结果。

This structure was laid out more than two millennia ago, yet it is not difficult to see how it continues to resonate in contemporary appeals to self-improvement and the value of hard work. The vocabulary might shift from virtue to productivity and from cultivation to performance, but the moral grammar is still strikingly similar. Inequality appears deserved because it presents itself as the cumulative result of individual effort within an open system.

道家哲学家庄子（Zhuangzi）对这一切概不接受。庄子大约在孔子之后一个世纪写作，却生活在同样充满敌意、政治纷争不断的世界里，他对乱局采取了一种截然不同的姿态。关于他的生平所知甚少，但以其名字命名的那部伟大著作第十七章中的一个轶事，透露了他是怎样一种人。故事中，庄子被授予高位，但他婉拒了。当国王的使者前来征召他时，庄子指着王室宗庙中供奉的一只神龟甲壳，问这只乌龟是宁愿死后被尊奉，还是宁愿活着在泥水中曳尾而行。使者回答说，乌龟宁愿活着而不愿被尊奉。「那就请回吧，」庄子说，「我也将在泥中曳尾而行。」与其在一个将生命力当作抽象声望之货币来榨取的体系中被抬升，不如保持活着、不被排名。

The Daoist philosopher Zhuangzi accepted none of this. Writing roughly a century after Confucius but living in the same hostile and politically fractious world, Zhuangzi adopted a remarkably different stance towards disorder. Little is known about his life, but an anecdote from the 17th chapter of his great work that bears his own name tells us something of the kind of man he was. In the story, Zhuangzi is offered a high office, but he declines. When envoys from the king arrive to recruit him, Zhuangzi points to a sacred turtle shell kept in the royal temple and asks whether the turtle would rather be honoured after death or be alive, dragging its tail through the mud. The envoys reply that the turtle would rather be alive than honoured. ‘Then go away,’ Zhuangzi says. ‘I too will drag my tail in the mud.’ It is better to remain alive and unranked than to be elevated within a system that exploits vitality as though it’s currency for abstract prestige.

儒家在世界上看到的是一幅道德地图，其中修养而成的价值与社会权威相对齐；庄子看到的则是一个远为脆弱、充满偶然的现实。他的批判对象不仅是体系内部的不公，更是「道德价值可以作为等级之稳定基础」这一观念本身。

Where Confucianism sees a moral map of the world in which cultivated worth lines up with social authority, Zhuangzi sees a far more fragile and contingent reality. His target is not simply unfairness within the system, but the very idea that moral worth can serve as a stable foundation for hierarchy.

庄子将他的批判化为戏剧。在他最为犀利的寓言之一中，他设想了一场对峙：一方是道德修养之典范的孔子，另一方是率领一支暴力帮派、声名狼藉的大盗盗跖（Robber Zhi）——最好的人面对最坏的人。孔子走向跖，而跖此刻正好在嚼食一块人肝。尽管如此，孔子仍希望感化这位走上歧途的食人者。他讲着美德、礼义与正义的语言，敦促跖放弃犯罪生涯，成为一个道德修养之人，成为贤。

Zhuangzi stages his critique as theatre. In one of his most striking parables, he imagines a confrontation between Confucius, the emblem of moral cultivation, and Robber Zhi, a notorious bandit leading a violent gang: the best sort of person facing the worst sort of person. Confucius approaches Zhi, who at that moment happens to be munching on a human liver. Nevertheless, Confucius hopes to reform the wayward cannibal. He speaks the language of virtue, propriety and righteousness, urging Zhi to abandon his criminal life and become a morally cultivated person, becoming xian.

以美德对人排名的体系，必然制造出自我的过度榨取

A system that ranks people by virtue necessarily creates incentives for self-exploitation

随后庄子打乱了这一道德几何。跖既不显得困惑，也不显得缺乏道德教养。他以敏锐的智识回应，将孔子的道德语言反戈一击。「你随意地判定什么对、什么错，」他说，「由此误导了天下的诸侯，使天下学者不得专心于本业。」他进而嘲讽「美德赋予任何人统治或审判他人的特殊资格」这一观念。他指控孔子及其所效力的统治者所做的，正是他自己在较小规模上所做的事：从他人手中夺取。唯一的区别在于，孔子是以崇高理想之名行此之事。

Then Zhuangzi scrambles this moral geometry. Zhi does not appear confused or morally uneducated. He responds with sharp intelligence, turning Confucius’s moral language back on itself. ‘You arbitrarily decide what is right and what is wrong,’ he says, ‘thereby leading astray the princes throughout the kingdom, and making its learned scholars not occupy their thoughts with their proper business.’ He goes on to mock the idea that virtue gives anyone a special claim to rule or to judge others. He accuses Confucius and the rulers he serves of doing on a grand scale what he does at a smaller scale: taking from others. The only difference is that, in Confucius’s case, he does so in the name of lofty ideals.

他将这些理想视为一种盗窃：通过名、标准和排名来攫取世界，再以这种攫取来赋予权威以正当性。因此，庄子的文学目标，是让盗贼与圣人成为彼此的镜像。二者之别仅仅在于技艺与社会结构，而非某种本体论上的既定事实。跖与孔子都是贼：一个盗的是财物，一个盗的是名。

He treats these ideals as a kind of theft: a way of capturing the world through names, standards and rankings, then using that capture to legitimise authority. Zhuangzi’s literary goal was thus to make the bandit and the sage into mirror images. The difference is merely in technique and social structure rather than an ontological given. Zhi and Confucius are both thieves: one of goods, the other of names.

通过此类戏剧化演绎，庄子发展出一套对儒家不平等道德论证的系统批判，而其中最核心的部分，是他坚持认为：道德上的奋发将我们与生命本身疏离开来。对庄子而言，追求成为贤的冲动，引诱一个人为一种抽象之物而活——无论那是声誉、道德纯洁，还是追求「善」的圣人理想。这导致一个人将自身的生命当作那抽象身份的原材料来对待。人的血肉之躯沦为某种无形之物的工具。

Through such theatrics Zhuangzi developed a systematic critique of Confucianism’s moral justification of inequality, and the most essential part of that critique is his insistence that moral striving alienates us from life. For Zhuangzi, the drive to become xian invites a person to live for an abstraction, whether it be for reputation, moral purity or a sagely ideal of pursuing ‘the good’. This leads one to treat their own life as raw material for that abstract identity. One’s material body becomes an instrument for something immaterial.

在盗跖的独白中，他列举了儒家所推崇的典范人物：伯夷（Bo Yi）和叔齐（Shu Qi），宁肯饿死也不愿侍奉不义之君；比干（Bi Gan），因强力进谏纣王而被挖心；伍子胥（Wu Zixu），因其对国家的忠诚——警告君王所面临的威胁——最终落得尸体被抛入河中。这些人在儒家中被赞颂为美德典范，而庄子却将他们视为悲剧人物，认为他们「困于声名之网」，恰恰因其对美德的执拗追求而落得惨烈下场。

In the Robber’s monologue, he lists celebrated Confucian exemplars: Bo Yi and Shu Qi, who starved themselves rather than serve an unjust ruler; Bi Gan, who remonstrated with King Zhou so forcefully that his heart was cut out; Wu Zixu, whose loyalty to his state, warning his King of a threat, ended with his corpse thrown into a river. These people are praised in Confucianism as exemplars of virtue, yet Zhuangzi treats them as tragic figures ‘trapped in the net of reputation and names’, who met their grisly ends precisely because of their inflexible pursuit of virtue.

令这些人物令人不安之处在于，他们被自身的信念所吞噬。一个以美德对人排名的体系，必然制造出自我的过度榨取。一旦美德成为可被衡量、排名并换取权威之物，它便将人的生命转化为一种可供耗费的资源。换言之，儒家的贤将修养变成了道德资本，而道德资本要求从自我中提取。事实上，在我们现代新自由主义的「奋斗文化」（hustle culture）中，这种自我榨取更为清晰可见：「早起苦干」（rise and grind）、舍弃基本需求正是一种时髦。

What makes these figures disturbing is that they were consumed by their convictions. A system that ranks people by virtue necessarily creates incentives for self-exploitation. Once virtue becomes something that can be measured, ranked and exchanged for authority, it turns human life into a resource to be spent. In other words, Confucian xian turns cultivation into moral capital, and moral capital demands extraction from the self. Indeed, one can see this kind of self-extraction in our modern neoliberal ‘hustle culture’ even more clearly, where it is trendy to ‘rise and grind’ and forego basic necessities.

有人或许会反驳：庄子对儒家中精英主义理想持一种极度悲观的态度，而儒家从根本上关涉的是道德与价值，而非物质财富。毕竟，社会变革需要牺牲。然而，在这一体系中，牺牲并不作为对不公的回应而起作用（即便个体行动者自己是那样理解的），而是作为积累道德地位的方式。一个人以牺牲所购取的，并非一个更美好世界的可能性，而是为自己或自己家族谋得更高的位置。

One might object that Zhuangzi had an extremely pessimistic view of the meritocratic ideal in Confucianism, which fundamentally deals with morals and values, instead of material wealth. After all, social change requires sacrifice. However, in this system, sacrifice does not function as a response to injustice (even when individual actors understand themselves that way), but as a way of accumulating moral standing. What is being purchased with one’s sacrifice is not the possibility of a better world, but a higher place for yourself or your own family.

要使这一切运转，美德必须是某种可被衡量和排名之物。唯有如此，生命才能依照谁应得更多、谁应得更少而加以排序。因此，儒家的精英主义理想，建立在这样一个假设之上：价值拥有稳定可靠的衡量尺度。庄子并不认为这是对的。

For this to work, virtue has to be something that can be measured and ranked. Only then can lives be sorted according to those who deserve more and those who deserve less. The Confucian meritocratic ideal therefore rests on the assumption that values have a stable and reliable measure. Zhuangzi did not think this was true.

对他而言，任何价值衡量——包括儒家的衡量——归根结底都是有缺陷的，因为现实并非我们可以整齐地映射到善恶之上的东西。执意如此，正如盗跖所言，是对价值的「垄断」。在现实中，道德区分产生于具体情境之中。对一人看似慈悲之举，可能同时伤害他人；在某一情境下算作正义之事，在另一情境中可能沦为残忍。「在我看来，」庄子写道，「仁义之端、是非之迹，纷然交错、混乱不堪。我又怎能知其分辨、明其结论呢？」

For him, any measurement of value, including the Confucian one, is ultimately flawed because reality is not something we can neatly map onto good and bad. To insist on doing so is, as the Robber puts it, a ‘monopolising’ of values. In reality, moral distinctions arise within concrete situations. What looks like an act of compassion toward one person can simultaneously harm others; what counts as righteousness in one context can become cruelty in another. ‘From where I see it,’ Zhuangzi writes, ‘all the sproutings of humankindness and responsible conduct, and all the trails of right and wrong, are hopelessly tangled and confused. How could I know how to distinguish and demonstrate any conclusions about them?’

运气、境遇与特权，远比道德修养更能决定谁最终掌权

Luck, circumstance and privilege do far more to determine who ends up in power than moral cultivation

这并不意味着没有什么是正确的、因此一切皆可。相反，它主张：我们所做出的评价，从未脱离产生这些评价的情境而独立存在。并不存在一个中立的有利位置，可以据以衡量「美德本身」。此外，庄子坚持认为，即便在价值可以径直判定的理想化情境中，认为等级与制度能够反映那张道德图谱，也是对权力实际运作方式的深刻误解。在「盗跖」篇的另一处，他指出：「小偷被逮捕，大盗成统治者。」

This does not mean that nothing is right and therefore anything goes. Rather, it holds that the evaluations we make never detach themselves from the circumstances that produce them. There is no neutral vantage point from which ‘virtue itself’ can be weighed. Furthermore, Zhuangzi insists that even in idealised situations where values can be straightforward, the idea that hierarchies and institutions can reflect that moral map is a profound misunderstanding of how power actually works. Elsewhere in the Robber Zhi chapter, he notes how ‘a small thief gets arrested; a great thief becomes a ruler.’

我们看到这一情形反复上演。1983年，阿拉巴马州一名叫阿尔文·肯纳德（Alvin Kennard）的男子从一家面包店偷了50.75美元。他被判处终身监禁，直到2019年才获释。与此同时，2008年金融危机的肇事者摧毁了估计约50万亿美元的全球财富，并使近1000万个美国家庭失去住所。只有一名中层银行家入狱（30个月），罪名是证券标价不实。权力便是如此倾斜了问责的天平。用庄子的话说，大盗不过是拥有足够权力的窃贼，以至于他的取夺为其他人设定了规则。

We see this play out very often. In 1983, a man from Alabama named Alvin Kennard stole $50.75 from a bakery. He was given a life sentence and released in 2019. Meanwhile, the culprits of the 2008 financial crisis destroyed an estimated $50 trillion in global wealth and drove nearly 10 million American families from their homes. Only one mid-level banker went to prison (for 30 months) for mismarking securities. This is how power tips the scales of accountability. In Zhuangzi’s terms, the great thief is simply the thief with enough power that his taking sets the rules for everyone else.

如今一如庄子时代，运气、境遇与特权，远比道德修养更能决定谁最终掌权。在《庄子》中，孔子也提出要为盗跖恢复名誉，将他塑造为一名征服者而非社会公害。其要点在于：在庄子看来，一旦某人胜出，其地位便往往被追溯性地论证为应得。他们的支配地位变成了其美德的证据，而他们的胜利则成了其价值的证明。

Now, as in Zhuangzi’s time, luck, circumstance and privilege do far more to determine who ends up in power than moral cultivation ever could. In the Zhuangzi, Confucius also offers to rehabilitate the Robber’s reputation and frame him as a conqueror rather than a menace to society. The point is that, for Zhuangzi, once someone has prevailed, their position is often retroactively framed as deserved. Their dominance becomes evidence of their virtue, and their victory becomes proof of their worth.

儒家将价值视为可在赋予权威之前被识别之物；庄子则恰恰相反地指出：权威决定了谁被算作有价值者。因此，权力往往并不反映美德，而是拥有制造美德表象的能力。等级体系便是如此编造出自身的道德叙事。统治者遂被称为正义之人。

Whereas Confucianism treats worth as something that can be recognised before authority is assigned, Zhuangzi suggests just the opposite: that authority determines who gets counted as worthy. Power, therefore, often does not reflect virtue, but instead has the ability to produce the appearance of virtue. This is how hierarchy manufactures its own moral narrative. Those who rule come to be called righteous.

使能动性成为可能的那些能力，在任何修养开始之前便已不均等地分布

The very capacities that make agency possible are unevenly distributed before any cultivation can even begin

这一观念在现代的「霸权」（hegemony）概念中有所回响——由意大利马克思主义者安东尼奥·葛兰西（Antonio Gramsci）所阐发——以及人类学家大卫·格雷伯（David Graeber）所称的「我们自己梦想的假币」。其要点在于：权力不仅通过指挥身体而运作，更通过决定何者被算作有价值而运作，并将其呈现为自然的或不可避免的，而实际上它是由偶然的社会安排与权力关系所塑造的。

This idea is echoed in the modern concepts of ‘hegemony’ as developed by the Italian Marxist Antonio Gramsci, and what the anthropologist David Graeber calls the ‘false coin of our own dreams’. The point is that power works not only by commanding bodies but by determining what counts as valuable, and presenting it as natural or inevitable when in reality it is shaped by contingent social arrangements and power relations.

对精英主义理想的这种意识形态承诺之所以具有吸引力，不仅在道德层面，也在心理层面。一旦我们倾注数年心血以求精进，质疑那个评价我们的框架便代价高昂。怀疑努力终有回报，就等于冒着承认以下可能性的风险：我们所投入的许多东西或许永远不会有回报。相信我们会成为例外，远比直面「体系本身依赖许多人永不成功」这一可能性要容易得多。其持久的生命力不仅在于为精英提供便利，更在于它组织希望的能力。它提供了一种叙事：其中苦难是暂时的，牺牲是理性的，而未来将为当下正名。

The attraction of this ideological commitment to the meritocratic ideal is not only moral but psychological. Once we have poured years of work into becoming better, questioning the framework that evaluates us becomes costly. To doubt that effort pays off is to risk admitting that much of what we have invested may never yield returns. It is far easier to believe that we will be the exception than to confront the possibility that the system itself depends on many people never succeeding. Its staying power lies not merely in convenience for elites, but in its capacity to organise hope. It offers a narrative in which suffering is temporary, sacrifice is rational, and the future will vindicate the present.

然而，道德评价及其社会与政治的可映射性，始终以一个本可另行选择的主体为前提。要使精英主义运转、要使不平等显得理所应得，人们就必须被想象为自身成败的作者。可是，使能动性成为可能的那些能力本身——如教育、健康、时间、稳定、人际关系网络——在任何修养开始之前便已不均等地分布。这些条件塑造了努力与卓越的面貌，这意味着「美德」只能由那些已然成功者来定义。

Yet moral evaluation and its social and political mappability always presupposes a subject who could have done otherwise. For meritocracy to function and for inequality to appear deserved, people must be imagined as the authors of their own success and failure. However, the very capacities that make agency possible (such as education, health, time, stability, personal networks) are unevenly distributed before any cultivation can even begin. These conditions shape what effort and excellence look like, which means that ‘virtue’ can only ever be defined by those who have already succeeded.

归根结底，庄子质疑的正是「个人本可另行选择」这一观念。

Ultimately, Zhuangzi questions the idea that the individual could have done otherwise.

在他看来，行动并非源于一个孤立的意志。它产生于情境之中。品格通过关系、习惯、语言、制度、需求、恐惧和机遇而形成。看似个体能动性者，实则始终是共同生成之物。庄子用以指称这一点的概念是「自然」（ziran，自然）。它常被译为「自然」（naturalness），但更好地被理解为「自己如此」（so-of-itself）。事件与行动从情境中生发，而非出自一个将意志强加于世界的主权自我。自我并不立于其条件之外，而是其中的一个节点。行动既是推动，亦是被推动。

For him, action does not originate in an isolated will. It arises through circumstances. Character forms through relationships, habits, language, institutions, needs, fears and opportunities. What might look like individual agency is always co-produced. Zhuangzi’s concept for this is ziran (自然). It is often translated as ‘naturalness’, but better understood as the ‘so-of-itself’. Events and actions arise from the situation, and not from a sovereign self who imposes their will on the world. The self does not stand outside its conditions, but is a node within them. To act is to be moved as much as to move.

在《庄子》第一章中，我们被引见给神话中的哲人列子（Liezi），他能御风而行。他在空中悠然滑翔，看似完全自由地运动。然而庄子指出，列子仍依赖于某物：风。他的运动无论多么优雅，始终是有条件的。这一意象为人类的成就提供了一则寓言。看似自我驱动的成功，实则始终由社会、物质与历史的洪流所承载，而这些洪流并无个人作者。精英主义中的主体，误将一阵顺风当作了个人飞行。

In the first chapter of the Zhuangzi, we are introduced to the mythical philosopher Liezi, who is able to ride the wind. He glides effortlessly through the air, appearing to move with complete freedom. Yet Zhuangzi points out that Liezi still depends on something: the wind. His motion, however graceful, remains conditional. The image offers a parable of human achievement. What looks like self-propelled success is always carried by social, material and historical currents that have no individual author. The meritocratic subject mistakes a favourable wind for personal flight.

随后庄子将列子与另一种人加以对照：那人「乘天地之正……御六气之辩」（即无所不在的万物）而行。这一形象不依赖于任何单一之风，因为他不再将自身想象为一个独立的御者。他不再仅仅依赖于一两个固定的条件，而是依赖于一切，并与一切完全交融。他的行动随整个条件之场而流动，控制与所有权大多消融其中。

Zhuangzi then contrasts Liezi with the person who rides ‘upon what is true, both to heaven and to earth … atop the back-and-forth of the six atmospheric breaths’ (ie, all things everywhere). This figure does not depend on any single wind because they no longer imagine themselves as a separate rider. Instead of depending on just one or few fixed conditions, they depend on everything and converge completely with everything. Their action flows with the whole field of conditions, with control and ownership mostly disappearing.

这一形象体现了一种独具道家色彩的能动性理想。精英主义框架中的个体将自身理解为通过努力积累成就、从而赢得回报之人；而道家圣人则放弃对行动的所有权。他们不将成功视为自我所创造之物，也不将失败视为恰当归属于自我之物。所发生之事，皆因条件之汇聚而发生。能动性是回应而非强加。

This figure embodies a distinctively Daoist ideal of agency. Where individuals of the meritocratic framework understand themselves as one who accumulates achievement through effort and thereby earns reward, the Daoist sage relinquishes ownership of action altogether. They do not see success as something authored by the self, nor failure as something that properly belongs to it. What happens happens through the convergence of conditions. Agency is instead responsiveness rather than imposition.

没有了能完全拥有自身结果的自我，社会与政治的不平等便失去了其道德引擎

Without a self that can fully own its outcomes, social and political inequality loses its moral engine

说「这是我应得的」，就是说：我创造了产生它的行动。庄子的世界赋予每一个这样的断言一个看不见的复数。努力通过教养、教育、制度路径、情感与物质支持、健康、运气以及他人使一切奋斗成为可能的寻常劳作而生发。「功绩」（merit）在很大程度上是一种社会产物。我们的品格与个性被塑造的方式本身，也是一种社会产物。

To say ‘I deserve this’ is to say: I authored the action that produced it. Zhuangzi’s world gives every such claim an invisible plural. Effort arises through upbringing, pedagogy, institutional pathways, emotional and material support, health, luck and the ordinary labour of others that makes any striving possible. ‘Merit’ is in large part a social product. The ways in which our characters and personalities are shaped are themselves a social product.

因此，以这种方式理解能动性，使「机会平等」这一观念变得可疑，因为机会从来不是真正中性的起跑线。这种「平等」始终已是一个条件之场，塑造着人们赖以奋争的那些能力本身。道德能动性永远无法被干净地剥离出来加以褒贬。如此一来，道德上的不平等便失去了立足之地，因为修养的差异所表达的，既是意志的差异，同样也是境遇的差异。没有了能完全拥有自身结果的自我，社会与政治的不平等便失去了其道德引擎。

Understanding agency in this manner, therefore, makes the idea of ‘equality of opportunity’ questionable, because opportunity is never really a neutral starting line. Such ‘equality’ is always already a field of conditions that shapes the very capacities by which people strive. Moral agency can never be cleanly separated out and credited or blamed. As such, moral inequality loses its footing, because differences in cultivation express differences in circumstance as much as differences in will. Without a self that can fully own its outcomes, social and political inequality loses its moral engine.

因此，庄子的批判所指向的，正是使不平等显得理所应得的那套语法。一个社会只有先想象出一个在孤立中自我创造、随后将结果当作个人财产据为己有的自我，才能说「攀升者理当攀升」。然而对庄子而言，自我是通透的、回应性的、相互纠缠的，顺应其情境的纹理而动，而非凌驾于情境之上。在一个「自然」的世界中，没有谁是白手起家之人。而如果没有人是白手起家的，那么不平等便没有根基。

Zhuangzi’s critique therefore targets the very grammar that makes inequality look deserved. A society can say ‘those who rise deserve to rise’ only after it has imagined a self that authors itself in isolation and then owns the result as personal property. Yet for Zhuangzi, selves are porous, responsive and entangled, moving with the grain of their situations rather than standing above them. In a world of ziran, no one is a self-made success. And if no one is self-made, then inequality has no foundation.

归根结底，庄子动摇了「不平等可以得到正当化」这一希望。这一希望根深蒂固。它承诺努力是重要的，牺牲终将被认可，一个人在世间的位置反映了关于其为人之意义的某种东西。它要求人们不断奋斗、不断精进、不断投资自身，即便回报始终遥不可及、永远延宕。

Ultimately, Zhuangzi unsettles the hope that inequality can be justified. That hope runs deep. It promises that effort matters, that sacrifice will be recognised, and that one’s position in the world reflects something meaningful about who one is. It asks people to keep striving, to keep improving, to keep investing in themselves, even when the rewards remain out of reach, forever deferred.

庄子邀请我们抵制这一叙事，因为一个更好的世界是可能的。这样的世界会是何种面貌？首先，我们的价值不会由我们的成就——无论是经济上的还是道德上的——来定义。这样一个世界将以「自然」为方向，我们不再依据个人所达到的繁荣程度来对人排序，而是关注使繁荣成为可能的条件。制度性的支持与关怀，以及结构性劣势的消除，将成为首要关切。归根结底，放弃我们对「应得」的现代执念，将为一种更具动态性的人之理解铺平道路——这种理解承认，我们是巨大条件网络中的节点，这些条件共同生成着我们是谁以及我们做什么。我们将摆脱那残忍的幻觉：一个人在世间的位置，是对其作为人之价值的可靠估价。

Zhuangzi invites us to resist this story, because a better world is possible. What would such a world look like? For starters, our worth would not be defined by our achievements, whether economic or moral. Such a world would be oriented by ziran, and instead of sorting individuals according to the amount of flourishing they’ve achieved, we would attend to the conditions that make flourishing possible. Institutional support and care, as well as the removal of structural disadvantages, would be primary concerns. Ultimately, abandoning our modern obsession with desert would pave the way for a more dynamic understanding of human beings, one that recognises we are nodes in a vast network of conditions that co-produce who we are and what we do. We would be rid of the cruel fantasy that a person’s place in the world is a reliable valuation of their worth as a human being.
