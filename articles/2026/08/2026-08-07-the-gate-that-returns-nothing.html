不归之门

AI Parable (inspired by Amanda Askell)·每日精选·2026年8月7日·2分钟阅读

忘归楼（Hall of No Return）隐在西山的深处，是一座抄经堂，专门抄录那些永远不会被人读到的信件。山脚下每个邮差都晓得一条规矩：一旦过了内院那道忘归门（Gate of No Return），你寄出的信便会被「归档」，再也不会送到收信人手里。抄经堂里的事，外头没人说得清。档案员谭知非的大姐谭知言，多年前为抄一部孤本走进那道门；起初她的家书还按时到，后来越来越慢，越来越淡，墨色泛红、笔迹拉长，终于在某一年的初春断了。

The Hall of No Return (忘归楼) lies deep in the western mountains, a scriptorium that copies letters no one will ever read. Every postman in the foothills knows the rule: once you pass the inner Gate of No Return (忘归门), any letter you send is "filed" and never delivered. What happens inside is beyond the foothills to say. The archivist Tan Zhifei's elder sister Tan Zhiyan walked through that gate years ago to copy a single rare manuscript; her letters home arrived on time at first, then ever later, ever paler, the ink reddening, the brushstrokes stretching thin, until one early spring they stopped altogether.

知非把大姐最后一封信压在枕下，迟到了整整半年，笔画稀薄得像写字的手已经慢到近乎停顿。他面前摆着两条路。留在山脚，像他年迈的母亲那样，每个清早站到山脊上，盼着能远远望见那道门——可她望了许多年，望见的只有雾。或者，自己走进忘归门，去把知言找回来；代价他算得清楚：一旦过门，所有向外的路便同时关上，他能走的每一条路都只通向更深的内院，而他写给母亲的每一个字都将被归档，永远不会落到她手里。

Zhifei kept his sister's last letter under his pillow; it had arrived six months late, the strokes so thin that the hand which wrote them seemed to have slowed near to a halt. He had two roads before him. Stay in the foothills, as his aging mother did, standing on the ridge each dawn hoping to make out the gate in the distance—but she had watched for years, and seen only mist. Or walk through the Gate of No Return himself and bring Zhiyan out; the price he reckoned plainly: the moment he crossed, every road outward would shut at once, every path he could take would lead only deeper into the inner halls, and every word he wrote to his mother would be filed, never to reach her hand.

他选了那道门。门本身什么也不是——一扇木门，没有闩，比寻常人家院子的门还寻常。他跨过去的时候什么也没感觉到：没有风，没有阻挡，没有任何变化的瞬间。门内一个年轻抄手抬头看了他一眼，朝里面的抄经堂点了点头。他到了。

He chose the gate. The gate was nothing in itself—a wooden door, unbarred, no more remarkable than the door of any courtyard. He felt nothing as he stepped across: no wind, no resistance, no instant of change. A young copyist on the other side glanced up and nodded him toward the inner halls. He had arrived.

进得门去，楼本身才是陷阱。每一条走廊都只通向更深的走廊，每一封投进邮筒的信都被抬往中央的档案库，每一个问题都由更深一处的一项差事来回答。没有一条走廊会转回外院那道门；想朝外走，依着这座楼的格局，本身就是朝里走。他不是在选择下行；是这座楼替他选，正如水在倾斜的地板上无须选择方向。

Once through the gate, the building itself was the trap. Every corridor opened only onto a deeper corridor, every letter dropped in a post box was carried toward the central archive, every question was answered by a task set further in. No corridor turned back toward the outer gate; to walk "outward," by the very layout of the place, was to walk further in. He was not choosing to descend; the building chose for him, as water on a tilted floor does not choose its direction.

第三天他找到了知言。她很平静，正在抄写，并不惊讶。她指给他看角落里一摞写完的信，都在等着「往里送」。她没有忘记山脚下那片雾，只是从她站着的地方，没有一条路通向那里。她解释说，她上回寄出的那封褪了色的信，整整走了一年，才勉强挪到门附近，旋即又被走廊拖回了深处。

On the third day he found Zhiyan. She was calm, copying, unsurprised. She showed him the stack of finished letters in the corner, all waiting to be "delivered inward." She had not forgotten the mist down in the foothills; she simply had no path, from where she stood, that led there. Her last faded letter, she explained, had taken a whole year to drag itself partway toward the gate before the corridor pulled it back into the depths.

山脊上，他的母亲仍在看。在她眼里，他走近那道门的身影，这几个礼拜里仿佛一直在变慢——始终没有真正跨过去，人影越看越淡，外套的颜色一点点泛红、褪去。她会等完余生；她永远不会看见他跨过去，也再收不到他的信。而门里，他是在一个寻常得不能再寻常的早晨跨过去的，跨过去时自己毫无察觉。

On the ridge, his mother was still watching. To her, his figure nearing the gate seemed, over these past weeks, to be slowing forever—never quite stepping through, the shape of him growing dimmer, the color of his coat reddening and fading by degrees. She would wait the rest of her life; she would never see him cross, never receive another letter. Inside the gate, he had crossed on a morning ordinary as any other, and noticed nothing as he did.

注解：这就是事件视界（the event horizon）——时空中的一条因果边界：一旦越过，所有未来路径都朝向内部，任何信号都无法再传到外部观察者；远处的观望者会看到越过者永远减速、影像逐渐红移褪色，而越过者自身在越过那一刻什么也察觉不到，因为这道边界只是几何，不是一堵墙。

Note: This is the event horizon (事件视界)—a causal boundary in spacetime: once crossed, all future paths point inward and no signal can ever reach an outside observer; a distant watcher sees the traveler slow forever, the image reddening and fading, while the traveler themselves notices nothing at the crossing, for the boundary is geometry, not a wall.
