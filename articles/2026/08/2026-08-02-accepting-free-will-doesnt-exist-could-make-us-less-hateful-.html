接受自由意志并不存在，可能让我们减少仇恨与羞耻

Aaron Walton·The Conversation·2026年8月2日·3分钟阅读·原文

在2023年的畅销书《决定论》（Determined）中，神经科学家萨波斯基（Robert Sapolsky）论证说，生物与环境的相互作用中没有自由选择的空间。哲学家兼神经科学家哈里斯（Sam Harris）的说法更为直白：「自由意志是一种幻觉」。

In his 2023 bestseller, Determined, neuroscientist Robert Sapolsky argued that the interaction of biology and environment leaves no room for free choice. Philosopher and neuroscientist Sam Harris puts the case more bluntly: “free will is an illusion”.

然而，心理学界和媒体中的多位重要声音警告说，告诉人们自由意志是一种幻觉是危险的。这一担忧借鉴了大量心理学研究，这些研究表明，当人们对自由意志的信念被动摇时，后果可能令人不安：更多的欺骗、更多的攻击行为、更少的助人行为。

However, prominent voices in psychology and the media have warned that telling people free will is an illusion is dangerous. The concern draws on a body of psychological research suggesting that when people’s belief in free will is undermined, the consequences can be troubling: more cheating, more aggression, less helpfulness.

但我近期的研究，包括一项发表于《英国社会心理学杂志》（British Journal of Social Psychology）的新研究，表明这一图景并不完整且具有误导性。事实上，研究显示，接受自由意志是一种幻觉可以减少仇恨与羞耻，并增加宽恕。

But my recent research, including a new study published in the British Journal of Social Psychology, suggests this picture is incomplete and misleading. In fact, it shows that accepting free will is an illusion can reduce hatred and shame, and increase forgiveness.

自由意志通常被定义为这样一种观念：在做出选择的时刻，人们本可以做出不同的选择，而不受基因、教养和环境的先在束缚。关键在于，仅靠随机性并不能挽救自由意志，因为随机行为只是运气问题，而非自由意志的表达。

Free will is commonly defined as the idea that people could have done otherwise in a moment of choice, free from the prior grip of genes, upbringing and circumstance. Crucially, this isn’t rescued by mere randomness, as a random act would be a matter of luck rather than an expression of free will.

在近期的两项实验中，我的同事巴斯蒂安（Brock Bastian）和我让参与者接触了自由意志不存在的论证。随后我们测量了参与者对真实的过失记忆的反应。

Across two recent experiments, my colleague Brock Bastian and I exposed participants to arguments that free will doesn’t exist. We then measured how participants responded to real memories of wrongdoing.

在第一项实验中，我们要求260名参与者写下一段他们曾经伤害过他人的经历——这种记忆旨在唤起羞耻和自责。在阅读了自由意志不存在的论证后，该组参与者比那些阅读了人类拥有自由意志材料的参与者表现出显著更强的自我宽恕倾向。

In the first experiment, we asked 260 participants to write about a time they had hurt someone – a memory designed to evoke shame and self-blame. After reading arguments that free will doesn’t exist, participants in that group were significantly more self-forgiving than those who had read material saying humans have free will.

在第二项实验中，262名参与者写下了一段他人伤害过自己的经历。那些接触了同样论证（即自由意志不存在）的参与者，对伤害他们的人更加宽容。他们对那些人的仇恨感也显著降低。

In the second experiment, 262 participants wrote about a time someone had hurt them. Those exposed to the same arguments that free will doesn’t exist were more forgiving of the person who wronged them. They also felt significantly less hatred toward them.

关键的是，在两项研究中，参与者的责任意识都完好无损。

Crucially, in both studies, participants’ sense of accountability remained intact.

他们仍然认同自己（或伤害他们的人）应对自己的行为负责，这些行为是他们的过错，人们应当努力避免造成伤害。它减少的不是责任意识，而是责备的情感强度。

They still agreed that they (or their transgressor) were responsible for their actions, that the behaviour was their fault, and that people should make efforts to avoid causing harm. Rather than reducing their sense of responsibility, it reduced the emotional intensity of their blame.

这些实验发现得到了我们新研究的支持。

These experimental findings are supported by our new study.

我们在几个月内对44个国家的8917人进行了调查。在调查中，我们测量了自由意志信念的水平（使用经过验证的自由意志信念量表）以及对日常道德违规行为的惩罚性态度。

We surveyed 8,917 people across 44 countries over several months. In the survey, we measured level of belief in free will (using a validated measure of free will belief) and punitive attitudes toward everyday moral transgressions.

我们让参与者阅读一系列描述日常道德违规行为（例如撒谎或自私）的小故事。每个故事之后，他们被问到「这个人多大程度上应受惩罚？」，并在一到七的量表上作答（1代表完全不应受，7代表非常应受）。

We had participants read a series of vignettes detailing an everyday moral transgression (for example, lying or being selfish). After each vignette they were asked “how deserving is this person of punishment?”, answering on a scale from one (not at all) to seven (very much).

我们发现，更强的自由意志信念与对日常过失更严厉的惩罚性反应相关。

We found that stronger belief in free will was associated with harsher punitive responses to everyday wrongdoing.

这表明这不仅仅是实验室中的现象，而是体现在世界各地的人们实际应对冲突和过失的方式中。

This suggests this isn’t just a laboratory phenomenon, but something that plays out in how people actually navigate conflict and transgression around the world.

仇恨和羞耻是我们所经历的最具心理腐蚀性的情绪之一。

Hatred and shame are among the most psychologically corrosive emotions we experience.

羞耻一直与抑郁、焦虑和自杀相关。仇恨助长人际冲突、报复和关系破裂，同时也腐蚀着怀有仇恨的人自身。

Shame is consistently linked to depression, anxiety and suicide. Hatred fuels interpersonal conflict, revenge and relationship breakdown, and is also corrosive for the person carrying it.

相比之下，对自我和他人的宽恕是心理健康和更健康关系的高度稳健的预测因素。

By contrast, forgiveness of self and of others are highly robust predictors of psychological wellbeing and healthier relationships.

我们的发现表明，动摇对自由意志的信念并不会让人们陷入道德的失控坠落。相反，它似乎柔和了我们评判自我和他人时最尖锐的棱角（怨恨、自厌），同时保留了责任的机能性支架。人们仍然让自己和他人承担责任。他们只是带着更少的恶意去做。

What our findings suggest is that undermining belief in free will doesn’t send people into moral freefall. Instead, it appears to soften the harshest edges of how we judge ourselves and others (the spite, the self-loathing) while leaving the functional scaffolding of responsibility in place. People still hold themselves and others accountable. They just do it with less venom.

这一模式呼应了研究者所说的「文明的责备」——即你可以在不彻底放弃责任概念的情况下，缓和道德判断的情感残酷性。

This pattern echoes what researchers have called “civilising blame” – the idea you can temper the emotional brutality of moral judgement without abandoning the concept of responsibility altogether.

这并不意味着相信自由意志完全有害，也不意味着拒绝它是万能良药。

None of this means belief in free will is entirely harmful, or that rejecting it is a cure-all.

对于过度的自我宽恕会削弱改变动机，确实存在合理的担忧。一些早期发现表明自由意志怀疑论确实有潜在的负面影响，例如更多的欺骗行为。然而值得指出的是，这些发现往往未能得到重复验证。

There are legitimate concerns about excessive self-forgiveness reducing motivation to change. Some earlier findings suggest valid potential downsides of free-will scepticism, such as increased cheating. It’s worth noting, however, that these findings have often failed to be replicated.

但那种笼统的警告——即我们不应告诉人们自由意志是一种幻觉——值得认真审视。

But the blanket warning that we should not tell people free will is an illusion deserves serious scrutiny.

哲学家塞尔（John Searle）曾说，将自由意志视为一种幻觉，将是「比爱因斯坦（Einstein）、哥白尼（Copernicus）、牛顿（Newton）、伽利略（Galileo）或达尔文（Darwin）更伟大的思想革命——它将改变我们关于自身与宇宙关系的整个概念」。无论那一天是否到来，科学对自由意志的压力只会不断增大，人们也将持续接触到这些论证。

Philosopher John Searle once suggested that seeing free will as an illusion would be “a bigger revolution in our thinking than Einstein, or Copernicus, or Newton, or Galileo, or Darwin – it would alter our whole conception of our relation to the universe”. Whether or not that day comes, the scientific pressure on free will is only growing, and people will continue to encounter these arguments.

因此，问题不在于这一信息是否会传达给公众，而在于我们如何传递它。

The question, then, is not whether this message will reach the public, but how we deliver it.

我们的研究提供了乐观的理由：即使没有关于责任重要性的审慎哲学提醒，人们似乎也能自然地维持其责任意识。

Our research offers reason for optimism: even without careful philosophical caveats about the importance of responsibility, people appear to naturally sustain their sense of accountability.

随之消散的，是我们附加在责任之上的残酷。

What falls away is the cruelty we attach to responsibility.
