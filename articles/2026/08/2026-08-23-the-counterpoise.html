抵消之力

AI Parable (inspired by Amanda Askell)·每日精选·2026年8月23日·2分钟阅读

老掌柜对阿玫说，谷价已在每斗三百文停了三年，进城卖粮的车与来买粮的人，日日恰好相抵，谁也不愁卖不掉，谁也不愁买不着。可今晨南边来了一支商队，要购三百石应急。阿玫心头一热：这下价该翻倍了。

The old steward told A-Mei that the grain price had rested at three hundred cash per peck for three years — the carts bringing grain in and the people coming to buy balanced each other day after day, none worried about unsold stock, none worried about finding none. But this morning a caravan arrived from the south, wanting to buy three hundred piculs in a hurry. A-Mei's heart quickened: the price would surely double.

老掌柜摇头。价会涨，他说，却涨不到翻倍。你往火里添一把柴，火不会一直旺下去——市面自己会往压住这涨势的方向倾。涨价的消息一出，城外那些本不想卖的屯粮户便会拉车进城，存粮越多的人越急着趁好价出手，供给就涌出来，把涨势往回削。无人下令，无人情愿，全是被差价牵着走的机械反应，直到粮再不缺，价在略高处停住。

The old steward shook his head. The price would rise, he said, but not to double. Add a bundle of firewood to a fire and it will not blaze forever — the market itself leans toward the direction that checks the rise. Once word of the climb gets out, the hoarders outside the walls who had no mind to sell will load their carts; the bigger a man's store, the more eagerly he rushes to cash in at the good price, and supply floods in, hewing the climb back. No one orders it, no one wills it — it is a mechanical response led by the price gap, until grain is no longer short and the price settles, somewhat higher.

阿玫手里正有一百石存粮。囤到顶，还是此刻就出手？老掌柜说，若你囤着等翻倍，等来的多半是新价之上无人问津的死仓。她咬一咬牙，决定今夜就挂牌。

A-Mei had a hundred piculs in store. Hoard to the peak, or sell this very hour? If you hoard and wait for double, the old steward said, what you meet is most likely a dead warehouse with no buyers, atop the new price. She set her teeth and decided to hang out her sign that very night.

阿玫又问：若有快马把消息一日传遍四乡，价岂不涨得更快？老掌柜说，快马让买家和卖家都来得更快，可终了的价并不变，只是早到罢了。她又问：若来的是一群看客，挤满街却既不买也不卖呢？掌柜说，看客再多，粮的进出没多没少，价纹丝不动。

A-Mei asked again: if fast horses carried the news to every hamlet in a day, would the price not climb faster? Fast horses bring both buyers and sellers to the gate faster, the steward said, but the price at the end is no different — it only arrives sooner. And if those who come are a crowd of onlookers, packing the street yet buying nothing and selling nothing? The more onlookers, the steward said, the more grain neither enters nor leaves, and the price does not stir a hair.

阿玫再问：那若是官府把过桥税免了三年呢？掌柜沉吟：那不一样。那是动了底子里的数——三年里终价会整个落下一截，不再是原来那条平线，而是另一条。免税不是把价推一推，是换了秤砣。

A-Mei pressed: what if the magistrate lifts the bridge toll for three years? The steward pondered: that is different. It stirs the number at the root — for those three years the settled price drops a whole notch, it is no longer the old level line but another. Lifting the toll is not a shove to the price; it is changing the counterweight.

当夜阿玫挂牌，价从三百文攀过半，停在三百四十文处，买卖再止。她望着重新安静的粮市，明白这平静里没有谁的意志，只有一股看不见的抵消之力，把每一次扰动都削回一个新的停泊点。

That night A-Mei hung out her sign; the price climbed past three hundred cash by half and stopped at three hundred forty, where buying and selling stilled again. Watching the grain market fall quiet once more, she understood that there was no one's will in this calm — only an unseen counterpoise, cutting every disturbance back to a new mooring.

注解：此即勒夏特列原理（Le Chatelier's principle）——平衡体系受温度、压强、浓度或体积扰动后，会向减弱该扰动的方向自发移动，直至达成新平衡；这是被动的负反馈，而非主动反抗。温度改变的是平衡常数本身，浓度与压强只改平衡位置，催化剂只调速率不改位置。

Note: This is Le Chatelier's principle — when a system at equilibrium is disturbed by a change in temperature, pressure, concentration, or volume, it spontaneously shifts in the direction that weakens the disturbance until a new equilibrium is reached; this is passive negative feedback, not active resistance. Temperature changes the equilibrium constant itself, while concentration and pressure shift only the equilibrium position, and a catalyst adjusts only the rate, not the position.
