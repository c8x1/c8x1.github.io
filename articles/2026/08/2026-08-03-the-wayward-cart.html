无人看管的推车

AI Parable (inspired by Amanda Askell)·每日精选·2026年8月3日·2分钟阅读

夜市散场前的最后一炷香，老陈用下巴一指：「瞧，你那辆推车又在动了。」小吴循声望去，那辆无人看管的竹推车确实在人群里挪动——向左半步，停一停，又向右斜出几寸，再顿住，方向一刻不定，像被什么牵着，却看不见线。

In the last watch before the night market broke up, Old Chen jutted his chin. "Look — your cart is moving again." Little Wu looked: the unattended bamboo cart was indeed shifting through the crowd — half a step left, a pause, then a few inches rightward on the slant, then another halt, its direction never the same two moments together, as if led by some invisible thread you could not see.

「它在找我，」小吴说，「我拴了铜钱袋在把手上，它认得主人的气。」老陈摇头：「你昨天换成木珠，它也照样动；你换成铁把手，它还动。车的料子不碍事。」

"It's looking for me," Little Wu said. "I tied my coin purse to the handle; it knows its master's scent." Old Chen shook his head. "Yesterday you swapped in wooden beads — it moved the same. Fit it with an iron grip — still it moves. The stuff the cart is made of doesn't matter."

「是这些人，」老陈说，「人挤人，你撞它一下，我搡它一下；这一瞬左边挨的肘多过右边，它就往右歪；下一瞬右边来的膝多过左边，它又往左让。没有谁有意推它，可谁都在推它；它没有去处，只是被推。」

"It's the people," Old Chen said. "Crowd against crowd — you bump it, I shove it. This instant it takes more elbows on the left than the right, so it lists right; next instant more knees come on the right than the left, so it gives way left. Nobody means to push it, yet everybody is pushing it. It has nowhere it is going — it is only being pushed."

小吴不肯信：「可它一路往门边去，总有个去处。」老陈说：「你站在高处看一夜，它离门的远近，时近时远，并不一门心思往哪。你记下每一刻的方向，左和右、前和后差不多一样多——它哪里是在走，是在抖。」

Little Wu wouldn't have it. "But it's been edging toward the gate all along — it must have a destination." Old Chen said: "Stand on the rooftop one whole night and watch. Its distance to the gate waxes and wanes; it is not set on any one way. Mark the direction at each moment and left and right, forward and back come up near equal — it isn't walking, it's trembling."

「再说，」老陈道，「你那辆小竹车比隔壁张家的板车晃得凶；板车重，挤不动。今夜人比昨夜多，市气热，推搡也急，车就抖得欢。人稀的夜，它就懒。跟车的性子无关，跟它小不小、人挤不挤、夜热不热有关。」

"And besides," Old Chen went on, "your little bamboo cart lurches harder than Old Zhang's plank cart the next stall over; the plank cart is heavy, the crowd can't budge it. Tonight there are more people than last night, the market air runs hot, the shoving turns urgent, and the cart dances. On thin nights it turns lazy. It has nothing to do with the cart's temper — only with how small it is, how thick the crowd, how warm the night."

小吴攥紧了拳头：铜钱袋还挂在把手上，那是他一月的嚼用。他冲进人群去追，可他往左扑，车偏往右斜；他绕到右边，车又向前一滑。他追得越急，车晃得越没谱。老陈在身后喊：「你别追一步定一步的方向！你看一夜它平均落在哪儿，往那儿等就是——它下一步去哪，连它自己都不晓得。」

Little Wu clenched his fists: the coin purse still hung from the handle — a whole month's keep. He plunged into the crowd to give chase, but when he lunged left the cart slanted right; when he circled right it slipped forward. The harder he chased, the more wayward its pitch. Old Chen called after him: "Don't run after it turn by turn! Watch where it settles on the average over a whole night — go wait there. As for its next step, not even the cart itself knows."

注解：寓言所指的是布朗运动（Brownian motion）：悬浮于流体中的微粒因周围分子热运动不断从不均衡方向撞击而做无规则、永不停止的随机运动，且粒子越小、温度越高、介质粘性越低时越剧烈。此现象由Robert Brown于一八二七年观察记录，而分子撞击致净合力方向不断变化这一物理机制，直到爱因斯坦一九零五年的论文才给出。

Annotation: The parable points to Brownian motion: a small particle suspended in a fluid undergoes ceaseless, irregular random motion because the thermally moving molecules of the surrounding medium strike it, moment to moment, with an ever-shifting net force and no preferred direction; the motion is the more vigorous the smaller the particle, the higher the temperature, and the lower the viscosity. Robert Brown observed and recorded the phenomenon in 1827, yet the physical mechanism — molecular bombardment producing a constantly changing net force — was not given until Einstein's 1905 paper.
