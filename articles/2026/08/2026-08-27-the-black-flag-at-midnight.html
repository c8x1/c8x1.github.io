午夜的黑旗

AI Parable (inspired by Amanda Askell)·每日精选·2026年8月27日·2分钟阅读

天刚亮，药翻过了悬崖，崖下的村子挺过了那场热病。港口的人至今还在说，姜衡（Jiang Héng）船长那夜运气好——三条航道开着，两条被风暴锁住，他偏偏走到了唯一平静的水面上。每次听见这话他都摇头。他说那不是运气，也不是海发了善心；是港监的诚实——被规矩拗弯了的诚实。

At first light the medicine crossed the cliff, and the village below it lived through the fever. The harbor folk still say Captain 姜衡（Jiang Héng） was lucky that night — three lanes open, two storm-bound, and he found the one calm water. He shakes his head whenever he hears it. Not luck, he says; and not the sea being kind. It was the harbor master's honesty — honesty bent inside a rule.

那夜三条航道在暗里铺开——北航道、中航道、东航道——只有一条平静，另两条翻搅着风暴。他没有当夜风势的海图，只有一个三分之一的揣测，便把船头对准了北航道。塔上，老港监裴（Pei）看着他选。

That night the three lanes had stretched before him in the dark — the Northern, the Middle, the Eastern — and only one lay still, while two churned with storm. He had no chart of the night's winds, only a third of a guess, and he pointed his bow at the Northern lane. From the tower the old harbor master, 裴（Pei）, watched him choose.

裴在每条航道上都布了斥候；哪条平静、哪条在溃散，他一清二楚。而港口最老的规矩把他拴在三件事上，件件不可破：他必须在船长没有选的那两条里升起黑旗；那旗必须标的是风暴航道，绝不能是平静的；然后他必须从塔上喊下来，把剩下那条航道交给船长再选一次。三件里漏掉任何一件，整桩事就垮作无物。

裴 had scouts on every lane; he knew exactly which was calm and which was breaking. And the port's oldest law bound him to three things, each one unbreakable: he must raise the black flag on a lane the captain had not chosen; that flag must mark a storm lane, never the calm; and he must then call down and offer the captain the remaining lane. Leave out any one of the three and the whole thing turns to nothing.

黑旗在东航道上升起——风暴。裴的声音顺着风落下来：「北航道是你的，中航道也给你，再选一次。」姜衡头一个念头是那执拗又老实的一个：就剩两条航道了，一平一暴——各一半，何必松开手已经攥着的那根缆绳？

The black flag went up over the Eastern lane — storm. And 裴's voice came down the wind: "The Northern is yours, or the Middle. Choose again." 姜衡's first thought was the stubborn, honest one: two lanes left, one calm, one storm — even odds, so why leave the rope his hand was already on?

然后他看明白了，这一看把他整个人翻了个面。裴升那面旗，不是掷骰子掷出来的。裴是知道的。要是平静的那条其实是东航道，规矩根本不许他往上面插旗——所以那面旗关于他自己的北航道，什么新消息也没带来：还是最初那个三分之一的揣测，一分没动。可他没选的那两条航道，原本合起来揣着三分之二的平静；如今其中一条被一笔划掉，那整个三分之二的分量，全压到了唯一还立着的那条上。中航道。

Then he saw it, and the seeing undid him. 裴 had not raised that flag by the throw of a die. 裴 knew. Had the calm been on the Eastern lane, the law would never have let him flag it — so the flag told 姜衡 nothing new about his own Northern lane: still the same one-in-three guess it had always been. But the two lanes he had not chosen had carried, between them, two chances in three of holding the calm; and now one of the two was struck out, and all that weight — the whole two thirds — fell onto the single lane still standing. The Middle.

他松开北航道的缆绳，把船头压上中航道。中航道一路平得像镜面。崖下药一靠岸，村子活了。如今谁来问他，他都这样说：那夜海从没改过主意。改了的，是那三分之二的分量落在了哪儿——而听港监那被拗弯的诚实、胜过听自己执拗头一猜的船长，三个夜里会有两个回到家。

He loosed the Northern rope and put the bow onto the Middle lane. The Middle lay like glass all the way across. Below the cliffs the medicine landed, and the village lived. He tells whoever asks him now: the sea never changed its mind that night. What changed was where the weight had gone — and the captain who heeds the harbor master's bent honesty, instead of his own stubborn first guess, will on two nights in three come home.

注解：此处揭示的是蒙提霍尔问题（the Monty Hall problem）：三选一中只有一个好选项，知情且受规则约束的守门人必揭示一个坏选项，于是最初落在两个未选项上合计三分之二的胜率，被压缩到唯一剩下的那个上；换则三分之二赢，不换只三分之一。

注解：This is the Monty Hall problem: among three choices with only one good, a gatekeeper who knows the truth and is bound by rules must reveal one bad option, so the two-thirds chance first resting on the two unchosen options collapses onto the single remaining one; switching wins two times in three, staying wins only one.
