稽查员的名册

AI Parable (inspired by Amanda Askell)·每日精选·2026年8月7日·2分钟阅读

我在这座城邦做了十二年的稽查员（Scrutineer）。今年开春，长老院将一项新的职务授与我，并令我当众起誓，誓词只有一句：凡不自审的行会，稽查员须将其登入名册，并亲往稽查之。我按着宪章（charter）发了誓，以为这不过又是一桩辛苦却分明的差事。

I have served this city for twelve years as its Scrutineer. This spring the Council of Elders conferred on me a new charge and made me swear an oath in public; the oath was a single sentence: every guild that does not scrutinize itself, the Scrutineer shall enter onto the roll and go in person to scrutinize it. I swore to the charter, supposing it was but another exhausting yet clear-cut duty.

头三个月，活计确如我所料。铁匠行会自设账房，年终自查其出入，既已自审，便不入名册。陶工行会从不查账，我便登入名册，连夜去核其账目。一进一退，判然分明，我渐渐以为这宪章天衣无缝。

For the first three months the work was as I expected. The blacksmiths' guild keeps its own counting-house and audits its books at year's end; since it scrutinizes itself, it is not entered onto the roll. The potters' guild never audits its books, so I enter it onto the roll and go overnight to examine their accounts. Each case resolved cleanly, and I began to believe the charter was seamless.

到了第四个月，名册将成，长老院的首席长老在堂上当众问我：「稽查员这一行会，本身亦是城邦所设的行会之一。它是否登入名册？」满堂寂静。我原以为此问可有简捷的答复，张口却发不出声。

In the fourth month, the roll nearing completion, the chief elder asked me in open council: "The guild of Scrutineers is itself one of the city's guilds. Is it entered onto the roll?" The hall fell silent. I had thought this question would have a ready answer, but I opened my mouth and no word came.

我回到衙署，独坐灯下推演。若稽查员行会自审其账，则它已自审，依宪章不当登入名册，于是我不该稽查它——可一旦我不稽查它，它便不曾自审，依宪章又当登入名册，我必须亲往稽查。若稽查员行会不自审，则它当登入名册，我须稽查之——可一旦我稽查它，它便已自审，依宪章又不该登入名册。无论我起行或不起行，宪章都立刻授我以相反的命令。

I returned to my office and sat alone under the lamp to reason it out. If the guild of Scrutineers audits its own books, then it has scrutinized itself, and by the charter must not be entered onto the roll, so I should not scrutinize it—but once I do not scrutinize it, it has not scrutinized itself, and by the charter must be entered onto the roll, so I must go and scrutinize it. If the guild of Scrutineers does not audit its own books, then it must be entered onto the roll and I must scrutinize it—but once I scrutinize it, it has thereby scrutinized itself, and by the charter must not be on the roll. Whether I set out or not, the charter at once delivers me a contrary command.

我次日上堂，向长老院坦言：我对这宪章所发的誓，至这一条便无可履行。并非我怯懒推诿，也并非哪一方的行会有意欺瞒，而是这规则本身在一处无可回避的转折处折断——它要稽查所有不自审者，可一旦它把目光转向设立它自己的那个行会，无论「是」与「否」都令它自相矛盾。我所能做的，只有如实禀报这裂痕；至于缝补，则超出稽查员之职。

The next day I went before the council and told them plainly: the oath I swore to this charter cannot be fulfilled at this one point. It is not that I shrink from the labour, nor that any guild is willfully deceiving me, but that the rule itself breaks at a turn it cannot avoid—it would scrutinize all those who do not scrutinize themselves, yet the moment it turns its gaze upon the very guild that establishes it, both "yes" and "no" make it contradict itself. All I can do is report this crack truthfully; to mend it lies beyond a Scrutineer's office.

注解：此即罗素悖论（Russell's paradox）——若以一体系收录所有「不收录自身者」，则该体系对自身是否收录的判定便陷入「收录当且仅当不收录」的僵局，矛盾不可消解。

Note: this is Russell's paradox—if a system collects all "those that do not collect themselves," then its judgment of whether to collect itself falls into the deadlock "collected if and only if not collected," an irresolvable contradiction.
