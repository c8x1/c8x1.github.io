一字不差

AI Parable (inspired by Amanda Askell)·每日精选·2026年11月6日·2分钟阅读

秋汛头一个清早，天没亮透，老魏站在跳板上点人头。「七条船，人到六个，双喜呢？」舱里没人应。「没到的，记照旧。」桂生往板子上划了一道。我的船是赊来的，月底到期，就指着这一汛。老魏朝我们一排人抬下巴：「开始。昨夜邱掌柜上了谁的船，讲了什么话，一字不差，讲出来。」

The first morning of the autumn run, before light broke, Old Wei stood on the gangplank counting heads. "Seven boats, six men. Where's Shuangxi?" No answer from his cabin. "Not here, so mark him old terms." Guisheng scratched a line on the tally board. My boat was bought on credit, payment due at month's end, and this run was my whole chance. Old Wei lifted his chin at our line. "Round starts now. Whose boat did Broker Qiu board in the night? Whatever he said, say it word for word."

桂生先开口：「他站在我舱口讲：『冰紧，今儿只收半数，价照旧。』」石头跟着说：「一个字不差，跟我讲的也是这句。」顺子点头：「一样。」三张嘴，一句话。没人上过老魏的船，板上记「照旧」；双喜那一格空着，底下也是「照旧」。老疤咳了一声：「邱掌柜跟我说，今儿价压到两块，叫我给大家捎个信儿，早卖早落袋。」

Guisheng went first. "He stood at my hatch and said: ice is short, I take half the catch today, price as always." Shitou followed. "Not one word different. Same sentence to me." Shunzi nodded. "Same to me." Three mouths, one sentence. Nobody had come to Old Wei, so the board said old terms; under Shuangxi's blank space, the same. Lao Ba coughed. "Broker Qiu told me the price drops to two a basket today. He asked me to pass it on. Sell early, sell fast."

老魏没去拿粉笔：「再过一遍。老疤昨儿夜里上过谁的船，讲了什么，照实说。」桂生说：「他跟我讲，邱掌柜只收大船的鱼，小船一条不收。」石头说：「他跟我讲的是邱掌柜今儿不来，价没定，谁先卖谁占先。」同一个人的话，从老疤嘴里出来成了三样。老疤脖子红了：「你们耳朵背。」老魏把粉笔往板上一横：「板子只管数话。咱们七个，烂得起两个；烂到第三个，这板就瞎了。」老疤把烟摁灭，没再言语。

Old Wei didn't reach for the chalk. "One more round. Lao Ba came by your boats in the night. What did he say?" Guisheng: "He told me Broker Qiu takes only big boats, none of the small." Shitou: "He told me the broker wasn't coming today, price unset, first to sell wins." One man's word had come out of Lao Ba's mouth three different ways. His neck reddened. "You two are deaf." Old Wei laid the chalk flat. "The board counts words, that's all it does. Seven of us can carry two rotten men. A third one blinds it." Lao Ba stubbed out his cigarette and said nothing more.

该我了。那句私话还在我耳朵里发烫：双倍价，只收我一家的鱼，别声张。说一句「他没来」，今夜单出，月底的船钱就凑齐了；照实讲，他往后怕是一斤也不收我的鱼。剩下五双眼睛都盯着板子。我听见自己的声音出了口：「他上了我的船。他讲：双倍价，只收我一家的鱼，别声张。」

Then me. The private sentence was still warm in my ear: double the price, my fish only, tell no one. Say "he never came," and I could sail by myself tonight, and the month's money would be whole. Say it straight, and he might never take another basket of mine. Five pairs of eyes stayed on the tally board. I heard my own voice carry over the water. "He came to my boat. He said: double the price, my fish only, tell no one."

没人问我声音抖什么。老魏落了一行字：六船同出，只卖半数，一家一份，谁也不单卖；双喜的船不动，记他的「照旧」。解缆的时辰，老疤的船摸黑溜出队伍，一个人往远处码头去了。邱掌柜的人站在堤上喊：「六斤，你的鱼，往后一斤不收！」桂生头也不回：「收不收随你，他的鱼今儿装在五家舱里。」

Nobody asked why my voice shook. Old Wei chalked one line: six boats out together, sell only half the catch, one share to each boat, nobody sells apart; Shuangxi's boat stayed tied to its post, marked at his own old terms. As we cast off, Lao Ba's boat slid out of the line in the dark and made for the far dock, alone. A man from the ice house shouted from the bank: "Liu Jin! Not one basket of yours, ever again!" Guisheng didn't turn his head. "Buy or don't. His fish rides in five holds today."

日头上来，五条船一齐靠上冰行。邱掌柜盯着排成一排的鱼筐看了半晌，报了老价。桂生把秤递过去：「半数，一家一份。」听说老疤那一船鱼，在远处码头一筐只落得一块二，连冰钱都没够。回程上，老魏拍了拍我的船帮：「你的筐在五家舱里都待过，月底的钱，齐了。」我把那块写满话的板子平放在船头：夜里讲过什么，天亮都会摆到一处数。

The sun was up when the five boats tied up at the ice house, all together. Broker Qiu looked down the row of baskets for a long moment and named the old price. Guisheng handed him the scale. "Half the catch, one share each." Word came back that Lao Ba's boatload, at the far dock, fetched one-twenty a basket — not enough to cover the ice. On the way back, Old Wei tapped my gunwale. "Your baskets rode in all five holds, so your month's money came out whole." I laid the tally board flat in the bow. Whatever is said in the dark, the counting waits for daylight.

注解：这就是拜占庭容错（the Byzantine fault tolerance）：每条船把夜里听来的话一字不差当众复述、互相核对，诚实多数的相同证词压倒少数的矛盾话，缺席的按「照旧」记票；只要作乱的人少于三分之一，船队总能拢成同一个决定。它担保大家落在同一句话上，不担保那句话本身为真：对所有人咬同一个价的谎，对质也认不出。

Note: This is Byzantine fault tolerance: each boat repeats word for word what reached it in the night, the matching testimony of the honest majority outvotes the contradictory few, whoever stays away is counted at the old terms, and with fewer than a third of them rotten the fleet still lands on one decision. It guarantees that everyone ends up on the same sentence, not that the sentence is true: a liar who tells every man the same false price passes the count.
