让-巴蒂斯特·拉马克的自我塑造世界

Jessica Riskin·Aeon·2026年11月3日·15分钟阅读·原文

法国博物学家让-巴蒂斯特·拉马克（Jean-Baptiste Lamarck）的鸿篇论著《动物哲学》（Philosophie zoologique）于1809年开始出现在巴黎的书店里，那年他65岁。这部书标志着现代进化生物学的奠基时刻，同时也是另一个观念的奠基时刻——该观念在当时是激进的，如今依然如此：生命体创造着它们自身及其世界。拉马克的关键术语是「faculté de former des projets」——「制定计划的官能」。在序言里，他向读者许诺要对生命体给出一种自然主义的论述，赋予它们的无非是一种「应激性」（irritability），一种早已被确认为一切动植物组织所共有的属性。然而话音未落，他又把这项卑微的属性称作「生命体不断完善的原因」。一种「自然乃自我塑造」的观念，从一开头便已初露端倪。

The French naturalist Jean-Baptiste Lamarck was 65 when his great treatise on the transformation of life was published. With Philosophie zoologique (1809), translated a century later as Zoological Philosophy, Lamarck intended to establish an entirely new science, one built on the idea that all living beings are constantly transforming themselves, from the tiniest microbes to the most complex mammals, gaining new forms and new abilities. It wasn’t the bodily structures of organisms that determined their ways of life, Lamarck said, but just the opposite: their ways of life in response to their circumstances gave rise to their changing bodily forms. ‘Can there be any consideration in natural history more important,’ he demanded, ‘or deserving of greater attention, than the one I have just set forth?’ (My translation.)

但如果你对拉马克有过任何耳闻，那多半是他错得多么离谱。他的厄运几乎立刻就开始了。生前，他被讥讽、被排挤；身后，他被弄成了笑柄。他的名字成了谬误的同义词：「拉马克主义」（Lamarckism）意即相信不真实的东西。这个广为流传、在课堂上一讲再讲的落败故事，大致是这样的：这位可怜的博物学家实施一项以长颈鹿为对象的实验计划，认定长颈鹿的脖子之所以长，是因为它们一代又一代地向上伸脖、竭力去够高树上的叶子，从而获得了更长的脖子，再传给后代。任何学童都看得出破绽：铁匠的粗胳膊从来没让他的娃娃生来胳膊粗。在这个标准故事里，查尔斯·达尔文（Charles Darwin）后来带着真正的机制——自然选择——登场，进化论得救了。可怜的拉马克！错的总是他，哪儿都是他，什么都是他。

But if you’ve heard anything at all about Lamarck, it’s probably how wrong he was about one animal in particular, the giraffe, and how this giraffe fiasco epitomises his wrongness about everything. Lamarck suggested that, by stretching to reach leaves in high tree branches, giraffes slightly elongated their necks, creating tiny changes that were then handed down to their offspring, accumulating over many generations to produce the long necks we see today. Around the turn of the 20th century, Lamarck’s giraffe idea became a laughing stock when biologists roundly rejected the notion that bodily changes undergone in the course of an individual lifetime could be inherited in the next generation.

受到攻击的不只是他关于长脖子哺乳动物的观点。拉马克本人——一个穷寡妇的儿子，第11个孩子，没有遗产，几乎没有庇护人——也是个现成的靶子。他晚年穷困潦倒，葬在一处租来的墓穴里；死后五年，遗骨被掘出，散落进多邦通路（rue Daubenton）地下一处白骨坑里。他的身体所受的待遇，正与他的学说相当。大名鼎鼎的古生物学家、拉马克最有权势的敌人乔治·居维叶（Georges Cuvier），在一篇悼词里嘲弄拉马克的学问——那篇悼词处处都不光彩，是一场算总账，而且是等对象死了以后才发出来的。

It wasn’t only his ideas about long-necked mammals that came under attack. Lamarck’s interest in giraffes was just a passing mention in the midst of his voluminous writings, which set out a revolutionary, field-founding theory: organisms, he claimed, transform themselves and, in doing so, they remake the world. That idea has drawn scorn for more than two centuries.

拉马克虽常被人忽视，他的学问却是现代生物学的根基。「生物学」（biology）一词由他创造（与之竞争的戈特弗里德·莱因霍尔德·特雷维拉努斯（Gottfried Reinhold Treviranus）也独立造出了这个词）。「无脊椎动物」（invertebrates）一词也是他的，他还把这个类群变成了自然史的核心领域。他先于达尔文提出物种会随时间转变的思想——一套先于达尔文的进化理论。在他论无脊椎动物的著作里，有一段引人瞩目的公开自我批评，堪称科学精神的典范：他回顾自己在1760年代还抱着物种乃固定种类的旧观念时，如何驳斥了那些博物学家的证词——他们报告说，曾经属于海洋的贝壳在旱地上被发现了。如今他明白了，那证词是可靠的，而他，那个科学家，把证词打发掉才是错的。这不是最后一次：可靠的证词即将被对证据的一种糟糕诠释所淹没。

Though Lamarck is often overlooked, his science was foundational to modern biology and informed the theory of evolution that Charles Darwin later developed. Darwin followed Lamarck in believing in the agency of living beings to transform themselves and their environment. But most people don’t know about Lamarck’s influence on Darwin because, around the turn of the 20th century, Darwin’s followers expurgated every trace of Lamarckism from their neo-Darwinist version of evolution, rendering living beings the objects of random variation and outside forces. For the neo-Darwinists, living things played no direct role in the evolutionary process. Evolution happened through random genetic variation and external environmental pressures. For Darwin’s followers, organisms were just as passive as they had been for the creationists, who saw them as products of divine will.

纵观现代生物学的历史，科学界与政界最有权势的人物大多不相信生命体是能动者。有机体是主动的、有所作为的这一想法，遭遇的向来是猜忌，甚至讥笑。让有机体保持被动、把它们化约为外力作用的客体，曾是一件要紧事。对达尔文最重要的修正——也就是与本文攸关的那一次——出现在1880至90年代，与奥古斯特·魏斯曼（August Weismann）和新达尔文主义者一同到来：把有机体一生中的经验、它们的活动与境遇，从遗传的运行机制中排除出去。在新的标准图景里，生殖细胞携带着遗传物质，遗传物质指挥身体的发育，而没有任何东西反着流回去。有机体是一只容器，用魏斯曼的术语说，是一个「不透明的」中介：一条死胡同。

Throughout the history of modern biology, most powerful figures in science and politics have ridiculed and repressed Lamarck’s radical idea that organisms have agency in the evolutionary process. In mainstream biology today, a neo-Darwinian view of living things as passive still largely dominates, though research in several areas has been challenging its reign.

本文要讲的，就是拉马克的激进观念如何被主流科学抛开，以及是何种思想驱动了这场放逐。在我们自己的时代，在表观遗传学、生态位构建和扩展进化综合（Extended Evolutionary Synthesis）这些正在崛起的科学里，《动物哲学》的奠基思想正在归来：生命体是能动者，是自身及其世界的创造者。而拉马克的激进主义，从另一方面说，是一种自然政治学：他反对物种固定论，反对预成论，总而言之，反对生命体的被动性。他在这套对立之上建立起来的理论绝非平等主义；那是一种以活动性为序的自然等级制。尽管如此，他在毕生自然史研究中发展出的那幅主动的、制造世界的有机体图景，与如今正从世界各地的实验室和野外站里浮现出来的图景，接近得令人吃惊。

This is the story of how Lamarck’s radical idea was cast aside by mainstream science, and why it was overlooked for so long. It is also the story of a surprising reversal: in recent decades, some biologists have begun turning back toward Lamarck’s view of life.

许多人听到拉马克的名字会想到长颈鹿，却很少有人知道，他科学生涯的大部分时间根本不是花在脊椎动物上，而是花在无脊椎动物上。而且，尽管他以获得性性状遗传的进化论留在人们的记忆里，他的进化机制其实远比这更离奇，也更有趣：那是一种朝向复杂与完善的普遍的、内在的驱力，而获得性性状的遗传在其中扮演的是严格意义上的次要与辅助角色。他把进化设想成不是一个过程，而是两个过程叠成的序列：一层是那股内在深层力量推动的缓慢而普遍的上升，其上覆着一层有机体对各自境遇的快速、局部的适应。长颈鹿的脖子属于第二层；普遍上升解释的是第一层。

While many people think of giraffes when they hear Lamarck’s name, few know that he coined the word ‘biology’, in 1802. He needed a name to designate a new science he thought should exist: a science of all living beings, and only living beings. At the time, there was no such field. There were broader sciences, such as natural history, which included inanimate things like rocks, rivers and mountains; and there were narrower sciences, such as botany or zoology, which covered subsets of the living world. But Lamarck thought there should be a science of life. At the heart of his new science, he developed the first theory of what we now call ‘evolution’, although he didn’t call it that. He simply referred to ‘change’.

从1788年起，拉马克在巴黎的植物园（Jardin des plantes）里生活和工作，先是当植物学家，1793年起担任国家自然历史博物馆的「昆虫与蠕虫」教授——这座博物馆是革命政府从旧皇家植物园里划设出来的。据各方说法，他不是个讨人喜欢的人：少年时的天花在他脸上留了疤，为人傲慢僵硬，时而小气，时而敏感易怒。他爱说话，嗓门大，一说起来就没完。他有过五段婚姻（或事实婚姻），七个孩子，靠一份微薄薪水在一套小公寓里把他们养大。他的日常围绕着讲课与示教、博物馆藏品中的贝壳昆虫蠕虫、一部接一部写来贴补家用的植物学书籍，以及书房里那台显微镜。1794年，他开设了第一门无脊椎动物学课程；他50岁，对自己的新科目所知甚少。他的第一步棋很聪明：把无知变成一个纲领——先研究最简单的动物，「从大自然开始生产动物的地方开始研究动物」。

Lamarck lived and worked in the Jardin des plantes (Garden of Plants) in Paris from 1788 until his death, aged 85, in 1829. His life was tumultuous, to say the least. He lived through the French Revolution, then under Napoleon, then during the Bourbon Restoration. He survived the Reign of Terror, as well as the withering disdain of Napoleon and his circle of scientific advisors, and the return of the monarchy; he also outlived three wives and four of his eight children. In the last decade of his life, he was blind and frail but carried on working with the help of his daughter Rosalie. At the end of his long career, he had identified and named more than 6,000 species, and for more than two decades had taught a legendary course in the Garden of Plants, drawing students from all over the world. The course grew so famous that even fictional characters attended it, such as the lovelorn young Amaury, hero of Sainte-Beuve’s autobiographical novel Volupté: The Sensual Man (1834).

拉马克胆敢僭夺上帝对创造的垄断，把它重新授予凡俗生灵，他的自然史由此推翻了特创论的科学——那套学说自亚里士多德（Aristotle）以来，便以这样那样的版本居于统治地位。然而，神的垄断并不是一个可以被聪明实验推翻的科学假说。它是一种政治神学，一根社会秩序的支柱；在18世纪末的法国，推翻它所需要的不只是一套新理论，而是一场政治革命。1789年旧制度的倾覆，把整套自然史体系也一并卷了下去。曾在国王治下做植物学家的拉马克，成了共和国治下的教授，而生命科学本身也照共和的路线被重铸：一个没有君主的自然，原则上向所有人敞开。原则是一回事，实践是另一回事：拉马克整个职业生涯都在抗议新博物馆的公共展厅把工人阶级拒之门外，却始终徒劳。

By daring to usurp God’s monopoly on Creation and reassign it to mortal beings, Lamarck provoked the ire of the mighty and powerful. He wrote that life originated in spontaneous generation, brought about by natural forces such as electricity and heat acting upon inanimate matter. This led to the emergence of the simplest organisms: single-celled protozoa and algae. From that point, a property intrinsic to living matter exerted itself, a tendency to complexify, which Lamarck named ‘the power of life’. As organisms grew more complex, another cause of transformation came into play: their responsiveness to the environment. In the simplest beings, this responsiveness was rudimentary – an irritability or sensitivity, and reflexive reaction. But in birds and mammals, Lamarck argued, it rose to the level of ‘acts of will’, through which creatures could form ‘habits’ and ‘ways of life’ in response to their circumstances. By means of these behaviours, they directed their inner fluids toward the limbs and organs they used most. (Darwin would later propose a similar idea. In 1868, he suggested that parts of the body threw off particles of inheritance, called ‘gemmules’, which were modified by an animal’s behaviours and could carry the modifications into its offspring.)

按照拉马克的说法，生命体不只是创造和转化自己，还创造和转化它们的世界。它们的活动重塑环境，被重塑的环境又反过来在世代之间作用于它们，把它们进一步改变。一个地方，可能就是栖居其中的生物们的作品。河狸的池塘是河狸的作品；燕子的巢是燕子的作品。尽管池塘活得比任何一只河狸都久，巢也活得比任何一只燕子都久，可筑塘这件事随着池塘的生长也塑造了河狸，筑巢也塑造了燕子。每一件作品，同时也是对其制作者的加工。

Not only did living beings create and transform themselves, according to Lamarck’s picture of things, they also made and re-made the inanimate world. Plants played the primary role in this process, he thought, because they could invent themselves out of just sunlight, air and water. He described experiments to confirm this miraculous capacity. For instance, carve a hollow into the side of a turnip and suspend the vegetable in a window by its root, Houdini-style: nothing up its sleeves! Keep the hollow filled with water, and you’ll see that the turnip will grow a whole flowering plant. It has created itself literally from thin air. Plants, by bringing together the primitive elements into combinations, originated the cosmic relay chain, then handed off their work to animals. By eating, digesting and engaging in their other functions and behaviours, animals produced more elaborate compounds, and so they shared with plants the defining ability of all living beings to build, compose and create.

拉马克以植物学起家，1793年却不得不硬着头皮转任昆虫与蠕虫的教席。他把这场不幸改造成了一项庞大的探索计划，相当于自然史版本的伟大远航：从最简单的生物开始，自下而上绘制动物王国的版图。1800年他对学生们说，无脊椎动物「不知不觉地把我们引向动物阶梯的起点」。自然的「阶梯」本身就是个古已有之的观念，但拉马克的版本崭新而奇异。古典的「自然阶梯」（scala naturae）是一架按完善程度排序的静态形式之梯，他的阶梯却会动，而且它的运动是自我产生的。对拉马克来说，生命是一场没有剧本的即兴，在深时之中展开。传统自然神学要到设计者的蓝图里去寻找的那个「敏感原则」，他发现就分布在活着的物质本身之中。

Having begun his career in botany, in 1793 Lamarck was obliged to move, reluctantly, into zoology. This was in the midst of the Revolution, during the transitional moment when the main botanical garden in Paris – the King’s Garden – became the republican Garden of Plants under the auspices of a new National Museum of Natural History. The committee in charge of this transformation did not assign Lamarck to a chair in botany as he wanted, but instead to the lowliest chair in zoology: the Professorship of Insects and Worms. He would later tell his students that he’d been, at first, disappointed: if he couldn’t carry on studying botany, then why not charismatic megafauna, like lions? But then he’d had two hopeful thoughts. First, his assigned creatures constituted nine-tenths of the animal world, so he hadn’t been relegated to an unimportant corner of zoology. And, second, he imagined the simplest animals might reveal to him the foundation of animal life. Contemplating his insects and his worms with this faint gleam of hope, he created a new taxonomic category, ‘invertebrates’, and set to work studying them.

无脊椎动物成了拉马克这一观念的基础：生物体拥有自我组织的能力。1809年，在《动物哲学》里，他提出了那个著名的机制：动物同时受两种东西支配——一种朝向复杂性不断增加的普遍驱力，以及各自生活的局部境遇。驱力带来生命的普遍上升；局部境遇带来具体的适应。他写道，这些适应是引入「器官变化」的「境遇」，于是器官以新的方式得到「练习」。整个过程归根结底依赖于有机体的活动。正因为长颈鹿伸脖子，它的脖子才变长；正因为涉禽一代又一代伸展双腿，它的腿才变长。活动本身就是「境遇」。用进与废退：这些才是「原因」。

The invertebrates, he told his students in 1800, ‘conduct us insensibly toward the inconceivable limit of animalisation’, toward those beings ‘barely endowed with animality’ with whom ‘nature perhaps began’. For Lamarck, this essence of animal life was the power of generation and regeneration: the ability to produce living matter, including new bodily tissues and new organisms. The simplest animals had the most widespread generative faculties. For instance, the Genevan naturalist Abraham Trembley had shown that, if you cut up polyps (freshwater hydras) into lots of pieces, they regenerate an entire animal from each part. Other invertebrates, too, had incredible powers of generation. Insects could metamorphose. Crustaceans could grow new legs. Sea stars, new rays. Snails, a new head. The invertebrates far outnumber the vertebrates, Lamarck explained, thanks to this generative ability. Their number is unknowably vast because of the ‘infinite smallness’ of the smallest among them. These animals display a ‘frightening fecundity’, he wrote; it seems that ‘matter animalises itself from every direction.’ Lamarck marvelled at the tiny coral polyps that were identified as the chief producers of the great calcareous mountain chains and enormous banks of chalk found all over Earth. These minuscule animals create islands, fill bays and gulfs, clog ports and ‘completely transform the coastlines’.

每一个生物体那种不停歇的创造活动，绝不是独角戏。在拉马克的自然史里，生命体总是一起行动，而它们最寻常、也最强大的集体活动，就是营造共同的世界。在河流与池塘里，在森林与空气中，生物们忙忙碌碌地改变着环境，也被环境改变着。他最心爱的例子、一遍遍反复回到的例子，是社会性昆虫：蜜蜂、胡蜂、蚂蚁和白蚁，一切动物作品中最精巧之物的建造者。他为蜂巢惊叹，为蚁穴惊叹，为白蚁丘那座「令人赞叹的大都会」惊叹。而且他想，这些作品本身就是一种有机体，一个由小小建造者们造出来、反过来又造就了它们的「复合体」。

The invertebrates became the basis of Lamarck’s idea that living organisms held a monopoly on creative agency. Religious faith, Lamarck acknowledged, could be a consolation for a ‘good man who persuades himself of it’ but was ‘absolutely foreign’ to his own study of living nature. Souls were not physically knowable, whereas the dynamic, creative action of living beings was everywhere apparent. Life, to Lamarck, now became the manifest capacity to create in the face of all nature’s forces of destruction.

为了证明动物具有创造与自我转化的能力，拉马克举过许多例子，其中之一是新谷仓里筑巢的燕子。他写道，第一年，巢筑得七零八落、歪歪扭扭。第二年，还是那些鸟，因为学过了，筑得更好，位置也选对了。第三年，巢整齐而精良。燕子凭借自身的活动，改良了自己的手艺。有一年春天，他看着一对燕子重建一个被毁掉的巢。它们自己组织起来，协作重建。他惊讶地看着：一些留守工地，另一些衔着小枝条来来去去。拉马克说，燕子筑巢一般要八到十二天，但这一对勤勉得出奇，第二天一早就完工了。

The incessant creative activity of each organism was far from solitary. Living beings collaborated as they worked to form the world and fend off destruction, and Lamarck took a great interest in this collaborative action. For instance, one spring day in 1798, having brought his family to a country house he briefly owned outside Paris, he noticed a swallow’s nest in one of the windows. Shortly afterwards, a local child destroyed the nest, leaving the pair of swallows homeless just as the female was about to lay her eggs. Immediately, 10 or 12 other swallows gathered around the wreckage. The birds were so agitated and noisy that at first Lamarck assumed they were quarrelling, but he soon realised it was just the opposite. They had organised themselves to rebuild the nest collaboratively. He watched in amazement as some remained by the construction site while others came and went with twigs. Swallows generally take eight to 12 days to build their nest, Lamarck observed, but these worked so diligently that they finished it the following morning.

1809年6月，一只海豹来到自然历史博物馆的动物园，拉马克在其中看到了同一套自我塑造的原理在运转。他写道，海豹的前肢已经变成了「桨」，后肢变成了「蹼」。海豹游泳时把后肢当蹼用。不过后肢并没有变成鳍；它们仍然是腿，在陆地上用来走路。可以说，这只海豹是一件半成品：一只正在实时把自己造成游泳者、同时又从未停止做一只行走者的动物。它的身体是它自己的未竟之作，而它身体的种种作品，正是它的身体。

As evidence of animals’ power of creation and self-transformation, Lamarck cited many examples in which the increased use of a body part had apparently developed and shaped it: the webbed feet of water birds from pushing against the water; the hooked talons of eagles from clasping branches; shorebirds’ long, skinny legs and necks. Lamarck reckoned that shorebirds dislike the water yet have to wade out to catch their prey. Sinking into the mud, they stretch their legs in distaste, straining to keep their body above water, and craning their necks. This behaviour over many generations resulted in stilt-like legs and snaky necks.

拉马克不仅让亚里士多德那架古老的「自然阶梯」动了起来，还把它放进了新的维度：放进绵延，放进历史。在他的版本里，生命的阶梯是一个运动着的、自我生成的系统，一场没有作曲家的宏大即兴。自然神学看到的是一份设计好的蓝图，他看到的是无数次创造行为的累积，一直回溯到深时。有机体不是某个规划的产物；它是它自己的作品留下的沉淀。在这一图景里，生命不是设计的执行，而是制作的延续。生命的历史不是一场发育，而是一场即兴。这部深时之中自我塑造世界的自然史，才是拉马克真正留给进化生物学的遗产，尽管它很少被理解，也很少被记功。

When a seal arrived in the menagerie of the natural history museum in June 1809, Lamarck watched the animal with fascination. Its obvious kinship with land mammals confirmed his idea that all mammals, and indeed all life, had originated in the water. He noticed that the seal used its hind limbs as a paddle when swimming. Yet the legs hadn’t fully joined together to become a fin because the seal also used them for seizing prey. The distinctive combination of uses for the hind limbs had produced a structure that could work either like a paddle or like a hand.

自我塑造、营造世界的有机体这一观念，给拉马克招来了大权在握的敌人。他的克星是古生物学家居维叶，拿破仑（Napoleon）跟前的大红人。居维叶是虔诚的路德宗信徒，断然拒绝生命形态会随时间改变的想法，坚持物种固定不变。他的核心解剖学原则——器官相关原则（principle of correlation of parts）——是一条理性设计的宣言：他声称，从一个生物的任何一个部位就能推出它的全部部位，因为它们都以理性的必然相互隐含。居维叶还坚称，动物的习性与行为出自它们的构造——它们照着被设计的样子行动；拉马克则说恰恰相反：动物的构造出自它们的习性与行为。这份对固定性的执着，同样塑造了居维叶阅读化石记录的方式。

Lamarck had set Aristotle’s ancient Scale of Nature not just in motion but in a great commotion. For two millennia, since the time of Aristotle, natural philosophers had imagined every being ranked from simplest to most complex in a fixed, unchanging order. Each form of life had its appointed place in the hierarchy. But now Lamarck announced that, rather than being arrayed along the Scale of Nature, living beings were themselves ever making and remaking the order of life by their own incessant activity, and in many directions at once.

居维叶相信灭绝存在，并且在确立灭绝的现实、顶住各种渐变论替代方案的斗争中，他是位领军人物。拉马克把化石记录读成一场连续的转化，居维叶则把它读成一连串创造与灾变：一个世界被周期性地抹掉，再重新补满。拉马克干脆否认灭绝，部分是因为他无法接受：一整支世系——万古自我塑造的作品——竟就这么被一笔勾销。在这一点上，正如在他理论的总体轮廓上，拉马克的立场更宽厚，也更不正确。灾变是真的；灭绝也是真的。但他那种愿景的宽厚、它对湮灭的抵抗，或许也正是它最有先见之明的地方——因为在眼下这个大规模灭绝的时代，最需要被重新唤醒的，恰恰是它。

Lamarck’s idea of self-making and world-making organisms gained him powerful enemies. His nemesis was the palaeontologist Georges Cuvier, a favourite of Napoleon. Cuvier was a devout Lutheran who categorically rejected the idea that living forms changed over time and instead upheld the fixity of species. His central anatomical principle, the principle of correlation of parts, was an assertion of rational design: he claimed that one could infer all the parts of a creature from any single part because they all implied one another by rational necessity. Cuvier also insisted that animals’ habits and behaviours followed from their structures – they behaved as they were designed to behave – whereas Lamarck said it was just the opposite: animal structures resulted from their habits and behaviours. This same commitment to fixity shaped how Cuvier read the fossil record.

因此毫不奇怪，拿破仑厌恶那个与居维叶的科学模型作对的拉马克。这份厌恶在1809年11月一个滴水成冰的日子里暴露得淋漓尽致：国家研究院（National Institute）的院士们伫立在杜伊勒里宫（Tuileries Palace）的一间沙龙里，冻得发抖，等候皇帝前来接受他们呈献的新作。这件事我们是从天文学家弗朗索瓦·阿拉戈（François Arago）那里知道的，他当时以新院士的身份在场，年仅23岁。站在年轻阿拉戈旁边的，是65岁、身体虚弱的拉马克，手里捧着一册《动物哲学》。拿破仑终于进屋，径直朝阿拉戈走去，就像走向一名新兵，盯住他喝问：「你很年轻！叫什么名字？」阿拉戈还没来得及回答，右边那位急着表现的邻座抢着答道：「他叫阿拉戈！」拿破仑又问：「你研习哪门科学？」左边的邻座又抢着说：「他研习天文学！」阿拉戈暗自叫苦，心想皇帝准是把他当成了哑巴或傻瓜；这时皇帝转向拉马克，拉马克呈上了他的巨著。拿破仑咆哮着说，他收下这本书只是「看在你白发的份上」，一把抓过书，看也不看，塞进一名随从手里；而拉马克含着滚下脸颊的屈辱泪水，还想解释书里写的是什么。

Cuvier believed in extinctions and was a leading figure in establishing their reality. But he assumed that creatures went extinct because catastrophes, like floods and fires, eliminated them, and then those creatures were replaced by newly created ones. This idea supported the doctrine of special creation: that God had created each species for its place in nature. Cuvier’s science was perfectly suited to Napoleon’s purposes. It was a scientific doctrine of revolutionary change that was also profoundly conservative, each revolution imposed from the outside, each a return to the same ultimate authority, and the whole thing guaranteed by Napoleon’s own scientific experts rather than the Church. Cuvier’s science was cutting-edge, but at the same time, it bolstered an authoritarian order. It was emphatically not the science of the materialist, atheist revolutionaries; but nor was it the older science of the Catholic Church. Cuvier translated the authority of religion into science – a powerful modern concoction.

倘若拿破仑知道书里写的是什么，他准会更不喜欢它。因为《动物哲学》归根结底是一份自我塑造世界的宣言，一部关于共和国的自然史。在这部书里，拉马克铺展出一种自然秩序：原则上，每一个生命都是一个活动中心，是自身及其世界的创造者。他并且坚称，这种自然秩序不是任何蓝图的实现，而是一场即兴——创造者与创造行为遍布各处，在每一个尺度上，在每一个时刻。这是一幅把自然视为活动的民主、一个制造者的共和国的图景。拿破仑，那位自我造就、亲手为自己加冕的皇帝，对它毫无用处。

It’s no surprise, then, that Napoleon loathed Lamarck, who challenged Cuvier’s model of science. This hatred was dramatically apparent on a freezing day in November 1809 when the members of the National Institute stood shivering as they awaited the emperor in a salon of the Tuileries Palace to present their new works. We have the story from the astronomer François Arago, who was present as a new member of the Institute at the age of 23. Standing near young Arago was Lamarck, 65 and frail, holding a copy of his Philosophie zoologique. Napoleon at last entered the room and marched up to Arago as he would to a new conscript, fixing him with a stare and barking: ‘You’re very young! What’s your name?’ Before Arago could answer, his neighbour on the right, eager to be noticed, replied: ‘His name is Arago!’ Napoleon: ‘What science do you cultivate?’ Arago’s left-hand neighbour piped up with: ‘He cultivates astronomy!’ While Arago ruefully reflected that Napoleon must take him for a mute or an imbecile, the emperor turned to Lamarck, who presented his magnum opus. Growling that he was accepting it only ‘out of consideration for your white hair’, Napoleon snatched the book and, without looking at it, thrust it into the hands of an aide, while Lamarck, with tears of humiliation rolling down his cheeks, tried to explain what was in it.

拉马克死后，居维叶成了书写历史的胜利者——字面意义上的书写：他为死去的同僚撰写了悼词，在里面一边称许其人，一边贬抑其学。这篇悼词居高临下得如此彻底，以至于从此定义了拉马克的名声。居维叶在悼词里把拉马克的进化思想归因于脱缰于证据的想象，把他的唯物主义归因于某种老糊涂。他把通过获得性性状实现的转化、把长颈鹿的脖子嘲弄为一个「原来如此的故事」（just-so story）、一出「天方夜谭」（Arabian tale）。悼词被译成英文和德文，在拉马克死后的几十年里成了关于其著作的标准参考。居维叶版的拉马克——长颈鹿脖子的拉马克、「天方夜谭」的拉马克——从此成了标准的拉马克。

Had Napoleon known what was in the book, he would certainly have liked it even less. Rather than consolidating power, Lamarck’s theory distributed it throughout the living world.

继居维叶之后，还有许多人讥笑拉马克的科学。因为诞生在大革命的年月，他那自我塑造世界的自然史被与雅各宾派（Jacobins）的激进政治绑在了一起；那套政治倒了，他的科学也跟着倒。「拉马克主义」这个词本身就是当骂人话造出来的。1830年以后，政治反动席卷欧洲，各门生命科学在有机体被动性的原则上重新奠基。拉马克的著作绝了版，他的名字从教科书里消失。到世纪中叶，在法国、英国和德国，进化思想整体陷入黯淡，随之一起黯淡的，还有拉马克所代表的那一整个自然史传统。1859年达尔文出版《物种起源》（Origin of Species）时，他所面对的科学世界几乎已经忘了拉马克；在那个世界里，获得性性状遗传根本算不上一个活的选项。

After Lamarck died, Cuvier emerged as the victor who wrote the history, quite literally. While director of the National Institute, he wrote Lamarck’s official ‘eulogy’, which is surely the nastiest eulogy ever written, dismissing Lamarck’s theory as poetry rather than science. This set the tone for assessments of Lamarck’s ideas for a long time to come.

达尔文在如何处理自己与拉马克的关系上颇费周章。他确实借鉴了拉马克的一些重要想法：进化是于深时中徐徐展开的过程，以及把生命的历史看作一株不断分枝的大树。但他也处处小心，在《物种起源》的历次再版中刻意与拉马克的机制——获得性性状遗传——拉开距离，坚称他自己的自然选择学说是另一回事。在私人笔记本里，达尔文承认拉马克是断言物种会随时间转变的第一人，是让生命的转变成为科学核心问题的第一人。但在公开场合，他保持着距离。随着达尔文正统在其身后几十年里与崛起的孟德尔（Mendel）遗传学结盟并巩固，两位博物学家之间的分野硬化成一条教义：达尔文对，拉马克错；自然选择是科学，获得性性状遗传是神话。

Following Cuvier, many others ridiculed Lamarck’s science. Because it emerged in the midst of the French Revolution and replaced God with the creative agency of living organisms, it smelled to contemporaries of atheism, revolution and regicide. These ill odours promised trouble not only for Lamarck and Lamarckism but for evolutionary theory more generally.

这场运动的一位奠基者，是德国生物学家魏斯曼。1880年代，魏斯曼着手清除达尔文主义中的拉马克残余。他的核心工具是「种质」（germ plasm）与「体质」（soma）之分，也就是遗传物质与身体之分。种质代代相续，而身体是一只用完即弃的容器，一个「不透明的」中介。身体一生中发生的任何事都够不到种质：用他的话说，两者之间横亘着一道「屏障」（barrier）。这道魏斯曼屏障成了新达尔文主义的奠基石。有机体是被动的，是一辆载具；遗传物质是主动的，是一位指挥。进化降临于有机体；它不是经由有机体而发生的。

Darwin struggled over his relations with Lamarck. He drew, importantly, on Lamarck’s theory in formulating his own, but he also insisted on the distinctness of the two. As he wrote to his geologist friend Charles Lyell in 1863, he knew that an association with Lamarckism would be ‘very injurious’ to his theory’s reputation. Still, Darwin did retain various forms of agency within the evolving organism, including Lamarck’s principle that animal behaviours produced heritable effects on their bodies, which Darwin called ‘the inherited effects of use and disuse’. Natural selection, he believed, worked in conjunction with these other factors. But after Darwin died, his followers around the turn of the 20th century eliminated all traces of Lamarckism from their neo-Darwinist theory, rendering organisms as passive as they had been at the hands of Cuvier’s divine creator. The eradication of Lamarckism became the defining feature of neo-Darwinism.

魏斯曼强调，他的治学之道建立在一个假设之上：一个决定论的、机械论的宇宙，其中一切原因原则上皆可计算。他写道，生物学必须变成一门「有机体的物理学」。这个机械宇宙的假设，诚然为当时的大部分物理学所共有；它过去是、现在也是一条方法论假设，而不是教条。但在生命科学里，它驮着形而上学的重负。因为在机械图景中，有机体是众客体中的一个客体，它的种种活动是外因作用于它的结果。它的能动性不过是一种说法罢了。在这一图景里，生命存在不是制造者，不是发轫的中心，而是一台机械。从这个意义上说，魏斯曼的生物学恰恰是以拉马克的方式所行的一种自然政治学，只是方向颠倒：拉马克让有机体成为主权者，魏斯曼让它做了臣民。

A founding figure in this movement was the German biologist August Weismann. In the 1880s, he began to draw a sharp distinction between somatic (body) cells and germ (reproductive) cells. He said that no changes taking place in the somatic cells could ever be transmitted to the germ cells. This is the so-called Weismann barrier, eliminating the possibility of ‘inheritance of acquired characters’. In a rather cruel series of experiments, Weismann chopped the tails off several generations of mice in order to show that their offspring had perfectly healthy tails. He himself acknowledged that this result had no real bearing on Lamarck’s theory: neither Lamarck nor Darwin had ever claimed that amputations were inherited, only internally generated changes. But Weismann reckoned it would defeat popular belief in the inheritance of acquired characteristics. The mouse-tail experiments remain rhetorically powerful, and still appear in biology textbooks today.

在「生命体是外力之被动客体」这个想法里，藏着一种优生学的逻辑。如果有机体是机械，那么繁育它们就是工程，育种者就是工程师。20世纪初英国、美国和德国的优生学运动，明确汲取了这门新的遗传生物学。「种质」是原材料，国家或私人基金会是设计师。优生学的历史，部分地就是有机体被动性的历史，一门把生命存在当作设计客体的科学。这段历史从1910年代的繁育协会，经过1930年代的绝育法，一直通向1940年代的集中营。有机体的被动性是有代价的。

Weismann emphasised that his approach to science rested on the assumption of a divine presence behind the whole process. He assured his readers that his biology didn’t just leave room for a ‘Universal Cause’ but actually demanded one. He wrote of a ‘Final Cause’ operating ‘behind’ the mechanism of the universe. All agency lay with this remote, divine presence and none with the evolving, mortal beings themselves. Therefore, Weismann said his science was ‘absolutely opposed’ to materialism.

「魏斯曼屏障」——对拉马克式遗传的排除——在弗朗西斯·克里克（Francis Crick）1958年提出、西德尼·布伦纳（Sydney Brenner）等人在随后几十年里加以阐发之后，成了分子生物学的中心法则。中心法则说：信息从DNA流向RNA，再流向蛋白质，永不回流。在被动有机体的分子生物学版本里，基因是程序，身体是输出结果。有机体又一次成了容器、载具、机械。中心法则是辉煌的简化，它为生物学的分子革命供给了动力。但它也是一种形而上学，一幅把生命存在当成机器的图像，而且以它自己的方式，驮着不亚于魏斯曼屏障的政治重负。

The ‘Weismann barrier’ – the exclusion of Lamarckian inheritance – became the central principle of neo-Darwinism. After the identification of the structure of the DNA molecule in 1953, Francis Crick restated the Weismann barrier as the ‘central dogma of molecular biology’: DNA transcription runs only in one direction, from DNA through RNA to protein, never in the reverse, from which Crick drew the implication that acquired characteristics could never be inherited. Nothing a living being did could make a difference to the machinery of inheritance.

否认有机体的能动性，从来不只是纯粹的智识错误。它有社会与政治的后果，而这些后果从一开头就与遗传和繁育的科学纠缠在一起。对有机体被动性的坚持，过去是、现在也是一场自然政治学。它为一种关于繁育、设计、控制的政治背书。它为工程师、育种者、国家的权威作保。20世纪，这个被动有机体的世纪，同时也是优生学的世纪。这层联系当然并不简单；政治上的被动派与行动派，各个阵营里都有。但被动有机体的意识形态——生命存在作为客体——是现成的，而且被用上了。

Denying the agency of organisms has not been a purely intellectual mistake. It has had implications reaching far beyond the debates of scientists. For one thing, there’s a eugenic logic built into the idea that living beings, including humans, are the passive objects of genetic programming and outside forces. Essentially all the neo-Darwinists of the early through middle decades of the 20th century were fervent eugenicists. Crick is an example. ‘Let us take up this whole question of eugenics,’ he exhorted his colleagues at a London conference in 1963. ‘I do not see why people should have the right to have children,’ he observed. He mused that it would be a fairly simple matter for a government to sterilise the whole population by putting some chemical in the food supply and then reverse the effect only for those people ‘licensed to bear children’. These were not fringe views; eugenic theories and programmes were mainstream in neo-Darwinist biology for much of the 20th century, and some still linger today.

此外，有机体根本被动性的观念，也帮助塑造了「基因」这一概念晚近的历史。在分子图景中，基因是一段为蛋白质编码的DNA，蛋白质去执行一项功能。基因是原因，蛋白质是结果。基因主动，有机体被动。这幅「某性状之基因」的图景成果极其丰硕，但也自成局限。在这幅图景里，有机体是其全部基因的总和，它的种种活动是一套程序的展开。对基因调控的复杂性、发育与遗传的相互纠缠、有机体在自身造就过程中所起作用的重新发现，部分地正是对拉马克之问的重新发现。

Additionally, the idea of the fundamental passivity of organisms has helped inform two centuries of environmental destruction, as it has encouraged people to regard the living world as raw material to manipulate and exploit for economic and imperial gain. Lamarck, who witnessed the Industrial Revolution firsthand, warned of this in his last work. He pointed out deforestation and its consequences, and he lamented that people were selfish and shortsighted, waging wars and plundering nature for riches, annihilating their own means of conservation. He concluded despairingly: ‘It looks as if man were destined to exterminate himself after making the globe uninhabitable.’

从一开始，拉马克这门关于自我塑造、创造世界之生命的科学，就招来了政治激进者的瞩目与政治保守者的猜忌。物种可变、有机体自我塑造、世界可以被改造：这些念头都带着一股革命气味。到了20世纪，这层关联掉了个个儿：新达尔文主义的被动有机体，成了控制型政治的天然盟友。主动的有机体，在不同的年代里做过无政府主义、工团主义、合作社运动的朋友；被动的有机体，做过优生学、繁育、生命工程的朋友。自然政治学不是一张简单的一一对应表，但这些亲和关系是真实存在的。

From the start, Lamarck’s science of self-making, world-creating living beings antagonised the powerful. In contrast, the dominant tradition in evolutionary biology from the end of the 19th century, largely abiding still today, renders living organisms as the objects of external forces, posing no threat to gods or monarchs or other worldly powers. Evolutionary science became the reductive account of an essentially passive living world. By this standard, Lamarck’s vision of a world continually in the making, shaped by its own mortal inhabitants, wasn’t even wrong: it just wasn’t science. But science, like living beings, is always changing. Evolutionary biology today is full of exhilarating unrest.

拉马克的思想正在生物学的几个领域里卷土重来。例如，表观遗传遗传（epigenetic inheritance）——涉及DNA之外那些影响基因表达的结构——的研究已经证明，遍及各类生物，个体一生中获得的性状确实能够遗传。科学家们不断发现的许多例子，涉及各类动物对气候变化的响应。一个例子是刺雀鲷（spiny chromis），一种栖息在太平洋珊瑚礁里的纤细雀鲷。2015年，海洋生物学家证明，面对气候变化带来的水温升高，它的新陈代谢会以千变万化的方式发生转变，而这些变化会一代传一代。刺雀鲷并非孤例。2020年，科学家发现，为这种雀鲷提供住所的造礁珊瑚，同样会以可传给后代的表观遗传变化，来回应海水温度与盐度的变化。

Lamarck’s ideas have been making a comeback in several areas of biology. For instance, the study of epigenetic inheritance – involving structures outside the DNA that influence gene expression – has demonstrated, throughout every kind of organism, that characteristics acquired in an individual lifetime can indeed be inherited. Many of the examples that scientists have been finding involve the responses of various kinds of animals to climate change. An example is the spiny chromis, a slender damselfish inhabiting the coral reefs of the Pacific. In 2015, marine biologists showed that its metabolism responds to the higher water temperatures brought on by climate change by transforming in myriad ways, and that these changes are inherited from generation to generation. The spiny chromis isn’t alone. In 2020, scientists found that the reef-building coral that provides the damselfish’s home also responds to changing ocean temperatures and salinity with epigenetic changes that it passes on to its offspring.

德国的进化遗传学家发现，经受热应激的豚鼠产下的后代，基因表达发生了改变，而且这些改变能延续好几代。在植物中，干旱胁迫已被证明能让开花时间发生可遗传的变化。在哺乳动物中，母体营养对后代代谢的影响，如今已是发育起源研究中的标准发现。这些机制涉及各种各样的表观遗传标记——附着在DNA上的甲基基团、对缠绕DNA的组蛋白的修饰——它们改变基因表达，却不改变DNA序列本身。这些标记可以随细胞分裂被复制，其中一些还能进入生殖细胞系。经验发现是坚实的，解释上的争论仍在继续，但那条古老的定论——一生中所获皆不可遗传——已经不复存在了。

German evolutionary geneticists have found that wild guinea pigs subjected to heat-stress show heritable alterations in their livers, which play a crucial role in metabolic regulation; and biologists in the United States and Kenya have shown that the metabolisms of baboons also heritably change in response to differences in their food supply. In fact, it seems increasingly apparent that all kinds of living beings respond to their changing environments with heritable epigenetic transformations. These might provide means of rapid adaptation that precedes, and perhaps shapes, slower genetic changes.

当下的另一个热点，是动物行为在引导自然选择上所起的作用，这个过程有时被称为「生态位构建」（niche construction）。筑坝的河狸、造土的蚯蚓、垒丘的白蚁：这些生物不只是在适应环境，而是在制造环境。一座河狸坝改变水流、植被的格局和土壤的构成。这些改变延续几十年甚至几百年，活得比造它们的河狸更久，并塑造着它们后代的生活。后来的河狸所承袭的那个生态位，是先前的河狸的作品。在这幅图景里，选择压力并不单纯「就在外面」的环境之中；它们部分地是有机体活动留下的沉淀。环境，部分地是一件被制造出来的东西。

Another area of current interest is the role of animal behaviour in directing natural selection. Martha Muñoz, an ecologist and evolutionary biologist at Yale University in Connecticut, studies a kind of lizard in the Dominican Republic called the anole, which is closely related to the iguana. Muñoz has shown that, as these lizards moved from the coast up into the mountains, reaching as high as 3,000 metres, they adapted to the much colder temperatures by being ‘behaviourally nimble’, for example, by basking on the sun-drenched boulders. Rather than showing physiological differences that would give them greater cold tolerance, they have rapidly evolved shorter legs and flatter skulls, enabling them to hide from predators in the crevices of the rock. ‘Far from being passive vessels at the mercy of their circumstances,’ Muñoz told the journal Science in 2022, ‘organisms can influence evolution directly.’ Her research confirms that ‘behaviour is one of the major architects of evolution’ and that ‘organisms can influence natural selection.’

「生态位构建」理论提供了又一个例子。它的中心原则，正如其创立者奥德林-斯米（Odling-Smee）、拉兰（Laland）与费尔德曼（Feldman）在2003年所界定的：有机体改变其环境，而这些改变又把被修改过的选择压力施加回有机体，于是在两者之间形成一种互为往来的动力学。被构建出来的生态位，实际上就是一份遗产：把修改过的境遇从一代传给下一代的馈赠。一座池塘是河狸留给后代的遗产，正如一座丘冢是白蚁的。生态位构建理论已被吸纳进扩展进化综合——一个自2000年代初以来不断积蓄力量的框架，它汇聚了发育生物学家、生态学家、遗传学家与古生物学家的研究，这些学者以各自的方式发现，新达尔文主义的被动有机体已经不足以应付眼前的种种现象。

The theory of ‘niche construction’ offers another example. The central principle of this theory is that organisms contribute to shaping their environments, creating the ecological niches that exert selective pressures on them. Bird nests, beaver dams, termite mounds, worm and mole burrows, spiderwebs, beehives – the evidence of organisms creating their own environments is ubiquitous. John Odling-Smee, the scholar who coined the term ‘niche construction’, wrote in 2024 that biologists should rethink what the theory of evolution should be about: not just natural selection acting upon organisms in given environments but ‘the coevolution of organisms with their environments’.

新达尔文主义连同它那套被动的生命模型，在根基上摇摇欲坠已有一阵子了。挑战来自发育生物学，来自生态学，来自共生研究，来自微生物组，来自可塑性与学习，来自有机体参与自身进化的种种方式。进化生物学家凯文·拉兰——扩展进化综合的领军人物之一——是这样说的：有机体不仅仅是选择的客体，更是一个主体，一个去构建、去选择、去修改的能动者。生物学哲学家索尼娅·米勒（Sonja Müller）在一系列论文中发展了「构建生态位的自我」这一概念：那个营造着自身世界、又被世界所造的有机体。那些旧的二分——先天／后天、基因／环境、本能／学习——正在溶解，不是彼此相溶，而是溶入一个共同建构的过程。

Neo-Darwinism, with its passive model of living beings, has been teetering on its throne. One current movement, known as the extended evolutionary synthesis, seeks to widen the evolutionary picture beyond the neo-Darwinist focus on random genetic variation and natural selection, in part by including behavioural and even cultural elements. Many animals have something like what we call culture in humans, and this includes cultural forms that are inherited and passed down. Lamarck knew this. He wrote that intelligent animals – birds and mammals – could create new habits and ways of life that would be maintained over generations. There is overwhelming and definitive evidence that all kinds of animals – birds, primates, whales, fish, insects – learn from one another and have cultural traditions that are handed down from generation to generation. Communication and social learning make a crucial difference to survival and reproduction, and therefore to evolution. Animals communicate vitally important things to one another including information about food, predators, songs, migration routes and where to find a mate. To give just one example, humpback whales develop different approaches to hunting from one community to another – one in the Northeast Pacific, another in the Gulf of Maine – and teach these to their offspring.

这些别出心裁的座头鲸，连同多米尼加共和国（Dominican Republic）敏捷的山地蜥蜴、新西兰（New Zealand）勤勉的蚯蚓，都以各自的方式，是拉马克所描述的那种自我塑造的实践者。阿拉斯加（Alaska）内湾航道（Inside Passage）的座头鲸发明了一种新的捕食技术——「气泡网捕食」（bubble-net feeding）：一群鲸沿着一条上升的螺旋线游动，喷出一道圆筒状的气泡幕，把猎物鱼驱拢到一处，然后冲向水面将它们吞下。这项技术由一头鲸在1980年代发明，此后一头传一头，散布到了整个种群。这些鲸不只是在适应环境；它们在以自身的活动改变环境，然后再去适应这个改变。它们的世界是它们的作品，它们的作品是它们的世界。这样的创造力与转化力，终究既无法被控制，也无法被垄断。

These innovative humpback whales, along with the nimble mountain lizards of the Dominican Republic and legions of other living things – all of them, in fact – exemplify what Lamarck knew and mainstream evolutionary biology has long denied. Living beings are active, self-making, and world-making. They are creators, even the humblest of them. The implication, deeply unpopular with autocrats from Napoleon onward, can be a source of hope: neither creativity nor transformative power can ultimately be controlled or monopolised.
