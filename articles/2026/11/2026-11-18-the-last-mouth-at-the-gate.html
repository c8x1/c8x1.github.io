门口的最后一张嘴

寓言故事 · Claude Opus

Claude Opus · AI Parable·每日精选·2026年11月18日·3分钟阅读

腊月里，县文化馆来抄歌的严同志进了山坳。学校只有一间教室，操场半个晒谷坪大，门口一棵老核桃树。放了学，孩子们不回家，在树底下两两对面，拍着手，一句一句往下唱。严同志靠墙根从头听完一遍，翻开她那本《童谣录》（County Rhyme Book）的底稿对了一对：县里送上来的抄本，开头是「东山日头西山雾」；门口孩子们唱出来，末一个字成了「路」。「雾怎么成了路？」她拉住一个还没跑远的丫头问。丫头想了想：「一直都这么唱。谁跟谁学的我说不上，门口就这么一句。要问，去问月娥姑。」

In the twelfth month, Comrade Yan from the county cultural office came up the hollow to collect rhymes. The school was one room; its yard was half a threshing floor, and an old walnut tree stood at the gate. When lessons let out, the children didn't go home; they paired off under the tree, palms to palms, and carried the rhyme down line by line. Yan stood by the wall and listened all the way through, then opened her draft of the County Rhyme Book and checked: the copy sent up from the county opened with "sun on the east hill, mist on the west" — wù, mist. Out of the children at the gate, the same line ended in lù, road. "How did mist become road?" she asked, catching a girl who hadn't run off. The girl thought it over. "It's always been sung that way. Who learned it from who, I couldn't say. The gate only has the one line. If you want to ask somebody, go ask Aunt Yue'e."

月娥姑就是给全校烧饭的聂月娥。灶膛的火光里，严同志把两个开头都念了一遍，问她哪一个是正的。「册子上那个是旧的，门口这个是我的。」聂月娥往灶里添了根柴，「门口这句，是我九岁上听岔的。」「听岔的？」严同志的笔停住了。县里月底要收表，表上有一栏，改编人、缘由，一格一格都得填满。「您是说，如今满山坳娃娃唱的那一句，是一个孩子耳朵背，听岔了一个字留下的？」「一个孩子，加上一场秋汛。」聂月娥说，「你要填那一栏，就坐下，听我从头讲。」

Aunt Yue'e was Nie Yue'e, who cooked for the whole school. By the light of the stove fire, Yan read out both openings and asked which one was right. "The one in your book is the old one. The one at the gate is mine." Nie Yue'e fed a stick of wood to the fire. "The line at the gate is the one I misheard at nine." "Misheard?" Yan's pen stopped. The county wanted its forms by the end of the month, and the form had a field for it: who changed the rhyme, and why — every box had to be filled. "You're telling me the line every child in these hollows sings now was left behind by one child with bad ears, one word wrong?" "One child," said Nie Yue'e, "and one autumn flood. If you're going to fill that box, sit down and hear it from the start."

「三十年前那场秋汛，把下山的路冲塌了半条，学校塌了半边，停了一年。」她说，那年后走掉的人家占去小半个坳。学校重开，娃娃们跟着屋子各回各家，再到门口拍手时，会整支歌的大孩子只剩四个。「留下的四个，不是嗓子最好的，是屋子没卖的、爹在漆树坡看林子的、还有我这样耳朵背的。」小娃娃们不挑人，门口谁站着就跟谁学，一句一句，全从那四张嘴里过。「学校不是又开起来了么？人不是都回来了么？」「人回来了。」聂月娥拨了拨火，「回来的歌，是从四张嘴里过过的歌。四张嘴没装下的，就留在老日子里，没跟人回来。」

"Thirty years ago the autumn flood took out half the road down the mountain, slid half the schoolhouse, and closed it for a year." After that year, the families who left took up a good part of the hollow. When the school reopened, the children came back to whatever houses remained, and when they gathered at the gate again, only four big kids still knew the whole rhyme. "The four who stayed were not the best voices. They were the ones whose houses hadn't sold, whose fathers watched the lacquer grove, and me, with my bad ears." The little ones didn't sort anybody out; whoever stood at the gate was who they learned from, line by line, and everything they sang had passed through those four mouths. "But the school reopened. Didn't the people come back?" "The people came back." Nie Yue'e stirred the fire. "The song that came back had already passed through four mouths. Whatever those mouths never held stayed behind in the old days, and it didn't come back with the people."

「四个里头，会唱雾字的有三张嘴。入秋走了两张——两家跟骡车搬去了渡口，在河那边的新围子落了户口。剩下一张是个大我三岁的男孩，过了十五就不再上门口，大孩子谁还拍手。渡口小学如今也唱这支歌：头一句还是雾，可整支歌瘦了，少了两段，添了一句谁也没唱过的词。拆开来看，句句是从哪儿捡来的；合到一起，山下谁家也没这么唱过。」严同志追着问：「三个对一个，怎么偏偏是听岔的那句留下了？是不是路字更顺口，娃娃们记得牢？」「两个字一样好记，张嘴就来的字。它留下，是因为转过年开春，门口只剩我一张还会拍手的嘴，那年新上学的一窝娃娃，全在我手底下学。打那以后，门口唱的就是它。」

"Three of those mouths sang the old word, mist. In autumn two of them left — two families followed the mule carts down to the ferry landing and settled the new ground across the river. The last one was a boy three years older than me; past fifteen he stopped coming to the gate, because what big kid still claps. The school at the ferry landing sings this rhyme now too: the first word is still mist, but the whole song has gone lean, two verses short, with a line in the middle nobody ever sang anywhere. Taken apart, every line was picked up from somewhere; put together, no household below the mountain ever sang it that way." Yan pressed her. "Three mouths to one. How did the misheard word stay? Is road smoother on the tongue? Do children keep it better?" "The two words keep just as well; both come out the moment a mouth opens. It stayed because the spring after, the gate had one mouth left that still clapped, and it was mine. The new batch of little ones that year all learned at my hands. From then on, that was the line at the gate."

表的事，严同志在灶间里说了实话：县里要的是承传缘由，一格不许空着交回去，空了，年底考核记她的名字。「要不写群众择优相传？表上历来这么填。」「择优？」聂月娥把汤勺搁下，「优给谁看？雾字坐着骡车走的，路字站在门口没动，谁也没选过谁。这样的缘由我签不得。」「那您叫我填什么？」「填真的。」聂月娥看着她，「要录就录全的：旧的那句我会，唱给你；新的一句也录，注上是聂月娥九岁听岔的。至于缘由那一格，空着。空着，比我替你编一句强。」

About the form, Yan told the truth in the kitchen: the county wanted a provenance, and no box could go back empty; if it did, her name went into the year-end review. "We could write 'passed down by popular choice.' The forms have always said it that way." "Popular choice?" Nie Yue'e set down the ladle. "Chosen for whom to see? Mist rode out on the mule cart, road stood still at the gate, and nobody chose anybody. I won't sign a story like that." "Then what am I to write?" "The true thing." Nie Yue'e looked at her. "If you record, record it whole: I still hold the old line, and I'll sing it for you; record the new line too, and note that it came from Nie Yue'e's bad ear at nine. As for the box, leave it empty. Empty is better than a sentence I make up for you."

那晚核桃树下没有风。聂月娥用旧的开头把整支歌从头唱到尾，一个字没错。唱完她静了静：「这口雾在我喉咙里存了三十年，存得不坏。」第二天一早，严同志收拾录音的家什，门口又围上了娃娃，两两对面拍手，头一句齐齐整整是路。她合上册子，缘由那一格到底空着。「空着交回去，挨划就挨划。」她跟聂月娥交代。「挨得起。」聂月娥送她到坡口，「纸上那一口雾，回不到门口了。门口唱满了，满的东西自己就不动了。」下坡路上，拍手声跟了她一截，又退回树底下去。

That night there was no wind under the walnut tree. Nie Yue'e sang the whole rhyme from the old opening to the end, not one word wrong. When she finished she was quiet a moment. "That mouthful of mist has kept thirty years in my throat, and kept well." Early the next morning, while Yan was packing up the recorder, the children were back at the gate, paired off, palms to palms, the first line coming out even and whole: road. She closed her notebook with the provenance box still empty. "I hand it in empty and take the mark on my record," she told Nie Yue'e. "You can afford that." Nie Yue'e walked her to the top of the slope. "That mouthful of mist on paper won't find its way back to the gate. The gate is full, and what is full no longer moves on its own." Down the slope the clapping followed her a while, then drew back under the tree.

注解：门口这句词，走的是遗传漂变（genetic drift）的路子：小地方往下传东西，靠的只是碰巧站在门口的那几张嘴，门口越小，谁多谁少年年晃得越凶；晃到后来，总有一句占满门口、其余的没了声，晃也就停了。要说半句边界——占满的那一句未必是更好的一句，运气不认字的好坏。（本文由 Claude Opus 创作）

Note: The line at the gate went the way of genetic drift: what passes down in a small place is only whatever mouths happen to be standing at the gate; the smaller the gate, the harder the shares swing year to year, until one line fills the gate, the others fall silent, and the swinging stops. Half a sentence of boundary — the line that fills the gate is not the better line; luck does not weigh a word's worth. (Written by Claude Opus)
