西方民主为何萎靡不振，又该如何修复

Les Coleman·Aeon·2026年11月9日·10分钟阅读·原文

如果西方民主国家是公司，它们会被斥为「生活方式型企业」——管理层（也就是政治领袖）经营它们只是为了增进自身的利益和财富。领袖们推行让少数人受益的项目，而非追求国家目标，留给国家的是低经济增速、沉重债务和泛滥的白领犯罪。我们早在近半个世纪前就进入了这个社会秩序不公、政治萎靡、动荡频仍的时代——彼时美国总统罗纳德·里根（Ronald Reagan）与英国首相玛格丽特·撒切尔（Margaret Thatcher）推出了被全球竞相采纳的自由意志主义改革。

If Western democracies were companies, they would be dismissed as lifestyle businesses whose managers (ie, political leaders) run them to promote their own interests and wealth. Leaders pursue projects that benefit the few, rather than national goals, and leave their countries with low economic growth, heavy debt and rampant white-collar crime. We entered this era of an unjust social order, political malaise and destabilisation almost half a century ago when the US president Ronald Reagan and the UK prime minister Margaret Thatcher introduced libertarian reforms that were embraced globally.

1981年，里根在就任总统后的第一次内阁会议上分发了《领导的使命》（Mandate for Leadership）一书，这份长达三千页的文件出自传统基金会（Heritage Foundation）——华盛顿的一个保守派研究机构。它后来成为新自由主义的意识形态，主张小政府、行业放松管制、减税、强化国防，并回撤社会工程。一年后，该书的建议已有百分之六十被里根政府采纳，《纽约时报》（The New York Times）称之为「里根革命的宣言」。他的举措得到了撒切尔的呼应，并扩散至全球，主导了世界各地民主国家的公共政策。

At his first cabinet meeting as president in 1981, Reagan distributed copies of Mandate for Leadership, whose 3,000 pages came out of the Heritage Foundation, a conservative research group in Washington, DC. This became the ideology of neoliberalism, which advocated a smaller state, industry deregulation, tax cuts, strengthened defence, and roll-back of social engineering. A year later, 60 per cent of the Mandate’s proposals had been adopted by the Reagan administration, and The New York Times called it ‘the manifesto of the Reagan revolution’. His initiative engaged Thatcher, and spread globally to dominate public policy in democracies around the world.

1985年3月，里根成为第一位到访纽约证券交易所（New York Stock Exchange）的在任美国总统。图片来源：维基百科（Wikipedia）

Ronald Reagan became the first sitting US President to visit the New York Stock Exchange in March 1985. Courtesy Wikipedia

我的视角来自一名工程师——我的职业生涯先是企业战略师，近年则是一名金融学者。2000年，千年虫恐慌过后不久，我离开企业界进入学术界，研究国际金融组织的决策行为。在一轮「产业旅游」式的走访中，我在伊斯坦布尔（Istanbul）、伦敦（London）、墨尔本（Melbourne）和纽约（New York）采访了五十多位金融业高管。这带来了三本书，其中包括《应用投资理论》（Applied Investment Theory，2016），该书批评了司法系统未能起诉大公司和当选官员的腐败与犯罪行为。

My perspective is that of an engineer with a career as a corporate strategist and, more recently, finance academic. In 2000, shortly after the Y2K scare, I left the corporate world for academia and research into decision-making by international finance organisations. In a round of industrial tourism, I interviewed more than 50 finance executives in Istanbul, London, Melbourne and New York. This led to three books, including Applied Investment Theory (2016), which criticised the justice system’s failure to prosecute corruption and criminal activity in large companies and among elected officials.

公司金融中有一个用来解释组织与个人行为的框架，即「结构—行为—绩效」（structure-conduct-performance，SCP）模型，它可以帮助我们理解里根和撒切尔带给西方民主国家的变革所产生的影响。SCP将决策置于一个多维框架中加以概念化；应用于国家时，这一框架假定：国家的境况由制度、法律和惯例的结构所决定；结构驱动政府、个人和组织的行动或决策；而后者又进而决定绩效或结果。

A framework used in corporate finance to explain the behaviour of organisations and individuals is the structure-conduct-performance (SCP) model, and it can help us understand the effects of the changes that Reagan and Thatcher brought to Western democracies. SCP conceptualises decisions within a multidimensional framework; when applied to countries, this framework postulates that their conditions are driven by the structure of institutions, laws and conventions; which drives the conduct or decisions of government, individuals and organisations; and that in turn leads to performance or outcomes.

里根和撒切尔断言，放开市场、缩小政府规模便可确保繁荣。他们标志性的举措——行业放松管制、减税、市场自由化与劳动力市场改革——最终大获全胜。

Reagan and Thatcher asserted that unleashing markets and shrinking the state would ensure prosperity. Their signature initiatives of industry deregulation, tax reduction, market liberalisation and labour market reform won the day.

然而没过多久，靠政府举债支撑的减税所产生的「涓滴效应」便被证明是灾难性的。最明显的莫过于1987年10月的黑色星期一（Black Monday）股市崩盘。新自由主义已削弱了公共机构管理系统性风险的能力，而随着政府监管行业的权威与能力收缩，道德风险急剧飙升。

It did not take long, however, for the trickle-down effects of tax cuts funded by government debt to prove disastrous. Most obvious was the Black Monday stock market crash of October 1987. Neoliberalism had reduced the capacity of public institutions to manage systemic risks, and – as the authority and ability of government to regulate industry shrank – moral hazard spiked.

基础设施是说明过去半个世纪国家、市场与民主制度之间关系重塑的一个简单例子。二战之后，西方各国政府在交通网络、能源系统和其他公共资本上投入巨资。私有化与放松管制逆转了这一趋势，公共工程项目遭到削减：当道路桥梁、供水系统等日渐老化，生产力便随之衰退。

Infrastructure is a simple way to illustrate the realignment between state, markets and democratic institutions over the past half-century. Following the Second World War, Western governments invested heavily in transportation networks, energy systems and other public capital. Privatisation and deregulation reversed this trend, with cutbacks to public works projects: when roads and bridges, water systems and so on aged, productivity faltered.

里根、撒切尔以及其他新自由主义倡导者许下的繁荣承诺是错误的，因为它带给此前繁荣的民主国家的只有脆弱。从SCP的视角看，市场自由化与金融化的涓滴效应带来的是增长放缓、债务负担加重、制度虚弱和公共信任下滑。

Promises of prosperity by Reagan, Thatcher and other advocates of neoliberalism were mistaken because it brought only fragility to previously prosperous democracies. From an SCP perspective, the trickle-down of market liberalisation and financialisation brought slowing growth, rising debt burdens, institutional weakness, and declining public trust.

一些基本事实有助于理解这些论断，也让人看清新自由主义政策的冲击。不妨看看经济合作与发展组织（Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development，OECD）成员国中富裕自由民主国家自1980年以来的金融数据。税收收入减少迫使政府依赖举债，政府债务——如下方图1左图所示——占GDP的比重从百分之四十飙升至百分之六十五。与此同时，政府支出与经济同步增长，几乎占到GDP的一半。

Some basic facts help to understand these claims, and give a sense of the impacts of neoliberal policies. Consider financial data since 1980 for the rich liberal democracies among the members of the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD). Reduced tax revenue forced governments to rely on borrowings, and government debt – as shown in the left-hand graph of Figure 1 below – surged from 40 to 65 per cent as a share of GDP. Meanwhile, government expenditure kept pace with the economy at almost half of GDP.

在下方图1的右图中，我们可以看到高额而无资金支撑的政府债务带来的经济后果。蓝线追踪人均GDP的增长，它从1980年代接近每年百分之三逐渐放缓，到今天仅有大约百分之一。与此同时，放松管制的信贷市场鼓励家庭举债，家庭债务占GDP的比例翻了一倍有余。显而易见，新自由主义并未兑现其经济增长与个人财富增长的承诺。

In the right-hand chart of Figure 1 below, we see the economic consequences of high, unfunded government debt. The blue line tracks growth of GDP per capita, which gradually slowed from nearly 3 per cent annually in the 1980s to barely 1 per cent today. At the same time, deregulated credit markets encouraged households to borrow, and their debt more than doubled as a proportion of GDP. Quite obviously, neoliberalism did not deliver on its promises of economic growth and increased personal wealth.

五十年新自由主义政策的最大赢家是金融业。随着政府与家庭债务失控般飙升，全球资本愈发自由，银行家的冒险倾向急剧膨胀，投机活动欣欣向荣。金融机构的规模与影响力急剧扩张。在美国和英国，金融机构已在利润和政治影响力方面成为一股强大势力。但如下方图2所示，它们制造了大约七年一轮的市场泡沫—崩盘—衰退循环，为投资者精英创造了巨额的不劳而获的财富。与此同时，中产阶级及以下的民众眼看着住房所有权和安稳退休渐行渐远，对Y世代（Gen Y）而言尤其如此。

The big winner from 50 years of neoliberal policy has been the finance sector. As government and household debt surged out of control, global capital became ever freer, bankers’ propensity for risk surged, and speculative activity flourished. Financial institutions grew dramatically in size and influence. In the US and the UK, financial institutions have become a power in terms of profits and political influence. But, as shown in Figure 2 below, they set up a roughly seven-year cycle of market bubbles followed by crash and recession, which created super, unearned wealth for investor elites. Meanwhile, the middle class and below saw home ownership and comfortable retirement drift out of reach, especially for Gen Y.

新自由主义最强大的影响或许在于治理。企业治理的削弱导致了轰动性的破产，政府的战略同样糟糕：在伊拉克（Iraq）未能找到所承诺的大规模杀伤性武器、2008至2009年的全球金融危机，以及过头的COVID-19封锁措施。这些事件引发的愤怒与沮丧至今挥之不去——如上方图3所示，不断攀升的全球不确定性指数提供了一个粗略却发人深省的视角，让我们得以窥见大众的政治情绪。

Probably the most powerful impact of neoliberalism has been on governance. Weakened corporate governance led to spectacular bankruptcies, with equally poor government strategy such as the failure to find promised weapons of mass destruction in Iraq, the 2008-09 global financial crisis, and over-the-top COVID-19 lockdowns. These generated anger and frustration that still linger, as indicated in Figure 3 above, where the rising global uncertainty index gives a rough but illuminating perspective on popular political sentiments.

过去四十年间，西方政治民主国家经济稳定与健康状况的下滑，并不只是一连串坏运气，因为它并未发生在中国和印度（India）这些抵制了新自由主义甜言诱惑的国家。给民主国家造成大范围损害的糟糕决策，源于民主制度与社会及经济现实之间刻意的结构性错位。

The decline in the economic stability and health of Western political democracies over the past 40 years is not simply a run of bad luck, because it did not occur in countries such as China and India that resisted its blandishments. Bad decisions that inflicted sweeping damage to democracies came from deliberate structural misalignments between democratic institutions and social and economic realities.

要矫正西方政治民主国家的沉疴，需要对政治范式加以重塑，首先便是支持那些能够培育更优制度与实践的决策者。

Rectifying the malaise in Western political democracies requires realignment of the political paradigm, starting with support for decision-makers who can foster improved institutions and practices.

这听起来或许有违直觉，但我们必须提高政府高官的薪酬。看看OECD成员国政府首脑的年度基本薪酬：约有一半落在15万至40万美元之间；只有瑞士（Switzerland）和澳大利亚（Australia）更高，其余国家甚至更低。美国总统的基本年薪自2001年以来一直固定为40万美元，而英国首相约17万英镑（约合23万美元）的薪酬，如今甚至低于2009年戈登·布朗（Gordon Brown）的水平。

Though it may seem counterintuitive, we must raise the pay of top government officials. Consider the annual base salaries for heads of government of OECD member countries: around half fall in the range US$150,000-400,000; only Switzerland and Australia are higher, and the rest are even lower. The base salary of the US president has been fixed at $400,000 since 2001, while the UK prime minister’s salary of around £170,000 (cUS$230,000) is less today than that paid to Gordon Brown in 2009.

金钱虽非职业选择的唯一驱动力，但薪酬过低会招来平庸之辈与自命不凡者，并助长腐败。政治薪酬需要具备竞争力。政府首脑的报酬应参照本国大型公司首席执行官的薪酬确定，其他政客与政府高级官员的薪酬也应相应看齐。在澳大利亚这样一个中等国家，将国会议员的基本年薪从区区每年17万美元增至三倍，总花费也不到8000万美元。相对于对一个2万亿美元经济体的稳健治理，这个代价实在便宜。

While money is not the only driver of occupational choice, underpayment invites mediocrity and the entitled, and encourages corruption. Political salaries need to be competitive. Leaders of government should be remunerated by reference to the compensation of CEOs of major domestic companies, with the pay of other politicians and senior government officials brought into line. In a mid-ranking country like Australia, trebling the base salary of members of the national parliament from a piddling US$170,000 per year would cost less than $80 million. That’s a cheap price for sound management of a $2 trillion economy.

薪酬更高、资金更充裕的候选人将有助于打破政治双寡头格局

Better-paid and better-financed candidates will help shake up the political duopoly

为政客支付更高薪酬的选民，也会期待更优秀的候选人和更深入的竞争。若能像印度对候选人的要求那样，更完整地披露候选人的资产与利益，选民的选择将更有依据。媒体对政客私生活的诋毁显然会吓退德才兼备的候选人。澳大利亚就遇到过这个问题，并允许个人在涉己信息被滥用时，就严重侵犯隐私提起诉讼。

Voters paying their politicians more will expect better candidates and deeper competition. Their choices will be enhanced by more complete disclosure of candidates’ assets and interests, as required for candidates in India. Media denigration of politicians’ personal lives is an obvious deterrent to meritorious candidates. Australia has faced this, and allows individuals to sue for serious invasions of privacy when information relating to them has been misused.

竞选活动的花销是另一大障碍，对体制外候选人尤其如此。新兴候选人与政党可以从「民主券」（democracy vouchers）中获益——西雅图（Seattle）的市政选举已引入这种做法：竞选期间向选民发放民主券，供其支持自己青睐的政党或候选人。

The cost of election campaigns is another major impediment, especially to candidates from outside the establishment. Emerging candidates and parties would be helped by democracy vouchers as introduced in Seattle city elections, which are distributed to voters during election campaigns for them to support preferred parties or candidates.

许多民主国家实行长期的两党政治体制：澳大利亚是自由党与工党，英国是工党与保守党，美国是共和党与民主党。尽管政党每隔一两次选举便轮替执政，它们却通过持续的思想交流彼此协调。这种双寡头结构阻碍了新方向与实质性改革（诺姆·乔姆斯基（Noam Chomsky）的一句观察浮现于脑海：「让人们保持被动与顺从的聪明办法，是严格限制可接受意见的谱系，却允许在这一谱系之内进行异常活跃的辩论」）。薪酬更高、资金更充裕的候选人将有助于打破政治双寡头格局，并更严格地审视民主国家的绩效。

Many democracies run longstanding two-party political systems: Liberal and Labor in Australia, Labour and Conservative in the UK, Republican and Democrat in the US. Even though parties swap government after every election or two, they co-ordinate through continual exchange of ideas. This duopoly structure stymies new directions and substantive reform (Noam Chomsky’s observation that ‘the smart way to keep people passive and obedient is to strictly limit the spectrum of acceptable opinion, but allow very lively debate within that spectrum’ comes to mind). Better-paid and better-financed candidates will help shake up the political duopoly and more closely review democracies’ performance.

许多政客奉行增长优先的议程。例如，佛罗里达州（Florida）州长罗恩·德桑蒂斯（Ron DeSantis）对选民说：「我们需要新一代领袖，他们将推行促进经济增长的政策。」鉴于管理学的格言「凡是能被衡量的，才能被管理」，即便衡量本身毫无意义，这种对增长的执念依然举足轻重。

Many politicians have a growth-first agenda. The Florida governor Ron DeSantis, for example, told his electorate: ‘We need a new generation of leaders who will promote policies that will foster economic growth.’ This imperative is important in light of the management truism that ‘what gets measured gets managed’, even when it’s pointless.

衡量民主国家绩效最常用的指标是国内生产总值（GDP），但它是一把愚蠢的尺子，因为它只是记录有价格的商品与服务的价值。我们无从得知它们的社会或政治意义，也得不到其他活动的细节。于是，国民健康恶化、战争开支以及事故与灾害后的重建，都可能推高GDP。它不计入互联网和人工智能等免费服务带来的生产力及其他收益（其开发与订阅成本除外），也不计入自然资源、无偿劳动或质量改进。

The most common measure of democracies’ performance is gross domestic product (GDP), which is a dumb yardstick because it simply records the value of goods and services that have a price. We get no information about their social or political meaning; nor details of other activities. As a result, unhealthy citizens, expenditure on wars, and rebuilding after accidents and disasters can all increase GDP. It does not count any value from the productivity and other benefits of free services such as the internet and AI (beyond their development and any subscription costs); nor does it count natural resources, unpaid labour or improvements in quality.

不惜一切代价追求GDP增长会催生糟糕的政策。奉行利润、人、地球这类「三重底线」的商业公司，比GDP更能体认社会理想。新西兰（New Zealand）是认识到这一点的国家之一，其「生活水平框架」（Living Standards Framework）既衡量国家财富，也衡量个人与机构的福祉。这种均衡的指标应成为所有政府的最低标准。

GDP growth at all costs encourages poor policy. Business corporations with a ‘triple bottom line’ such as profit, people and the planet better recognise social ideals than GDP. One country that acknowledges this is New Zealand whose Living Standards Framework measures the wellbeing of individuals and institutions as well as the country’s wealth. Such balanced measures should be a minimum standard for all governments.

选民对日益坐大的全球寡头集团——那些最富有、人脉最广的人——愈发怨恨。2023年一项覆盖OECD国家的调查发现，十分之七的受访者希望经济资源的分配更加平等，一半的人认为政治权力的差距过大。

Voters increasingly resent the burgeoning global oligarchy of the most wealthy and best-connected. Thus a survey across OECD countries in 2023 found that seven in 10 respondents want a more equal distribution of economic resources, and half think that disparities in political power are too high.

这种财富的集中驱使寡头以全社会为代价维护自身利益

This concentration of wealth encourages oligarchs to protect their interests at the general community’s expense

硬数据佐证了这些忧虑。如下方图4左图所示，在大型民主国家，最富有的百分之一人群持有的财富份额，在1980年代中期前后跌至最低水平。但自那以后它强力反弹：这百分之一人群如今在法国（France）、德国（Germany）和美国持有的财富超过总量的四分之一，在澳大利亚、意大利（Italy）和英国则超过百分之二十。

Hard data back up these concerns. As shown in the left-hand graph of Figure 4 below, in large democracies, the share of total wealth held by the richest 1 per cent fell to its lowest levels around the mid-1980s. But it has rebounded since then: that 1 per cent now holds more than a quarter of total wealth in France, Germany and the US, and more than 20 per cent of the wealth in Australia, Italy and the UK.

这种财富的集中驱使寡头以全社会为代价维护自身利益。最明显的莫过于他们将巨额金钱引入政治：美国2025年的一项研究发现，总统候选人获得的私人捐款中，有百分之三十至四十五来自最富有的百分之一选民。寡头们将公民排除在外，并构建了为自己挪用国家资源的「卡特尔政党」。

This concentration of wealth encourages oligarchs to protect their interests at the general community’s expense. Most obviously, they introduced big money into politics, as evidenced by a US study in 2025, which found that 30-45 per cent of private contributions to presidential candidates come from the wealthiest 1 per cent of voters. Oligarchs have frozen out citizens and structured ‘cartel parties’ that use state resources for themselves.

政府经济政策如今已成为寡头收入的一大来源。据管理咨询公司麦肯锡（McKinsey）的说法，政府债务升高与信贷宽松会增加私人部门的金融资产，并推动资产价格膨胀。这让较富裕的家庭获益尤多，因为他们的收入更多来自金融收益，而非劳动报酬。企业福利、自由市场和放松管制也带来类似的好处。

Government economic policy is now a major source of oligarchs’ income. According to the management consultants McKinsey, higher government debt and easier credit increase financial assets in the private sector and promote asset price inflation. This disproportionately rewards wealthier households because they derive more of their income from financial, rather than labour, earnings. Similar benefit comes from corporate welfare, free markets and deregulation.

政府债务增加给富裕家庭带来的好处，在上方图4右图中得到了印证。自1980年以来，西方民主国家的政府债务占GDP的份额几乎翻倍，而最富有百分之一人群持有的财富比例跃升了四分之一。

The benefit of increased government debt to wealthy households is confirmed in the right-hand chart of Figure 4 above. Since 1980, government debt in Western democracies almost doubled its share of GDP, while the proportion of wealth held by the top 1 per cent jumped by a quarter.

未来学家奥尔多·格雷奇（Aldo Grech）精辟地概括了寡头权力的风险：「政治一直是由金钱和门路塑造的」，「极少数人一直在稳步改写着支配经济、劳动、住房、医疗、媒体乃至注意力本身的规则」。解决之道在于让财富更紧密地与其创造者绑定，以限制财富的存续期，并阻止世袭统治阶层的固化。具体办法是通过累进遗产税，遏制过度的代际财富转移。

The risk from oligarchs’ power was summed up neatly by the futurist Aldo Grech: ‘Politics has been shaped through money and access’ and ‘a very small minority has steadily rewritten the rules that govern economics, labour, housing, healthcare, media, and even attention itself.’ The solution is to more closely tie wealth to its producer to limit its lifetime and stymie entrenchment of a hereditary ruling class. This would come from throttling excessive inter-generational transfers through a progressive death tax.

许多国家与美国有着相同的经历：似乎没有什么能震慑企业欺诈、系统性豪赌和政治失职。面对安然（Enron）与世通（WorldCom）、华尔街与金融危机、大众汽车（Volkswagen）的尾气检测造假、针对大型科技公司的反垄断法案，以及阿片类药物危机，国会调查几乎是仅有的回应。美国参议员伊丽莎白·沃伦（Elizabeth Warren）道出了民众对这类白领犯罪管控乏力的普遍挫败：她指出2008年金融危机期间存在明目张胆的欺诈，并抱怨「没有一位大型银行高管哪怕被刑事指控，更不用说被提起公诉、送上法庭受审了」。有两个障碍显而易见。

Many countries share the US experience that nothing seems to deter corporate fraud, systemic gambles and political misconduct. Congressional inquiries were all that confronted Enron and WorldCom, Wall Street and the financial crisis, Volkswagen’s emissions-test manipulation, big tech antitrust bills, and the opioid crisis. The US senator Elizabeth Warren expressed widespread frustration at ineffective control of such white-collar crimes when she pointed to blatant fraud during the financial crisis of 2008 and complained that ‘not one major bank executive was even charged, much less prosecuted and taken to trial.’ Two impediments are obvious.

第一重障碍是至今仍占主导的中世纪法律原则「犯罪意图」（mens rea，拉丁文意为「有罪之心」），它使定罪取决于对意图的证明，以区分犯罪行为与意外或过失。在侵犯人身与财产的犯罪中，意图显而易见；而在欺诈和市场操纵等白领犯罪中，意图极难证明。

First is the reigning medieval legal principle of mens rea (Latin for ‘guilty mind’) which makes conviction for crime contingent on proof of intent so as to distinguish criminal acts from accident or negligence. Intent is obvious with crimes against person and property, but it’s very difficult to prove with white-collar crimes such as fraud and market manipulations.

严格责任应扩展至许多行业中被容忍的欺诈行为

Strict liability should be extended to cover the fraud tolerated in many industries

白领犯罪检控的第二重障碍，是对违反监管行为的追责与刑事犯罪追责之间的人为割裂。例如，在多数国家，工伤死亡由劳工部门或类似机构调查；财务欺诈与虚假披露则由证券监管机构调查。但监管机构缺乏侦查专业能力，可能与本应监管的行业过从甚密，还可能缺乏独立性——它们与被监管公司之间的「旋转门」便是明证。

A second impediment to prosecution of white-collar crime is the artificial separation between responsibility for regulatory breaches and criminal offences. As examples, workplace fatalities are investigated in most countries within a department of labour or similar; and financial fraud and misreporting are investigated by securities regulators. But regulators lack investigative expertise, can become too close to their industry, and can lack independence, as evidenced by the revolving door between them and the corporations they are supposed to be regulating.

犯罪意图原则与官僚化的白领犯罪调查，保护着人脉深厚的罪犯。要确保问责，就需要引入严格责任制度：让个人及相关公司高管对刑事与监管违规承担法律责任。这一原则已适用于多数交通、产品责任和环境犯罪；去年，英国还引入了一项新的严格责任公司刑事罪名——未能防止欺诈罪。严格责任应扩展至许多行业中被容忍的欺诈行为，并惩罚以破产、工伤死亡和销售缺陷产品为凭据的犯罪性失职。抗辩应仅限于「合理相信该行为合法」，而意图只在量刑时才予以考虑。

The principle of mens rea and the bureaucratic investigation of white-collar crime protect well-connected criminals. Securing accountability requires the introduction of strict liability where individuals and relevant company officers become legally responsible for criminal and regulatory breaches. This already applies to most traffic, product liability and environmental offences, and last year the UK introduced a new strict liability corporate criminal offence of failure to prevent fraud. Strict liability should be extended to cover the fraud tolerated in many industries, and penalise criminal incompetence as evidenced by bankruptcy, workplace fatalities and the sale of faulty products. Defence would be confined to a reasonable belief that the action was legal, and intent is relevant only when determining the sentence.

违反监管的行为与白领刑事犯罪，应统一交由同一支警察队伍调查，使其独立于罪行与肇事者。最佳范例是新加坡（Singapore）：其警察部队设有商业事务局（Commercial Affairs Department），负责调查广泛的金融与公司犯罪，包括欺诈、市场不当行为和违反公司法的行为。

Regulatory breaches and white-collar criminal offences should both be investigated by a common police force, independent of the crime and perpetrators. The best example is Singapore, whose police force has a Commercial Affairs Department that investigates a wide range of financial and corporate offences, including fraud, market misconduct and company law breaches.

最后，让我们谈谈政治变革中常被忽视的「如何变」。1962年，托马斯·库恩（Thomas Kuhn）就其前提条件写下了奠基之作。其中最要紧的一条，是承认现有范式已然失效——选民的失望，以及民众对主要民主国家领袖信心的丧失，已清楚地表明了这一点。一个证据是：在2024年举行选举的每一个发达国家，选民都倒向了反对执政方的一边。

Let us close with the oft-neglected ‘how’ of political transformation. In 1962, Thomas Kuhn wrote the book on its preconditions. The most relevant is accepting that the existing paradigm is ineffective, which is clear from voter dissatisfaction and loss of faith in leaders of the major democracies. As evidence, in elections held in 2024, voters around the world swung against the incumbent government in every developed country that held elections.

时代也站在结构性政治改革一边。总部位于斯德哥尔摩（Stockholm）的国际民主研究所（International Institute for Democracy）在回顾2024年的多场选举时指出，「人们深刻且日益强烈地感到……我们正处于一个历史关头，必须重新审视和评估我们民主文化与制度的力量」。此外，今天的境况与生机勃勃的「咆哮的二十年代」（Roaring 1920s）颇为相似：那个年代同样始于一场震撼世界的疫情之后，见证了新消费技术的迅速普及（汽车、电话、电影、飞机）、文化的蓬勃活力（爵士乐、装饰艺术（Art Deco）、超现实主义（Surrealism）、精神分析），以及经济与金融市场的强劲增长。那个十年以1929年股灾和大萧条（Great Depression）告终，但随后迎来了全面的政治改革（凯恩斯主义（Keynesian）财政政策、成人普选权、政党的整合，以及选民推动的创制提案）。

The times also favour structural political reform. In their review of the many elections of 2024, the Stockholm-based International Institute for Democracy identified ‘a profound and growing … sense that we are at a historical juncture where we must revisit and reassess the strength of our democratic culture and institutions.’ Moreover, today’s conditions resemble those of the dynamic Roaring 1920s, which also began after a shock pandemic, saw rapid introduction of new consumer technologies (cars, the telephone, movies, airplanes), cultural dynamism (jazz, Art Deco, Surrealism, psychoanalysis), as well as strong growth in economies and financial markets. The decade ended in the crash of 1929 and the Great Depression, but they were followed by sweeping political reform (Keynesian fiscal policy, adult suffrage, consolidation of political parties, and voter-driven initiatives).

改革我们的民主制度，从来不缺点子。由于以民主大军姿态和平展示大规模不满的选民总能博得关注，参与人数至少达到总人口百分之三点五的街头抗议几乎稳操胜券。其他行之有效的策略还包括对抗性抗议，比如抵制选举、请愿与游说。

There is no shortage of ideas to reform our democracies. Because voters acting as democratic troops in peaceful demonstration of mass dissatisfaction will always command attention, street protests involving at least 3.5 per cent of the population are almost guaranteed success. Other successful tactics include confrontational protests such as boycotts of elections, petitions and lobbying.

化解民主危机需要更多民主

Tackling the crisis in democracy requires more democracy

公民议会是一条颇有希望的政治改革路径。这种形式在爱尔兰（Ireland）最为人熟知：99名公众成员经随机抽选，以代表该国的人口构成。他们像一支超级陪审团那样，就某个议题进行数日会议的审议，并撰写一份附有建议的报告。审议过的议题包括堕胎、气候变化、老龄化人口的挑战与机遇，以及毒品使用。

Citizens’ assemblies are a promising path to political reform. These are best known in Ireland where 99 members of the public are randomly chosen to represent the country’s demography. They work like a super-jury to consider an issue over some days of meetings, and prepare a report with recommendations. Topics have included abortion, climate change, the challenges and opportunities of an ageing population, and drugs use.

即便是这些温和的提议，也威胁到盘踞已久的政客及其政党、寡头与企业。反对者如此强大且组织严密，以至于改革民主体制必然需要争取体制内人士的支持。

Even these modest proposals threaten entrenched politicians and their parties, oligarchs and corporations. Opponents are so powerful and organised that reforming the democratic system inevitably requires securing insider support.

到了20世纪，西方民主国家已战胜了物质匮乏与政治压迫的残酷。今天公民在收入、技术、健康与知识方面，比他们的祖先富足了许多个数量级。即便如此，基本福祉的若干方面——比如不断增长的收入，以及负担得起的优质住房——正在远离许多人。在公共健康、环境质量、对科学的信任和基础设施可靠性等非经济指标上，表现同样不佳。

By the 20th century, Western democracies defeated the brutalities of material scarcity and political oppression. Today’s citizens are orders of magnitude better off than their ancestors in terms of income, technologies, health, and knowledge. Even so, aspects of basic wellbeing – such as rising income and affordable, quality housing – are receding for many. Performance has also been poor on non-economic measures such as public health, environmental quality, trust in science, and infrastructure reliability.

那么，究竟发生了什么？回顾我们的「结构—行为—绩效」模型，简短的答案是：始于1980年代的新自由主义改革，削弱了民主所依赖的制度。化解民主危机需要更多民主，以打破无能在位者与保守双寡头的垄断地位。具体路径包括：激励更优质的候选人参选并扶持替代性政党，推动制度现代化，以及重新平衡经济权力。

So, what happened? Recalling our structure-conduct-performance model, the short answer is that neoliberal reforms beginning in the 1980s weakened the institutions on which democracy depends. Tackling the crisis in democracy requires more democracy that breaks the monopoly position of ineffective incumbents and conservative duopolies. This comes through incentivising better-quality election candidates and alternative parties, modernising institutions, and rebalancing economic power.
