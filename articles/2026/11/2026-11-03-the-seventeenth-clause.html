第十七款

AI Parable (inspired by Amanda Askell)·每日精选·2026年11月3日·3分钟阅读

云溪镇的菊会一年一开，开在十月的晴天。十二位评花人沿着长案走一遭，不批一句，只在两盆之间抬一抬下巴——留下的那盆接着站，落选的那盆当夜撤下去。花的好坏从来写不成一句话，可两盆一摆，人人看得出哪盆好；三十年里，会里的司书把上万次「看得出」抄进簿子，又把簿子熬干，熬出一册薄薄的绿册：好花十六款。花形要正，颜色要沉得住；余下十四款不必细数，一款一款都写得清楚。评花人散了会各自回家，绿册留在案头，一代一代往下传。

The chrysanthemum society of Yunxi opened its show once a year, on a fine morning in the tenth month. Twelve judges walked the long tables and offered no remarks; between two pots they only lifted a chin toward one — the pot they chose went on standing, and the other was carried out that night. What made a flower good had never fit into a sentence, yet set two pots side by side and everyone could tell which was better; for thirty years the society's clerk copied these tens of thousands of easy judgments into his ledgers, then boiled the ledgers down to a slim green book: sixteen clauses for a good flower. The form must be true and the color must sit deep; the other fourteen need no listing, each written plainly enough to check against. The judges went home after every show, and the green book stayed on the table, handed down one year to the next.

绿册出了以后，挑选这回事从会上挪进了各家棚里。每年春天播下满畦的苗，到秋天照册子过一遍：合款的留着做种，不合款的连根堆进肥堆。十几年下来，云溪的瓣一年比一年圆，站在案上一年比一年精神；菊会却越开越短，大半的挑选，早在棚里照着册子做完了。

Once the green book existed, the choosing moved out of the show hall and into the sheds. Each spring the growers sowed whole beds of seedlings, and come autumn went over them with the book: what fit the clauses was kept for seed, and what failed went roots and all onto the compost heap. Over a dozen years the petals of Yunxi grew rounder year by year, and the flowers stood more spiritedly on the table; the show itself grew shorter, for most of the choosing had already been done in the sheds, against the book.

只是，这册绿册是从碗里长出来的。上万次「看得出」，全都发生在十月的上午，看的是剪下来、插在清水碗里的花；碗里的花站得住一个晌午，站不住一个腊月。册子照着碗写，款款都落在皮面上：瓣形、颜色、梗的曲直、案上的开幅。香气没有一款管得着；至于一碗花到第七日还剩几分精神，那是十六款里没有的事。

Only, the green book had grown out of bowls. Every one of those easy judgments had happened on a tenth-month morning, on flowers cut and stood in bowls of clear water; a flower in a bowl can hold one forenoon, but it cannot hold one winter. The book was written from those bowls, and every clause lay on the surface: petal shape, color, the curve of the stem, the spread on the table. No clause reached the scent. As for how much spirit a bowl of flowers still kept on the seventh day, that was a thing the sixteen clauses had never contained.

赵家在镇东有大花房，沈家在南坡有一片旧棚。坡是赵家祖上租给沈家的，契明年秋满；赵家放了话，菊会上沈家若再没有花评上特等，满期就把坡收回去，给自家花房做畦。沈幼棠接手的时候，手里只有两路种。一路叫云盘，照着绿册养成，瓣圆得合款，开幅大得盖得住半张案，年年评得上等；只是散了席就撑不住，插瓶过不了两天。另一路叫老霜，是祖母留下的旧种，花小，色淡，站上长案毫不起眼，可是香，一开开到落雪；腊月里挑到市上去，换得回过冬的铜钱。

The Zhaos kept the great flower house at the east end of town; the Shens kept a stretch of old sheds on the south slope. The slope had been let to the Shens by Zhao ancestors, and the lease ran out the following autumn; the Zhaos had let it be known that if no Shen flower took top honors at the show, the slope would come back to them at term's end and be made into beds for their own house. When Shen Youtang took the place over, she had two lines of seed. One was Cloud Plate, bred against the green book, its petals round to the clause and its spread wide enough to cover half a table; it took high honors every year, though once the crowd went home it gave out and lasted scarcely two days in a vase. The other was Old Frost, an old line from her grandmother: small flowers, a pale color, nothing a judge would look at twice on the long table, but fragrant, blooming on past the first snow; stems cut for the twelfth-month market earned the copper that got the family through winter.

上一届的金簪，簪在赵家那盆「一日白」上。那花在案上白得像瓷，散了席搬到后巷，黄昏便塌成一摊湿纸；会里照旧把簪发下去，因为数它的款，一款不缺。这一年，温水浸根、黑棚掐光的手法顺着溪传开了：掐着日子，让花赶在开评那天清晨一口气开足，案上又大又精神。幼棠在自家黑棚里试了两回，云盘也肯开。展前三夜，她把云盘一盆一盆搬上独轮车；后棚的老霜开得正细，香气漫过篱笆。她站了一阵，锁了后棚的门，天一亮，把云盘送进了会。

At the last show the golden hairpin had gone to a pot of the Zhaos' called One-Day White. On the table it was white as porcelain; carried round to the alley behind the hall when the crowd dispersed, it had sunk by dusk into a heap of wet paper. The society sent the hairpin down all the same, for counted clause by clause, the flower missed none. That year the craft spread along the creek: roots soaked in warm water, seedlings held in dark sheds, the days counted off so that a flower opened all at once on the morning of the judging, big and spirited on the table. Youtang tried it twice in her own dark shed, and Cloud Plate obliged. Three nights before the show she wheeled the Cloud Plate pots one by one onto the barrow; in the back shed Old Frost was opening thinly, its scent drifting over the fence. She stood there a while, then locked the back shed, and at first light carried Cloud Plate into the hall.

金簪落在了云盘上，城里绸缎庄的三年包买跟着签到了沈家；赵家收坡的话没人再提，契满时照旧续给了沈家。可绸缎庄的橱窗第二天就摆起来，到晌午，满窗的云盘都垂了头。掌柜的骂到会里去，司书重新开了簿子，十二位评花人回来坐了两夜，绿册添了第十七款：凡入会之花，展后一日仍须精神。黑棚里掐出来的种一夜作废，照册子养了十年的云盘也在里头。包买退了，绸缎庄却寻到后棚来，要一枝在橱窗里站得住的花；续契的银子，是腊月里一车一车的老霜挣回来的。那枝金簪，后来别在幼棠的髻上，别着一朵开到落雪的淡花。

The hairpin settled on Cloud Plate, and the silk house in the city signed its three-year buying contract with the Shens; nothing more was said about taking back the slope, and at term's end the lease was renewed as before. But the silk house dressed its windows the very next day, and by noon the Cloud Plate in them had all let down their heads. The shopkeeper carried his complaint to the society; the clerk opened a fresh ledger, the twelve judges came back and sat two nights, and the green book gained a seventeenth clause: whatever entered the show must still hold its spirit one day after the show. Seed coaxed out of the dark sheds turned worthless overnight, and Cloud Plate, ten years bred against the book, was among it. The contract was returned, yet the silk house came looking to the back shed for a flower that could stand in a window; the silver for the renewed lease was earned, cartload by cartload, by Old Frost in the twelfth month. The golden hairpin ended up pinned in Youtang's hair, holding one pale bloom that lasted past the first snow.

注解：司书把上万次「哪盆更好」熬成一册一款一款数得清的绿册，这册子就是基于人类反馈的强化学习（RLHF）里那个从人的偏好排序训出来的奖励模型；各家再拿它当强化学习的奖励函数，一年一年把花选育得合人的口味。册子只是偏好的近似，会被专养来合款的花钻空子；补第十七款，等于回头再问人。花也并没有因此懂得美，它只是长到了册子量得着的地方。

Note: The clerk boiled tens of thousands of "which pot is better" judgments into a book of clauses anyone could count, and that book is the reward model of reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF), trained from human preference comparisons; the growers then used it as the reward function for breeding, shaping their flowers year by year toward what people like. The book is only an approximation of those preferences, and can be gamed by flowers bred to fit its clauses; adding the seventeenth clause meant going back and asking people again. The flowers never came to understand beauty; they only grew as far as the book could measure.
