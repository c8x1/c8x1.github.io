一契两半

AI Parable (inspired by Amanda Askell)·每日精选·2026年11月17日·2分钟阅读

我们七块圩田，围一道总渠过活。渠一头通海，也一年一年往里淤。每隔几年，开春头一个大潮的早晨，七道闸得同时提起：七块圩一夜放出的水合在一处，才够把渠底的淤沙一气推回海里。这是圩约的头一条。我是北圩的水书，叫阿汀，管信，也管账。

Our seven polders live around one main channel. The channel runs to the sea at one end, and silts inward year by year. Every few years, on the morning of the first big spring tide, all seven gates must be lifted at once: the water seven polders release in a single night, joined into one body, is what can push the silt off the channel floor and back to sea in one breath. That is the first line of the polder charter. I am Ating, water-clerk of the North Polder; I keep the letters, and I keep the accounts.

今年大潮在十二日。圩约另有一条：闸要提就七圩同日提；差一圩，水势就散，提了的圩白放一圩水，还要按约受罚。北圩把五年的公积都换了新闸板，赔不起，更等不起——错过这一潮，夏汛的雨就积在圩里出不去，下半年的豆地要泡在水底。十一日黄昏前，这个早晨必须说定。

This year's big tide fell on the twelfth. The charter has another line: if the gates lift, all seven polders lift on the same day. One polder short, and the current scatters — the polders that opened release their water for nothing, and pay the charter fine on top. North Polder had put five years of common fund into new gate boards; we could not pay a fine, and we could not wait either. Miss this tide, and the summer rains would stand in our fields with nowhere to go; the bean fields would spend the second half of the year underwater. By dusk on the eleventh, the morning had to be agreed.

圩与圩隔着水路，冬天董事们不出门，信一律走渡口。渡房有本信簿：凡过水的信，渡工都要抄录一行，寄信的圩压上半契——木契对剖，茬口的锯齿一圩一个样式，两半对上，信便做不得假。信簿放在渡房，按圩约，哪一圩都能来查。

The polders sit apart across the water, and in winter the directors do not travel; letters all go by the ferry. The ferry house keeps a letter register: every letter that crosses the water is copied onto it by the ferryman, with the sending polder's half-tally pressed beside it. The tallies are split lengthwise, each with its own jagged break; when the halves match, a letter cannot be faked. The register sits in the ferry house, and under the charter any polder may come and read it.

腊月里我把日子拟好，六封信发了出去。回信陆续过水：南圩、东坡都回了十二日，字都压着各自的半契。到初八，水柳圩的董会主席蒲九也回了，信上写「十二日，众圩皆允」。我照簿收档，收完多翻了一页——五日前，他给东坡也过过一封信，簿上那行是「十日先开，北圩已允」。两行字都是他的笔迹，两枚半契都对得上茬口。

In the twelfth month I drafted the date and sent six letters across. The replies came back through the water: South Polder and East Slope both wrote the twelfth, each under its own half-tally. On the eighth day of the month, Pu Jiu, chairman of Waterwillow Polder, wrote too: "The twelfth; all polders have agreed." I filed it by the register, and turned one page more — five days earlier, a letter of his to East Slope had also crossed the water, and the register's line for it read: "Open on the tenth; North Polder has agreed." Both lines were in his hand; both half-tallies matched their breaks.

我把信簿上那两行抄了，请渡工对压，发给其余五圩。各圩来查簿的、来对茬口的，在渡房桌边围了一圈。蒲九没到。水柳圩的圩户到了——走外围旧绕河的三条驳船是蒲九一个人的营生，圩里的豆地却跟北圩一样泡不起。旧绕河的渡钱养的是蒲九，总渠一通，那笔钱就断了。

I copied the two lines from the register, had the ferryman countermark them, and sent them to the other five polders. They came to read the register and match the breaks, and crowded a ring around the ferry-house table. Pu Jiu did not come. Waterwillow's households did — the three barges running the old outer route were Pu Jiu's business alone; the polder's bean fields could no more afford to drown than ours. The outer route's tolls fed Pu Jiu; once the channel ran, that money stopped.

圩户们当场换了水柳圩押契的人，蒲九的三条船照圩约折了罚金，抵误期迟报的费。十一日黄昏前，七枚半契压上了同一个日子：十二日。

The households replaced Waterwillow's pledged delegate on the spot, and Pu Jiu's three barges were fined under the charter to cover the cost of the late reckoning. Before dusk on the eleventh, seven half-tallies were pressed beside the same date: the twelfth.

十二日早晨，潮到平流，七道闸一气提起。七圩的水在渠心撞成一股，从我们的新闸板下涌出去，把两年的淤沙推过沙口，一直推回海里。入夏的雨水有处可去了，五年的公积没有白花。那两行簿字我抄了一份，钉在北圩的账本里——不为记蒲九，为记那本簿。

On the morning of the twelfth, at the slack, the seven gates went up in one motion. The seven polders' waters met in the channel's heart and pushed as one, past our new gate boards, carrying two years of silt out past the sand mouth and back into the sea. The summer rains would have somewhere to go, and the five years of common fund had not been spent for nothing. I copied the two register lines and pinned them into North Polder's account book — not for Pu Jiu's sake, but for the register's.

注解：拜占庭将军问题（the Byzantine generals problem）：一群只能递信、不能见面的人，须在「同动或同停」上说定一个决定，而其中有人对不同的人说不同的话——渡房那本信簿让蒲九的两封信当面对质，谎才拆穿。边界：半契挡得住冒名，挡不住说谎；若无这样的簿，说谎的人占到总数三分之一，再怎么两两对照也救不回来。

Note: the Byzantine generals problem: a group who can only send letters and never meet must settle one decision — move together or hold together — while one of them says different things to different people. The ferry-house register set Pu Jiu's two letters face to face, and that is how the lie broke. Boundary: a matched tally stops forgery, not lying; without such a register, once the liars reach a third of the whole, no amount of comparing letters will save the day.
