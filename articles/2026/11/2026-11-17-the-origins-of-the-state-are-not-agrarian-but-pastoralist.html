国家的起源并非农耕，而是游牧

Lhamsuren Munkh-Erdene·Aeon·2026年11月17日·26分钟阅读·原文

运输是文明的支柱，这正是控制霍尔木兹海峡（Strait of Hormuz）能赋予全球筹码的原因。由于石油驱动着全球九成以上的交通运输，这种权力的根源并不在于这条水道本身，而在于对全球流动性的掌控。切断全部石油供应，几乎会使所有运输、通信与补给线陷入瘫痪：城市崩溃、国家解体、社会瓦解、人口锐减，把人类扔回前工业时代。而没有役畜，人类恐怕至今仍停留在石器时代。

Transportation is the backbone of civilisation, which is why controlling the Strait of Hormuz grants global leverage. Because oil fuels more than 90 per cent of global transit, the source of this power lies not in the waterway, but in the control of global mobility. Halting all oil supplies paralyses virtually all transport, communication and supply lines: cities collapse, states disintegrate, societies break down and populations die off, throwing humanity back to the pre-industrial age. Without transport animals, humanity would still be back in the Stone Age.

没有运输，就不可能有城市与国家，因为人口与资源便无从集中。没有运输，就意味着没有任何超越人力的运输，徒步行走与肩扛背负成为仅有的移动方式。这一底线决定了人类共同体的规模与结构。生产局限于能搬回家的东西，从而划定了人类可以定居与拓殖的范围。生产能力与资源供给使共同体保持小规模，并迫使它们分裂与扩散。规模决定结构：小，就意味着不分层、人人平等。归根结底，行走与背负这种体能上的固有限度，构成了对不平等的天然免疫：它使共同体保持小规模、平等且自治，使其土地免遭征服。

No city or state can exist without transportation, for there can be no concentration of population and resources. No transportation means no extrahuman transportation, leaving walking and carrying as the only means of transit. This baseline determined the size and structure of human communities. Production was limited to what could be carried home, circumscribing the areas where humans could settle and colonise. Production capacity and resource availability kept communities small, forcing them to split and disperse. Because size dictates structure, small meant undifferentiated and egalitarian. Ultimately, the inherent limit of the physical capability of travel and portage formed a natural immunity to inequality: it kept communities small, egalitarian and autonomous, leaving their lands beyond conquest.

只要这一约束存在，人类共同体便与亚马逊盆地（Amazon Basin）或新几内亚高地（New Guinea Highlands）的社群相差无几。动物的驯化与舟船的发明解放了人类，使长距离的动员成为可能。凭借这种新获得的能力，共同体不再需要迁就眼前的环境，而是可以营建城市、整合广袤的地域——条条大路通罗马。是运输——而非谷物——缔造了国家与文明，并使之延续至今。让我们从当下回溯国家的起源，来审视这一力量。

As long as this constraint existed, communities resembled those of the Amazon Basin or the New Guinea Highlands. The domestication of animals and the invention of boats liberated humanity, making long-distance mobilisation possible. With this newfound capacity, communities no longer needed to fit their immediate environments; instead, they could build cities and integrate expansive areas - all roads lead to Rome. Transportation - not grain - is what brought the state and civilisation into existence, and what continues to maintain them. Let's examine this power by looking from the present back to the origin of the state.

谁主宰了海洋，谁就主宰了世界！没有海军与空军，美国便无法掌握全球触角，也无法在世界各地投送力量。欧洲殖民帝国同样如此，归根结底，它们是由军舰与炮舰锻造而成的。在更早的时代，维京人（Vikings）靠长船构筑帝国，阿兹特克人（Aztecs）靠独木舟，埃及人靠内河舟船，罗马人、希腊人与腓尼基人（Phoenicians）则靠桨帆战船。

Whoever rules the waves rules the world! Without its navy and air force, the United States cannot command global reach or project power worldwide. The same was true of European colonial empires; ultimately, warships and gunboats forged them. In earlier periods, Vikings built their empire on longships, Aztecs on canoes, Egyptians on riverboats, and Romans, Greeks and Phoenicians on galleys.

常言道：马上得天下易。在陆地上，马属动物——驴与马——锻造了国家与文明，而美洲驼之于印加（Inka）帝国亦然。乌尔（Ur）的库加（kunga）战车展示了美索不达米亚城市的军事实力，阿拜多斯（Abydos）的驴群则证明了驴在早期埃及王权中的关键作用。到公元前三千纪末期，欧亚大陆中部的游牧民已在辛塔什塔（Sintashta）为他们的君王整辆殉葬马战车——那是当时的高技术、高价值战争机器。马战车后来成为商与周之权力的象征；公元前1046年，周便是凭借四千辆战车击溃了商。

Conquering the world on horseback is easy, as the saying goes. On land, equids - donkeys and horses - forged states and civilisations, while the llama did the same for the Inkas. Ur's kunga chariots project the military might of Mesopotamian cities, and the Abydos donkeys demonstrate their vital role in early Egyptian kingship. By the end of the 3rd millennium BCE, Central Eurasian nomads were entombing entire horse chariots - the high-tech, high-value war machines of their time - to honour their kings in Sintashta. Horse chariots later symbolised the power of the Shang and the Zhou; the latter deployed 4,000 chariots to overwhelm the former in 1046 BCE.

「骑手登上历史舞台，宛如一个新的人类品种」——德国社会学家亚历山大·吕斯托（Alexander Rüstow）在1950年代称之为半人马。欧亚游牧民早已将其坐骑培育为现代家马，为一个将从根本上重塑世界的帝国时代铺平了道路。在近四千年里，马主宰着人类历史。即便对海上强权而言，马依然是征服的关键；从迦太基（Carthage）到大英帝国，无不依赖马匹向内陆扩张。弗朗西斯科·皮萨罗（Francisco Pizarro）征服印加，是马匹之力对抗一个以美洲驼运输为基础、本质上靠步行的帝国的绝佳例证。马还使北美的科曼奇人（Comanche）得以缔造自己的帝国，并与欧洲强权抗衡近一个世纪。

'The rider appears on the stage of history like a new breed of man' - a centaur, as the German sociologist Alexander Rüstow put it in the 1950s. Eurasian nomads had long bred their mounts into the modern domestic horse, paving the way for an age of empires that would fundamentally reshape the world. Horses dominated human history for nearly four millennia. Even for maritime powers, horses remained key to conquest; from Carthage to the British, all relied on horses for inland expansion. Francisco Pizarro's conquest of the Inkas is a prime illustration of the power of horses against an essentially pedestrian empire built on llama transportation. Horses also enabled the Comanche in North America to forge their empire and compete with European powers for nearly a century.

科曼奇帝国？他们真有用来建构国家的谷物吗？没有，他们没有谷物——更谈不上任何农业。养马与猎野牛维系着他们的生计。事实上，欧亚草原的所有强权——包括蒙古人——都依靠游牧经济。他们最充裕的，是超乎人力的运输；他们最匮乏的，恰是农产品。归根结底，建构国家并不需要谷物，却离不开超越人力的运输。

The Comanche Empire? Did they actually have grain to build a state? No, they had no grain - nor any agriculture, for that matter. Horse husbandry and bison hunting sustained their livelihood. In fact, all the Eurasian steppe powers - including the Mongols - relied on pastoralism. What they enjoyed most was an abundance of extrahuman transportation; what they lacked most was agricultural produce. Ultimately, building a state does not require grain, but it does demand extrahuman transportation.

且看罗马帝国与蒙古帝国的政治地理：罗马人所能抵达之处，即舰船所能抵达之处；蒙古人所能抵达之处，即马匹所能抵达之处。欧洲殖民帝国同样如此，一切归根结底都是超乎人力的运输之力。驴、马以及所有其他畜群动物，皆是游牧民的创造。正是超越人力的运输使国家与文明成为可能；这将游牧经济置于国家与文明的起点，也把欧亚草原置于人类历史的中心。

Look at the political geography of the Roman and Mongol empires: the Romans could reach where ships could reach, and the Mongols could reach where horses could reach. The same is true of European colonial empires; it all comes down to the power of extrahuman transportation. Donkeys, horses and all other herd animals are the creations of pastoralists. Extrahuman transportation made the state and civilisation possible; this places pastoralism at the origin of the state and civilisation, and the Eurasian steppe at the centre of human history.

然而，中国中心主义（Sinocentric）范式——那种以中国为中心、占据主导地位的人类历史与文明观——在结构上抹去了超乎人力的运输，连同游牧经济、草原帝国与卡里斯玛在国家形成中的角色，从而否定了欧亚草原作为人类历史首要中心的地位。正是这一框架塑造着我们当前对世界历史的理解，留给我们一种以困陷、宿命与绝望为底色的图景。唯有意识到这一范式的存在，我们才能摆脱这种阴郁的展望，更透彻地理解我们所处的世界，并开辟一条更为光明的道路。

Yet the Sinocentric paradigm - the predominant vision of human history and civilisation with China at its centre - structurally erases extrahuman transportation and, with it, pastoralism, steppe empires and charisma from state formation, thereby negating the Eurasian steppe as the prime centre of human history. This framework shapes our current understanding of world history, leaving us with a view defined by entrapment, doom and despair. Becoming aware of this paradigm is essential if we are to break free from this bleak outlook, better comprehend the world we live in, and chart a brighter future.

学者们虽批评世界历史书写带有欧洲中心论偏见，但其概念框架与叙事却深受中国中心主义的影响。这是因为国家起源——进而文明诞生——的问题，正是塑造我们世界历史观的核心问题。这一观点最新且最具权威的表述，是斯科特（James C. Scott）的《反谷》（Against the Grain: A Deep History of the Earliest States，2017），以及他的《不被统治的艺术》（The Art of Not Being Governed，2009）。约拉姆·迈沙尔（Joram Mayshar）及其同事的〈国家起源：土地生产力抑或可榨取性〉（The Origin of the State: Land Productivity or Appropriability）则是这一观点在经济学上的重述。读者也许会反驳说，斯科特几乎只聚焦于美索不达米亚，鲜少提及中国。然而，他的底层框架实际上重新表述了美国东方学家拉铁摩尔（Owen Lattimore）影响深远的著作《中国的内亚边疆》（Inner Asian Frontiers of China，1940）所确立的范式。正因如此，斯科特不仅频频提及「黄河沿岸的黄土土壤」，还直接引用拉铁摩尔的话，声称「在古代中国的黄土核心区，灌溉带来了惊人的回报」。

While scholars critique world history for its Eurocentric bias, its conceptual framework and narrative are profoundly Sinocentric. This is because the origin of the state - and consequently the birth of civilisation - is the question that shapes our vision of world history. The latest authoritative iteration of this view is James C Scott's Against the Grain: A Deep History of the Earliest States (2017), along with his The Art of Not Being Governed (2009). 'The Origin of the State: Land Productivity or Appropriability' by Joram Mayshar and colleagues is its economic restatement. Scott, readers might counter, focuses almost exclusively on Mesopotamia and rarely mentions China. Yet his underlying framework actually rearticulates the paradigm established by the American Orientalist Owen Lattimore's influential work, Inner Asian Frontiers of China (1940). Precisely for this reason, Scott not only frequently mentions 'the loess soils along the Yellow River', but also, directly quoting Lattimore, claims that 'irrigation was spectacularly rewarding in the loess core of ancient China'.

拉铁摩尔试图为中华民国对蒙古、满洲国（Manchukuo）、西藏与东突厥斯坦（Eastern Turkestan）的领土主张提供正当性。为此，他提出了谷物论，把国家与文明的起源定位于黄河流域，将中国塑造为「东方的摇篮」。拉铁摩尔把弗雷德里克·杰克逊·特纳（Frederick Jackson Turner）的美国边疆学说套用到这一区域，将中国框定为文明中心，而把上述领土贬为「内亚」（Inner Asia）——不过是一片类似美国西部的地缘历史边疆。这样一来，拉铁摩尔便抛弃了他先前信奉的传统征服论——即中国早期国家及其后的大多数帝国皆由欧亚游牧征服者锻造，而非本土自身动力的产物。对征服论的否定，意味着否定超乎人力的运输与游牧经济作为通向国家的力量，转而推崇定居生活与谷物农业。

Lattimore sought to legitimise the Republic of China's claims over Mongolia, Manchukuo, Tibet and Eastern Turkestan. To this end, he advanced the grain thesis to locate the origins of statehood and civilisation in the Yellow River Valley, positioning China as the Cradle of the East. By mapping Frederick Jackson Turner's American frontier thesis onto the region, Lattimore framed China as the civilised centre and reduced the aforementioned territories to 'Inner Asia' - a mere geohistorical frontier akin to the American West. In doing so, Lattimore abandoned the traditional conquest theory - a framework he had previously espoused - which held that both China's early states and most of its subsequent empires were forged by Eurasian nomadic conquerors rather than indigenous dynamics. The rejection of the conquest theory was a repudiation of extrahuman transportation and pastoralism as forces that led to statehood, and an exaltation of sedentism and grain agriculture instead.

使拉铁摩尔论断在学界根深蒂固的，是卡内罗（Robert Carneiro）的限制（circumscription）假说，以〈国家起源的一种理论〉（A Theory of the Origin of the State）为题于1970年发表于《科学》（Science）杂志。不仅是常规的历史叙事，一大批著名思想家——包括迈克尔·曼（Michael Mann）、贾雷德·戴蒙德（Jared Diamond）、弗朗西斯·福山（Francis Fukuyama）与尤瓦尔·诺亚·赫拉利（Yuval Noah Harari）——都以不同面貌普及了卡内罗的理论。即便大卫·格雷伯（David Graeber）与大卫·温格罗（David Wengrow），最终也仍未超出这一框架。

The theory that entrenched Lattimore's thesis in academia is Robert Carneiro's circumscription hypothesis, published in the journal Science as 'A Theory of the Origin of the State' (1970). Not just conventional narratives, but a wide array of prominent thinkers - including Michael Mann, Jared Diamond, Francis Fukuyama and Yuval Noah Harari - popularised Carneiro's theory in different guises. Even David Graeber and David Wengrow ultimately remain within this framework.

曼的《社会权力的来源》（The Sources of Social Power，1986）借鉴卡内罗「受限农业土地」的概念，提出没有「逃生路线」的生态之笼与社会之笼；戴蒙德的《枪炮、病菌与钢铁》（Guns, Germs, and Steel，1997）同样聚焦于「无处可逃」的地区，以分离出产生国家兴起所需人口压力的环境条件。福山的《政治秩序的起源》（The Origins of Political Order，2011）则更为直白地界定了原生国家形成的首要条件：「人口必须在物理上受到约束，从而密度得以提升」，以及「臣民在遭受胁迫时无法逃走」。当戴蒙德2002年在《自然》（Nature）上撰文称，农业带来的可储存粮食盈余「导致了社会分层、政治集中化与常备军」时，他不过是把拉铁摩尔—卡内罗的人口压力教条合理化为一个「自催化」过程。赫拉利的《人类简史》（Sapiens，2011）及其疫病缠身的「奢华陷阱」，斯科特的《不被统治的艺术》与《反谷》，以及格雷伯与温格罗哀叹人类是一个「被自己作物奴役」的物种——归根结底，都源自同一个谷物论。

Drawing on Carneiro's concept of circumscribed agricultural land, Mann's The Sources of Social Power (1986) posits ecological and social cages with no 'escape route', while Diamond's Guns, Germs, and Steel (1997) similarly focuses on regions with 'nowhere to flee' to isolate the environmental conditions that generate the population pressure needed for the rise of the state. Fukuyama's The Origins of Political Order (2011), however, is far more direct in delineating the primary conditions for pristine state formation: 'that population needs to be physically constrained so that it increases in density' and 'that subjects cannot run away when coerced'. When Diamond wrote in Nature in 2002 that agricultural storable food surpluses 'led to social stratification, political centralisation and standing armies', he was merely rationalising the Lattimore-Carneiro population-pressure dogma as an 'autocatalytic' process. Harari's Sapiens (2011), with its disease-ridden 'luxury trap', Scott's The Art of Not Being Governed, along with his Against the Grain, and Graeber and Wengrow's lamentation that ours is a species 'enslaved to its crops' all ultimately stem from the same grain thesis.

诚然，拉铁摩尔—卡内罗框架依赖人口压力模型，主张农业与定居引发人口过剩，从而迫使等级制度与国家的产生。然而，卡内罗的限制理论要求美索不达米亚、尼罗河谷与秘鲁沿海等地区已彻底人满为患。但实证性的聚落调查却显示，这些地区的人口密度根本不足以耗尽可耕地，有些地区在国家形成初期甚至经历了人口流失。既然不存在催化国家形成的人口压力，考古学发现便使卡内罗的理论不攻自破。

Indeed, the Lattimore-Carneiro framework relied on a population-pressure model, arguing that agriculture and sedentism triggered overpopulation, forcing the creation of hierarchies and states. However, Carneiro's circumscription thesis required areas such as Mesopotamia, the Nile Valley and the Peruvian coast to be entirely overcrowded. Instead, empirical settlement surveys revealed that these areas lacked the demographic density to exhaust arable land, with some even experiencing depopulation during early state-formation. Because no population pressure existed to catalyse state formation, archaeological findings invalidated Carneiro's theory.

斯科特的新意，既不在于「政治作物」的修辞，也不在于他高度聚焦的「冲积土或黄土」上的「受限生态位」，而在于他理论上的障眼法：动态的气候限制。他笔下肥沃的冲积土或黄土，映照着卡内罗受限的农业土地，只不过规模极小——塑造它的是千年大旱，而非地理屏障。归根结底，斯科特挪用了卡内罗的资源集中模型——即一片吸引周边人群汇聚的资源丰饶栖息地——却以干旱作为驱动力为其增压。总之，干旱一方面把地貌重塑为局部之笼，一方面驱使人口向内集聚，正是这一点使斯科特得以把乌鲁克（Uruk）区区250公顷的占地单独划为那块受限的农业土地。斯科特的模型在此是一次挽救中国中心主义范式的巧妙操盘。而我们将会看到，这一范式脱胎于冷战政治，与考古证据并不相符。

Scott's novelty lies neither in his rhetoric of 'political crops', nor in his laser-focused 'circumscribed ecological niches' of 'alluvial or loess soils', but rather in his theoretical sleight of hand: dynamic climatic circumscription. His fertile alluvial or loess soils mirror Carneiro's circumscribed agricultural land, though on a minuscule scale, engineered by a millennium-long drought rather than geographic barriers. Ultimately, Scott repurposes Carneiro's model of resource concentration - a resource-rich habitat that draws people from surrounding regions - yet he supercharges it with the driving force of drought. In sum, drought - simultaneously reconfiguring the physical terrain into a localised cage and driving populations inward - enables Scott to isolate the minuscule, 250-hectare footprint of Uruk as the circumscribed agricultural land. Scott's model here is a clever manoeuvre to salvage the Sinocentric paradigm. That paradigm, as we shall see, owes its formation to Cold War politics and is at odds with archaeological evidence.

无论出于气候还是地理，农业之笼在本质上都是一种反流动性的叙事框架，在结构上将游牧经济从国家的形成中抹去。然而，这种抹除并不仅是概念上的缺陷，它已写入该范式的基因之中，因为这个框架从根基上就是针对流动性而建构的，并且极力主张将流动性排除在外。抹除以认知剽窃的形式出现：把牲畜驯化与游牧经济的发明权归于农耕者，用极度严苛的定义推迟游牧经济出现的年代，并诉诸排斥性的叙事框架。

Whether climatic or geographical, the agricultural cage is inherently an anti-mobility framing that structurally erases pastoralism from the formation of the state. This erasure is, however, not just a conceptual flaw; it is written into the paradigm's DNA, as the framework is fundamentally built against mobility, and actively argues for its exclusion. The erasure takes the form of epistemic theft by way of attributing the authorship of domestication of livestock and pastoralism to farmers, delaying the date of the advent of pastoralism through hyper-restrictive definitions, and relying on exclusionary framing. While the initial framework was a politically motivated, instrumental scheme, subsequent models have resulted from paradigmatic biases.

引致这种抹除式叙事的问题，并非一般意义上的国家起源，而是国家在今日中国这片土地上的出现——具体而言，它是土生土长的，还是诞生于游牧征服。这一问题关联着一个激烈的政治两难：满洲，以及由此延伸的大清帝国（Daiching Empire，即清王朝），究竟是中国帝国还是内亚帝国？这场争论是一个关于中国之定义及其疆域划定的政治问题。具体来说，中国应当被界定为限于明代行省的「中国本土」，还是涵盖大清帝国疆域的「帝制中国」？

The question that led to this erasive framing was not the origin of the state in general, but its emergence in what is now China - specifically, whether it was indigenous or born of nomadic conquest. This issue was linked to a raging political dilemma: did Manchuria, and by extension the Daiching Empire (also known as the Qing dynasty), constitute a Chinese or an Inner Asian empire? This debate was a political question concerning the definition of China and the delineation of its territory. Specifically, should China be defined as 'China proper', restricted to the Ming provinces, or as 'China imperial', encompassing the domains of the Daiching Empire.

随着满洲国的宣告成立，这场争论升级为加剧的政治危机。日本学界主张，大清帝国是一个征服了中国的内亚国家，满洲人是一个理应独立的独特民族。中国民族主义政权及其学界则针锋相对，主张该帝国本质上属于中国，满洲自古以来便是中国不可分割的领土。与此同时，当时的批判性学界在实质上认同日本的观点；关于中国国家起源的主导框架是入侵论，即中国早期国家及其后诸多帝国皆由欧亚游牧征服者锻造。至关重要的是，直到那时，拉铁摩尔本人正是这一理论的拥护者。

The controversy escalated into a heightened political crisis with the declaration of Manchukuo. Japanese scholarship argued that the Daiching Empire was an Inner Asian state that had conquered China, and that the Manchus were a distinct people deserving independence. The Chinese nationalist regime and its scholarship countered, arguing that the empire was inherently Chinese, and that Manchuria had been an integral part of China since antiquity. Meanwhile, contemporary critical scholarship essentially agreed with the Japanese view; the dominant framework for the origins of the state in China was the invasion theory, which argued that early Chinese states, as well as many subsequent empires, were forged by Eurasian nomadic conquerors. Crucially, until then, Lattimore himself had been a proponent of this theory.

中华民国本是依托门户开放政策（Open Door policy，由美国外交官于1899年草创）而成立的法理虚构。然而，正是这个政权宣称对蒙古、满洲、东突厥斯坦与西藏拥有主权。到1930年代，门户开放体制已然瓦解：外蒙古已于1911年宣布独立，西藏于1912年独立，满洲国则于1932年脱离中国，并吞了内蒙古东部；内蒙古其余地区由亲日政府统治，东突厥斯坦亦于1933年宣布独立。

The Republic of China was a legal fiction enabled by the Open Door policy (authored by US diplomats in 1899). Yet, this very regime claimed sovereignty over Mongolia, Manchuria, Eastern Turkestan and Tibet. By the 1930s, however, the Open Door order was in tatters: Outer Mongolia had declared independence in 1911, Tibet in 1912, and Manchukuo had broken away in 1932, absorbing Eastern Inner Mongolia. The rest of Inner Mongolia was ruled by a Japanese-allied government, while Eastern Turkestan declared independence in 1933.

承认大清帝国为内亚国家，意味着放弃中国对上述所有地区的主张，剥掉这个政权据拉铁摩尔估算接近其宣称疆域三分之二的领土。对西方而言更糟的是，苏联将成为最终的受益者——这是该政权及其主要缔造者美国都决意阻止的结局。这个政权必须确保其领土在性质上本属中国，而这就需要学术研究来支撑这一主张。这要求驳斥入侵论，并重新把中国框定为「东方的摇篮」。拉铁摩尔的著作《中国的内亚边疆》完成的正是这项任务。

Recognising the Daiching Empire as an Inner Asian state meant forfeiting China's claim over all of these regions, stripping the regime of what Lattimore estimated at nearly two-thirds of its claimed territory. Worse still for the West, the Soviet Union was poised to become the ultimate beneficiary - an outcome the regime and its primary architect, the US, were determined to prevent. The regime had to ensure that its territory emerged as inherently Chinese, and scholarship was needed to substantiate this claim. This required refuting the invasion theory and reframing China as the Cradle of the East. Lattimore's book, Inner Asian Frontiers of China, accomplished precisely this.

拉铁摩尔彻底背弃了自己先前的立场，在中国民族主义学术的基础上，援引特纳的美国边疆学说，把蒙古、满洲、中国突厥斯坦（今日的新疆）与西藏重新框定为中国的边疆。要把这些领土贬抑为一片纯粹的地理区域——「内亚」——并把它们改造成单纯的边疆，就必须把这个区域描绘成无国家、部落化与蛮荒的。倘若蒙古等地仍被视为国家，它们便无法被概念化为边疆，而只能被视为独立的邻邦；因此，他的框架所需要的是一片「无主之地」（terra nullius）。

Executing a complete about-face from his earlier position, building on Chinese nationalist scholarship, and drawing on Turner's American frontier thesis, Lattimore reframed Mongolia, Manchuria, Chinese Turkistan (today's Xinjiang) and Tibet as the frontiers of China. Reducing these territories to a mere geographic area - Inner Asia - and rebranding them as mere frontiers required rendering the region stateless, tribal and barbarian. Had Mongolia and the others remained states, they could not have been conceptualised as frontiers, but rather as independent neighbours; his framework thus demanded a terra nullius.

要驳斥入侵论，就必须解除它最强大的征服武器——流动性——并用另一种框架取而代之。拉铁摩尔的做法，是把流动性塑造为一种反国家力量，用进化唯物主义替换入侵论。他此前曾主张流动性为蒙古政治组织提供了「滚雪球」效应，如今却反其道而行，率先把流动性等同于涣散。如此一来，他便把先前被理解为终极动员与集中机制的东西，变成了一个天然具有离散性的要素。这种重新框定使征服论归于失效，把这一理论的决定性武器变成了反国家的力量。

Refuting the invasion theory required disarming it of its most potent weapon of conquest - mobility - and supplanting it with an alternative framework. Lattimore accomplished this by casting mobility as an anti-state force, substituting the invasion thesis with evolutionary materialism. Inverting his earlier argument that mobility provided a 'snowball' effect to Mongol political organisation, Lattimore was the first to equate mobility with dispersal. He thereby transformed what was previously understood as the ultimate mobilising and concentrating mechanism into an inherently dispersive element. This reframing neutralised conquest theory, turning its defining weapon into an anti-state force.

此外，拉铁摩尔确立流动性为游牧经济的本质特征，不动性为灌溉农业的本质特征，进而提出财富与权力正相关、流动性与财富及权力负相关的主张，由此推出了他的「财富对抗流动性」论题。他断言「积累并储存起来的谷物，毫无疑问是基本实财的标准」，从而确保了「财富对流动性的优势地位」。如此，拉铁摩尔不仅抹去了流动性作为缔造国家的力量，还把谷物确立为这种力量本身。事实上，拉铁摩尔正是谷物论的始作俑者，而在他的框架中，国家呈现为一座谷仓。

Furthermore, by establishing mobility as the defining feature of pastoralism, and immobility as that of irrigated agriculture, Lattimore posited both a positive correlation between wealth and power and a negative one between mobility and wealth/power, thus advancing his 'wealth versus mobility' thesis. And by asserting that 'grain, accumulated and stored, was beyond question the standard of real wealth in essentials', he guaranteed 'the ascendancy of wealth over mobility'. In this manner, Lattimore not only erased mobility as the state-making force but also established grain as that very force. In fact, Lattimore is the author of the grain thesis, and in his framework, the state emerges as a grain store.

因此，流动性在此并未被视作单纯的分散离散，而是被当成了致贫机制，而非国家建构的驱动力。拉铁摩尔（Owen Lattimore）宣称「唯有贫穷的游牧者才是纯粹的游牧者」，而文化人类学家萨林斯（Marshall Sahlins）等后学则指出，游牧者的财富「是一种负担」，它「很快就会与他的流动性构成矛盾」。哈扎诺夫（Anatoly Khazanov）将这一论题又推进一步，主张畜牧经济天然不自给自足（non-autarkic），甚至具有反自给自足（anti-autarkic）的倾向，因而从根本上无力孕育国家。

Mobility, thus, emerged not merely as dispersal, but as a mechanism of destitution, rather than a driver of state-building. While Lattimore declared that 'it is the poor nomad who is pure nomad,' intellectual successors like the cultural anthropologist Marshall Sahlins observed that a nomad's wealth 'is a burden' that 'soon poses a contradiction to his mobility.' Anatoly Khazanov pushed this thesis further, arguing that pastoralism is inherently non-self-sufficient (non-autarkic) or even anti-autarkic, thereby rendering it fundamentally incapable of producing a state.

与此同时，拉铁摩尔绘制了一幅由环境决定的社会版图——森林狩猎氏族、草原畜牧部落与黄土农业国家——并叠加广度与强度两个维度，由此确立人口压力为国家形成的主要催化剂。既然农业总体上被认定为唯一能够生成国家的经济形态，灌溉农业的集约程度之高便足以引发严重的人口压力。拉铁摩尔辨认出一整片连续的农业地带，它被「大片不宜人居的地带」所环围——一侧是「荒凉的高地」与内亚（Inner Asia）的「屏障地带」，另一侧是太平洋——由此，他把中国绘制成一块被围限（circumscribed）的农业之地。

At the same time, by mapping environmentally determined societies - specifically, forest-hunting clans, steppe-pastoral tribes, and loess-agricultural states - and adding extensive and intensive dimensions, Lattimore established population pressure as the primary catalyst for state-formation. While agriculture in general emerged as uniquely capable of generating a state, irrigated agriculture was intensive enough to trigger severe demographic strain. Identifying a continuous agricultural expanse enclosed by 'an immense mass of inhospitable terrain' - namely, 'the bleak highlands' and 'the Inner Asian barrier lands' on one side, and the Pacific Ocean on the other - Lattimore mapped China as a circumscribed agricultural land.

为了把中国塑造成「东方的摇篮」（Cradle of the East），并将内亚收归为中国边陲，拉铁摩尔把内亚畜牧描绘成农业扩张的副产品，把全部历史能动性都归于农夫。他宣称游牧者「在文化上、很可能也在血统上与早期中国人相近」，从而将两个人群设定为同源。把草原畜牧的年代定在公元前4世纪——视之为中国集约农业的一支——使拉铁摩尔得以垄断国家形成的时间线，并消解入侵理论。

To cast China as the Cradle of the East and claim Inner Asia as a Chinese frontier, Lattimore framed Inner Asian pastoralism as a byproduct of agricultural expansion, attributing all historical agency to farmers. By claiming pastoralists were 'akin by culture and probably by blood to the early Chinese', he cast both populations as homologous. Dating steppe pastoralism to the 4th century BCE - as an offshoot of intensive Chinese agriculture - allowed Lattimore to monopolise the state-formation timeline and neutralise the invasion theory.

为了卡住畜牧起源的时间线，他炮制了一套极为苛刻的定义：他以过于宽泛为由，摈弃了「真正的」「严格的」「常态的」之类的范畴，仅把「畜牧游牧」（pastoral nomadism）定义为「游牧的极端」，或「草原游牧的严格或极端阶段」。这一框架把畜牧等同于骑马术的出现，实际上也就等同于骑兵的兴起。

To stall the timeline of pastoralism's emergence, he devised a hyper-restrictive definition: dismissing categories like 'true', 'strict' or 'normal' as overly broad, he defined 'pastoral nomadism' exclusively as 'the extreme of nomadism' or 'the strict or extreme phases of steppe nomadism'. This framework equates pastoralism with the advent of horseback riding and, in effect, with the rise of cavalry.

诚然，所有这些论断都与当时的学术知识——包括考古学与语言学——相抵触。彼时考古学已经表明：骑马游牧与动物风格艺术约于公元前1500年出现于黑海地区，随后向东传播，经蒙古直至鄂尔多斯（Ordos）——拉铁摩尔对这一事实心知肚明。同样，马伯乐（Henri Maspero）等语言学家也已证明，汉语与突厥语族、蒙古语族及通古斯语族「毫无关系可言，甚至连一点遥远的联系都没有」。尽管如此，拉铁摩尔将畜牧动物的驯化归功于农夫、以及他对畜牧那套极度苛刻的定义，还是在学界成了范式。哈扎诺夫把拉铁摩尔的论题浇筑定型：他声称唯有拥有确定植物性剩余的定居群体才能驯化动物，并把「纯畜牧游牧」刻画为游牧的「最极端形式」。就这样，流动性与畜牧被从国家形成的进程中除名了。

To be sure, all of these claims conflicted with contemporary scholarly knowledge, including archaeology and linguistics. Back then, archaeology had shown that horse-riding nomadism and animal art emerged around 1500 BCE in the Black Sea region before spreading eastward through Mongolia to Ordos - a fact Lattimore was aware of. Similarly, linguists such as Henri Maspero had shown that the Chinese language 'has no relation whatever, not even a distant connection with' Turkic, Mongolic and Tungusic languages. Nonetheless, Lattimore's attribution of pastoral animal domestication to farmers and his hyper-restrictive definition of pastoralism became paradigmatic in the field. Khazanov cemented Lattimore's thesis: he claimed that only sedentary groups with definite vegetable surpluses could domesticate animals, and characterised 'pure pastoral nomadism' as 'the most extreme form' of nomadism. In this manner, mobility and pastoralism were written out of the state-formation process.

然而，泽德（Melinda Zeder）的现代研究表明：「植物与动物的驯化大致发生在同一时期，最初管理的迹象……至少可以上溯到距今11,500校准年前[即公元前9550年]，甚至更早。」此外，我们现在知道，早期动物驯化「只发生在其野生祖先地理分布范围的一个子集之内」，且「往往展开于远离植物驯化中心的地区」。至关重要的是，多数畜群动物是经由猎物路径（prey pathway）而非共生路径（commensal pathway）得到驯化的，而畜牧者驯化马属动物——驴与马——凭借的却是定向路径（directed pathway）。然而，斯科特（James C. Scott）一面承认农业与畜牧约在12,000年前同时出现，一面却宣称「被视为农业之延伸的专业化畜牧」给国家带来了「分散与流动的问题」，从而继续延续拉铁摩尔那套被推迟的时间线与限制性的定义。

However, modern scholarship by Melinda Zeder shows that 'plant and animal domestication occurred at roughly the same time, with signs of initial management … reaching back to at least 11,500 cal BP [ie, 9550 BCE], if not earlier.' Furthermore, we now know that early animal domestication 'only took place within a subset of the geographical distribution of the wild ancestors', unfolding 'often in regions distant from centres of plant domestication.' Critically, while most herd animals were domesticated via the prey pathway rather than a commensal one, pastoralists domesticated equids - donkeys and horses - via a directed pathway. Yet, by claiming that 'specialised pastoralism, seen as an outgrowth of agriculture' confronts the state with a 'problem of dispersal and mobility', Scott continues to perpetuate Lattimore's delayed timeline and restrictive definition - even while acknowledging that agriculture and pastoralism emerged concurrently roughly 12,000 years ago.

到20世纪70年代，拉铁摩尔的框架已成该领域的主导正统。新进化论的领军人物塞尔维斯（Elman Service）盛赞其为「开辟道路的生态学理论」，弗里德（Morton Fried）则承认拉铁摩尔「提出过一个激发了我自己理论的理论」。最终，新进化论将征服理论取而代之，成为主流范式。弗里德的社会分层图式（平等社会、等级社会、阶层社会与国家）与塞尔维斯的社会政治类型学（游群、部落、酋邦与国家）把集约农业定位为唯一足以支撑国家的经济基础，既强化了拉铁摩尔的灌溉农业论题，也为进化论的唯物主义奠定了基石。于是，国家——无论作为上层阶级的工具，还是作为管理性建制——从根本上显现为一种经济工具和一种进化产物。

By the 1970s, Lattimore's framework had become the dominant orthodoxy. Elman Service, a leading neo-evolutionist, hailed it as 'the pathfinding ecological theory', while Morton Fried acknowledged that Lattimore 'proposed a theory that stimulated my own'. Ultimately, neo-evolutionism displaced conquest theory as the mainstream paradigm. By positioning intensive agriculture as the sole economic foundation capable of sustaining the state, Fried's social stratification scheme (egalitarian, rank, stratified, and state) and Service's sociopolitical typology (band, tribe, chiefdom, and state) reinforced Lattimore's irrigated agriculture thesis while anchoring evolutionary materialism. Consequently, the state - either as an instrument of the upper class or as a managerial institution - emerges fundamentally as an economic instrument and an evolutionary product.

弗里德与塞尔维斯分别从社会分化与功能分化中推导出国家，二者都拒斥征服理论。塞尔维斯的模型基本上是对19世纪资产阶级唯物主义的精细化，而弗里德的框架强调由内部经济分化驱动的冲突，与马克思主义模型相合。卡内罗（Robert Carneiro）同样拒斥征服理论；尽管他纳入了战争与强制，其模型仍不外乎弗里德框架的一种铺陈。作为新进化论思想最具影响力的模型之一，卡内罗的框架正是这一范式偏见的直接产物，其实际效果是将拉铁摩尔的框架放大至全球规模。中国被确立为「东方的摇篮」，内亚则被贬入部落世界和「蛮夷的『外暗之地』」。流动性、畜牧与部落制被实质性地混为一谈，直到1999年汉弗莱（Caroline Humphrey）与斯尼思（David Sneath）解构了「游牧」这一范畴以及「流浪游牧者」的迷思，才为斯尼思日后揭穿部落模型——一个彻头彻尾的错误再现——铺平了道路。与此同时，在一批运用原始史料的历史学术中，阿特伍德（Christopher P Atwood）令人信服地论证了部落模型根本站不住脚。

By deriving the state from either social or functional differentiation, both Fried and Service rejected conquest theory. Whereas Service's model is basically a refinement of 19th-century bourgeois materialism, Fried's framework aligns with the Marxist model by emphasising conflict driven by internal economic differentiation. Carneiro likewise rejected conquest theory; despite his incorporating warfare and coercion, his model remains an elaboration of Fried's framework. As one of the most influential models of neo-evolutionary thought, Carneiro's framework was a direct product of this paradigmatic bias, and it effectively resulted in the global scaling of Lattimore's framework. China was established as the Cradle of the East, while Inner Asia was relegated to a tribal world and 'the barbarian "outer darkness"'. Mobility, pastoralism and tribalism were essentially conflated until Caroline Humphrey and David Sneath deconstructed the category of 'nomadism' and the myth of the wandering nomad in 1999, paving the way for Sneath to later debunk the tribal model as a complete misrepresentation. Meanwhile, in a corpus of historical scholarship utilising primary sources, Christopher P Atwood compellingly demonstrates that the tribal model is untenable.

然而，斯科特不仅把拉铁摩尔的框架连同其中早已被驳倒的理论原封不动地复活，还将其重新包装，以服务于自己的无政府主义事业。承袭拉铁摩尔，斯科特坚称内亚畜牧——连同其人口——不过是中国农业扩张的副产品。他断言修筑长城既为抵御外侵、也为圈禁中国的纳税人，这同样与拉铁摩尔的立场如出一辙。斯科特最惊人的一招，是把流动性抬升为自由的一种基本形式。拉铁摩尔笔下自由游牧者的观念——他们怀有「自由的观念、自由移动、自由迁徙」——与斯科特的无政府主义论题严丝合缝，促使他把流动性推崇为一种保存自由的力量，并将其重新塑造为根本性的防国家策略。

However, Scott not only resurrects Lattimore's framework in its entirety along with its long-refuted theories but also repackages it to fit his own anarchist project. Following Lattimore, Scott maintains that Inner Asian pastoralism - along with its population - was merely a byproduct of Chinese agricultural expansion. His assertion that the Great Wall was built as much to contain Chinese taxpayers as to repel external invasions likewise mirrors Lattimore's position. Scott's most spectacular move is his elevation of mobility into a primary form of freedom. Lattimore's notion of free nomads - with their 'idea of freedom, free movement, free migration' - nicely dovetailed with Scott's anarchist thesis, prompting him to valorise mobility as a freedom-preserving force and recast it as the fundamental state-preventing strategy.

这一观念在《人类新史》（The Dawn of Everything，2021）中得到了清晰的回响：格雷伯（David Graeber）与温格罗（David Wengrow）将「移动的自由」理论化为「基本的、甚至是首要的自由形式」之一。与此同时，斯科特的谷物论题及其背后的农业范式总体上强势地引导着我们的思维，使两位作者绝望地宣告「我们被困住了」，且「我们是一个已沦为自身作物之奴的物种」。如此，流动性便成为一种根本上的反国家、反支配、护持自由的力量——只可惜它在那万能的谷物面前全然无力，而我们连同一切非人力的运输手段，都已沦为谷物的奴仆。中国中心主义（Sinocentric）范式正是这样被构筑出来的，它正是如此将畜牧从国家形成中抹除。这一范式的目标是消灭流动性，其理论框架在结构上便使学术思考与探究天然地不利于畜牧。归根结底，这正是中国中心主义范式在思想上困住我们的方式。

This conceptualisation clearly finds its echo in The Dawn of Everything (2021), where Graeber and Wengrow theorise 'the freedom to move' as one of the 'fundamental, even primary, forms of freedom'. At the same time, Scott's grain thesis, in particular, and agricultural paradigm, in general, powerfully guide our thinking, leading the two authors to declare in despair that 'we got stuck' and that 'ours is a species that has become enslaved to its crops.' Mobility thus emerged as a fundamentally anti-state, anti-domination and freedom-preserving force, albeit one that proved impotent against the all-powerful grain to which we, along with all our extrahuman transportation, are enslaved. This is how the Sinocentric paradigm was devised and how it erases pastoralism from state-formation. The objective of this paradigm is to eliminate mobility, and its theoretical frameworks inherently structure scholarly thinking and enquiry against pastoralism. Ultimately, this is how the Sinocentric paradigm traps us intellectually.

随着流动性与畜牧被抹除、内亚被贬入部落蛮荒，中国中心主义范式把中国的国家形成垄断性地归于中国人，同时把中国确立为昔日大清（Daiching）疆域之内唯一的帝国与文明。然而，历史上确有内亚帝国存在——尤其是大清帝国（Daiching Empire）——而该政权对内亚之主权主张能否自圆其说，正系于对这一个个帝国身份的认定。这就要求把它们彻底抹除。其手法——拉铁摩尔本人早在1934年描绘门户开放政策强加于蒙古人、满人、维吾尔人与藏人头上的严酷现实时便已点明——就是「否认他们的历史，令其交出自己的民族与种族身份」，并最终在观念上征服他们。值得注意的是，这正是当今中国政府所奉行的政策。

With the erasure of mobility and pastoralism and the relegation of Inner Asia into tribal barbarity, the Sinocentric paradigm monopolised state-formation in China for the Chinese while establishing China as the sole empire and civilisation within the former Daiching domain. However, there were Inner Asian empires - especially the Daiching Empire - on whose identification the integrity of the regime's claim over Inner Asia depended. This demanded their total erasure. The method - as Lattimore himself observed in 1934 while depicting the grim reality that the Open Door policy had imposed upon the Mongols, the Manchus, the Uighurs and the Tibetans - was to 'deny their history and surrender their national and racial identities' and, ultimately, to conquer them conceptually. Notably, this is the exact policy that the current Chinese administration pursues today.

这意味着把所有征服过中国的帝国统统收编为本质上属于中国的帝国，并构造出一个万世长存的中华帝国。要做到这一点，必须解决两个难题：征服的事实，以及征服帝国自身的独特身份。由于这些征服并非孤立事件，而是次数多得惊人，一套精巧的框架便应运而生。

This meant the appropriation of all the empires that conquered China as inherently Chinese, and the construction of an everlasting Chinese empire. To achieve this, two problems had to be solved: the reality of conquests and the distinct identity of the conquering empires. Because these conquests were not isolated incidents but occurred in overwhelming numbers, a sophisticated framework had to be devised.

为了收编内亚帝国，拉铁摩尔设计了「中国王朝循环」理论——一件目的论的装置，借着一层决定论式历史法则的外衣，为中国民族主义的主张提供强有力的正当化。通过把一切帝国化约为区区王朝，这一循环制造出一个绵延不绝的中国国家的幻象：历朝历代只是前后相替，而帝国本身岿然不动。于是，征服被抹除为区区统治家族的更迭，帝国自身却完好无损。其结果是，中华帝国显得独一无二地永无止境——一个万世不易的政治体，与包括罗马帝国（Roman Empire）在内的其他一切历史强权终将消亡的轨迹形成鲜明对照。

To appropriate Inner Asian empires, Lattimore devised the theory of Chinese dynastic cycles, a teleological device that powerfully legitimises Chinese nationalist claims through a veneer of deterministic, historical law. By reducing all empires to mere dynasties, this cycle creates the illusion of an enduring Chinese state where successive dynasties simply replace one another. Conquests are thus erased as mere changes in ruling houses while the empire itself remains intact. Consequently, the Chinese empire emerges as uniquely endless - an everlasting polity standing in stark contrast to the mortal trajectories of all other historical powers, including the Roman Empire.

为了让这个万世帝国在本质上属于中国，并否认征服帝国的自身身份——与英国的「积累型」模式、俄国的「合并型」模式相对照——拉铁摩尔锻造出「第三大类型」：中国的「吸收型」帝国。与「征服型」帝国不同，这是一个「防御性帝国」——它不去征服别人，而是被别人征服，却通过吸收征服者而依然是中国。于是，这个政治体便不是作为征服性的政治强权，而是作为以同化为本的文化共同体而呈现。更有甚者，既然中国被确立为文明本身，内亚被贬为蛮荒，那么一切内亚的征服便都成了蛮人向文明的迈进。汉化（Sinicisation）乃是「一个长期有效的提议：向任何对进步感兴趣的个体或群体提供变得文明的机会」——这就是文明开化的使命。

To make this everlasting empire inherently Chinese and to deny the identity of the conquering empires - in contrast to British 'accumulative' and Russian 'incorporative' models - Lattimore forged 'the third great type': the Chinese 'absorptive' empire. Unlike 'conquest' empires, this was a 'defensive empire' - one that did not conquer but was conquered, yet remained Chinese by absorbing its conquerors. Consequently, the polity emerged not as a conquering political power, but as a cultural community predicated on assimilation. Furthermore, as China was established as civilisation itself, and Inner Asia was reduced to barbarity, all Inner Asian conquests represented a barbarian advancement toward civilisation. Sinicisation was a 'standing offer of the opportunity to become civilised, to any individual or group interested in progress' - the civilising mission.

然而，拉铁摩尔这些高度个人化的概念建构恰恰颠倒了现实。就定义而言，帝国是一种扩张性、兼并性的政治体；界定帝国的是扩张，驱动帝国的是征服。帝国也绝非文化同质；它的本性恰恰是无所不包、等级森严而异质多元的。因此，拉铁摩尔那中国式吸收型帝国确乎是一个真正的异数。何况，数百种彼此不能通话的语言变体分属至少八个主要语群，单是汉语族（Sinitic）——遑论作为整体的中国——就已体现出巨大的文化多样性。

However, Lattimore's highly idiosyncratic conceptualisations invert reality. Empire, by definition, is an expansionist, incorporative polity; what defines an empire is expansion, and what drives it is conquest. It is also anything but culturally homogeneous; its very nature is all-encompassing, hierarchical and heterogeneous. Thus, Lattimore's Chinese absorptive empire is a veritable anomaly. Moreover, with hundreds of mutually unintelligible varieties divided across at least eight major language groups, even the Sinitic group - let alone China as a whole - represents immense cultural diversity.

事实上，拉铁摩尔的「吸收型」帝国正是中国民族主义同化叙事的政治化身，它把中国描绘成一块坚实的、同质化的整体——「种族、语言与文化皆同质」，在那里「蛮夷尽数为中国人所吸收；他们变得和其他中国人一样，都是中国人」。于是，满人尽数被中国人同化，变得和其他中国人并无二致；因此，满洲帝国也就和其他任何王朝一样，是一个中国王朝。倘若所有蒙古人也遭逢了满人的命运，中华帝国便会成为世上前所未见的最大连片陆上帝国；那样一来，德雷尔（June Teufel Dreyer）所谓的「天下套路」（tianxia trope）——中国中心主义的世界乃更佳世界的观念——便会拥有一个可畏的先例。

In fact, Lattimore's 'absorptive' empire was the political embodiment of the Chinese nationalist narrative of assimilative power, depicting China as a solid, homogenising mass, 'homogeneous in race, language, and culture', where the 'barbarians were all absorbed by the Chinese; they became Chinese like any other Chinese.' Hence, the Manchus were all assimilated by the Chinese and became Chinese like any other; therefore, the Manchu empire was a Chinese dynasty like any other. Had all the Mongols shared the Manchu fate, the Chinese empire would have been the largest contiguous land empire the world had ever seen; then, what June Teufel Dreyer called the 'tianxia trope' - the idea that a Sinocentric world is the better one - would have possessed a formidable precedent.

尽管如此，拉铁摩尔的框架还是旋即取得了霸权地位。中国被实体化为一个万世帝国，其历史呈现为一连串不曾中断的王朝循环，并伴随着可以预见的汉化模式。与「中国人的世界秩序」相配合，这一范式凝定为不容置疑的教条。如汉学家欧立德（Mark C Elliott）所言，拉铁摩尔崛起为「所有中国史学者背后的思想教父」，亦为中国边疆研究的奠基人。直到20世纪90年代，柯娇燕（Pamela Crossley）、罗友枝（Evelyn Rawski）与欧立德等学者才终于打破行列，向汉化命题发起挑战，催生了日后的新清史（New Qing History）学派。该学派证明：大清帝国是一个内亚帝国，满人直至帝国覆亡始终保有自己的独特身份。事实上，正如笔者近期研究所强调的，大清帝国是一个征服并殖民了中国的满蒙贵族帝国。

Nevertheless, Lattimore's framework immediately became hegemonic. China was reified as an everlasting empire, and its history emerged as unbroken dynastic cycles with a predictable Sinicisation pattern. Paired with the Chinese world order, the paradigm crystallised into unchallenged dogma. Lattimore emerged 'as the intellectual godfather of all historians of China' (as the sinologist Mark C Elliott put it), and the founder of Chinese frontier studies. Only in the 1990s did scholars like Pamela Crossley, Evelyn Rawski and Elliott finally break ranks to challenge the Sinicisation thesis, instigating what would become the New Qing History school, which demonstrates that the Daiching Empire was an Inner Asian empire and that the Manchus preserved their distinct identity until its very demise. In fact, as my recent scholarship highlights, the Daiching Empire was a Manchu-Mongolian aristocratic empire that conquered and colonised China.

尽管这一范式推行了一场彻头彻尾的认识论改造，它却始终未能完全压制批判性的学术。相反，它催生出种种刺目的反常，以致这框架本身反倒成了反常。其中最大的反常，便是欧亚草原诸帝国的兴起：在人类历史的大部分时间里，这些帝国支配着各个农业地区——包括今日中国的大部分乃至全部。

Though the paradigm enacted a wholesale epistemic overhaul, it never succeeded in entirely suppressing critical scholarship. Instead, it produced jarring anomalies that effectively rendered the framework itself an anomaly. The greatest of these anomalies is the rise of the Eurasian steppe empires that dominated agricultural regions - including much or all of present-day China - for much of human history.

然而，按照这一范式的裁定，欧亚草原上受制于地理、生态、经济与政治的种种约束，不可能存在国家——更遑论帝国。如斯科特所论，畜牧乃是头号的防国家与避国家策略，欧亚草原正是典型的非国家空间。可是，自历史的黎明直至20世纪，欧亚草原始终是众多帝国的家园，其中许多征服并统治了毗邻的农业地区。这对该范式构成了巨大的反常；更进一步，由于这些游牧帝国——最显著者是蒙古帝国（Mongol Empire）——从根本上塑造了世界，人类历史本身在这个框架内部竟也成了反常。这一反常从根本上动摇了该范式：一种把人类历史判为反常的理论，其自身便固有反常，因为理论本应解释现实，而不是摈斥现实。

However, as the paradigm dictates, there can be no state - let alone an empire - on the Eurasian steppe due to geographical, ecological, economic and political constraints. As Scott argues, pastoralism is the foremost state-preventing and state-evading strategy, and the Eurasian steppe is the quintessential nonstate space. Yet, from the dawn of history down to the 20th century, the Eurasian steppe has been the home of numerous empires, many of which conquered and ruled neighbouring agricultural regions. This constitutes a massive anomaly to the paradigm; indeed, because these nomadic empires - most notably the Mongol Empire - fundamentally shaped the world, human history itself becomes an anomaly within this framework. This particular anomaly fundamentally undermines the paradigm: a theory that renders human history anomalous is inherently anomalous itself, for a theory is meant to explain reality, not dismiss it.

悖论还要更深，它从根本上将中国中心主义范式连根拔起。依照这一框架，早期区域国家皆由谷物农耕者建立，而帝国则永远是本质意义上的中国。然而，中国最早的国家——商与周——并非黄河谷物的造物，而是西部内亚征服的造物。再者，自约公元前1600年商的兴起，到1912年大清帝国的覆亡，内亚游牧者始终统治着农耕人口。近三千年来，中国整体或其局部被吸纳进他们的帝国，该地区的大多数国家均为游牧者所建，其中包括多数幅员最广、国祚最长的帝国。倘若谷物果真是国家形成的主要引擎，这一切本不该发生；事实恰恰相反。

The paradox runs deeper, fundamentally uprooting the Sinocentric paradigm at its core. According to this framework, early regional states were founded exclusively by grain agriculturalists, and the empire was always inherently Chinese. However, the earliest states in China - the Shang and the Zhou - were not the making of Yellow River grain, but of western Inner Asian conquests. Moreover, from the rise of the Shang around 1600 BCE to the fall of the Daiching Empire in 1912, Inner Asian nomads consistently ruled over agricultural populations. China, in whole or in part, was absorbed into their empires for nearly three millennia, with nomads building the majority of the region's states, including most of its expansive and longest-lasting empires. If grain were the primary engine of state formation, none of this should have occurred; instead, the exact opposite would have been true.

事实上，现代学术日益把中国的早期国家形成归因于游牧征服——这实际上印证了先于拉铁摩尔问世的诸种征服理论框架。梅维恒（Victor H Mair）的研究表明：从始于商周的起点，到大清的终点——其间也包括赫赫有名的唐——中国始终是内亚帝国缔造者们持续不断的创造。就连秦与汉，也带有「很可能是中央欧亚（Central Eurasia）的遗产」。尤为惊人的是，该地区历史上的83个政治体中，仅有31个源出汉语族。梅维恒的结论是，这些北方征服「对黄河流域最早国家的创建[以及]欧亚庞大帝国的建造负有责任」。欧亚草原由此显露真容：它才是国家形成与文明的真正摇篮。

Indeed, modern scholarship increasingly attributes early Chinese state formation to nomadic conquest - effectively validating the conquest theory frameworks that predated Lattimore. Victor H Mair's research demonstrates that, from the beginning in the Shang and Zhou, to the end in the Daiching - including the celebrated Tang - China was a constant creation of Inner Asian empire-builders. Even the Qin and Han carried a 'likely Central Eurasian heritage'. Strikingly, out of 83 historical polities in the region, only 31 were of Sinitic origin. Mair concludes that these northern conquests 'were responsible for the creation of the first states in the Yellow River Valley [and] the building of vast empires in Eurasia.' The Eurasian steppe thus comes to light as the true cradle of state formation and civilisation.

学术愈是向前推进，入侵理论便愈获印证，中国中心主义范式便愈见崩塌。然而斯科特依旧抹除欧亚草原诸帝国，将蒙古人、满人、匈人（Huns）、莫卧儿人（Mughals）与奥斯曼人（Ottomans）一律化约为「蛮夷『明星』」。他坚称，这些帝国「与更早、更小的非国家人群——狩猎采集者、刀耕火种者、沿海觅食者、牧人——并无种类上的不同，而后者正是劫掠小国并与小国贸易的人群」。

The more scholarship advances, the more the invasion theory is validated, and the more the Sinocentric paradigm crumbles. Scott, however, still erases the Eurasian steppe empires by reducing the Mongols, the Manchus, the Huns, the Mughals and the Ottomans to 'barbarian "stars"'. He insists that they 'were no different in kind from earlier and smaller nonstate peoples - hunters and gatherers, swiddeners, coastal foragers, herdsmen - who raided small states and traded with them.'

进化论的唯物主义把国家形成奠基于经济，将国家化约为单纯的制度性上层建筑，从而把经济之外的强力与征服整个排除在方程之外。于是，国家便成了富裕、人口众多而勤勉的社会所独享的禁脔与应得之物。国家的兴起不仅被描述为可以预见，更被描述为自动而必然——在斯科特与迈沙尔（Mayshar）等学者看来，这一假定如此不证自明，以至于他们根本不屑加以论证。这一范式转移从根本上改变了我们对政治权力之起源与本质的理解。

By grounding state-formation in the economy - rendering the state a mere institutional superstructure - evolutionary materialism eliminates extra-economic force and conquest from the equation altogether. Consequently, the state emerges as the exclusive preserve and just deserts of wealthy, populous, industrious societies. Its ascent is framed not just as predictable, but as automatic and inevitable - an assumption so self-evident to scholars like Scott and Mayshar et al that they do not bother to address it at all. This paradigm shift fundamentally alters our understanding of the origin and nature of political power.

与之形成鲜明对照的是，韦伯（Max Weber）的理论提供了一个唯心主义框架，其明确提出正是为了对抗进化论唯物主义。在这种观点看来，历史由伟大的观念、新的启示、强大的信仰与开创性的发明所推动——韦伯将这些变革性力量凝练为一个单一概念：魅力（charisma）。「魅力」，他在《经济与社会》（Economy and Society，1921）中写道，「是一种伟大的革命力量」——是「历史上那种特殊的、具有创造性的革命力量」。魅力——无论它「属于宗教、伦理、艺术、科学、政治或其他类型」——居于一切人类制度、组织与技艺的根基。因此，政治、宗教、艺术与科学的种种建制，诸如国家、教会与学术界，都是一代代魅力型或天才式领导留下的遗产。而国家——军事魅力与魅力型战争首领（warlord）征服的造物——正是其中最关键者。由此，每一项技术突破——从非人力运输（extrahuman transportation）到iPhone——归根结底都是魅力的创造。

In sharp contrast, Max Weber's theory provides an idealist framework explicitly formulated to counter evolutionary materialism. In this view, history is driven by great ideas, new revelations, powerful beliefs and groundbreaking inventions - transformative forces that Weber conceptualised under a single term: charisma. 'Charisma,' he writes in Economy and Society (1921), 'is the great revolutionary force' - 'the specifically creative revolutionary force of history'. Charisma - whether it 'be of a religious, ethical, artistic, scientific, political, or other kind' - sits at the root of all human institutions, organisations and arts. Consequently, the institutions of politics, religion, art and the sciences, such as the state, the church and academia, are legacies of generations of charismatic or gifted leadership. The state - a creation of military charisma and charismatic warlord conquest - is the most critical of them. As such, every technological breakthrough - from extrahuman transportation to the iPhone - is ultimately a creation of charisma.

然而，恰恰是弗里德（Morton Fried）与塞维斯（Elman Service），把韦伯这一伟大的革命力量从国家形成乃至整个历史中抹除了。他们挪用了韦伯的政治权威框架，却将其肢解得面目全非。在《国家与文明的起源》（Origins of the State and Civilization，1975）中，塞维斯「对韦伯的思想施以了某种暴力」，把韦伯的魅力型权威对应到「平等—分支型社会」（egalitarian-segmental societies），把传统型权威对应到「酋邦」（chiefdoms），把法理型权威对应到「古代文明的政府」，以此「极好地概括」了那些社会中的领导形态。就这样，塞维斯把韦伯的魅力型权威与传统型权威一并逐出了历史与文明。至于魅力，塞维斯与弗里德更是异口同声，把它放逐到史前的原始状态、超自然的领域或疯狂之中——正如弗里德本人在《政治社会的演化》（Evolution of Political Society，1967）中所言，那不过是「一个魅力型疯子，恰好又是最大亲属群体的谱系首领」。如此一来，他们不仅实际上抹除了韦伯心目中建国的主要动力，还把它渲染成奇异的、疯狂的、部落式的、野蛮的东西，并将其放逐到内亚（Inner Asia）。这使内亚既成为军事魅力的摇篮，也成为它的坟墓，而内亚诸帝国则被贬为军事疯子们的过度行径。

However, it is precisely Fried and Service who erased Weber's great revolutionary force from state-formation as well as history. They co-opted Weber's framework of political authority but mutilated it, rendering it unrecognisable. 'Doing some violence to Weber's thought,' in Origins of the State and Civilization (1975), Service mapped Weber's charismatic authority onto 'egalitarian-segmental societies', his traditional authority onto 'chiefdoms', and his legal authority onto 'the government of archaic civilisations' 'to epitomise very well the leadership' in those societies. Thus, Service proscribed Weber's charismatic and traditional authorities from history and civilisation. As for charisma, both Service and Fried unanimously banished it to pre-historic primitivity, to the sphere of the supernatural, or to madness - 'a charismatic madman who just happens to be the genealogical leader of the largest kin group,' as Fried himself put it in Evolution of Political Society (1967). In doing so, they not only effectively erased Weber's principal agent of state-building but also made it exotic, mad, tribal and barbarian, exiling it to Inner Asia. This transformed Inner Asia into both the womb and tomb of military charisma, and Inner Asian empires into the excesses of military madmen.

由于二人还把传统型权威从国家与文明中抹除，他们便都把官僚制——韦伯法理型权威的终极表现——设定为国家的决定性形态，从而搭建起一种理论脚手架，令一切前现代的政治形态（如家产制，patrimonialism）都变得不可见。然而在韦伯看来，官僚制是一种非人格化的法治统治，其中官僚所服从的唯有「法律」。事实上，韦伯的法理型权威是对现代自由宪政国家及其德国变体「法治国」（Rechtsstaat）的抽象；他的官僚制是现代文官体系；他的官僚则是现代文官。

Since they also erased traditional authority from the state and civilisation, they both posited bureaucracy - the ultimate expression of Weber's legal authority - as the definitive form of the state, producing a theoretical scaffolding that renders all premodern political formations, such as patrimonialism, invisible. However, for Weber, bureaucracy is an impersonal rule of law where 'what [the bureaucrat] obeys is only "the law".' In fact, Weber's legal authority is an abstraction of the modern liberal constitutional state (and its German variant, the Rechtsstaat); his bureaucracy is a modern civil service; and his bureaucrat is a modern civil servant.

尽管韦伯指出「官僚制最早是在家产制国家中发展起来的」，他仍将其与自己的法理型权威及与之相应的官僚制断然区分开来。传统型权威之下的官僚制服务的是「主人」或国王，而非非人格化的规则或法律。正因如此，韦伯将其识别为「家产制官吏」（patrimonial officialdom），并系统地将其与「官僚制官吏」（bureaucratic officialdom）相对照。事实上，家产制国家，即家产制王朝国家，正是韦伯传统型权威的首要体现，并构成一切前宪政国家的主要形式。然而，进化论唯物主义者把韦伯的传统型权威贬放到酋邦之中，从而把家产制国家从历史中清除出去，实际上将国家与官僚制等量齐观。

Though Weber noted that 'bureaucracy has first developed in patrimonial states', he categorically distinguished it from his legal authority and its corresponding bureaucracy. Bureaucracy under traditional authority serves the 'master' or the king rather than impersonal rules or the law. For this reason, Weber identifies it as 'patrimonial officialdom' and comprehensively contrasts it with 'bureaucratic officialdom'. In fact, the patrimonial state, or patrimonial dynastic state, is the prime embodiment of Weber's traditional authority and serves as the major form of all pre-constitutional states. However, by consigning Weber's traditional authority to chiefdoms, evolutionary materialists purged the patrimonial state from history, effectively equating the state with bureaucracy.

以官僚制来定义国家——或将国家等同于官僚制——无异于用官僚制取代君主制、贵族制与民主制。国家是一种主权的统治；因此，并不存在名为官僚制的统治形式或政治体制。官僚制既非统治，也非政治。没有任何主权者或统治者是经由任命而产生的。主权与任命互相排斥。不可能存在被任命的主权者：一经任命，其人便是仆役，而非主权者。同样，也不可能有通过攫取、选举或指定而产生的官僚制和官僚；职位之攫取与官僚制互相排斥。如果说统治排斥任命，那么攫取便排斥官僚制。官僚制追随统治并服务于统治；官僚制不过是国家的行政机器。归根结底，不被执行的规则不是法律，不在主权之下的土地不是领土，不经统治者任命的权威不是官僚制。正是统治创生出法律、领土与官僚制。中国中心主义（Sinocentric）范式将国家等同于官僚制，不仅把韦伯的理论上下颠倒，还误导了我们。

Defining the state by bureaucracy - or equating it with bureaucracy - is tantamount to replacing monarchy, aristocracy and democracy with bureaucracy. The state is a sovereign rulership; hence, there is no form of rulership or political regime called bureaucracy. Bureaucracy is neither rulership nor political. No sovereign, or ruler, is made by appointment. Sovereignty and appointment are mutually exclusive. There can be no appointed sovereign: if appointed, the officeholder is a servant, not a sovereign. Likewise, there can be no bureaucracy and no bureaucrat by virtue of appropriation, election or designation; the appropriation of office and bureaucracy are mutually exclusive. If rulership excludes appointment, appropriation excludes bureaucracy. Bureaucracy follows rulership and serves it; bureaucracy is merely the administrative apparatus of the state. Ultimately, no rule is law if it is not enforced, no land is territory if it is not under sovereignty, and no authority is a bureaucracy if it is not appointed by a ruler. It is rulership that creates law, territory and bureaucracy. By equating the state with bureaucracy, the Sinocentric paradigm not only turned Weber's theory upside down but also misled us.

颇具讽刺意味的是，韦伯实际上把前现代的「中央集权且高度发达的行政」构想为征服统治的产物。为了维持支配，国家发展出一套镇压机器来驯化其臣民。出于必然，这套机器「在被征服的领土上最为强大，因为那里持续威胁着」征服者。韦伯指出：

Ironically, Weber actually conceptualises the premodern 'centralised and highly developed administration' as a product of conquest rule. To maintain domination, the state develops a repressive apparatus to domesticate its subjects. By necessity, it becomes 'strongest in conquered territories, because of the continuous threat to' the conquerors. Weber notes:

征服者出于必然，必须亲自垄断权力，尤其是军事权力。然而，由于语言、文化及其他地方性障碍，他们不得不倚赖当地的沟通中介。可是，赋予这些中介世袭的军事权力，无异于另立与之抗衡的权力中心——满人早期建置「三藩」（Three Feudatories）即是明证。既能把权力集中于自己手中、又能高效驯化并管理被征服民众的最有效手段，便是任命易于罢免的民政官员。

Conquerors by necessity had to monopolise power, especially military power, themselves. Yet, because of linguistic, cultural and other local barriers, they had to rely on local interlocutors. However, giving them hereditary military power amounted to creating rival power centres, as the early Manchu creation of the Three Feudatories demonstrates. The most effective means to concentrate power in their own hands, and at the same time efficiently domesticate and administer the conquered population, was to appoint easily removable civilian administrative officials.

然而，学者们——包括斯科特（James C. Scott）、福山（Francis Fukuyama）、格雷伯（David Graeber）与温格罗（David Wengrow）——都把国家等同于官僚制，以搜寻官僚制的痕迹来寻找国家。于是，斯科特自信地断言：「正是在这类环境中，最早的官僚制国家才最有可能兴起。」格雷伯与温格罗更进一步，提出了一种本质上将国家定义为官僚制的新概念——国家乃是「主权、官僚制与一个竞争性政治场域的结合」。显然，让·博丹（Jean Bodin）与托马斯·霍布斯（Thomas Hobbes）的主权，或韦伯的暴力垄断，都不足以构成国家。的确，格雷伯与温格罗一面「撇开『国家』谈论主权」，一面搜寻「一台庞大的官僚机器」来寻找国家。归根结底，成为国家即是成为官僚制。有鉴于此，福山在2011年宣称：「唯有中国在马克斯·韦伯所界定的意义上创造了现代国家……发展出了一套中央集权、统一划一的官僚行政体系。」继而他又宣称：「我把中国当作国家形成的范式，并追问为什么其他文明没有复制它所走的道路。」福山由此将中国定位为常态，把世界其余部分——包括希腊、罗马乃至整个欧洲——塑造成「偏离常规」的异例，亦即拉铁摩尔（Owen Lattimore）所说的「野蛮人瘟疫」（barbarian plague）。中国中心主义范式就这样把中国置于「天下」的中心与顶点。

However, scholars - including Scott, Fukuyama, Graeber and Wengrow - equate the state with bureaucracy, and search for traces of bureaucracy to find the state. Thus, Scott confidently asserts that 'it was only in such settings that the first bureaucratic states were likely to arise.' Graeber and Wengrow go further to advance a novel conceptualisation that essentially defines the state as bureaucracy, 'as a combination of sovereignty, bureaucracy and a competitive political field.' Clearly, Jean Bodin's and Thomas Hobbes's sovereignty or Weber's monopoly of violence is not enough to constitute the state. Indeed, Graeber and Wengrow discourse 'on sovereignty without "the state"' and search for 'a great bureaucratic machine' to find a state. Ultimately, to be a state is to be a bureaucracy. For this reason, Fukuyama in 2011 declares that 'China alone created a modern state in the terms defined by Max Weber … in developing a centralised, uniform system of bureaucratic administration'. Consequently, by declaring: 'I take China as a paradigm of state formation and ask why other civilisations didn't replicate the path it followed,' Fukuyama positions China as the norm, thereby casting the rest of the world - including Greece, Rome and Europe as a whole - as a 'divergent' abnormality, that is, what Lattimore called a 'barbarian plague'. The Sinocentric paradigm thus places China at the centre and the summit of 'All under Heaven'.

我们本想通过研究国家的主流理论以及最早国家的深历史，来了解国家的起源。然而，我们真的学到了关于国家起源的什么吗？抑或，正如斯科特所言，我们学到的仅仅是那些「可以作为课税基础」的东西——即国家的「生产基础」？迈沙尔（Mayshar）及其同事同样主张「等级制的出现源于转向依赖可攫取的谷类作物」，他们也论证了谷物如何充当课税的基础。这就是斯科特的国家起源理论，而且可以说，它在根本上是一种关于国家生产基础的理论，而非关于国家起源的理论。然而，这恰恰是国家起源问题上居支配地位的理论——那个盛行的进化论、唯物论、人口压力范式，我称之为中国中心主义范式。归根结底，这一范式是一份殖民遗产，它取代了征服论（conquest theory）。

We set out to learn the origins of the state by studying its preeminent theories and the deep history of the earliest states. Yet, have we truly learned anything about the state's origins? Or, have we instead, as Scott notes, merely learned what 'can serve as a basis for taxation' - namely, its 'productive base'? Mayshar and his colleagues, who similarly argue that 'hierarchy arose as a result of the shift to dependence on appropriable cereal grains', also demonstrate how grain serves as a basis for taxation. This is Scott's theory of the origin of the state, and it is - one might say - fundamentally a theory of the state's productive base, not of its origin. Yet, this is the preeminent theory of state origins - the reigning evolutionary, materialist, population-pressure paradigm that I call the Sinocentric paradigm. Ultimately, this paradigm represents a colonial legacy that displaced conquest theory.

那么，什么是征服论？它对国家起源有何论断？国家究竟是什么，又该如何界定？值得注意的是，两种理论接受的是同一个国家定义。「国家是在特定领土内（成功地）主张对合法使用物理暴力之垄断的人类共同体」，这是一个普遍接受的定义。韦伯从社会学角度将国家「仅仅按照它所特有的手段——如同任何政治联合体一样，即物理暴力的使用」来界定，亦即「对物理暴力之合法使用的垄断」（着重号为原文所有）。简言之，国家就是行使合法暴力垄断的制度。因此，在寻找国家起源时，我们应当考察这一制度如何兴起。而这正是征服论所做的工作。

What, then, is conquest theory, and what does it say about the origin of the state? What exactly is the state, and how is it defined? Remarkably, both theories accept the same definition of the state. That the state is a human community that (successfully) claims the monopoly of the legitimate use of physical force within a given territory is a universally accepted definition. Weber defines the state sociologically 'only in terms of the specific means peculiar to it, as to every political association, namely, the use of physical force,' that is, 'the monopoly of the legitimate use of physical force' (italics original). In short, the state is the institution that exercises a monopoly on legitimate violence. Therefore, when looking for the origin of the state, we should examine how this institution arose. That is exactly what conquest theory does.

因此，在寻找国家起源时，征服论考察的是物理暴力——亦即暴力制度——的兴起及其垄断的确立。这一框架把那种物理暴力定位于战团（warband），把垄断定位于战争首领，并探究这一垄断如何变得既必要又正当。具体而言，它在战团内部对领导与组织的需要之中发现垄断，在战团对安全的需要之中发现正当性。最后，它探究催生这种暴力或使之成为必要的种种条件，并把这些条件定位于战争状态之中。

Therefore, in looking for the origin of the state, conquest theory examines the rise of physical force - that is, the institution of violence - and the establishment of a monopoly over it. This framework locates that physical force in the warband and the monopoly in the warlord, and explores how that monopoly becomes both necessary and legitimate. Specifically, it finds the monopoly in the need for leadership and organisation within the warband, and the legitimacy in the warband's need for security. Finally, it explores the conditions that give rise to, or necessitate, this force, and locates those conditions in a state of war.

战争使生命与自由变得稀缺，从而必然要求以战团的形式出现物理暴力。战团以武士结社（consociation）的形态涌现——一个围绕生死进行共同政治斗争的共同体，一个武士兄弟会——在其中，个体被期待为了群体的利益直面死亡。战争还把战团锻造成一个理性的规范共同体：用韦伯的话说，「防范内外敌人的迫切安全需要，促使立法与司法日益理性化」。因此，战争把战团造就为武士兄弟会与法理型结社。

Making life and liberty scarce, war necessitates physical force in the form of a warband. A warband emerges as a warrior consociation - a community of common political struggle over life and death, and a warrior brotherhood - where individuals are expected to face death in the group's interest. War also forges the warband into a rational normative community: in Weber's words, a 'pressing need for security against internal and foreign enemies induces a growing rationalisation of lawmaking and lawfinding.' Thus, war creates the warband as a warrior fraternity and a rational-legal consociation.

战争要求统一、组织、纪律与领导，它不仅把战团塑造成凝聚一体的群体，也把战争首领造就为其领袖。而统一、组织与纪律——这三者都需要集中的权威与权力的垄断——又使战争首领成为发号施令、强制服从的君主。最终，战争通过把战团的安全置于至高无上的地位，使暴力获得正当性。正如韦伯所写：「然而，暴力唯有在下述情形中——至少起初如此——才能获得正当性：它所针对的，是兄弟会中行叛卖之举，或因抗命与怯懦而损害群体的成员。」因此，战团本身就是一个成功主张对合法使用物理暴力之垄断的人类共同体。如此，战团的兴起便标志着国家的开端。于是，征服论在战团的兴起之中，同时定位了军事分化与军事宪制——国家的原型。一个胜利的战团，一旦在某片土地上成功建立起对某个人口的暴力垄断，便成为国家。

Demanding unity, organisation, discipline and leadership, war not only moulds a warband into a cohesive group but also fashions the warlord as its leader. In turn, the unity, organisation and discipline - which require both centralised authority and the monopolisation of power - render the warlord a monarch who commands and compels obedience. Ultimately, by rendering the security of the warband paramount, war makes violence legitimate. As Weber writes: 'Violence acquires legitimacy only in those cases, however - at least initially - in which it is directed against members of the fraternity who have acted treasonably or who have harmed it by disobedience or cowardice.' Therefore, a warband is a human community that successfully claims a monopoly on the legitimate use of physical force. As such, the rise of the warband marks the beginning of the state. Thus, in the rise of the warband, conquest theory locates both the military differentiation and the military constitution - the archetype of the state. A victorious warband that has successfully established a monopoly of violence over a population in a stretch of land becomes the state.

因此，国家在根本上是一种政治支配关系——具体而言，是凭借权威（界定为发号施令的权力与服从的义务）而实行的支配——最终奠基于对生命与自由的暴力垄断之上。这是一种既以威胁生命与自由、又以保护生命与自由为基础的支配；这正是暴力垄断的根本缘由，而暴力垄断即构成主权。简言之，战争以战团的形式带来作为战争权力的王权，又以战争首领其人带来作为战争领袖的国王。正如韦伯所言：「无论何处，国王首先都是战争首领」，而「王权通常是被永久化了的魅力型战争领导权，并为驯化手无寸铁的臣民而发展出了一套镇压机器」。这完全是韦伯本人的理论，而这种解读使他成为最具概念建构力、却又遭到深刻误解的征服论者之一。

Therefore, the state is fundamentally a relation of political domination - specifically, domination by virtue of authority (defined as the power to command and the duty to obey) - based ultimately upon a monopoly of violence over life and liberty. It is a domination founded on both the threat to and the protection of life and liberty; this is the fundamental reason for the monopoly of violence, which constitutes sovereignty. In short, war brings kingship as a war power in the form of a warband, and the king as a war leader in the person of a warlord. As Weber observes, 'Everywhere the king is primarily a warlord,' and 'kingship is normally charismatic war leadership that has become permanent and has developed a repressive apparatus for the domestication of the unarmed subjects.' This is entirely Weber's theory, and this reading makes him one of the most conceptual, yet profoundly misunderstood, conquest theorists.

导致国家兴起的，并非资源的盈余、稀缺或可攫取性，而是对生命与自由直接的、不经中介的垄断。这种支配并非以资源控制为中介，而是以对生命与自由的直接控制为中介。正因如此，政治不仅有其自身的独立存在，政治关系也是首要而根本的。是政治宪制决定并界定了社会与经济。因此，私有财产、土地稀缺、社会分层与社会阶级——乃至社会与经济本身——皆是国家的结果而非前提，是最终经由征服、强制臣服、剥夺与政治整合而锻造出的制度。

It is not resource surplus, scarcity or appropriability that leads to the rise of the state; instead, it is the direct and unmediated monopoly over life and liberty. This domination is not mediated by resource control, but by direct control over life and liberty. For this reason, not only does the political exist on its own terms, but political relationships are also primary and fundamental. It is the political constitution that determines and defines both the social and the economic. Consequently, private property, land scarcity, social stratification and social classes - and, indeed, society and economy - are consequences of the state rather than preconditions for it, institutions ultimately forged by conquest, subjugation, expropriation and political integration.

然而，催生国家的并非战争本身或单纯的武装冲突，而是征服——亦即征服战争——这正是该理论得名征服论的缘由。这一模型把流动性，亦即非人力运输，视为决定性的革命力量。没有非人力运输，就不可能形成大型社会，诸共同体便被迫分裂与离散，正如在亚马逊地区（Amazonia）所观察到的那样。归根结底，正是非人力运输使征服与建国成为可能。

However, it is not just war per se or armed conflict, but conquest - or wars of conquest - that gives rise to the state, which is why the theory is called conquest theory. This model sees mobility or extrahuman transportation as the decisive revolutionary force. Without extrahuman transportation, forming a large society is impossible, and communities are forced to split and disperse, as observed in Amazonia. Ultimately, extrahuman transportation is what enables conquest and state-building.

非人力运输赋予其拥有者以力量，使动员——进而使人支配人的统治——得以在广袤地域上实现。通过使动员与集中成为可能，非人力运输不仅催生了战团的兴起，还借由便利征服而从根本上改变了战争的性质。战争转变为征服的征战，一种建国的事业，它使诸共同体及其土地沦为被征服、被强制整合进更大社会的首要目标。征服与建国创造了吕斯托（Rüstow）在20世纪50年代所称的「开端构型」（opening configuration）。这一「新构型」充当「人类生活内外诸因素的一种新的自然聚合」，为「一切高等文化的发展」提供了「开端构型」——即国家与社会。

Extrahuman transportation empowered those who possessed it, and made mobilisation - and, consequently, the domination of man over man - possible over large areas. By making mobilisation and concentration possible, extrahuman transportation not only led to the rise of the warband but also, by facilitating conquest, fundamentally changed the nature of war. War was transformed into campaigns of conquest, an enterprise of state-building that rendered communities and their lands the prime targets for subjugation and forced integration into a larger society. Conquest and state-building created what in the 1950s Rüstow termed an 'opening configuration'. This 'new configuration' served as 'a new natural aggregation of external and internal factors of human life', providing the 'opening configuration for the development of all high cultures' - the state and society.

由于畜牧者（pastoralists）拥有以牧养牲畜为形式的非人力运输，征服论主张，国家最早兴起于游牧民之中，继而由他们征服邻近的农耕地区。因此，征服论首先是一种关于游牧民征服定居农耕者的理论。除高度流动性之外，该理论还把军事领导、组织、纪律、武艺与尚武精神认定为国家兴起的关键文化因素，并指出这些品质在畜牧者中间极为发达。无可否认，在严酷且难以预料的环境下驱赶大群牲畜频繁长途迁徙，自然会养成这些领导才能与尚武特质。

Because pastoralists possessed extrahuman transportation in the form of herding animals, the conquest theory argues that the state first arose among nomads, who then conquered neighbouring farming regions. For this reason, the conquest theory is primarily a theory of nomadic conquest over sedentary farmers. In addition to high mobility, the theory identifies military leadership, organisation, discipline, martial skills and a warrior ethos as key cultural factors in the rise of the state, noting that these qualities are highly developed among pastoralists. Undeniably, frequent long-distance migration with large herds under harsh and unpredictable conditions naturally fosters these leadership and martial attributes.

尽管骑马武士游牧民的征服在国家与文明的兴起中最具深远影响，征服论所勾勒的历史序列却更为宽广。它设定了一系列随时间推移而展开的征服：先是猎人征服耕作者，继而是牧人征服种植者与农民，最终是欧洲殖民列强的征服。征服论绝不为征服颂功；相反，它把征服视为制造专制的、双层的、阶级性的国家的过程，而这类国家的毒性遗产正是我们必须直面并克服的。的确，征服论提供了一种截然不同的愿景与框架，用以理解人类历程及其历史轨迹。它呈现的愿景，既非不断演进的进步，亦非命定的必然，但同样不是永久的困陷或厄运。

While conquests by equestrian warrior nomads were the most consequential in the rise of the state and civilisation, the conquest theory maps a broader historical sequence. It posits a series of conquests over time: first of hunters over cultivators, then of herdsmen over planters and peasants and, finally, of European colonial powers. Conquest theory by no means valorises conquest; instead, it views it as creating despotic, two-strata class states whose toxic legacies we must confront and overcome. Indeed, conquest theory offers a radically different vision and framework to make sense of the human journey and its historical trajectory. It presents a vision neither of ever-evolving progress nor of inevitability, but equally not of permanent entrapment or doom.

透过这一视角，恰如诸次征服一样，现代每一项进步的政治、社会、经济与文化成就，恰似前一时代的每一次征服，皆源自一场魅力型革命——无论其是政治的、社会的、经济的还是科学的——旨在破除制度性不义与自然限制的苦涩遗产。从自由主义革命与立宪主义的早期奠基性变革，到全球去殖民化，再到人权与环境权框架；从工业革命，到STEM（科学、技术、工程与数学）之兴起，再到人工智能的降临——无论好坏，这一切皆是魅力型观念、个人、事业与运动的果实。归根结底，推动历史的并非进化那台看不见的引擎，而是人类能动性之火。

Through this lens, much like conquests, each and every progressive political, social, economic and cultural achievement of the modern era, just like each conquest of the previous era, stems from a charismatic revolution - be it political, social, economic or scientific - aimed at dismantling the bitter legacies of institutional injustice and natural limitations. From the early foundational shifts of liberal revolutions and constitutionalism, to global decolonisation, to human and environmental rights frameworks; and from the Industrial Revolution, to the rise of STEM, to the advent of AI - all are, for better or worse, the fruits of charismatic ideas, individuals, endeavours and movements. Ultimately, it is not the invisible engine of evolution that drives history, but the fire of human agency.

尽管人类历程始终是对自由的不懈追求，其轨迹却被构想为一段过渡：从专制的、双层的、以阶级为基础的「掠夺国家」（State of robbery），走向未来的「自由民公民身份」（Freemen's Citizenship）——一如奥本海默（Franz Oppenheimer）在1914年的表述——亦即一个自治的社会，其间历经包括宪政国家在内的一系列国家形态。然而，进步之路既非线性，亦不一致。解放的每一步都为历史的偶然性所形塑，一路遭遇严重的挫败、系统性的反扑与深刻的失败。况且，今日魅力型革命的成就，往往变成明日的制度陷阱与堡垒：一个时代的解放观念，硬化为下一个时代的系统性屏障。因此，进步始终困于革命与制度化周而复始的循环。纵有这些断裂与制约，核心的驱力依然不屈不挠：魅力——一种深植于人性、深属个体、深为个人的自由冲动。

Though the human journey remains a persistent pursuit of freedom, its trajectory is conceptualised as a transition from the despotic, two-strata, class-based 'State of robbery' to the 'Freemen's Citizenship' of the future (as Franz Oppenheimer put it in 1914) - a self-governing society - progressing through a series of states, including constitutional ones. However, the path of progress is neither linear nor consistent. Every step of liberation has been shaped by historical contingencies, facing severe setbacks, systemic backlash and profound failures along the way. Moreover, the achievements of today's charismatic revolutions routinely become tomorrow's institutional trap and bulwark: the liberating ideas of one era harden into the systemic barriers of the next. Progress, therefore, remains trapped in a perpetual cycle of revolution and institutionalisation. Despite these fractures and constraints, the core drive remains unyielding: charisma - a deeply human, deeply individual, and deeply personal impulse for freedom.

透过这一视角，中国中心主义范式误解了人类的过去、现在与未来；它把过去合理化为生态与演化的产物；又把现在自然化为既定之事，把未来自然化为命定之事。它误导我们，把我们的行止归咎于我们的人口数量。它把曾经的和如今的文化之物归于自然，又把曾经的和如今的自然之物设想为文化。

Through this lens, the Sinocentric paradigm misconstrues the human past, present and future; it rationalises the past as a product of ecology and evolution; and it naturalises the present as given, and the future as fated. It misguides us, blaming our numbers for our conduct. It attributes what was, and is, cultural to nature, and supposes what was, and is, natural to be cultural.

征服论赋予我们一个框架，使我们不仅能挣脱中国中心主义范式，还能批判性地重估人类历史，完全收复我们的能动性，并有目的地擘画自己的未来。唯有克服中国中心主义范式并拥抱征服论，我们才能建构起一部充分领会本文开篇所阐述的非人力运输之重大意义的人类史。这样一部叙述，将不止于格雷伯与温格罗认为必要的那种新人类史；它将提供一个框架，最终把人类历史从被当作异例对待的境地中拯救出来。

Conquest theory arms us with a framework to not only break free from the Sinocentric paradigm but also to critically re-evaluate human history, fully reclaim our agency, and purposefully chart our own future. Only by overcoming the Sinocentric paradigm and embracing conquest theory can we construct a human history that fully appreciates the significance of the extrahuman transportation outlined at the outset of this essay. Such an account will be more than the new history of humanity that Graeber and Wengrow deemed necessary; it will provide a framework that finally rescues human history from being treated as an anomaly.
