思维的能量

The energy of thought

David Colaço & Philipp Haueis·Aeon·2026年11月28日·10分钟阅读·原文

理解心智，在一定程度上是一项建模工作，而哲学家和科学家已经创建数量惊人的模型。有人把心智比作计算机；有人强调符号和「心理语」（mentalese），即一种假想出来的「思维语言」；也有人从大脑及其物理的、神经的基础出发。这些模型未必互相冲突：例如，心理学家艾伦·巴德利（Alan Baddeley）和格雷厄姆·希奇（Graham Hitch）提出的「工作记忆」（working memory）模型，可以与计算神经科学家拉杰什·拉奥（Rajesh Rao）、达纳·巴拉德（Dana Ballard）和卡尔·弗里斯顿（Karl Friston）所构想的视觉或注意的「预测加工」（predictive processing）模型互为补充。这个领域已经相当拥挤，而且还在不断扩张。

Making sense of the mind is, in part, a matter of modelling, and philosophers and scientists have created an astonishing number of models. Some liken the mind to a computer. Some emphasise symbols and ‘mentalese’, a hypothesised ‘language of thought’. Some start from the brain and its physical, neural basis. These models aren’t necessarily in conflict: for instance, the ‘working memory’ model proposed by the psychologists Alan Baddeley and Graham Hitch may complement ‘predictive processing’ models of vision or attention theorised by the computational neuroscientists Rajesh Rao, Dana Ballard and Karl Friston. The domain is crowded, and still growing.

然而，几乎所有这些模型都忽视了某种对人类心智运作至关重要的东西：代谢。它们描述心智在计算什么，或者神经元如何发出信号，却不涉及大脑获取能量、维持稳态、清除废物的代谢工作。若不考虑代谢，我们的模型就有脱离现实约束的危险，让理解心智这一古老目标沦为一场徒劳的追逐。

However, nearly all of these models neglect something essential to how the human mind functions: metabolism. They describe what the mind computes, or how its neurons signal, but not the metabolic work by which the brain acquires energy, maintains homeostasis, and disposes of waste. Without taking metabolism into consideration, our models are in danger of becoming untethered from real-world constraints, turning the ancient goal of understanding the mind into a wild goose chase.

那么，如果我们认真对待大脑代谢，会发生什么？

So what happens if we take brain metabolism seriously?

「代谢」（metabolism）指的是一切生物系统——大脑也不例外——为维持生存而进行的活动。它既包括能量的使用，也包括维护、调节和废物清除。代谢为肌肉在奔跑时如何工作、肺在呼吸时如何工作、免疫系统在生病时如何工作等模型提供依据。既然人类心智与大脑脱不开关系，那么代谢理应为我们思考时大脑如何工作的模型提供依据。事实上，如今大多数学者都倾向于承认大脑对理解心智的重要性，即便他们常常把它排除在自己的模型之外。

‘M etabolism’ refers to activities that all biological systems, the brain included, carry out in order to stay alive. It includes energy use, but also maintenance, regulation and waste removal. Metabolism informs models of how our muscles work when we run, how our lungs work when we breathe, and how our immune system works when we get sick. Since the human mind has something to do with the brain, it stands to reason that metabolism should inform models of how our brain works when we think. And, indeed, most scholars nowadays tend to accept that the brain is important to understanding the mind, even if they leave it out of their models.

从代谢角度说，大脑是个奇特的器官。与人体的其他部位相比，它耗能极多：虽然只约占成年人体重的百分之二，却消耗了约百分之二十的热量。但它的效率又令人惊叹：大脑平均以约二十瓦的功率运转——比一只白炽灯泡还要低。换句话说，灵光一现（「灯泡时刻」，lightbulb moment）所消耗的能量，很可能还不如一只真正的灯泡。

The brain is a quirky organ, metabolically speaking. It consumes a lot of energy compared with other parts of the human body. Though it makes up around 2 per cent of an adult’s body mass, it consumes about 20 per cent of its calories. Yet it is impressively efficient. The brain on average operates at about 20 watts – less than an incandescent lightbulb. A ‘lightbulb moment’, in other words, probably consumes less energy than an actual lightbulb.

根据弗吉尼亚大学（University of Virginia）的威廉·利维（William Levy）和维多利亚·卡尔弗特（Victoria Calvert）的一项研究，传递信息的电信号（即「锋电位」，spike）只使用约三点五瓦的功率；而信息的存储方式——构建和强化突触（synapse）——则使用约一点三瓦。大脑何以能用这么少的能量做这么多的事，目前尚未完全弄清，但答案的一部分在于演化：大脑更高效的生物，在觅食和躲避天敌上拥有选择优势。人类心智正是在巨大的代谢约束之下运转的。

According to one study by William Levy and Victoria Calvert of the University of Virginia, the electrical signals (or ‘spikes’) that send information use only about 3.5 watts. Building and strengthening synapses, which is how information is stored, uses about 1.3 watts. It is not entirely understood how the brain can do so much with so little, but part of the answer is evolution: organisms with more efficient brains had a selective advantage for searching for food and avoiding predators. The human mind operates under tremendous metabolic constraints.

如果一个记忆模型要求系统做到其代谢无力支撑的事，这个模型就不成立

Amodel of memory is not viable if it requires a system to do more than its metabolism can support

聚焦代谢，似乎有悖于大量关于心智的哲学分析。许多哲学家在构建理论时把大脑放在一边，让心智摆脱其物质基础。原因之一是「多重可实现性」（multiple realisability）被广泛接受：这一观点由哲学家希拉里·普特南（Hilary Putnam）在二十世纪六十年代首次提出，主张同样的心智状态可以由不同的神经状态来实现——决定一个心智状态的是它做了什么，而非它由什么构成。这就解释了章鱼的大脑虽然与我们迥异，却仍然会感到疼痛：要紧的是那个状态本身，而不是物理基质。一些哲学家走得更远，采纳了约翰·豪格兰德（John Haugeland）的「媒介独立性」（medium independence）观念：如果心智状态就是计算——这是认知科学的主流观点——那么这些状态便独立于其栖身的特定媒介；心智状态由形式规则定义，而同样的规则可以在不同媒介中实现。人们熟悉的类比是计算机：同一套软件（心智）可以运行在不同的硬件（大脑）上。这两种观点都否定了心智与大脑之间存在一一对应，都主张心智可以就其自身而言得到富有成效的研究，代谢因此与建模无关。

Focusing on metabolism might seem to fly in the face of much philosophical analysis of the mind. Many philosophers set the brain aside when building their theories, freeing the mind from its substratum. One reason is the widespread acceptance of multiple realisability, a view first set out by the philosopher Hilary Putnam in the 1960s, which holds that the same mental states can be realised by different neural states. What determines a mental state is what it does, not what it’s made of. This is how an octopus could still feel pain, even if its brain is unlike ours – it’s the state that matters, not the physical substratum. Some philosophers go further, adopting John Haugeland’s notion of ‘medium independence’: if mental states are computations – a mainstream view in cognitive science – then these states are independent of the specific medium in which they are embodied. Mental states are defined by formal rules, and these same rules can be implemented in different media. The familiar analogy is the computer: the same software (the mind) can be run on different hardware (the brain). Both views reject the idea that there is a one-to-one match between the mind and the brain. They suggest that the mind can be fruitfully studied on its own terms, making metabolism irrelevant to modelling.

然而，即便是对大脑友好的哲学家，也基本无视了大脑代谢。帕特里夏·丘奇兰德（Patricia Churchland）曾敦促哲学家学习神经科学，她与他人合著的一九八八年论文，把计算神经科学的目标定义为解释大脑的信号如何被用来「表征和处理信息」；但维持这些信号的化学过程，在这一框架里失踪了。哲学家卡尔·克雷弗（Carl Craver）及同事贴近生物学地面，主张机制是「组织起来从而产生规律性变化的实体与活动」，例如神经递质与受体的结合。但在这里，让这些活动成为可能的能量同样只是充当背景条件，而非解释的一部分。

Yet even brain-friendly philosophers have largely disregarded brain metabolism. Patricia Churchland, who urged philosophers to learn neuroscience, co-wrote a 1988 paper that defined the aim of computational neuroscience as explaining how the brain’s signals are used ‘to represent and process information’. But the chemistry that maintains those signals was lost in this framing. The philosopher Carl Craver and colleagues stay close to the biological ground when they argue that mechanisms are ‘entities and activities organised such that they are productive of regular changes’, for example neurotransmitters binding to receptors. But here too the energy that makes those activities possible figures as a background condition rather than as part of the explanation.

我们的主张是：代谢约束了哪些心智模型是可行的。但这并不要求心智只能由大脑来实现：代谢约束收窄了选项，却并不锁定唯一答案。以记忆存储为例：它可以经由不同的大脑过程实现，也可以在非生物媒介（如硅基数字计算机）中实现。无论媒介为何，所有这些系统都会受到能量使用、维护和废物清除的约束。如果一个记忆模型要求系统做到其代谢无力支撑的事，这个模型就不成立；而研究代谢，正是我们弄清这一极限所在的方式。

We argue that metabolism constrains which models of the mind are viable. But that does not require the mind to be uniquely realised by the brain: metabolic constraints narrow the options without settling on a single one. Consider the example of memory storage. It could occur via different brain processes, or in nonbiological media like silicon-based digital computers. Whatever the medium, all these systems will be constrained by energy use, maintenance and waste removal. A model of memory is not viable if it requires a system to do more than its metabolism can support, and studying metabolism is how we find out where that limit lies.

因此，在为心智建模时，大脑这类物理系统所承受的代谢约束不容忽视。这意味着我们要重新审视对多重可实现性和媒介独立性等观点的立场：与其把它们当作坚定的形而上学承诺，不如把它们看作工作假设——它们是出色的起点，但在新的研究之后，随时可以修正。

So the metabolic constraints on physical systems like brains cannot be ignored when modelling the mind. This means rethinking our stance towards views like multiple realisability and medium independence. Rather than taking them to be firm metaphysical commitments, we should think of them as working hypotheses: great starting points, but open to revision after new research.

近来，哲学家马兹维塔·奇里穆塔（Mazviita Chirimuuta）、科里·马利（Corey Maley）和罗莎·曹（Rosa Cao）各自论证说，大脑中的信息加工依赖于信号幅度、代谢分子和能量供给。这提示心智与代谢之间存在紧密联系，但并不就此否证多重可实现性或媒介独立性；它表明的是，研究大脑代谢可以检验这些假设对理解心智到底有多大帮助。所以，关注代谢是重要的——哪怕未来的科学发现心智与大脑并无一一对应关系，发现心智可以存在于硅之中，或者发现身体与环境对认知不可或缺。

Recently, the philosophers Mazviita Chirimuuta, Corey Maley and Rosa Cao have each argued that information-processing in brains depends on signal amplitudes, metabolic molecules and energy supply. This suggests a tight link between mind and metabolism, but it does not decisively rule out multiple realisability or medium independence. Instead, it shows that studying brain metabolism can test how much these hypotheses help us understand the mind. Looking at metabolism is therefore important, even if future science finds out that there is no one-to-one relation between mind and brain, that minds can exist in silicon, or that body and environment are essential for cognition.

组成突触的分子几天内就会被更换，而有些记忆却能持续一生

Molecules that make up a synapse are replaced within days, yet some memories last a lifetime

过去二十五年来，我们对大脑代谢的理解取得了巨大进展，为研究心智的代谢约束提供了丰富材料。如今我们有了详尽的能量预算：大脑把约百分之十六的能量用于产生动作电位（action potential），百分之四十四用于在突触处以化学方式传递这些信号，百分之四十用于神经细胞的维护与修复。这意味着大脑的能量只有百分之六十用于信号发送，其余的都花在防止神经机器散架上面。根据神经科学家伊丽莎白·恩格尔（Elisabeth Engl）和戴维·阿特韦尔（David Attell）二〇一五年的一篇论文，用于维护和修复的能量「必然有助于限定大脑的认知能力，因为大脑的总能量使用约束着信息得以编码和加工的时间速率」。但这样的预算本身过于粗糙，不足以充当约束，在为单项认知能力建模时尤其如此；相比之下，信息加工的数值成本更为具体。根据理论神经科学家扬·卡尔博夫斯基（Jan Karbowski）及其同事的一项研究，在突触中存储一比特信息，大约要耗费五十万分子ATP——细胞的「燃料」。

The past 25 years have seen great advances in our understanding of brain metabolism, providing us with a wealth of material for studying metabolic constraints on the mind. We now have detailed energy budgets: the brain devotes about 16 per cent of its energy to generating action potentials, 44 per cent to transmitting these signals chemically at the synapse, and 40 per cent to maintaining and repairing neuron cells. This means that only 60 per cent of brain energy is for signalling, while the rest is spent keeping the neural machinery from falling apart. According to a 2015 paper by the neuroscientists Elisabeth Engl and David Attwell, energy for maintenance and repair ‘must contribute to limiting the cognitive abilities of the brain because the total energy use of the brain constrains the temporal rate at which information can be encoded and processed.’ But budgets like this are too coarse on their own to serve as constraints, especially when modelling individual cognitive capacities. Numerical costs of information-processing, by contrast, are more specific. According to one study by the theoretical neuroscientist Jan Karbowski and colleagues, storing one bit of information in the synapse costs about 500,000 ATP (the ‘fuel’ of the cell).

回到记忆这个例子。代谢如何帮助我们判断一个记忆模型是否可信？神经科学的主导理论框架认为，记忆存储于突触之中——即信号从一个神经元传向另一个神经元的接头部位。新记忆形成时，突触会发生改变，进而影响所传递的信号。其中一种改变被称为「赫布学习」（Hebbian learning），得名于神经心理学家唐纳德·赫布（Donald Hebb），他的工作被概括为那句口号「一起放电的神经元，连在一起」（此语并非他的原创）。

Return to the example of memory. How can metabolism help us assess whether a model of memory is plausible? The dominant theoretical framework in neuroscience is that memory is stored in synapses: the junctions where signals pass from one neuron another. When a new memory is formed, the synapses change, affecting the signals that are transmitted. One such change is known as ‘Hebbian learning’, named for the neuropsychologist Donald Hebb, whose work is summed up in the slogan (not his) ‘neurons that fire together, wire together’.

模型只有在解决一个理论的问题时才有用，而突触理论有两个大问题。第一个常被称为「干扰」（interference）：容易改变的突触擅长存储新记忆，却不擅长保住旧记忆。神经放电中几乎任何一点变化，都会轻微改变突触的分子构成；那么，新记忆又如何做到不覆写旧记忆？第二个问题叫「更替」（turnover）：组成突触的分子几天内就会被更换，而有些记忆却能持续一生。为了活下去，神经元必须不断重建自己衰朽的部件——一个神经元就像奥托·纽拉特（Otto Neurath）的那艘船，在海上被一块木板一块木板地重新造起来。在一个每个部件都在被更换的系统里，信息是如何幸存的？

Models are useful when they solve a theory’s problems, and the synaptic theory has two big ones. The first, often called interference, is that synapses that change easily are good at storing new memories but bad at keeping old ones. Almost any change in neural firing alters a synapse’s molecular makeup slightly. But then how do new memories keep from overwriting old ones? The second problem is called turnover. Molecules that make up a synapse are replaced within days, yet some memories last a lifetime. In order to stay alive, neurons must constantly rebuild their decaying parts. A neuron is like Otto Neurath’s ship, rebuilt plank by plank at sea. How does information survive in a system whose every part is being replaced?

2016年，斯特凡诺·富西（Stefano Fusi）与马库斯·本纳（Marcus Benna）发表了一个旨在解决干扰问题的数学模型。这个模型让我们把突触记忆过程想象成一排烧杯——每只烧杯代表突触处的一种化学过程——杯中盛着液体，彼此以管子相通。第一只烧杯的液位就是突触的强度：注入液体，突触便增强；抽走液体，突触便减弱。「随着时间推移，」本纳在一次访谈中解释说，「由此产生的液流会扩散到其他烧杯中，这正对应着记忆的长期存储。」由于那些更深层的烧杯注满与排空得更慢，它们保存着过往变化的痕迹，并不断把第一只烧杯拉回先前的液位。新记忆由此迅速形成，旧记忆却只缓慢消退，各杯液位渐趋持平。

In 2016, Stefano Fusi and Marcus Benna published a mathematical model to solve the interference problem. It asks us to imagine the synaptic memory process as a set of beakers – each standing for a chemical process at the synapse – filled with liquid and connected by tubes. The level in the first beaker is the synapse’s strength: adding liquid strengthens it, and removing liquid weakens it. ‘Over time,’ Benna explained in an interview, ‘the resulting flow of liquid will diffuse across the other beakers, corresponding to the long-term storage of memories.’ Because those deeper beakers fill and drain more slowly, they hold a trace of past changes and keep drawing the first beaker back towards its old level. New memories form quickly; old ones fade only slowly, as the levels even out.

然而，代谢约束使这个解决方案难以成立。烧杯模型描述的是这样一个系统：突触内的生化过程会随时间归于均平——液体可以朝任一方向流动，但终究会达到平衡。而在生物学中，陷入平衡就意味着死亡。正如物理学家埃尔温·薛定谔（Erwin Schrödinger）所言：「生命物质逃避趋于平衡的衰变。」神经元必须不断摄入并排出离子与分子才能存活。一旦达到平衡，神经元便丧失其组织性，再也无法执行在突触处存储记忆之类的功能。把代谢当作一种约束，我们就会看到，线性烧杯模型并没有解决既要形成新突触记忆、又要保住旧记忆的问题。如果建模者不考虑代谢的相关性，这一后果是很容易被忽略的。

However, metabolic constraints render this solution implausible. The beaker model describes a system in which biochemical processes in the synapse even out over time: liquid can flow one way or the other, but it ultimately reaches equilibrium. In biology, settling into equilibrium means death. As the physicist Erwin Schrödinger put it: ‘Living matter evades the decay to equilibrium.’ Neurons must take in and expel ions and molecules to stay alive. A neuron that reaches equilibrium loses its organisation and can no longer perform functions such as storing memories at the synapse. Treating metabolism as a constraint shows us that a linear beaker model does not solve the problem of forming new synaptic memories and keeping old ones. This consequence is easy to miss if modellers do not consider the relevance of metabolism.

不过，代谢本身指向了一个更好的模型。卡尔博夫斯基已发展出一个以代谢为核心的突触记忆模型。由于生命系统从不处于平衡态，他用非平衡态热力学的数学来构建这个模型。与烧杯模型不同，这一框架把突触的静息状态描述为与其环境处于非平衡之中，因而能够存储记忆。

Metabolism itself, though, points to a better model. Karbowski has developed a model of synaptic memory in which metabolism takes centre stage. Because living systems are never at equilibrium, he builds his model with the mathematics of nonequilibrium thermodynamics. Unlike the beaker model, this framework describes the resting state of the synapse as not being in equilibrium with its environment, and thus capable of storing memories.

留住旧记忆的成本如此之低，突触完全负担得起一边写入新记忆、一边保留旧记忆

Holding on to old memories costs so little that the synapse can afford to keep them while laying down new ones

这个模型把突触处的生化过程连结成循环，而非（如烧杯模型中那样的）线性序列。ATP为循环中各状态之间的转换提供动力，代谢因此被直接内置进模型之中。这些转换代表着突触如何增强或减弱，从而存储或抹除记忆。由于循环中各状态变化的速率彼此不同，它们永远达不到平衡。突触由此能够形成新记忆并长期保有它们，而不致死亡。

The model links biochemical processes at the synapse in cycles rather than in linear sequences (as in the beaker model). ATP powers transitions between states in the cycle, so metabolism is built into the model directly. These transitions represent how the synapse strengthens or weakens to store or erase memories. Because the state changes in the cycle have different rates, they never reach equilibrium. The synapse can form new memories and keep them over time without dying.

随后，卡尔博夫斯基把这个循环模型与信息处理的数值成本（例如每存储一比特记忆所耗的ATP）结合起来。他的计算表明，编码新记忆在能量上代价很低，而且在一定条件下，长期存储记忆同样代价很低。这或许可以解释，为什么干扰抹不去长久的记忆：留住它们的成本太低，突触完全负担得起一边写入新记忆、一边保留旧记忆。同一图景也为周转问题提供了答案：如果记忆被保存在突触主动维持的某种状态之中，而非依附于任何特定的分子，它就能比这些分子本身存在得更久——正如纽拉特之船恰恰因为不断翻修才得以持续漂浮。

Karbowski then combines the cyclic model with the numerical costs of information-processing (such as the ATP cost per bit of memory storage). His calculations show that encoding new memories is energetically cheap, and so is, under certain conditions, storing them for a long time. That may explain why interference doesn’t erase long-lasting memories: holding on to them costs so little that the synapse can afford to keep old memories while laying down new ones. The same picture suggests an answer to the turnover problem: if a memory is held in a state that the synapse actively maintains, rather than in any particular molecules, it can outlast the molecules themselves, much as Neurath’s ship stays afloat precisely because it is constantly being rebuilt.

说明代谢如何为模型提供启示的第二个例子，来自关于心智努力的研究。早晨刚开工时，你觉得神清气爽。可随着一天的时光艰难推进，当你写到第700封邮件、开到第五个会议时，原本轻松的任务变得令人筋疲力尽。这就是心智努力的代价。心智努力该如何建模？一种可能，是心理学家罗伊·鲍迈斯特（Roy Baumeister）及其同事提出的自我耗竭（ego depletion）模型。它假定，执行费力的任务依赖于一种有限的资源：大脑中的葡萄糖。表现的下降，便由这种资源的减少来解释。

Asecond example of how metabolism can inform models comes from research on mental effort. When you start work in the morning, you feel fresh. But as the day grinds on and you’re writing your 700th email and attending your fifth meeting, tasks that were easy become gruelling. This is the cost of mental effort. How can mental effort be modelled? One possibility is the ego depletion model, developed by the psychologist Roy Baumeister and colleagues. It posits that performing effortful tasks depends on a finite resource: glucose in the brain. The decrease in performance is explained by the decrease of the resource.

但这个模型在经验层面受到了挑战，例如其研究结果难以被重复。因此，罗伯特·库尔兹班（Robert Kurzban）等研究者转而投向心智努力的计算模型：在这些模型中，大脑会计算继续留在当前任务与切换到其他活动各自的收益与成本。支持者由此得出结论：计算模型更胜一筹，而代谢与心智努力的建模无关。

But the model has been challenged on empirical grounds, such as difficulties with replicating its findings. As a result, researchers such as Robert Kurzban and colleagues have turned towards computational models of mental effort, in which the brain calculates the benefits and costs of staying on task versus switching to another activity. Proponents conclude that computational models are superior, and that metabolism is not relevant to model mental effort.

然而，自我耗竭这类资源模型与心智努力计算模型之间的对立，其实是一种假象。自我耗竭模型的败落，部分正是拜代谢证据所赐：在执行高要求任务期间，大脑的葡萄糖消耗几乎不变——区区一个下午的自我控制，远不足以把它耗干。这正是代谢在做我们描述过的那件事：排除一个大脑运行不起的模型。而计算模型自身也面临代谢问题，因为权衡继续留在任务上的成本与收益，本身就是一项必须付出代价的工作。阿米泰·谢哈夫（Amitai Shenhav）、马修·博特温尼克（Matthew Botvinick）与克莱·霍尔罗伊德（Clay Holroyd）等神经科学家认为，这一计算过程发生在大脑中名为前扣带回皮层（anterior cingulate cortex）的区域。我们可以借助关于可用能量的证据，来评估描述这一过程的模型是否可行。

Yet the opposition between resource models such as ego depletion and computational models of mental effort is false. Ego depletion was undone partly by metabolic evidence: the brain’s glucose use barely changes during demanding tasks, far too little for an afternoon of self-control to run it down. That is metabolism doing just what we have described, ruling out a model the brain could not run. And computational models face metabolic questions of their own, because weighing the costs and benefits of staying on task is itself work that must be paid for. Neuroscientists such as Amitai Shenhav, Matthew Botvinick and Clay Holroyd believe this computational process takes place in a brain area known as the anterior cingulate cortex. We can use evidence about available energy to assess whether models of this process are feasible.

一些研究者已经把代谢整合进他们的心智努力模型。例如，亚历山大·泽农（Alexandre Zénon）及其同事假定，费力的任务要求大脑处理更多信息，因而消耗的能量略多一些。由于大脑的能量预算存在硬性上限，它必须把较少的能量花在其他任务上。泽农及其团队明确地把代谢与我们主观的努力感联系起来——这种感受或许反映了代谢变化在高要求任务中积累得有多快。而疲劳感，则可能反映了我们预期这些变化将会持续下去。

Some researchers already integrate metabolism into their models of mental effort. Alexandre Zénon and colleagues, for instance, posit that effortful tasks require the brain to process more information and therefore use slightly more energy. Since its energy budget has a hard upper limit, the brain needs to spend less energy on other tasks. Zénon and his team explicitly link metabolism to our subjective feeling of effort, which might reflect how fast metabolic changes build up during demanding tasks. Fatigue, in turn, might reflect our expectation that these changes will continue.

同样，霍尔罗伊德的「控制圈」（controllosphere）模型从大脑如何控制行为的理论出发，并援引一项经验发现：持续的心智努力会在神经元中产生废物。按照这个模型，努力感是发给大脑的一个信号，让它停止那些需要高度控制的任务，以便在造成损害之前把废物清除。关于心智努力的研究表明：把代谢纳入心智模型，并不只是理论上的可能——它已经是切实的现实。

Likewise, Holroyd’s ‘controllosphere’ model starts from the theory of how the brain controls behaviour and draws on empirical findings that sustained mental effort produces waste in neurons. On this model, the feeling of effort is a signal to the brain to stop tasks that require high control, so that waste can be cleared before causing damage. Research on mental effort shows that including metabolism into models of the mind is not a mere theoretical possibility. It is already a practical reality.

对代谢与能量使用的关注，能改善的不只是我们对生物体的认识，还能帮助我们理解人工神经网络——它正是人工智能突飞猛进的核心。尽管当前的发展速度前所未有，人工智能与大脑之间的联系却由来已久，可以追溯到1943年沃伦·麦卡洛克（Warren McCulloch）与沃尔特·皮茨（Walter Pitts）等先驱提出的第一个神经网络模型，或1958年弗兰克·罗森布拉特（Frank Rosenblatt）的「感知机」（perceptron）。近来大语言模型（LLM）之类的进展，重新点燃了一些根本性的追问：人工智能究竟是否真正智能，甚至是否拥有意识？在着手应对这些问题时，代谢与能量使用理应纳入考量。

Attention to metabolism and energy use can improve more than just our knowledge of biological organisms. It can also help our understanding of artificial neural networks at the heart of dramatic advances in artificial intelligence. While the speed of development is new, the link between AI and the brain is old, going back to the first neural network model in 1943, from pioneers such as Warren McCulloch and Walter Pitts, or ‘the perceptron’ of Frank Rosenblatt in 1958. Recent advances like LLMs (large language models) reignited existential questions about whether AI is really intelligent, or even conscious. Metabolism and energy use should inform how we approach these questions.

人工智能与能量使用之间的关系，在很大程度上是一片未经测绘的疆域，而以代谢为出发点的视角提示了该从哪里入手。超大规模数据中心耗能巨大，远超人脑运行的20瓦。然而，在哲学与认知科学中，媒介独立性之类的观点引导学者以抽象的、形式化的方式思考人工智能：尽管AI系统的物理基础可能各异，计算——按这种思路——却是同一个。正是这种对抽象计算的专注，促使卢克·克斯滕（Luke Kersten）与伦纳德·邓（Leonard Dung）等哲学家主张，AI系统原则上能够复制人类心智。但在大脑中，能量约束参与决定了一开始究竟会执行哪些计算。如果人工系统同样如此，那么一块芯片与一个大脑是否在运行「同样的」计算，就不能只是假设，而必须实际查明。

The relationship between AI and energy use is largely uncharted territory, and a metabolically informed perspective suggests where to start looking. Hyperscale data centres use a lot of energy, far beyond the 20 watts the human brain runs on. Yet in philosophy and cognitive science, views like medium independence lead scholars to think of AI in abstract, formal terms: while the physical basis of an AI system may vary, the computation, so the thought goes, is the same. This focus on abstract computation leads philosophers such as Luke Kersten and Leonard Dung to argue that AI systems could, in principle, duplicate human minds. But, in brains, energy constraints help determine which computations are performed in the first place. If the same holds for artificial systems, then whether a chip and a brain are running ‘the same’ computation cannot simply be assumed. It has to be found out.

我们的研究属于一场日益壮大的讨论：能量使用在为大脑与AI系统建模中所扮演的角色。例如，曹与神经科学家丹尼尔·亚明斯（Daniel Yamins）研究了AI模型如何以一种高度抽象化的形式，实现大脑用来响应新奇视觉刺激的机制。曹和亚明斯承认，大脑与工程化的AI面临不同的能量约束，但他们只从效率与优化的角度抽象地考量能量问题。我们则主张自下而上地重新思考AI的能量使用。一个AI系统如何使用能量，本身就是一个建模问题，它取决于规模、训练数据和电网基础设施的细节，正如大脑的能量使用取决于其代谢的细节。当我们只关注任务优化时，就会错过研究大脑与研究AI系统之间的这一关键平行关系。为AI的能量使用建模，能告诉我们能量上的差异在何时、以何种方式引出计算上的乃至认知上的差异。

Our research belongs to a growing discussion of energy use and its role in modelling both brains and AI systems. For example, Cao and the neuroscientist Daniel Yamins have studied how AI models implement a highly abstract version of the mechanism that brains use to respond to novel visual stimuli. Cao and Yamins recognise that brains and engineered AI face different energy constraints, yet they consider energetics only abstractly, in terms of efficiency and optimisation. We propose instead to rethink energy use in AI from the bottom up. How an AI system uses energy is a modelling problem, which depends on scale, training data and details of grid infrastructure, just as the brain’s energy use depends on the details of its metabolism. This crucial parallel between studying brains and AI systems is missed when we focus on task optimisation alone. Modelling AI energy use informs how and when energetic differences lead to computational, perhaps even cognitive, differences.

与大脑不同，AI系统是我们设计出来的东西，所以我们可以改变它们使用能量的方式，看看随后会发生什么。AI的能量使用是一个实际问题，而代谢研究可以帮助解决它。梅根·彼得斯（Megan Peters）等认知科学家已经在向大脑取经，以改进AI的能量效率。这些项目凸显出，研究大脑代谢能给AI模型构建之类的工程项目带来实际益处。它们同样具有理论上的益处：那些把能量使用纳入其中的项目，可以充当媒介独立性这类哲学立场的试金石。

Unlike brains, AI systems are things we design, so we can change how they use energy and see what follows. AI energy use is a practical problem that the study of metabolism can help tackle. Cognitive scientists such as Megan Peters are already looking to the brain to improve energy efficiency in AI. These projects highlight the practical benefits that come from studying brain metabolism for engineering projects like AI model building. They also have theoretical benefits: projects that include energy use serve as tests of philosophical positions like medium independence.

至于该如何为心智建模，借用我们同事帕特里克·麦吉弗恩（Patrick McGivern）的话说，现在是研究者采取「代谢转向」（metabolic turn）的时候了。认真对待系统如何使用能量、如何调节自身，将为我们理解心智时面对的那些最棘手的问题，提供崭新的解答途径。

When it comes to figuring out how to model minds, it is time that researchers take a ‘metabolic turn’, to quote our colleague Patrick McGivern. Taking seriously how systems use energy and regulate themselves gives us new ways of addressing the toughest questions that we face in making sense of the mind.
