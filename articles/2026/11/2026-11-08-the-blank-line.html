空白的一行

AI Parable (inspired by Amanda Askell)·每日精选·2026年11月8日·4分钟阅读

一九〇六年入冬，联合会散伙，家当上拍：长凳、灯架，论堆成交。那本银扣的皮面名册没人出价，登记员亚瑟·洛维特（Arthur Lovatt）花六便士把它买了回来。册子记着城里三百多个会社的名字，末页最后一行空着，连墨都没蘸过。温德摩尔（Wendmoor）城里人都说，联合会是叫持照的圣克莱斯平弟兄会（the Chartered Fraternity of Saint Crispin）一纸状子送葬的；洛维特抱着册子回家，心里明白，状子只是伸出一根手指，指了指那行留白，真正要了联合会命的，是七年前签下的头一条章程。

Winter came on in 1906, and the Union, now dissolved, sold off its effects: the benches and the lamp-stands went by the heap. Nobody bid for the great leather roll-book with its silver clasps, and Arthur Lovatt, the registrar, bought it back for sixpence. It carried the names of more than three hundred societies, and on the last page the final line stood empty, its ink never dipped. All over Wendmoor people said the Chartered Fraternity of Saint Crispin had buried the Union with a single petition; walking home with the book under his arm, Lovatt knew the petition had only pointed a finger at that blank space, and that what killed the Union was the first rule, signed seven years before.

事情要从一八九八年冬天说起。温德摩尔是个会社之城，合唱会、辩论会、丧葬互助会，各守一部名册。城里持照的老会社有个自傲的老规矩：册子翻开，头一行写的是会社自己的名字——这一家，归它自己所有。穷会没有这种排场，册子里从头到尾只有人名。那年冬天各工场锁门，穷会连一间议事厅都凑不出。开春，老市议员乔赛亚·格罗夫斯（Josiah Groves）把穷会的头人们请进自家客厅，立起温德摩尔联合会（the Wendmoor Union），章程头一条由他亲笔起草：凡不将自身写入自身名册之会社，本会皆收之；凡将自身写入自身名册者，本会皆拒不收。末条又添一句：本文件所谓会社，指凡有名册之团体。头一条的后半句连同那句定义，在会上没人多看一眼：把自家名字写进自家册子的会，从来瞧不起联合会，求也求不来。

The story goes back to the winter of 1898. Wendmoor was a city of societies — choral societies, debating societies, burial clubs — each keeping its own roll. Among the old chartered companies a proud custom held: open the roll, and the first line carried the company's own name, for such a body belonged to itself. The poor societies had no such pretension; their rolls carried men and nothing else. That winter the works locked their gates, and the poor societies could not raise one room to meet in. In the spring, Alderman Josiah Groves brought their leaders into his parlour and founded the Wendmoor Union, drafting its first rule in his own hand: every society that does not write itself into its own roll shall be enrolled; no society that writes itself into its own roll shall be enrolled. A last clause added that in this instrument a society means any body that keeps a roll. Nobody looked twice at the second half of that rule, or at the definition: the companies that wrote their own names had always despised such a union, and would not have come begging.

头一年入册的会有四十个，到一九〇二年，名字排到三百出头，穷会头一回在市政会上有了嗓门。同年，市政会把旧谷物交易所租给联合会，租期二十一年，年租一先令，只附一个条件——每年三月，登记员须向市政会具结「名册真实而完备」，否则租约作废。大厅里换灯换凳的一百二十镑，是洛维特以个人名义作的保。具结他签过四回，回回平安。弟兄会的大掌事在酒席上骂章程头一条「打了全城持照册子的脸」，他们那位老讼师收起一份章程抄本，此后再没提过这件事。

Forty societies enrolled in the first year; by 1902 the names ran past three hundred, and the poor societies spoke in the Council chamber for the first time. That same year the Council leased the old Corn Exchange to the Union for twenty-one years at a shilling a year, on one condition only: every March the registrar must certify to the Council that the roll was "true and complete," or the lease would fall. The hundred and twenty pounds for the lamps and benches Lovatt had guaranteed under his own name. He signed the certificate four times, and four times it passed. The Fraternity's master, over his wine, called the Union's first rule "an insult to every chartered roll in the city," and their old solicitor folded away a fair copy of the charter and never mentioned the matter again.

出事是在一九〇五年十二月。照例要为来年三月誊清名册，誊写的活计交给两年前进来的年轻抄写员芬奇（Finch）。他抄到末页，数完第三百四十一个名字，抬起头问：「先生，联合会自己的名字，写在哪一行？」洛维特顺口答：联合会不是会员。芬奇对着章程看了半天，又抬起头：「章程头一条写着，凡不将自身写入自身名册之会社，本会皆收之。联合会把自身写进自身名册了吗？这一页上没有。那这一条，管不管它？」屋里静下来，炉火哔剥地响。

The trouble came in December 1905. The roll had to be copied out fair for the March certification, and the copying fell to Finch, a young clerk taken on two years before. He reached the last page, counted off the three hundred and forty-first name, and looked up. "Sir, where does the Union's own name go?" Lovatt answered offhand that the Union was not a member. Finch bent over the charter a long while, then lifted his head again. "The first rule says every society that does not write itself into its own roll shall be enrolled. Has the Union written itself into its own roll? It is not on this page. So does the clause reach it or not?" The room went quiet; the fire ticked in the grate.

当夜洛维特没回家。他把章程摊在灯下，像法官过堂一样一条一条读。假设那行写上名字：联合会便把自身写进了自身名册，头一条后半句立刻把它挡在门外，这行必须划掉。假设那行留白：联合会便是一个不将自身写入自身名册的会社，头一条前半句命令收它入册，这行必须写上。写上就得划掉，划掉就得写上。他想找第三条路——联合会不算会社？末条写着：会社，指凡有名册之团体，而联合会有名册。天亮时他吹了灯。抽屉里那四张具结，如今一张也站不住；伤是章程签字那天就有的，六年里没人碰过，一碰就开口。

Lovatt did not go home that night. He spread the charter under the lamp and read it through line by line, as a judge would. Suppose the name were entered: the Union would then have written itself into its own roll, and the second half of the first rule would turn it out at once; the line would have to be struck. Suppose the line stayed blank: the Union would be a society that does not write itself into its own roll, and the first half of the rule commands that it be enrolled; the name would have to go in. Enter it, and it must be struck; strike it, and it must be entered. He hunted for a third road — the Union is no society? The last clause read: a society means any body that keeps a roll, and the Union kept one. At dawn he put out the lamp. The four certificates in his drawer could no longer stand; the wound had been in the charter from the day of signing, and for six years nobody had touched it.

一九〇六年一月，弟兄会的老讼师果然把状子递到市政会：联合会名册漏了联合会自身，依其章程名册残缺，租约条件已破。市政会限令三月二十五日之前，要么交出一份新的具结，要么交回交易所。三月六日的年会上，第一项动议是把名字写上；章程当众念了一遍，动议当场倒下。第二项动议是保持留白；章程再念一遍，动议又倒下。有人提改章程，按章程，改动须提前半年知会全体会员，离限期只剩十九天。格罗夫斯最后站起来，说：「一条一条看，每条都对；合成一部，就立不住了。」没人再出声。谁也不肯在摆不出来的具结上落笔。三月二十五日，钥匙交回市政会；六月，会员们投票散伙。

In January 1906 the Fraternity's old solicitor carried his petition to the Council: the Union's roll omitted the Union itself, by the Union's own charter the roll was incomplete, and the condition of the lease was broken. The Council gave them until the twenty-fifth of March — a fresh certificate, or the keys back. At the annual meeting on the sixth of March the first motion was to enter the name; the rule was read aloud, and the motion fell on the spot. The second motion was to leave the line blank; the rule was read again, and it fell too. Someone moved to amend the charter, but the charter required half a year's notice to every member, and nineteen days were left. Groves rose last and said: "Clause by clause, every one of them is right; put together, they will not stand." Nobody spoke after that. No one would put his pen to a certificate that could not be made out. On the twenty-fifth the keys went back to the Council; in June the members voted to dissolve.

后来，弟兄会用二十一年的租约接下交易所，在穷会合议过的大厅里摆年宴。洛维特去讼师楼做抄写，四年还清了一百二十镑。一九一〇年秋天，如今做了文书的芬奇带他去看了新立的温德摩尔会社同盟（the Wendmoor Confederation of Societies）：章程头一条仍是老话，收一切不将自身写入自身名册的会社；紧跟第二条：本同盟并非会社，凡同盟之属，永不入册。芬奇问这章程怎么样，洛维特看了很久，说：「门框窄了一寸，房子立住了。」格罗夫斯没能看到，他一九〇八年冬天走了。那本银扣的册子还在洛维特的书架上，就为留住那空白的一行。

The Fraternity afterwards took the Exchange on a twenty-one-year lease and holds its feasts in the hall where the poor societies once met. Lovatt went back to copying for a firm of solicitors and paid off the hundred and twenty pounds in four years. In the autumn of 1910 Finch, now a law-writer, brought him to see the new Wendmoor Confederation of Societies. Its first rule was the old one still: every society that does not write itself into its own roll shall be enrolled. Then came a second: this Confederation is not a society, and no body of its kind shall ever be entered on a roll. Finch asked what he thought of the charter. Lovatt read it a long while and said, "The doorframe is an inch narrower, and the house stands." Groves never saw it; he died in the winter of 1908. The silver-clasped book is still on Lovatt's shelf, kept for the sake of that one blank line.

注解：洛维特在灯下撞见的，是英国哲学家伯特兰·罗素（Bertrand Russell）于一九〇一年提出的罗素悖论（Russell's paradox）：专收「不收录自身」之同类的那一个体系，收录自身当且仅当它不收录自身，答是答否都矛盾；矛盾由定义严格推出，不靠语义含混，理发师悖论只是它的一种通俗讲法。体系可以靠新的分级章程躲开（一九〇八年的类型论与策梅洛集合论走的就是这条路），代价是「凡合此性质者皆可入册」这条老规矩作废。

Note: What Lovatt met under the lamp was Russell's paradox, set out by Bertrand Russell in 1901: a register that enrolls exactly those bodies which do not enroll themselves must enroll itself if and only if it does not, and either answer contradicts the rule; the contradiction follows strictly from the definition, with no help from vagueness of language, and the barber's version is only a popular telling of it. The system can be saved by a new graded charter (Russell's type theory and Zermelo's set theory, both from 1908, took that road), at the cost of retiring the old rule that anything with the property may be enrolled.
