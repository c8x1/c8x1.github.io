省日子的路

AI Parable (inspired by Amanda Askell)·每日精选·2026年11月7日·3分钟阅读

背盐的人上路，先削一根新杖。规矩是路上每过一天，杖上刻一道；到家那天，家里人拿杖去驿门口对日牌——日牌一天翻一块，翻了多少块，家里就过了多少天。老辈人把两条道分得清清楚楚：近道磨人；大环外头绕得远，他们管它叫省日子的路。大环出了驿就不回头，绕黑水、嘎果兰十一个站口，把半个云岭兜进一趟脚程；回来对牌，杖上添的道数，总比日牌翻的块数少。兜得越远，走得越急，少得越多。

A salt porter setting out cuts himself a new staff first. The rule is one notch for every day on the road; on the day he comes home, his family carries the staff to the station gate and checks it against the day-board — the board turns over one tag for each day, and however many tags lie turned, that is how many days the household has lived through. The old men keep the two roads cleanly apart: the near road wears a man down; the great ring, looping wide outside, they call the road that spares your days. Once the great ring leaves the station it does not double back; it loops past Heishui and Gaguolan and eleven way-stations, folding half the Yunling range into a single journey. A man comes home and sets his staff against the board, and the notches he has gained are always fewer than the tags the board has turned. The wider the loop, the harder the pace, the fewer still.

省下的日子去了哪里，驿后山没有学问，只有一条老例。早年间有个叫哑叔的脚夫接了趟急脚，走的是大环外头再套一圈的道——过黑水、嘎果兰，往西穿过瘴地和冰川，一趟把整条盐道走到头。他动身那天，闺女刚会走路；回来那天，闺女抱着自己的闺女在驿门口等他。日牌翻过去六千块，他杖上添了九十一道。哑叔不觉得亏，见人就比划：路太长，日子不够花在道上。跑大环的还都遇过一桩事：对向错身的两个脚夫，过后各人咬定，错身那几日对方的杖刻得比自己的稀。到了驿里，关账房只回一句，路上的事路上算，进了驿门才算得清。

Where the spared days go, Yihoushan has no learning to offer, only one handed-down case. In the old years a porter called Uncle Ya took a desperate errand, by the road that loops outside the great ring, one ring beyond it — past Heishui and Gaguolan, west through the fever marshes and the glaciers, walking the whole salt road to its end in a single go. The day he set out, his daughter had just learned to walk; the day he came back, she was waiting at the station gate with her own daughter in her arms. The day-board had turned six thousand tags; his staff had gained ninety-one notches. Uncle Ya counted himself no poorer; he would gesture at anyone who would watch: the road was too long, the days were not enough to spend on it. Every runner of the great ring has met this too: two porters who cross each other walking opposite ways, and afterwards each will swear that in the days of the passing the other's notches came sparser than his own. At the station, Clerk Guan answers with one line: on the road, reckon as the road does; inside the station gate is where it can be reckoned clear.

邵六斤背上债那年，杏儿七岁，正换牙。秋汛冲了他替行里背的一趟盐，盐化了水，行里照章索赔，五十吊。关账房把两条路摆给他：近道一日一回，脚钱六十文，赔清得八百多天，天天见得着闺女；大环一趟十八吊，走三趟连利钱都清，杖上统共添不满二百道，只是家里这头的日子，得他自己认。邵六斤在家坐了半夜，天亮削了根新杖，临走跟杏儿说：「爹去一趟大的，回来带雾渡坪的糖。」

The year Shao Liujin took on his debt, Xing'er was seven and changing her teeth. The autumn floods had swallowed a load of salt he was carrying for the salt house; the salt dissolved into the water, and the house claimed its price by the rules: fifty strings. Clerk Guan laid the two roads out for him: the near road, home every day, sixty cash a day, eight hundred days and more to clear the debt, and his daughter in sight every evening; the great ring, eighteen strings a trip, three trips to clear debt and interest together, fewer than two hundred notches gained in all — only the days on the household's side, those he would have to accept for himself. Shao Liujin sat up half the night; at first light he cut a new staff, and leaving, he said to Xing'er: "Your pa is going on a big one; I'll bring you back sugar from Wuduping."

头一趟，他杖上添了七十道，日牌翻了五百块。走到黑水，捎来的口信说杏儿又掉了颗牙，是新换的底下那颗。第二趟他掐着日子赶，杖上只添了六十一道；走到嘎果兰，口信说邵家娘咳了血。也是那阵子，驿里新来的账房嫌两本账麻烦，把杖账并进牌账里记，两个月里黑水的盐压着发不动，行里多贴出一年的利钱，两头账目又分开记了。驿里的老话本来就在：杖上记的账走杖上的，日牌记的账走日牌的，两本不对，班次就乱。

On the first trip his staff gained seventy notches, and the day-board turned five hundred tags. At Heishui a carried message said Xing'er had lost another tooth — the new one coming in below. On the second trip he pressed the days hard, and the staff gained only sixty-one; at Gaguolan the message said his mother had coughed blood. In those same months a new clerk at the station, tired of keeping two ledgers, folded the staff-ledger into the board-ledger; for two months the Heishui salt sat unshipped, the salt house paid out a year's interest beyond its due, and the two ledgers were parted again. The station's old saying had been there all along: accounts kept on the staff travel by the staff, accounts kept on the board travel by the board; let the two fail to agree, and the schedules tangle.

第三趟走到黑水，驿里捎来的是丧信。邵家娘没了，杏儿和邻居们葬的她，坟在驿后山向阳的坡上。大环上没有折返的路，折回去等于从头再走一遍，邵六斤把丧信揣进怀里接着走。到家那天是腊月，开门的杏儿个头过了灶台，牙早就换齐了。关账房当众对账：三趟，杖上一百九十一道，日牌一千五百三十七块。债连利清了，他手里还捏着几串钱——那是给杏儿买糖剩下的。

On the third trip, at Heishui, what the station sent was funeral news. His mother was gone; Xing'er and the neighbours had buried her, the grave on the sun-facing slope above Yihoushan. There is no turning back on the great ring — to turn back is to walk it again from the beginning — so Shao Liujin folded the news into his shirt and walked on. He came home in the twelfth month; the Xing'er who opened the door stood taller than the stove, her teeth long since grown in. Clerk Guan checked the ledgers before everyone: three trips, one hundred and ninety-one notches on the staff, one thousand five hundred and thirty-seven tags on the board. The debt was cleared with its interest, and he still held a few strings in his hand — what was left over after buying Xing'er's sugar.

糖是雾渡坪买的，杏儿接过去，说了声甜。她小时候要人拍着才睡得着的那些夜里，她爹的杖上一道也没有。后来邵六斤只走近道。有人问亏不亏，他不接这个话，只把杖给人看，近道一天一道，跟日牌翻得一样快。驿门口两个老脚夫喝了酒还在争当年的事，各说各的杖稀，掌了灯也没争散。又隔了一年，杏儿出门子。那天日牌翻过一块，邵六斤的杖上，也添了一道。

The sugar was bought at Wuduping; Xing'er took it and said it was sweet. In all those nights of her childhood when she could only sleep if someone patted her, her father's staff carried not one notch. Afterwards Shao Liujin walked only the near road. Asked whether he had lost out, he would not take up the question; he only showed the staff — the near road adds one notch a day, as fast as the day-board turns. Two old porters, into their wine at the station gate, were still arguing over that old affair, each swearing his own notches had come sparser; they had not settled it by lamplight. A year on, Xing'er married out of the house. That day the day-board turned over one tag, and on Shao Liujin's staff, one notch was added too.

注解：时间膨胀（time dilation）：跑大环的人穿过的地方越多、走得越急，自己过的日子就越少——他的杖比家里的日牌走得慢，走得越急差得越多。路上错身的两个脚夫，各看对方的杖走得慢，谁也没有看错；只有进了驿门对账，这笔账才算得清。

Note: Time dilation: the farther a man travels and the harder his pace, the fewer days he himself lives through — his staff runs slower than his household's day-board, and the harder the pace, the wider the gap. Two porters who cross each other on the road each see the other's staff running slow, and neither is mistaken; only inside the station gate, where the ledgers are checked, does that account come clear.
