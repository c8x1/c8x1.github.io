借丝的账

AI Parable (inspired by Amanda Askell)·每日精选·2026年11月15日·3分钟阅读

云家绣坊在青墩镇西头，五代人做绣，各家配色调线的事，一向由云家娘居中经手。这年入夏，云家娘病倒，当票压在枕头底下：祖传的枣木绷架当在东街裕丰当铺，端午日满，期满不赎就成了死当。她跟女儿停云说：「绷架要传给你，我闭眼前想摸着它。」赎架的钱差一截，差的正是账簿里六笔借丝往来的中人钱——每笔一成，端午前当面勾清一笔，才得一笔。账簿摊在灯下，停云一笔一笔地看：云家对染坊巷陈家一笔；陈家对香烛铺白家、北头宋家各一笔；白家对毡坊顾家一笔；顾家对集上阿圆一笔；阿圆对宋家一笔。六笔，散在五条巷子里，端午只剩三天。

The Yun embroidery workshop sat at the west end of Qingdun Town, five generations of needlework, and the matching and lending of thread between households had always run through the Yun mother's hands. In early summer the mother fell ill and could not rise. Under her pillow lay a pawn ticket: the ancestral jujube-wood embroidery frame, pledged to the Yufeng pawnshop on East Street, due at Duanwu, and past that day forfeit. She had told her daughter Tingyun, "The frame will pass to you; I want to touch it before I close my eyes." The ransom was short by a margin, and the margin sat in the ledger — six entries of borrowed silk, each worth a broker's tenth, collected only when the two houses settled face to face before Duanwu. Tingyun spread the ledger under the lamp and read it entry by entry: Yun against the Chen family of the dyers' lane, one; Chen against the Bai family of the incense shop and the Song household at the north end, one each; Bai against the Gu felt-maker, one; Gu against A'yuan, who kept a stall at the market, one; A'yuan against the Song household, one. Six entries scattered across five lanes, and three days left before Duanwu.

第一天她图顺脚，从集上阿圆的摊子起。阿圆对宋家的一笔，当面勾了；她随宋家娘子往北头，勾宋家对陈家的；陈家娘子又领她回染坊巷，把陈家欠云家的那笔也办了。这一笔勾完，她顺路回到家，看了娘一眼。灯下再翻账簿，她才发现岔子：勾掉的三笔是一顺下来的，余下的三笔——陈家对白家、白家对顾家、顾家对阿圆——都在东半镇，她人却在西头，明日还得空着脚重过半个镇子。三天勾三笔，脚程倒有一半花在没账的路上。

The first day she went for convenience, starting from A'yuan's stall at the market. The entry between A'yuan and the Song household was settled face to face; she walked on with Mistress Song to the north end and settled the Song-Chen entry; Mistress Chen led her back down the dyers' lane, and the Chen family's entry with the Yun house was settled too. With that done she slipped home and looked in on her mother. Opening the ledger again under the lamp, she saw the trouble: the three settled entries had run in one line, and the three left — Chen with Bai, Bai with Gu, Gu with A'yuan — all lay across the east half of town, while she stood at the west end, with half a town of empty road between her and them tomorrow. Three entries in a day, and half the walking spent on roads with no account on them.

她夜里睡不着，把账簿摊开，一户一户数。数着数着，看出一样东西：一户人家名下的笔数，有单有双。她自己家名下一笔，单；陈家三笔，也是单；白、顾、阿圆、宋四家，都是双。她拿笔在纸上画：一户一个圈，一笔账一条线，线连着圈。再用指头从阿圆的圈起，一条线一条线地走，走到单笔的圈就死住了，回不出去；从自家的圈起，一条线走到底，收在陈家，六条线一条不落；倒过来从陈家起，也成。双笔的圈起头，总要折返；单笔的圈起头，一路通到另一个单笔的圈，就到头了。她把纸压在账簿底下，吹了灯。

She lay awake that night with the ledger open, counting household by household. Somewhere in the counting she saw a thing: the entries under a household's name ran single or double. Her own house held one — single; the Chen family held three, single as well; the Bai, Gu, A'yuan and Song houses each held double. She took a brush and drew on paper — one circle for a household, one stroke for an entry, the strokes joining circle to circle. With her finger she walked the strokes out of A'yuan's circle, line by line: at a single-entry circle the trail died, and there was no walking out of it. From her own circle it ran stroke by stroke to the end and came to rest at the Chen circle, all six strokes behind it; run backward from Chen, the same. Start at a double-entry circle and you must double back; start at a single-entry circle and the road runs clear through to another single-entry circle, and there it ends. She pressed the paper under the ledger and put out the lamp.

娘的药已断了两日，第二日一早，她先到染坊巷口的药铺抓药，转身进了陈家的门。头一笔是陈家对白家的：陈家娘子同她去香烛铺；出了香烛铺，白掌柜又领她去毡坊，白家对顾家的一笔也勾了；顾家婶子收线入柜，末一笔顾家对阿圆，正赶上顾家往集上送毡，两下同路。一条线走到底，收在阿圆的摊子前；她买了针，日头才过晌午。头一天断在脚上的空路，这一天一步没走。灯下她翻开账簿：这一趟起头的陈家名下落单，收尾的阿圆也落单，夹在中间的白、顾两家都是双笔——昨夜纸上画的圈和线，走到街上来了。

Her mother's medicine had run out two days before, and the second morning she went first to the pharmacy at the mouth of the dyers' lane, then turned straight in at the Chen door. The first entry was Chen with Bai: Mistress Chen walked with her to the incense shop; out of the incense shop, Master Bai led her on to the felt-maker's, and the Bai-Gu entry was settled too; the Gu mistress took the thread into her cabinet, and the last entry, Gu with A'yuan, fell in along the road, for the Gu household was sending felt to the market that day and the two ways ran together. One line walked to its end, coming to rest in front of A'yuan's stall; she bought her needles, and the sun had barely passed noon. The empty road that had broken under her feet the first day, this day she did not walk at all. Under the lamp she opened the ledger: the house this round started from, Chen, held a single entry; the house it ended at, A'yuan, single as well; the two houses folded between, Bai and Gu, held double — the circles and strokes drawn on paper the night before had walked out into the street.

端午日一早，裕丰当铺刚下门板，停云把一包钱推上柜台，赎回了绷架。到家时娘正醒着。她把绷架放到娘的手边，娘的手在上面摸了一阵，从边框摸到铜楔，又摸回来。「账都清了？」「清了。一家不欠一家。」娘闭上眼，过了一会儿说：「你走这些巷子，比我当年走得明白。」窗外粽叶的香气漫进来，云家绣坊这一天没有开炉。

On the morning of Duanwu, when the Yufeng pawnshop had just taken down its shutters, Tingyun pushed a wrapped stack of cash across the counter and ransomed the frame. Her mother was awake when she got home. She set the frame by her mother's hand, and the old woman's fingers traveled over it a while, from the rim to the copper wedge and back. "The accounts all settled?" "Settled. No house owes another." Her mother closed her eyes; after a time she said, "You walk these lanes more clearly than I did at your age." The scent of zongzi leaves drifted in at the window, and the Yun workshop kept its fire cold that day.

注解：停云在账簿上看出的，是欧拉路径（Euler paths）的道理：把人家画作圈、往来画作线，一条路能否走遍每笔账而不折返，只看「落单」人家的数目——没有落单的，从谁家起头都能转回原处；恰有两个，就得从一家起到另一家收；再多，一条路走不完，得分几趟。她两回进陈家的门、两头到集上，都不碍事——账不许勾重，门口可以重进。

Note: What Tingyun read out of the ledger was the property of Euler paths: draw a circle for each household and a stroke for each exchange, and whether one trail can cover every entry without doubling back depends only on the number of single-entry households — with none, any starting house brings you back to where you began; with exactly two, the trail must set out from one and end at the other; with more, one trail will not cover it, and the rounds must be split. That she passed through the Chen door twice and crossed the market twice did no harm — an entry may be settled only once, but a doorstep may be crossed again.
