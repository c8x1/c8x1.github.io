认路的毛

AI Parable (inspired by Amanda Askell)·每日精选·2026年11月2日·4分钟阅读

夜里落了头一场霜，桑五在棚里守着十二包羊毛，听见有人拍门。来的是南行定级的曲晚，手里拎着一只小布袋。「呢坊退回来的三包头等，有一包是你的。桑五，开包那晚我摸了八遍才落章。」桑五把她让到灯下，先给火添了柴。「曲姑娘，牧场押在钱庄，明天大拍要是凑不上数，开春地就换了主。你有什么话，直说。」她从袋里拈出一撮毛摊在灯前，毛尖上的卷，和整包的不像一个娘生的。「这撮细毛，埋在你包角上。我爹落章三十年，我落了七年，章子从来没有回过手。今晚我来回这一回。」

The first frost of the year fell that night. Sang Wu was minding his twelve wool bales in the shed when someone knocked. It was Qu Wan, the grader from the south house, a small cloth bag in her hand. "Three top-grade bales came back from the mill, and one of them is yours. Sang, I felt through that bale eight times before I stamped it." He brought her under the lamp and fed the fire first. "Miss Qu, the pasture is mortgaged to the money house. If tomorrow's sale falls short of the number, the land has a new owner by spring. Whatever you came to say, say it." She picked a pinch of wool from the bag and spread it under the lamp; the crimp at the tips didn't belong to the fleece around it. "This pinch of fine wool was buried in your corner. My father stamped for thirty years, I have stamped for seven, and neither of us ever took a stamp back. Tonight I came to take one back."

桑五盯着那撮毛看了半天，说一群羊身上的毛，粗细本是天生的。曲晚不接这话，只讲开春北行也收过两包一样做过手脚的包角，打头等的是老关。「他开包不走肩线，走脊背，手法跟我两样，翻得一样快。我们俩是一个师门教出来的，毛病也是同一个。」桑五坐了半晌，才说是侄子小满弄出来的。开春小满把一包毛整个抖散，细毛粗毛拌匀，她上手就摸成了混等，价钱压得更低。后来他蹲在行里看她开包：每包都顺着肩线开，一路摸过去，步子从不换。他就把细公羊的毛掐成针脚那么细，顺着肩线一路埋下去，单看哪一撮都薄得手上觉不出，可她的手每落一下都带起一点细，整包的裁定就翻过去了。小满管这叫认路的毛。曲晚听完，静了半天，才说：「我爹打了三十年头等的包，离次等，原来只有一条肩线宽。」

Sang stared at the pinch of wool for a long time and said wool off one mob of sheep runs fine in places and coarse in others; that is born into it. Qu Wan didn't take that up. She only said the north house had taken two bales with corners worked the same way that spring, and the man who stamped them top grade was Old Guan. "He opens a bale along the spine, not the shoulder. His hands and mine are nothing alike, and his verdict tipped over just the same. One master taught the both of us, and the flaw is the same one too." Sang sat a long while before he said it was his nephew Xiaoman who had worked it out. In the spring Xiaoman had shaken out a whole bale and stirred the fine wool evenly through the coarse; her hands had read it as mixed grade on the first pass, and the price had been pushed lower. After that he crouched in her house and watched her open bales: every bale opened along the shoulder line, one rhythm of pulls from end to end, never a step off. So he plucked wool from the fine rams, teased it into strands no thicker than a stitch, and laid it along the shoulder line, each pinch too slight for any hand to feel on its own; yet every time her hand came down it lifted a little fineness with it, and the verdict on the whole bale tipped. Xiaoman called it wool that knew the road. When she had heard it all, Qu Wan was quiet a long time. Then she said, "My father stamped top grade for thirty years. It turns out top grade sat one shoulder line away from second."

桑五把火钳搁下了。「曲姑娘，我那十二包，老老实实是次等，抵不上钱庄的数，就那一包头的能把我从水里捞出来。你今晚装没看见，开春我拿头一茬细毛谢你。」曲晚说她的章不卖这个情。桑五换了个方向逼她：「你明早当众拆包，你那个从不回手的章就碎了。行里得养两个摸级的，慢一半，老客都要骂。再说苇家去年那批碱地毛，一根毛没人动过，你和老关都打了次等，打得笃定，呢坊裁开，织出来是头等的料。错的不是哪双手，是这门手艺认毛的路子。把我那包拆了，垫进去的毛你拆得出来；天生的，你拆给我看？」

Sang set the fire tongs down. "Miss Qu, my twelve bales are honestly second grade, and second grade doesn't reach the number I owe; that one worked-over bale is what lifts me out of the water. See nothing tonight, and come spring I'll thank you with the first clip of fine wool." Qu Wan said her stamp wasn't for sale at that price. Sang came at her from another side. "Open bales in front of the buyers tomorrow, and the stamp that never came back is broken. The house will have to keep two graders grading half as fast, and the old customers will curse. Then take the Wei family's alkali-flat clip from last year. Not one staple on it was ever touched; you and Old Guan both stamped it second, stamped it without a flicker of doubt, and when the mill cut it open, it wove into top-grade cloth. It isn't any pair of hands that's wrong, it's the road this craft reads wool by. Tear my bale apart and you'll pull out what was laid in. The born kind, tear that out and show me."

曲晚把那撮毛收回袋里。「天亮头一件事就是拆包，拆的不止你这一包，去年落过章的都抽着拆。亏的钱从我的年俸里扣，老客的骂我也认。苇家那样的，我当众把次等改成头等，章边添一行小字：改过。往后我这章落得慢，行里跟着贵，那是它本来就该有的价。」桑五在灯下坐到火塌了半边，起身把那包头等的往外拖。曲晚问他做什么。「明天你自己拆，和我头一个站出来认，是两码事。小满管那毛叫认路的毛，如今路认到我家门口，我把它堵在门里头。」他把包推到门边，又说：「拆了这包，钱庄的数还差一截。天一亮，我把那七只细毛公羊牵去牛市。地留住，羊断了线，往后这片地上生的，都是粗毛。」

Qu Wan put the pinch of wool back in her bag. "The first thing at daylight is opening bales, and not only yours. Everything I stamped last season gets pulled and re-cut. The loss comes out of my year's fee, and the customers' cursing is mine to carry. Bales like the Wei family's, I'll change in the open, second grade to top, with a small line added beside the stamp: revised. From now on my stamp will fall slowly and the house will run dear, and that is what it should always have cost." Sang sat under the lamp until half the fire fell in, then got up and dragged the top-grade bale toward the door. She asked what he was doing. "You cutting it open tomorrow, and me standing up first to own it, are two different things. Xiaoman calls that wool wool that knew the road. Now the road has come to my own gate, and I mean to block it inside the yard." He pushed the bale against the door and added, "With this one gone, the number I owe still comes up short. At first light I'll walk the seven fine rams to the cattle market. The land stays and the line of sheep ends. What grows on this ground from here on is coarse wool."

第二年大拍，曲晚的案子边上多了一块板，摆着两只筐：一筐装拆出来的垫角毛，一筐装苇家那样天生骗手的毛。她每天摸级前，先把两筐各摸一遍，南行北行翻出来的角，也都往她这儿送。桑五扛着自己次等的包上垛，顺口问她值不值。她说一天摸的包少了三成，行里添了第二双手；这账她天天记，偶尔吃亏的老实毛，也记在同一本上。开春又出了新花样：有人不再垫角，改成把针脚细的毛匀匀撒进整包，撒得又匀又薄，两行又都翻了，她重新蹲下去看自己的手。桑五说：「地还在，羊没了，毛倒睡得着觉。」曲晚没接这话，添完章边那行小字，把垫角毛的筐往里挪了挪，给它腾出半格空的。

By the next year's sale a plank had grown beside Qu Wan's grading table, holding two baskets: one for corners pulled out of bales, one for wool like the Wei family's that fools honest hands by birth. Before she graded anything each day she felt through both baskets, and the corners the two houses caught were all sent to her. Sang Wu shouldered his second-grade bales onto the stack and asked her, in passing, whether it was worth it. A third fewer bales a day, she said, and a second pair of hands on the house's wage; she kept the account daily, and the honest fleeces that now and then took a lower stamp were written in the same book. That spring a new turn came: nobody worked corners anymore; a grower had teased the stitch-fine wool thin and scattered it evenly through a whole bale, so thin and even that both houses tipped over again, and she was back down on her knees, watching her own hands. Sang said, "The land's still here, the sheep are gone, and the wool sleeps better at night." Qu Wan didn't answer. She finished the small line beside her stamp, shifted the basket of corners over, and left half a space empty in it.

注解：这说的是对抗样本（adversarial examples）——在正常输入上叠加人眼几乎察觉不到的微小改动，机器学习模型就会以高置信度错判类别，同一套改法还常能骗过别的模型，根源在高维模型的近似线性响应。补一句边界：寓言里垫角的毛拆开来还看得见，现实的改动往往细到任何人盯不出来；而且没人动过的真实输入同样能让模型笃定出错，蓄意构造并非必要条件。

Note: this is about adversarial examples: laying an almost imperceptible change onto a normal input makes a machine-learning model misclassify it with high confidence, and the same crafted change often transfers to other models, a weakness rooted in the near-linear behavior of high-dimensional models. One honest boundary: in the fable the laid-in wool can still be seen if a bale is torn open, while real perturbations are usually invisible even to a close look; and untouched, real inputs fool models too, so deliberate craft is not a necessary condition.
