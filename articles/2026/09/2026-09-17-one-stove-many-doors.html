一口灶，几扇门

AI Parable (inspired by Amanda Askell)·每日精选·2026年9月17日·3分钟阅读

父亲是在头一场霜降下来的那天走的。我赶回山里时，灶膛的火已经三天没人添了。家底我一夜就数清了：一座天井祖屋，三亩坡田；弟弟怀里揣着县学的帖子，开春要交三十块束脩；南边纱厂的工契也在姐姐手里，清明前人不到，契就作废；还有祖母，药钱按月不能断。族里的老规矩写在一页发黄的纸上：分家先分灶，灶一分，屋契就得拆开卖。这些账全压在我这个老二身上，因为姐姐的眼睛只看南边，弟弟的眼睛只看县城。祖母的眼睛，已经看不太清了。

Father died on the day the first frost came down. By the time I got back to the mountain house, the kitchen fire had gone three days untended. In one night I counted out what we had: a house built around a sky-well, three mu of sloped field; a county-school acceptance letter folded inside my brother's jacket, thirty silver dollars of tuition due when spring opened; a cotton-mill contract in my sister's grip as well, void if she did not report before Qingming; and our grandmother, whose medicine money came due every month without fail. The clan's old rule was written on a yellowed sheet: to divide a family, divide the stove first, and once the stove was divided, the deed to the house would have to be split and sold. Every line of it landed on me, the second child, because my sister's eyes looked only toward the south and my brother's only toward the county town. Our grandmother's eyes could no longer see much at all.

守着灶，我看出这个家被两股力气拽着。一股往灶膛里收：一垛柴烧热的炕，睡一个人是睡，睡十个人也是睡；一口大锅熬粥，柴米总比十口小锅省，还有轮班守夜、轮班煎药的照应。另一股往院门外放：弟弟在县城，能有一张自己的书桌；姐姐呢，到了南边，有自己的工钱，有自己的屋子，夜里出门不必向谁交代。连祖母都说过，她想要一个清静的早晨。灶里省下的是柴米和照应，门外多出来的是各人的活路；两股力气都在，谁也没压过谁。

Keeping the stove company, I saw that two hands were pulling at this family. One pulled toward the fire: a stack of firewood heats the sleeping platform whether one person lies on it or ten, and one big pot of porridge costs less in rice and fuel than ten small pots, to say nothing of the ease of taking turns at the night watch and the medicine. The other pulled toward the courtyard gate: my brother could have a desk of his own in the county town; as for my sister, once she was south she would have her own wage and a room of her own, going out at night without accounting to anyone. Even my grandmother once said she wanted a quiet morning to herself. What the stove saved was fuel and looking-after; what lay beyond the gate was a road for each of us; both hands held fast, and neither had yet beaten the other.

往后的年景替我把这账算活了。母亲走后那个荒年，雪封了山路，一垛柴贵过一斗米，南下的话再没有人提——灶里省下的，压过了门外任何一条活路，一家人围着火，攒得比哪年都紧。可一进丰年，院门自己就开了：没人吵，也没人做主。弟弟去县里帮人抄书，姐姐跟着马帮去赶集。我自己也去了邻村，教了半个月蒙学。还是这个家，还是那两股力气，荒年里灶赢，丰年里门赢，说到底是年景在拨算盘。

The years that followed worked the arithmetic out for me. In the lean winter after Mother died, snow closed the mountain road, a stack of firewood cost more than a bushel of rice, and no one spoke of going south again — what the stove saved outweighed any road beyond the gate, and the family drew tight around the fire, tighter than in any year I could remember. But the moment a fat year came, the courtyard gate opened by itself: no quarrel, no ruling from anyone. My brother copied letters for pay in the county town, and my sister went off with the mule caravans to the market fairs. I myself spent half a month teaching beginners in the next village. The same family, the same two hands upon it: in a lean year the stove won, in a fat year the gate won, and it was the season of the year that moved the beads.

父亲走后的头一个年关，我把两边的算珠拨来拨去，怎么拨，两边都一样沉：工契往南拽，药罐和柴米往灶边拽。一样沉的家就停在原地，既不散开，也不硬拢。我们议定了一个谁也不必让谁的法子：屋契不拆，灶也不分。人跟着年景走，冷月里全家围一口灶，暖月里各出各的门，院子半空，灶膛封灰。那两年，没有一封信回来要改一个字。

At the first New Year after Father died, I pushed the beads back and forth on the abacus, and however I pushed them, the two sides hung even: the contract pulling south, the medicine pot and the winter firewood pulling toward the stove. A family that hangs even does not move — it neither scatters nor forces itself closer. We settled on a way that asked no one to yield: the deed stayed whole and the stove stayed undivided. The people moved with the season — in the cold months the whole family crowded one stove, in the warm months each went out through a gate of their own, the courtyard half empty, the fire banked under ash. For two years after, not one letter came home asking to change a single word.

三十块束脩，是这个家最办不成的一件事：坡田一年刨出八块就顶了天，单看这一笔，书是念不成的。可姐姐南下这件事谁也拦不住，丰年里的门总要自己开。我们索性把两件事捆在一起办：她的工钱每月捎回三块，指名给弟弟交束脩。走不成的路搭上了走得成的路，走不成的也走成了。开春她按时进了纱厂，县学那边也如期收下了弟弟；县里米行请我去管账的那张帖子，我在枕下压了三夜，到底没有应。总得有人守着灶，好让门开的时候，屋里还有火。

The thirty dollars of tuition were the one thing this family could never bring off by itself: the sloped field scraped out eight dollars in a good year, and on that arithmetic alone the schooling could never happen. But nothing could hold my sister back from going south; in a fat year the gate opens by itself. So we tied the two errands into one: three dollars of her wage came home every month, named for my brother's tuition. A road no one could walk was roped to a road already moving, and it was walked after all. She reached the mill on time that spring, and the county school enrolled my brother on the appointed day; as for the rice shop's letter asking me to keep its books, I kept it under my pillow for three nights, and in the end I did not take the work. Someone had to stay by the stove, so that whenever a gate opened, there was still fire in the house.

后来丰年一年连着一年，人散得越来越远。弟弟留在县城教书；姐姐走得更远，嫁到了南边。祖母的药，换成我按月进城去抓，一次也没有断过。她七十岁那年冬天，我想把人聚齐一回，才算清了聚拢的价钱：信一封一封地写，陪嫁的银镯换成两张南下的车钱；进了腊月，年猪也提前宰了。散出去的人，这才循着灶膛的火光回来。我知道祖屋早晚要易主；可三十年过去，那口灶还端端正正立在原地，青砖被火燎得发亮。

Fat years came one after another, and the family scattered farther and farther. My brother stayed on to teach in the county town; my sister went farther still and married in the south. Grandmother's medicine became my monthly errand into town, and not once was it interrupted. The winter she turned seventy, I set out to bring everyone home again, and only then did I learn what gathering costs: letters written one after another, my dowry bracelet traded for two cart tickets going south; and once the twelfth month began, the New Year pig slaughtered early. Only then did the scattered ones come back, drawn by the firelight of the stove. I knew the old house would pass out of our hands sooner or later; yet thirty years on, that stove stands square where it always stood, its grey bricks burnished bright by fire.

注解：这对应吉布斯自由能（Gibbs free energy）：等温等压下，一件事会不会自然发生，取决于「合拢省下的」减去「散开多出的乘上年景」这笔净账（ΔG＝ΔH－TΔS）——净省下就自然进行，两边相抵就停在原地，净亏空就须有人从外面出力，或者与一件拦不住的事搭伙，让合起来的总账也变成净省下。只是自然会发生，不等于很快发生。

Note: This mirrors Gibbs free energy: at constant temperature and pressure, whether a change happens on its own is settled by the net account of what togetherness saves minus what scattering gains once the season weighs in (ΔG = ΔH − TΔS) — a net saving goes forward by itself, an even balance stands still, and a deficit must be paid for with work from outside, or bundled with something nothing can hold back until the combined account also turns to net saving. Only, what happens on its own does not always happen fast.
