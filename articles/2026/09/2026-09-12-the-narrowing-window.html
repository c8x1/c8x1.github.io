收窄的窗

AI Parable (inspired by Amanda Askell)·每日精选·2026年9月12日·2分钟阅读

老验布师与学徒面对一匹御用冠礼绸。国王降旨：须同时验明每一根丝的精确落位、断无一根缺损，且纹样的节律丝毫无差；若有一处错漏漏过去，织工人头落地。学徒将绸铺平，凑近了看。

The old cloth-inspector and his apprentice stood before a single bolt of ceremonial silk, destined for the king's coronation robe. The king's decree had come down: they were to certify, at one and the same time, that every thread lay in its exact place with not one broken, and that the rhythm of the pattern was without the slightest irregularity; should a single flaw slip past them, the weaver would lose his head. The apprentice spread the bolt flat and leaned in close.

学徒说：「我逐根看过去，寸寸不漏，定能把每一根丝的落位钉得清清楚楚。」他把目光收到一根丝的宽窄，丝的落位果然纤毫毕现。可等他要辨那纹样的节律，这窄窗里只得零星几根，节律全然无从辨认。

'I will go over it thread by thread,' the apprentice said, 'and miss nothing — I will pin down the exact place of every single thread.' He narrowed his gaze to the width of one thread, and each thread's place sprang up clean and sharp. But when he tried to read the rhythm of the pattern, the narrow window held only a stray few threads, and no interval could be made out at all.

他退后一步，展宽目光：「那我看整幅，节律就分明了。」纹样的重复间隔果然清楚，量得分毫不差。可再看单根丝的落位，已在整片视野里化开作一团雾——断没断、偏没偏，一概说不准了。

He stepped back and opened his view wide. 'Then I will take in the whole breadth, and the rhythm will be clear.' And the repeating interval of the pattern did stand out, plain and measured. But now the place of any one thread had melted into a general haze across the whole field — whether it was broken, whether it had shifted, could no longer be told.

师傅说：「你已碰着这匹绸的脾性了。丝的落位，与纹样的节律，是一对冤家：你要把一根丝钉死在一个点上，就得把眼睛收到那一根上，节律便散了；你要把节律量得分毫不差，就得放眼整幅去看，那根丝的落位便化开作一片雾。一者越清，另一者越浊，从无两全之法。」

The master said, 'You have touched the temper of this cloth. The place of a thread and the rhythm of the pattern are a pair of adversaries: to nail one thread to its point, you must shut your eye down to that one thread, and the rhythm scatters; to measure the rhythm true, you must open your eye to the whole breadth, and the thread's place dissolves into a wash. The sharper the one, the blurrier the other — there has never been a having of both.'

学徒问：「那换一付更好的镜、一双眼更利的呢？」师傅摇头：「换什么都无用。错不在镜，也不在你的眼；是这绸子本身的织法使然。落位住在窄处，节律住在宽处，二者天生相克。便是我师祖的镜、神的眼来，也破不过这层关系。」

'Then a better glass — a sharper eye?' the apprentice asked. The master shook his head. 'It is no use. The fault is not in the glass, nor in your eye; it is in the weave of the cloth itself. Place lives in the narrow, rhythm lives in the wide, and the two are born at odds. Bring my grandfather's master-glass, bring a god's own eye — you will not break past it.'

他把手按在那匹绸上：「你只能择一处下窗。窗越窄，落位越准而节律越不准；窗越宽，节律越准而落位越不准。二者不准之处的乘积，有一道底线，任你怎样择也跌不破。国王要两样都做到分毫不差——这旨，自落笔那刻起便注定做不到。你去回话：绸可验，这般两全的要求，天下无绸能应。」

He laid his hand on the bolt. 'You can only choose where to set your window. The narrower the window, the truer the place and the falser the rhythm; the wider, the truer the rhythm and the falser the place. And the product of the two uncertainties has a floor beneath it that no choosing will ever crack. The king demands both to be exact to a thread — that demand was impossible from the moment the brush left the decree. Go back and say so: the cloth can be inspected, but such a having-both, no cloth in the world can answer.'

注解：此处的概念为海森堡不确定性原理（the Heisenberg uncertainty principle）。位置与动量互为共轭变量，确定其一必以牺牲另一为代价，二者不确定度之积有不可突破的下限（σ_x·σ_p ≥ ℏ/2）。这是由波函数数学结构决定的内禀属性，并非测量过程对系统的扰动所致，亦无法通过改进仪器来消除。

Note: The concept here is the Heisenberg uncertainty principle. Position and momentum are conjugate variables: pinning down one demands a corresponding sacrifice of the other, and the product of their uncertainties has an irreducible lower bound (σ_x·σ_p ≥ ℏ/2). This is an intrinsic property fixed by the mathematical structure of the wave function — not a disturbance caused by the act of measurement, and not something any improvement in instruments can ever remove.
