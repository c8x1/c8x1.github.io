门口不住人

AI Parable (inspired by Amanda Askell)·每日精选·2026年9月21日·3分钟阅读

我在搪瓷厂的花纸车间描了六年花样：牡丹、双喜、奖字，描好了都送到喷花工段，落在脸盆和痰盂上。厂子早就半死不活，南边一家新开的彩印厂却来信指名要会描花的手，工钱翻倍，信末一行小字：四月三十为限。全车间都替我高兴，也只有替我高兴的份——南边好，这话厂里人人会说，可前几年里真过去的，一只手数得过来。要走，得先上三楼，进厂长室，把辞职信放在桌上，等他肯盖章、肯放档案。谁都知道该走；谁都没走成。

For six years I traced patterns in the decal room of an enamelware works: peonies, double happiness, the word for Prize, all sent on to the spray line to land on basins and spittoons. The works had been half-dead for years, when a new color-printing shop in the south wrote asking by name for a hand that could trace flowers, at double the pay, and at the bottom of the letter one small line: reply by the thirtieth of April. The whole room was glad for me, and only for me. The south was better; everyone in the works said so, yet in the years before, the ones who actually made it over could be counted on one hand. To go, you first went up to the third floor, into the director's office, laid your resignation on his desk, and waited for him to stamp it and release your file. Everyone knew we should go. Nobody managed it.

老宋是先去探路的。信递上去，厂长没骂也没拦，只把他的档案压进抽屉底，又往南边挂了个电话。半年后，南边的信等黄了，老宋还坐在原来那张桌子前，描的牡丹比从前更小。打那以后，三楼那条走廊静了整整半年，一个递信的都没有。车间里的账谁都会算：多一个电话，多压一个月，就够叫人把信重新收回抽屉；南边的工钱再高，也得人先出得了这扇门。那半年里，走出去的只有陈苹：没人陪，她自己进的那扇门。

Old Song went ahead to test the way. He handed in his letter, and the director neither raged nor refused; he only pressed the file to the bottom of a drawer and put a call through to the south. Half a year later the southern letter had died of waiting, and Old Song sat on at his old table, painting his peonies smaller than before. After that the third-floor corridor stayed silent a full half year; not one letter was handed up. Everyone in the workshop could do that arithmetic: one more phone call, one more month under the drawer, was enough to make a man fold his letter away again; the southern wages could be as high as they liked, a man still had to get out the door before he could earn them. In all that silence only one person left, Chen Ping. Nobody walked her; she went in through that door alone.

那扇门里的十五分钟，我是后来才慢慢懂的。人站在门口，一只脚已经不在这儿，另一只脚还没到南边。这栋楼两头不通气，能过人的地方只有那扇门；而那扇门，又是整条路上最难的一步。谁也没法在里面多待：话出了口，往后的路就不用再使劲，人自己会到那一边；话咽回去，人就退回原来的桌前。门里没有第三种人——门口这种地方，本来就住不了人。我问过走过去的人，那一刻本身是什么样子，谁都答不上来；能开口讲的时候，人已经站定了某一边。老赵是从同一扇门回来的，回来那一趟，他攒的勇气比走的时候还多。

Fifteen minutes inside that door; it took me years to understand them. A person stands at the doorway with one foot already out of this place and the other not yet in the south. The building has no second opening between its two lives; whoever passes must pass through that door, and that door is the hardest step of the whole road. No one can linger in it: once the words are out, the rest of the way takes no strength, you simply arrive on the far side; swallow them, and you are back at your old desk. There is no third kind of person in that room; a doorway, by its nature, is not a place anyone can live. I asked the ones who had crossed what the moment itself was like, and not one could tell me; by the time you can speak of it, you are already standing on one side or the other. Old Zhao came back through that same door, and for that return he had to gather more nerve than for leaving.

陈苹就是那半年里走的。如今她每月回来看她母亲，回来之前，总有人先写了信给她。她送人只送到三楼走廊口，自己在尽头那扇窗下站定，兜里揣一把瓜子；她不进去，也不替谁讲一句话。她在那儿一站，厂长室就小了一圈：信照递，章照盖，南边的工钱一分没多，厂里的薪水一分没少。经她送出去的人有几十个了，人还是那个人，笑还是那个笑。老赵进去那天，也是她陪着走的那条走廊。门朝哪边开，她从来不问；她只管把门放矮。

Chen Ping was the one who had left in the silence. Now she comes back every month to see her mother, and before each visit someone has usually written ahead to her. She walks you only as far as the head of the third-floor corridor, then takes her stand at the window in its far end, a bag of melon seeds in her pocket; she never goes in, and never speaks a word on anyone's behalf. With her standing there, the director's office shrinks a size: the letter is handed over all the same, the stamp comes down all the same, the southern pay not one fen higher, the works' pay not one fen lower. She has walked dozens of people through, and she is unchanged, the same person, the same laugh. On the day Old Zhao went back in, it was she who walked him down that corridor. Which way the door opens never matters to her; she only makes it lower.

四月里，厂里贴出了减薪的告示，整栋楼一下子烧了起来。平常的月份，一个月走掉一个就算快了；那一个礼拜，车间里空了九张桌子，抽屉里睡了一年的信，几天里都递了上去。我的限期也一天天压了过来。我给陈苹写了信。她礼拜二到的，在厂门口等我，陪我上了三楼，到走廊口站住，往尽头那扇窗下一靠。我进去了。

In April the works posted the wage-cut notice, and the whole building caught fire. In an ordinary month, one person getting out was counted fast; in that single week nine tables stood empty in the workshops, and letters that had slept a year in drawers were handed up within days. My own deadline pressed closer every day. I wrote to Chen Ping. She came on a Tuesday, waited for me at the factory gate, climbed the three floors with me, stopped at the head of the corridor, and settled herself by the far window. I went in.

那间屋子我记住的，只有玻璃板上的茶垢，和章落下去的那点红。四月二十七号，我的档案出了厂门，比限期早三天。厂长确实往南边挂了电话，南边回信只有一行，说电话里讲什么不要紧，他们要的是描花的手。下楼的时候，陈苹已经在面馆坐着了，桌上两碗面，碗边一小堆瓜子壳。老宋后来进了第二回门，那一回也是陈苹送的，成了。至于小何，我到今天也讲不清：他没攒过什么勇气，也没人见他从那扇门里过，可有一年，他人就在南边了，像从门板底下渗过去的。上个月有人捎信来，说陈苹还站在那扇窗下，窗前换了个攥着信的年轻人。

Of that room I remember only the tea stains on the glass desk pad and the small red of the stamp coming down. On the twenty-seventh of April my file left the factory gate, three days ahead of the deadline. The director did make his call to the south, and the reply was one line: whatever he says on the phone does not matter, we want the hand that traces flowers. When I came downstairs, Chen Ping was already seated at the noodle shop, two bowls on the table, a little heap of shells beside her bowl. Old Song went in a second time, and that time she walked him; it took. As for Little He, I cannot explain him to this day: he never gathered any nerve, and nobody ever saw him pass through that door, yet one year he was simply in the south, as if he had seeped under the door panel. Last month someone brought word that Chen Ping still stands at that window, and that before her now stands another young man with a letter crushed in his hand.

注解：那扇厂门说的是过渡态理论（transition state theory）：要走成一场转变，必得经过一处既非起点也非终点、又留不住的最高一刻，这里每高一分，过得去的人就少一大截；把门放矮的人自己毫发不减，也不改两头各是什么，只让人早些过去，朝哪边走都一样。只是门的高矮管得了快慢，管不了该不该；至于没攒勇气也从门板底下渗过去的，是这套道理照不见的角落。

Note: The factory door stands for transition state theory: to bring a change about, you must pass a highest moment that belongs to neither the starting side nor the ending side and cannot be held, and each added bit of height there leaves far fewer people getting through; whoever lowers the door loses nothing of herself, changes nothing about what either side pays, and only shortens the wait, in whichever direction people go. The honest edge is that a door's height governs how fast, never which way is right, and the rare one who seeps under it with no nerve at all is past what this theory can see.
