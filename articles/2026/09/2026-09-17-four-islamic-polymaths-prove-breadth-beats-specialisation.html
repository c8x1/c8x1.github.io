四位伊斯兰通才证明：广博胜过专精

Mariam Sabri·Aeon·2026年9月17日·11分钟阅读·原文

本学期，我的学生们正在学习四位塑造了伊斯兰科学的通才：比鲁尼（al-Biruni）、海什木（Alhazen）、阿维森纳（Avicenna）和花剌子米（al-Khwarizmi）。他们的境遇几乎截然不同：一位以笔墨摆脱了匮乏；一位被软禁了十年；一位将一个时代的医学知识凝练成一部统治欧洲大学五百年的教科书；还有一位则在那个时代最大的研究机构里工作。但他们都共有同一种深层本能：在数学、医学、天文学、哲学与诗歌之间自由穿行，用一个学科的假设去检验另一个学科。我的教室在巴基斯坦，学生们入学时热衷于谈论颠覆与就业，却很少谈广博。我希望他们透过这四段人生看到：这种开阔并非太平盛世的奢侈品；它催生了科学史上一些最经久不衰的突破。

This semester, my students are learning about four polymaths who shaped Islamic science: al-Biruni, Alhazen, Avicenna and al-Khwarizmi. Their circumstances could hardly have been more different: one wrote his way out of scarcity; one was held under house arrest for a decade; one distilled an era’s medical knowledge into a text that ruled European universities for 500 years; and the last worked at the largest research institution of his age. But each shared the same underlying instinct: to move freely between mathematics, medicine, astronomy, philosophy and poetry, testing one discipline’s assumptions against another’s. My classroom is in Pakistan, where students arrive eager to talk about disruption and employability, but rarely about breadth. I want them to see, through these four lives, that this kind of expansiveness isn’t a luxury for stable times; it has produced some of the most enduring breakthroughs in the history of science.

在博雅教育领域工作了十五年之后，我相信以希腊化风格重新整合各学科可以开创新路。我并不是在为「跨学科」做一场肤浅的辩护。我要主张的是更具体的东西：复兴曾经繁盛于穆斯林世界的通才气质。与其通过强制性的创业课程和应用方法课来痴迷于颠覆式创新，我们应当提供一种全然不同的模式——关于如何学习、以及学习是为了什么——尤其是因为我的学生们踏入大学校门时，早已带着一种狭隘的就业导向心态。

After 15 years working in liberal arts education, I believe a reintegration of disciplines in a Hellenistic style can lead the way. I’m not making a superficial case for ‘interdisciplinarity’. Instead, I’m arguing for something more specific: the recovery of the penchants of polymathy that once thrived in the Muslim world. Rather than fetishising disruptive innovation through compulsory courses on entrepreneurship and applied methods, we should offer an entirely different model of how to learn and what learning is for – especially since my students already enter university with a narrowly vocational mindset.

「通才之道」的英文 polymathy，其构词本身——poly（众多）加 mathy（学问）——便道出了多个知识世界的百衲拼装。在一个被战争、气候灾难与不公撕裂的世界里，通才之道提供了喘息与反思。它源自穆斯林世界本土的思想语汇，而非全球北方的强加。今天，通才之道让不同类型的思维汇聚于政治、经济与环境问题之上。在学生们读经典越来越少、为就业机会担忧越来越多的当下，通才的开阔恰能提供一条有意义的前行之路。这些正是伟大伊斯兰通才们留下的启示。

The word polymathy itself captures the bricolage of many (poly) worlds of knowledge (mathy). Polymathy offers respite and reflection in a world fractured by war, climate catastrophe and injustice. It is sourced from indigenous idioms of the Muslim world rather than an imposition by the Global North. Today, polymathy brings different kinds of thinking to bear on political, economic and environmental problems. At a time when students are reading less of the classic canon and worrying more about employment opportunities, the expansiveness of polymathy can offer a meaningful path forward. These are the lessons of the great Islamic polymaths.

我们故事的第一位主人公比鲁尼的一生，能让学生领悟科学的人性维度。他约于 973 年出生在中亚城市花剌子模（Khwarizm），成长中机会寥寥。但这并未束缚他：比鲁尼主动求学。依照伊斯兰通才教育的经典传统，他学习了伊斯兰教法、语法学、神学、天文学与数学诸门科学，以及历史学。纵观一生，匮乏从未成为比鲁尼的阻碍。事实上，匮乏自有其法，能让人珍视手边可得的一切知识——这是任何博雅学子的关键一课。

The life of our opening actor al-Biruni has much to teach students about the human dimensions of science. Born in the Central Asian city of Khwarizm around 973, he grew up with few opportunities. But that didn’t constrain him: al-Biruni sought an education. In the classic tradition of Islamic polymathy, he studied Islamic law, grammar, theology, the sciences of astronomy and mathematics, as well as history. Throughout his life, lack was not an impediment for al-Biruni. In fact, scarcity has a way of making one appreciate whatever knowledge is at their disposal – a key lesson for any liberal artist.

这种随机应变的本领，推动比鲁尼在自己精通的领域做出了关键贡献。职业生涯早期，他很难接触到科学仪器。但他没有气馁，而是凭着几本旧手册和有限的资源，自学制造仪器。比鲁尼的巧思还让他敢于挑战已确立地位的科学权威。例如，天文学家胡詹迪曾建造了一台巨型六分仪，用来测量地轴相对于运行轨道的倾角（即黄赤交角）。但胡詹迪得出的数值，在比鲁尼看来偏低。凭借自制六分仪的经验，比鲁尼找到了问题所在：胡詹迪的六分仪发生了下弯，导致结果失真。正是那份迫使比鲁尼自制六分仪的匮乏，让他得以看出资源远比他雄厚的人错在哪里。比鲁尼自己的测量结果，记录在《为精确测算各地距离而确定地点坐标》一书中，得到了同时代人及后世科学家的广泛认可。

This resourcefulness fuelled al-Biruni to make key contributions to his fields of expertise. Early in his career, he had limited access to scientific instruments. Undeterred, he taught himself to build them from a handful of old manuals and limited resources. Al-Biruni’s ingenuity also allowed him to take on established scientific leaders. The astronomer al-Khujandi, for instance, had built a massive sextant that he used to measure the tilt of Earth’s axis relative to its orbit (its obliquity). The number he yielded, though, struck al-Biruni as too low. Because of his experience in building his own sextants, he was able to identify the issue: al-Khujandi’s sextant sagged, distorting his results. The scarcity that had compelled al-Biruni to make his own sextants made it possible to see where others with far more resources had gone wrong. Al-Biruni’s own measurements, documented in The Determination of the Coordinates of Locations for Accurately Measuring the Distances between Places, were widely accepted by his contemporaries and later scientists.

比鲁尼的晚年生涯与著作深受伽色尼王朝（Ghaznavid empire）苏丹马哈茂德（Mahmud of Ghazni）的塑造，后者于 998 年至 1030 年在位。马哈茂德是一位杰出的军事领袖，据传一生未尝败绩，大幅拓展了他那根基不稳的帝国。在他征服的土地中，就有比鲁尼的故乡花剌子模；比鲁尼本人不久便被迫入马哈茂德的宫廷效力。在马哈茂德麾下，他游历了中亚、印度和阿拉伯诸地。这些岁月对他日后的学术贡献以及他选择研究的领域，起到了至关重要的作用。

Al-Biruni’s later life and works were shaped by Mahmud Ghazni, sultan of the Ghaznavid empire from 998 to 1030. Mahmud was a terrific military leader, reputed never to have lost a battle, who dramatically extended his unstable empire. Among the lands he conquered was al-Biruni’s native Khwarizm; al-Biruni himself was soon compelled to serve in Mahmud’s court. Under Mahmud, he travelled across Central Asia, India and the Arab lands. These years played a crucial role in shaping his intellectual contributions and the fields he chose to study.

颇具讽刺意味的是，比鲁尼的奴役处境反而给了他穿越山地与海岸的机会，也让他得以学习印度、波斯和阿拉伯的天文学传统。他测量地球半径的开创性方法，正源于其中一次旅程：在旁遮普（Punjab）的南达纳堡（Nandana Fort），他登上一座小山，测出地平线的俯角，进而计算出地球半径，精确度令人惊叹（按现代单位换算，他得出的数值为 3,928.77 英里）。

Al-Biruni’s servitude ironically gave him the opportunity to traverse mountainous and coastal terrains, as well as to learn from the astronomical traditions of India, Persia and Arabia. His groundbreaking method for measuring Earth’s radius grew out of one of these journeys: at Nandana Fort in Punjab, he climbed a hill, measured the angle of the dip of the horizon, and calculated Earth’s radius to within a remarkable degree of accuracy (modern conversions put his figure at 3,928.77 English miles).

当比鲁尼的同时代人大多信奉地心说时，他却认真琢磨过日心说，尽管并未正式将这一理论融入自己的整个思想体系（这比哥白尼早了约五百年）。而比鲁尼在海滨地区和阿拉伯沙漠的游历，则帮助他理解了峡谷的形成与沙子的成分。「随着时间的流逝，」他写道，「海洋会变成陆地，陆地也会变成海洋。」倘若比鲁尼从未离开中亚、从未被迫踏上横跨南亚与西亚的旅程，他绝不可能做出这一关键观察。比鲁尼的一生告诉今天的学生：匮乏与短缺未必是阻碍；它们可以成为创新的资源。

While most of al-Biruni’s fellow astronomers subscribed to geocentricism, he actually toyed with heliocentrism, though without formally integrating the theory into the rest of his thought (some 500 years before Copernicus). And al-Biruni’s travels to coastal areas and the Arabian deserts helped him understand the formation of canyons and composition of sand. ‘With the passing of time,’ he observed, ‘the sea becomes dry land, and dry land the sea.’ Had al-Biruni never left Central Asia and been compelled to undertake his travels across South and West Asia, he would never have made this critical observation. Al-Biruni’s life teaches students today that lack and scarcity need not be impediments; they can be resources for innovation.

创造力既可以来自未曾想象的种种可能，也可以出自始料未及的种种限制。我们接下来的这位通才海什木，其生平与著作正是创造力在约束之下——尤其是在囚禁之中——如何迸发的明证。贫困与囚禁虽都能激发创造力，但这两种约束在性质上截然不同：一个关乎匮乏，一个关乎禁锢。匮乏并不剥夺旅行的自由，而囚禁——不幸的是，对当今在压迫性政府治下求学的学生而言，这已是司空见惯的遭遇——却将人与充满无限可能的外部世界彻底隔绝。正是这些境遇，将比鲁尼与海什木区分开来。

Creativity can come as much from unimagined possibilities as from unforeseen limitations. The life and work of our next polymath, Alhazen, is a testament to how creativity emerges under constraints, particularly in captivity. While both poverty and captivity prompt creativity, these constraints are qualitatively different. One relates to scarcity and the other to immobility. Scarcity does not foreclose travel, while captivity – an unfortunately common experience for students working under oppressive governments today – disconnects one entirely from the outside world that offers a plethora of possibilities. These circumstances distinguished al-Biruni and Alhazen.

海什木（又名伊本·海赛姆，Ibn al-Haytham）是光学大家，约于 965 年出生在今伊拉克的巴士拉（Basra）。那里学术氛围浓厚，科学与文学成果斐然。阿拉伯语法学正是在此奠基；贾希兹在此探究从动物学到神学的一切学问；「精诚兄弟会」——一个由科学家与哲学家组成的秘密结社——在此编纂了一部浩瀚的百科全书，将数学、音乐与形而上学熔于一炉。正是这种国际化的书斋文化，塑造了海什木。

Alhazen (also known as Ibn al-Haytham) was a major scholar of optics, born around 965 in Basra, in modern-day Iraq, where there was a thriving intellectual climate that led to significant scientific and literary production. It was where the study of Arabic grammar was founded; where al-Jahiz explored everything from zoology to theology; where the Ikhwan al-Safa – a clandestine brotherhood of scientist-philosophers – compiled a massive encyclopaedia that wove together mathematics, music and metaphysics. It was this cosmopolitan and bookish culture that shaped Alhazen.

早年，他便以革命性的工程壮举闻名。而当他大胆提出在尼罗河上筑坝以调节水流时，更是超越了自己以往的所有成就。自开罗发号施令的法蒂玛王朝（Fatimid）哈里发哈基姆听闻了海什木这一非凡的提议。史称「疯子哈里发」的哈基姆，是一位极度古怪且睚眦必报的统治者，他迫害宗教少数派，还曾下令杀死开罗全城的狗（显然是被狗叫声烦扰了）。海什木抵达埃及后，沮丧地发现尼罗河绝非轻易可以驯服；他设想的大坝完全不可行。出于情理之中的恐惧——生怕失败会触怒疯子哈里发——海什木索性自称疯了。这一计谋奏效了：他躲过了处决，转而被软禁在家，直到哈基姆去世。

In his early days, he became famous for his revolutionary feats in engineering. But he outdid himself when he boldly proposed the construction of a dam on the River Nile to regulate its flow. The Fatimid caliph al-Hakim, ruling from Cairo, got wind of Alhazen’s extraordinary proposal. Known to history as the ‘Mad Caliph’, al-Hakim was a deeply eccentric and vindictive ruler, who persecuted religious minorities and once ordered that all the dogs in Cairo be killed (he was evidently annoyed by their barking). When Alhazen arrived in Egypt, he found to his dismay that the Nile could not be so easily tamed; his dam was entirely unfeasible. Reasonably fearing that his failure would upset the Mad Caliph, Alhazen himself claimed insanity. The ruse worked: he escaped execution and instead remained under house arrest until al-Hakim’s death.

海什木是最早提出视觉源于光线进入眼睛、而非眼睛发出光线的人之一

Alhazen was among the first to argue that vision results from light entering the eye, not rays emitted by it

海什木被软禁了十年。困居家中，唯有兴趣相伴，他在题为《光学书》的著作中发展出开创性的视觉理论。受制于软禁的物理局限，他把大量时间用于制造、摆弄和试验各种仪器，以帮助自己理解光的习性。渊博的学识是他囚禁期间唯一的资源。作为通才，海什木将几何学、天文学、光学与工程学的知识融会贯通。他建立数学方程来解释光的运作，研究诸多天文现象（尤其是恒星）以阐明亮度理论，并运用暗箱的工程学原理来模拟眼睛的功能。

Alhazen remained under house arrest for a decade. Bound to his abode with nothing but his interests, he developed groundbreaking theories of vision in a book entitled Kitab al-Manazir (‘Book of Optics’). Given the physical limitations of his house arrest, he spent a lot of time building, tinkering and experimenting with instruments that helped him understand the behaviour of light. His vast knowledge was his sole resource during the imprisonment. As a polymath, Alhazen combined his knowledge of geometry, astronomy, optics and engineering. He developed mathematical equations to explain how light worked, studied many astronomical phenomena, especially stars, to explain theories of brightness, and used engineering principles of the camera obscura to mimic the function of the eye.

他影响最深远的贡献至今仍是光学的基石。海什木是最早主张视觉源于光线进入眼睛、而非眼睛发出光线的人之一。这便是他影响深远的「入射说」。他的观点使他与众多人杰针锋相对——托勒密、欧几里得和盖伦，他们所持的光学「发射说」认为，眼睛向所见之物投射出光线。海什木则反驳道，这与天文学无法自洽：倘若眼睛必须先发出光线我们才能看见东西，那就无法解释为什么我们一睁开眼睛便能立即看到遥远的星辰。

His most influential contribution remains foundational to optics. Alhazen was among the first to argue that vision results from light entering the eye, not rays emitted by it. This was his influential theory of intromission. His views put him at odds with such greats as Ptolemy, Euclid and Galen, whose emission theory of optics held that the eye projected rays onto the objects it perceived. But Alhazen countered that this couldn’t be squared with astronomy: if the eye had to send out rays before we could see anything, there’d be no way to explain how we see distant stars the instant we open our eyes.

入射说的提出，还展现了海什木另一项关键能力：批判性思维。他的著作证明了一个事实：穆斯林通才并非仅仅消费、翻译或复制希腊知识，而是批判与创新。倘若海什木没有质疑希腊科学，没有在囚禁的境况之下，将光学、天文学与数学的知识与暗箱的实证演示相结合，他的入射说便不会诞生。

His development of the intromission theory revealed another crucial ability: his critical thinking. His works are a testament to the fact that Muslim polymaths did not just consume, translate or replicate Greek knowledge. Rather, they critiqued and innovated. Had Alhazen not critiqued Greek science, combining his knowledge of optics, astronomy and mathematics with practical demonstrations of the camera obscura – all under conditions of captivity – his intromission theory would not have emerged.

多数人认为入射说是海什木学术成就的巅峰，但实际上，他的通才之道还在光学与心理学之间建立了全新的联结。正如神经科学家查尔斯·格罗斯所言，海什木是「认识到眼动对知觉至关重要之第一人」，而眼动正是构成我们对视觉世界之意识的基础。海什木意识到，眼睛接收光线只是知觉过程中许多关键环节的第一步。他的光学理论解释了光如何物理性地抵达眼睛，但他坚持这仅仅是第一步——要把这种原始的感觉转化为真正有意识的知觉，还需要无意识推理、记忆与比较。这是一个惊人现代的见解，西方直到 19 世纪才将其阐明——倘若海什木狭隘地固守单一领域，他绝不会做出这一发现。

While most believe the theories of intromission were the pinnacle of Alhazen’s intellectual achievements, but in fact his polymathy also created novel connections between optics and psychology. As the neuroscientist Charles G Gross has argued, Alhazen was ‘the first to recognise the crucial importance of eye movement for perception’, thus constituting our consciousness of the visual world. Alhazen realised that the reception of light by the eye was the first of many crucial steps in perception. His optics explained how light physically reaches the eye, but insisted that it was only the first step – turning that raw sensation into an actual conscious percept required unconscious inference, memory and comparison. It is a startlingly modern view, which wouldn’t be articulated in the West until the 19th century – and had Alhazen been narrowly wedded to a single field he would never have made this discovery.

无论学生立志成为学者还是创业者，他们都需要去追寻各种不预设结果的多元体验。海什木在约束之下将创造力与批判性思维相结合，成就了他至今仍为人铭记的不凡功绩。

Whether students endeavour to be scholars or entrepreneurs, they need to seek out eclectic experiences without a predetermined outcome. Alhazen’s combination of creativity and critical thinking under constraints led to the extraordinary achievements for which he is still remembered.

伊斯兰世界最负盛名的通才，或许当属阿维森纳，他的学术活动期自 997 年至 1037 年。这位出身中亚布哈拉（Bukhara）的医师与哲学家广为人知，自幼便是神童，少时便已精通艰深的典籍与概念。虽然如今人们常援引的是他在医学上的贡献，但他最主要的成就，其实在于推动了科学方法的发展。与海什木颇为相似，阿维森纳在观察与实验之间、归纳与演绎之间自如切换，由此带来了医学与哲学上的突破，以及心理学和地质学上鲜为人知的贡献。

Perhaps the most prominent polymath from the Islamic world was Avicenna, who was intellectually active from 997 to 1037. A widely known physician and philosopher hailing from Bukhara in Central Asia, he was a child prodigy, demonstrating mastery over complex texts and concepts at a young age. While his contributions to medicine are often cited today, his contributions to the development of the scientific method were his primary achievements. Much like Alhazen, Avicenna moved fluidly from observation to experimentation, from induction to deduction, leading to his breakthroughs in medicine and philosophy as well as lesser-known contributions to psychology and geology.

阿维森纳的巨著《医典》试图将他那个时代的全部医学知识——希腊、波斯与印度的——压缩为一部系统性的参考书。该书完成于约 1025 年，共分五卷：生理学与疾病的一般原理；按字母顺序编排、收录约八百种药物的目录；按身体部位从头到脚排列的疾病；累及全身的病症，如发热；以及收录约六百五十种复方制剂的方书。但《医典》真正的独到之处在于其方法。阿维森纳制定了检验新药效果的规则，实际上在「临床试验」一词出现之前数百年就阐明了这一概念。《医典》于 12 世纪被译成拉丁文，此后逾五百年间一直是欧洲大学的标准医学教科书。不过，阿维森纳的天才不仅在于这一成就的实质内容，还在于我们今天所谓的「科学传播」：他既写清晰的散文，也写动人的诗。

Avicenna’s magnum opus, Al-Qanun fi al-Tibb (‘The Canon of Medicine’), was an attempt to compress the entire medical knowledge of his age – Greek, Persian and Indian – into a single, systematic reference. Completed around 1025, it unfolds across five books: general principles of physiology and disease; an alphabetical catalogue of some 800 medicinal substances; diseases organised by body part, from head to foot; conditions affecting the whole body, such as fevers; and a formulary of roughly 650 compound remedies. But what distinguishes the Canon is its method. Avicenna lays out the rules for testing a new drug’s effects, effectively articulating the idea of a clinical trial centuries before the term existed. Translated into Latin in the 12th century, the Canon became the standard medical textbook in European universities for more than half a millennium. Avicenna’s genius, though, lay not just in the substance of this achievement but in what we would now call science communication: he wrote lucid prose and evocative poetry.

当阿维森纳被逻辑难题困住时，他便去清真寺祈祷，直到心中的迷雾散去

When Avicenna was stuck on a logic problem, he headed to the mosque and prayed until his mental fog lifted

阿维森纳的贡献远不止于医学发现与创新。他对科学方法本身的贡献是开创性的。当他被逻辑难题困住时，他会把神秘主义当作一种方法：前往清真寺祈祷，直到心中的迷雾散去、难题豁然开朗。这在许多方面预示了冥想在当今帮助人们获得心智清明方面所扮演的角色。科学家们如今发现，换个环境、换种节奏，对研究者而言有奇效。与流行看法相反，正念并非解决古老问题的新方案。它是从诸多灵性传统中回收而来的古老建议，只是包上了一层易于接受的世俗外衣。透过从佛陀到阿维森纳的历史用法来看正念，能让这一实践获得超越硅谷式「颠覆」崇拜的可信度。它是一种与创新相连的古老修行。

Avicenna’s contributions far outweighed medical discoveries and innovations. His contribution to the scientific method itself was groundbreaking. When he was stuck on a logic problem, he used mysticism as a method: he headed to the mosque and prayed until his mental fog lifted and he solved the puzzle. In many ways, this anticipates the role that meditation plays today in achieving mental clarity. Scientists are now finding that a change of scenery and change of pace can work wonders for researchers. Contrary to popular belief, mindfulness is not a new solution to an old problem. It is a recycled recommendation from many spiritual traditions, packaged in a palatable secularism. Seeing mindfulness through its historic uses from Buddha to Avicenna gives the practice credibility beyond its fetishisation as a disruptive approach by Silicon Valley. It is an age-old practice linked to innovation.

每当我在科学史课上布置阿维森纳的自传时，学生们总会被一件极简单的事情震撼：宗教与科学的交织。二者并非素来势不两立，尽管如今的学生们被引导着以非历史的眼光如此相信。阿维森纳本人以祈祷攻克逻辑难题的习惯便是一例：对他而言，在理性停滞时求助于清真寺，并不是从严谨思考中退却，而是严谨思考的延伸，因为祈祷与逻辑探究指向同一个目标——把握一个艰深的真理。学生们一次次瞠目于历史上那些有信仰而非世俗化的科学家，且并不对他们的信仰体系妄加价值评判——这样的次数我已数不清了。其中也包括较近时代的科学家，比如诺贝尔物理学奖得主阿卜杜勒·萨拉姆，一位公开认同穆斯林身份的巴基斯坦人，他毫不认为自己的信仰与科学研究之间存在矛盾。

Whenever I assign Avicenna’s autobiography in my history of science class, my students are struck by something very simple: the intertwinement of religion and science. The two have not always been at loggerheads, as students are now ahistorically encouraged to believe. Avicenna’s own habit of praying his way through a difficult logic problem is a case in point: for him, turning to the mosque when reason stalled wasn’t a retreat from rigorous thought but an extension of it, since prayer and logical enquiry were both aimed at the same goal of grasping a difficult truth. I have lost count of the number of times students gawk at examples of scientists from history who were spiritual, not secular, without ascribing a value judgment to their belief systems. These also include relatively recent scientists such as the Nobel Laureate in Physics Abdus Salam, a Pakistani who openly identified as Muslim and saw no contradiction between his faith and his research.

此外，以丰美的语言阐释科学概念的，并非只有阿维森纳一人。修辞学是希腊与伊斯兰传统中主要的博雅学科之一，大多数学者都精于此道。这一传统延续到了 20 世纪的科学家身上，比如阿卜杜勒·萨拉姆，他终生热爱乌尔都语、波斯语、旁遮普语和英语诗歌；听过他粒子物理讲座的同事们说，他的雄辩让这门学科听起来宛如文学。对阿维森纳和萨拉姆而言，生动的语言并非事后附加在科学上的装点；它是思想得以想通的方式，也是让人记住这些思想的方式。在今天的博雅课堂上，向学生强调有效而动人的写作能力，是他们成功的关键。写作不是一项可以被 AI 取代的孤立技能。对于科学与创业领域的未来领袖，它对交流与认知都至关重要。阿维森纳的一生与遗产，见证了方法上的兼收并蓄与引人入胜的科学传播——而这两者，正因当今的专门化与狭隘的职业教育而日渐式微。

Furthermore, Avicenna was not alone in using lush language to explain scientific concepts. Rhetoric was one of the main liberal arts in the Greek and Islamic traditions, and most scholars were skilled in it. That tradition persisted to 20th-century scientists such as Abdus Salam, who carried a lifelong love of poetry in Urdu, Persian, Punjabi and English, and colleagues who heard him lecture on particle physics remarked that his eloquence made the subject sound like literature. For both Avicenna and Salam, vivid language wasn’t decoration bolted on to the science after the fact; it was part of how the ideas were thought through, and how they were made to stick. Emphasising effective and evocative writing skills to students in the liberal arts classroom today is key to their success. Writing is not an isolated skill that can be replaced by AI. It is fundamental to both communication and cognition for future leaders in science and entrepreneurship. Avicenna’s life and legacy attest to both methodological eclecticism and compelling science communication in ways that are waning today due to specialisation and narrow vocational education.

我们最后一位通才花剌子米，对穆斯林世界乃至更广范围的博雅课程而言都弥足珍贵。作为代数之父，其名更化作了「算法」一词的词源，他的工作表明：理论源于与实践问题的搏斗，而抽象能够解决现实的挑战。花剌子米约于 780 年出生在中亚，后来成长为伊斯兰科学的领军人物。他是巴格达「智慧宫」的关键学者——那是当时穆斯林世界最大的学术研究机构——他在数学、天文学和地理学上的渊博知识，正切合阿拔斯帝国在治理与行政上面临的实际问题。

Our final polymath, al-Khwarizmi, is invaluable to a liberal arts curriculum in the Muslim world and beyond. As the father of algebra and namesake of the word ‘algorithm’, his work shows that theory emerges from wrestling with practical problems and that abstraction can solve pragmatic challenges. Born in Central Asia around 780, al-Khwarizmi rose to prominence as a leader of Islamic science. He was a key scholar at the Bayt al-Hikmah (House of Wisdom) in Baghdad – then the largest institution of intellectual enquiry in the Muslim world – with a vast knowledge of mathematics, astronomy and geography that was relevant to the practical problems of governance and administration faced by the Abbasid empire.

他把自己的代数学论著《还原与对消计算概要》描述为一部实用的手册，一项「关于代数的著作，其内容仅限于代数计算中精妙而重要的部分，即人们在继承、遗赠、分产、诉讼和交易中经常需要用到的，也是在涉及测量、开凿运河、几何计算及其他各种各样事务的相互往来中所需要的」。在阿拔斯王朝之下，这些都是帝国成功治理所不可或缺的应用。尽管代数自有其抽象的魅力，但它从来就不是一门脱离现实需要的纯粹理论传统。

He described his treatise on algebra, the Kitab al-Mukhtasar fi Hisab al-Jabr wa al-Muqabala (‘The Concise Book of Calculation by Restoration and Balancing’), as a practical handbook, a ‘work on algebra, confining it to the fine and important parts of its calculations, such as people constantly require in cases of inheritance, legacies, partition, law-suits, and trade, and in all their dealings with one another where surveying, the digging of canals, geometrical computation, and other objects of various sorts and kinds are concerned.’ These were all imperative applications for successful imperial administration under the Abbasids. Despite the abstract attraction of algebra, it was never meant to be a purely theoretical tradition divorced from real-world needs.

花剌子米的代数学脱胎于实际问题——继承纠纷、土地测量、开凿运河

Al-Khwarizmi’s algebra grew out of practical problems – inheritance disputes, land surveys, canal digging

花剌子米最为后人铭记的是创立了代数学，但他的抱负还延伸到了天文学和地理学。为献给 813 年至 833 年在位的哈里发马蒙，他编撰了《信德欣德历表》，这部天文表描绘了太阳、月亮及已知五颗行星的运行，改编自早先从印度传入巴格达的材料。表中还包含一天中不同时刻影子长度的计算，这对确定礼拜时间和校准日晷至关重要。后来，在《大地形象书》中，他转向地理学，以 2,402 处地点的坐标修正并扩充了托勒密流传数百年的世界地图。纵观数学、天文学与地理学，贯穿花剌子米工作的主线是精确：坚持测量、制表与订正，而非简单承袭既有的知识。

Al-Khwarizmi is best remembered for founding algebra, but his ambitions extended into astronomy and geography as well. In dedication to Caliph al-Ma’mun, who reigned in 813-33, he composed his own Zij al-Sindhind, astronomical tables charting the movements of the Sun, the Moon and five known planets, adapting earlier material brought to Baghdad from India. The tables included calculations for the length of shadows cast at different times of day, essential for setting prayer times and calibrating sundials. Later, in the Kitab Surat al-Ard (‘Book of the Description of the Earth’), he turned to geography, correcting and expanding Ptolemy’s centuries-old map of the world with coordinates for 2,402 locations. Across mathematics, astronomy and geography alike, the through-line in al-Khwarizmi’s work is precision: an insistence on measuring, tabulating and correcting, rather than simply inheriting received knowledge.

在课堂上，理论本身往往才是我看到的真正障碍，尤其是对那些来自地理条件不利背景的学生而言——他们入学时应用知识娴熟，却未受过抽象思维的训练。巴基斯坦主流的学校课程鼓励死记硬背，而非批判性或抽象思维，因此理论在他们感觉中是陌生的、无用的。花剌子米的一生却给出了相反的答案：他的代数学脱胎于彻头彻尾的实际问题——继承纠纷、土地测量、开凿运河——但它的力量在于超越了这些问题本身，成为一种足够柔韧的方法，足以结构化任何 9 世纪的行政官员都无法想象的问题。换言之，理论不是实践的对立面；理论是实践被普遍化之后的模样。这正是值得带进博雅课堂的一课——如今的课堂太常把抽象当作奢侈品，而非一种技能。

Theory itself is often the real barrier I see in the classroom, especially among students from geographically disadvantaged backgrounds who arrive well practised in applied knowledge but not in abstraction. The mainstream Pakistani school curriculum rewards rote learning over critical or abstract thinking, so theory can feel foreign rather than useful. Al-Khwarizmi’s life argues otherwise: his algebra grew out of thoroughly practical problems – inheritance disputes, land surveys, canal digging – but its power lay in outlasting them, becoming a method supple enough to structure problems no 9th-century administrator could have imagined. Theory, in other words, isn’t the opposite of practice; it’s what practice becomes once it’s generalised. That is the lesson worth carrying into a liberal arts classroom that too often treats abstraction as a luxury rather than a skill.

这几位通才的一生提醒我们：约束可以通向创造与创新。比鲁尼和海什木的例子尤其说明了这一点，尽管二人所受约束的性质并不相同：匮乏使比鲁尼亲手制造仪器，而海什木的囚禁则迫使他向内求索，沉思视觉与心理学之间的关联。对阿维森纳和花剌子米而言，通才之路则源于机构资助与宫廷动荡，这既给了他们辗转于医学、哲学、数学、天文学与地理学之间的空间，也给了他们这样做的理由，让他们把希腊、波斯与印度的传统编织成新的综合。

The lives of these polymaths prompt us to appreciate that constraints can lead to creativity and innovation. This is especially illustrated by the examples of al-Biruni and Alhazen, even if the nature of those constraints is different: scarcity made al-Biruni create his own instruments, while Alhazen’s captivity forced him to look inward, contemplating the connections between vision and psychology. For Avicenna and al-Khwarizmi, polymathy grew instead out of institutional patronage and courtly upheaval, which gave them both the room and the reason to move between medicine, philosophy, mathematics, astronomy and geography as they wove Greek, Persian and Indian traditions into new syntheses.

将四人连为一体的，不是他们各自遭遇的特定约束或机遇，而是他们如何对待它们：没有人让单一学科为自己的思考划定边界。批判精神也是这种拒绝的核心，海什木驳斥发射说、力主入射说便是明证：通才们并非简单地继承既有知识，而是对其反复质询。理论与实践也从来不对立，阿维森纳的临床方法与花剌子米以精确为驱动的数学便是见证：精确与抽象，同广博本身一样，都是博雅教育的基石。最后，这四段人生共同拥有的一点，是多元赞助所带来的跨文化浸润。通才之道从来不是孤独的追求，而是由跨传统的交流滋养而成。

What unites the four is not the particular constraint or opportunity each encountered, but what they did with it: none let a single discipline set a boundary on their thinking. Critique was central to this refusal, too, as Alhazen’s disputation of emission theory in favour of intromission illustrates: polymaths did not simply inherit received knowledge; they interrogated it. And theory and practice were never opposed, as Avicenna’s clinical method and al-Khwarizmi’s precision-driven mathematics attest: exactitude and abstraction are as much cornerstones of a liberal arts education as breadth itself. What each of these lives shares, finally, is the cross-cultural exposure that pluralistic patronage made possible. Polymathy has never been a solitary pursuit, but one fed by contact across traditions.

这一课，对我在巴基斯坦的学生们而言意义重大。他们中的大多数人，将在这个被人工智能、气候冲击与政治动荡重塑的世界里建立自己的事业——而这个世界风云变幻之剧，远非任何单一学科所能令他们备好应对。倘若比鲁尼、海什木、阿维森纳和花剌子米能够将匮乏、囚禁、资助与动荡转化为新的求知之道，那么广博，便是迎接前方宏图大业的必要准备。

That lesson matters for my students in Pakistan today, most of whom will build careers in a world reshaped by AI, climate disruption and political upheaval far more volatile than any single discipline can prepare them for. If al-Biruni, Alhazen, Avicenna and al-Khwarizmi could turn scarcity, captivity, patronage and instability into new ways of knowing, then breadth is the necessary preparation for the tremendous work ahead.
